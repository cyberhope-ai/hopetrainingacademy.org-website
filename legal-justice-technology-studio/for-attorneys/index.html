Why learn with us

HTA's Career Technologist brings 20 years in law-enforcement and court technology — court recording, iRecord, digital evidence and the systems behind public-safety and court operations — connecting real legal workflows to the AI and cyber tools your firm uses now.

Two ways to work with us

Train your team — or grow it

Option A · Attorney modernization

Hands-on AI & cyber bootcamps

  • AI workflows for practice: cut hours off contract review, deposition summaries and client correspondence using secure enterprise AI — with source-checking and confidentiality discipline built in.
  • Cyber hygiene for small & mid-sized firms: protect client records and avoid malpractice risk from data breaches, grounded in our Security+ curriculum.
  • The "apprentice-ready" practice: learn how to delegate routine tech and AI tasks to a trained apprentice so you focus on high-value strategy.
  • Short, practical bootcamps and CLE-style workshops held in the HTA lab or at your firm.
Option B · Hire an apprentice

A pre-trained, AI-ready pipeline

  • DOL-registered apprentices trained in legal AI, e-discovery, document automation and law-firm cybersecurity.
  • Roles like legal operations specialist, e-discovery technician and AI-enabled paralegal, plus help desk and cybersecurity support.
  • Paid from day one · no placement fee · DOL paperwork handled.
  • Mentors prepared in a structured workplace-trainer method; SkillDNA gives you a verified record of what each apprentice can do.

Apprenticeship placements are developed with actual employers and depend on role fit, eligibility and funding — they are not promised in advance.

How we keep it safe

Demonstrations use synthetic and public educational matters only — never confidential client records. AI outputs are source-checked and human-reviewed; this is training, not legal advice. Some elements, including the VR courtroom and university and legal-partner collaborations, are in active development and are confirmed before being represented as committed.
