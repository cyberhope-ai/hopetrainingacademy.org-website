What you learn — hands on

The whole legal-technology toolkit

Research & AI

Responsible legal AI

Use OpenAI, Claude and Gemini to research and summarize legal questions — with strict training on source-checking, hallucination auditing and confidentiality. AI answers must trace back to the record or authority.

VerityQ Legal Presenter

Study, organize, present

VerityQ is CyberHope AI's first legal application. In the student edition you research law, compare opinions, organize a synthetic case record, test AI answers against sources, and present in the VR courtroom. Public and synthetic educational matters only — never confidential client records.

E-discovery

Evidence & document automation

Manage digital evidence, redaction and Bates workflows, and automated intake — the real tools of modern legal operations.

CompTIA · Security+

Law-firm cybersecurity

Apply HTA's flagship Security+ training to protecting client data — ransomware prevention, secure file sharing and the ethics of confidentiality.

Build it · Unity partner

Legal-app & VR studio

Move from using AI to building useful tools — hearing-prep checklists, evidence inventories, accessibility aids. As a Unity Education partner, our apprentices build the VR courtroom itself. Every prototype includes privacy, bias and human-review controls.

Governance · PrecognitionOS

Why trustworthy systems matter

PrecognitionOS is our legal & compliance platform for AI governance. See how governed AI, evidence lineage and auditable workflows support regulated legal and compliance work — not just how to prompt a chatbot.

What a studio cohort looks like

A six-session pilot

1
Justice-tech map. How a case moves from incident to court — and where records, evidence, transcription and courtroom systems fit. You leave with a career map.
2
Research & opinions. Use the VerityQ student edition to research a public legal question, compare opinions, and build a verified source ledger.
3
Evidence & governance. Digital recording, transcript workflows, evidence lineage and the human responsibility behind an accurate record — synthetic scenarios only.
4
Legal-app studio. Small teams prototype a useful, safe tool — a hearing-prep checklist, evidence inventory or source-verification helper — with an AI-risk checklist.
5
VR courtroom rehearsal. Organize exhibits, practice direct and opposing questions, rehearse an argument and present in the VR-enabled courtroom.
6
Careers & demo day. Connect the work to legal operations, help desk, QA, cybersecurity and court technology — and build an apprenticeship-readiness plan.
Built responsibly

No real client, police, court or confidential data is ever used — synthetic and public educational matters only. AI outputs must be source-checked; this is training, not legal advice, and human review is mandatory. We follow FERPA-aligned handling with parental permissions where required and age-appropriate tools. The VR courtroom and some partner elements are in active development; participation by outside institutions is confirmed before it is represented as committed.
