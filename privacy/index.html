Privacy Policy

Hope Training Academy — a program of Video Game Palooza Inc, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit

Effective date: September 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 10, 2026

Video Game Palooza Inc, doing business as Hope Training Academy ("Hope Training Academy," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through hopetrainingacademy.org and our communications, how we use it, and the choices you have — including for our SMS/text messaging program.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, and program interests when you submit a contact or enrollment form, request information, or communicate with us.
  • Automatically collected: basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used to operate and improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide information about our training programs and services.
  • To process enrollment and support your participation in our programs.
  • To send you administrative messages, appointment reminders, and — with your consent — updates about programs you have expressed interest in.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of our organization and community.

SMS / Text Messaging Program

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you agree to receive SMS/text messages from Hope Training Academy related to your inquiry, enrollment, appointment reminders, and program information. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or enrollment.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. All other categories of data exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, we will send a one-time confirmation and will no longer send SMS messages. For help, reply HELP or contact us at the information below. You may resubscribe at any time by opting in again.

How you opt in

You provide express consent to receive text messages by submitting your mobile number through a form on our website or by directly requesting text communication from us, and by affirmatively agreeing to receive messages at the point of collection.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing. We may share information only with service providers who help us operate our programs and communications, under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law. As stated above, SMS opt-in and consent information is never shared with third parties or affiliates.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of communications at any time using the methods described above or by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to adults and prospective students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.
