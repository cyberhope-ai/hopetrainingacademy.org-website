How to type faster: 6 tips that work

  • Use home row. Rest your fingers on A S D F and J K L ; and return there after every key.
  • Don't look down. Looking at the keys caps your speed. Accuracy first; speed follows.
  • Slow down to speed up. Practice at a pace where you make almost no mistakes, then push a little.
  • Practice daily, briefly. 10-15 minutes a day beats one long session a week.
  • Sit well. Wrists level, screen at eye height, feet flat.
  • Measure. Retake this test once a week to track your WPM.

Typical guide: around 40 WPM is a common everyday speed; many office and data-entry jobs ask for 40-60+ WPM with high accuracy.
