Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

What is a Registered Apprenticeship?

A paid job with structured training and mentoring, registered with the U.S. Department of Labor. When you finish, you earn a nationally recognized, portable credential.

Do I need experience or a degree?

No. Beginners start with a hands-on pre-apprenticeship on-ramp. You'll need reliable attendance and a willingness to learn.

What does it cost?

Registration, your skills profile and your success-coach conversation are free. Training is free for those who qualify for funding. Optional boosters are only for those who want them.

When do the AI data tracks start?

They're being added to our registered program now. Register and choose "AI data" and we'll contact you as soon as openings are confirmed.

Where is training?

At our Union Campus, 525 S Meridian St, Indianapolis, with online options for many courses.
