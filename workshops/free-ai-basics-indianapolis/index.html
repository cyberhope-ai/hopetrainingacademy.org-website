What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is technology that can recognize patterns, understand language, and generate text, images, and answers. It powers the chat assistants, photo tools, and smart features showing up in the apps people already use every day.

Think of today's AI tools as a very fast assistant: great at drafting, summarizing, and brainstorming, but they still need a person to ask the right question and check the answer. This workshop shows you how to do both, in plain language, with no technical background required.

How it grew

1950Alan Turing asks "Can machines think?" and proposes a test for machine intelligence.
1956The term "artificial intelligence" is coined at a summer research workshop at Dartmouth College.
1997IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
2022ChatGPT is released to the public, bringing generative AI to everyday users.
