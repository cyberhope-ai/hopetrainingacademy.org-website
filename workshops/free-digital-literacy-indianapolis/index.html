What is digital literacy?

Digital literacy is the everyday ability to find, use, and create information with technology: working a computer or phone, getting around the internet, sending email, filling out online forms, and knowing how to stay safe while you do it.

Think of it as the modern version of reading and writing. Job applications, benefits, banking, school portals, and doctor's appointments have all moved online. This workshop gives you a calm, hands-on starting point, with an instructor in the room to answer your questions.

How it grew

1971The first email is sent between networked computers, starting the age of digital communication.
1991The World Wide Web opens to the public, putting information a click away.
2007The smartphone era begins, putting the internet in almost every pocket.
2020Work, school, health care, and government services move online at massive scale.
