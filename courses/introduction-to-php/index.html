What is this field?

PHP (Hypertext Preprocessor) is a powerful, widely-used open-source scripting language especially suited for web development. It runs on the server, processing requests and generating dynamic HTML content that is then sent to the user's browser. This enables websites to be interactive, personalized, and connected to databases, forming the backbone of countless online applications, from e-commerce sites to social media platforms.

Think of PHP as the chef in a bustling restaurant kitchen. When you order a meal (request a webpage), PHP takes your order, gathers the necessary ingredients (data from a database), cooks up a custom dish (generates a dynamic webpage tailored to your request), and serves it to you. Without PHP, many of the interactive and data-driven experiences we expect online wouldn't be possible.

How the field grew

1994Rasmus Lerdorf creates 'Personal Home Page' tools, the precursor to PHP.
1995PHP/FI (Personal Home Page/Forms Interpreter) is publicly released.
1997PHP 3.0 is released, a complete rewrite that significantly expands its capabilities and user base.
2004PHP 5.0 introduces the powerful Zend Engine 2.0 and robust object-oriented programming features.
2015PHP 7.0 is released, bringing significant performance improvements and new language features.
