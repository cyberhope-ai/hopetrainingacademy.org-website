What is this field?

Brain health encompasses the ability to think, learn, and remember effectively, alongside maintaining emotional balance and overall mental well-being. It's about nurturing your most vital organ to ensure it functions optimally throughout your life, impacting everything from your mood to your decision-making abilities.

Think of your brain like a high-performance engine that requires the right fuel, regular maintenance, and occasional tune-ups to run smoothly. This program provides you with the owner's manual, offering insights and practical strategies based on recent scientific studies to keep your cognitive engine in peak condition.

How the field grew

1990sThe 'Decade of the Brain' initiative significantly advanced neuroscience research and public awareness.
Early 2000sEmergence of public interest and programs focused on cognitive training and brain fitness.
2010sGrowing scientific consensus on lifestyle's impact on neuroplasticity and long-term brain health.
