What is this field?

An Optical Manager oversees the daily operations of an optometry practice or optical retail store, ensuring smooth patient flow, managing inventory, handling financial records, and leading a team. This role combines clinical knowledge of eyewear dispensing with essential business administration skills, making them a vital link between patients, optometrists, and the business's success. They are often responsible for everything from ordering frames and lenses to training staff and ensuring customer satisfaction.

Think of an Optical Manager as the conductor of an orchestra, where the optometrist is the lead soloist and the patients are the audience. The manager ensures all instruments (staff, inventory, equipment) are in tune, the sheet music (prescriptions, financial records) is organized, and the performance (patient experience) is flawless. They blend technical expertise in optics with sharp business acumen to keep the entire operation running efficiently and profitably.

How the field grew

1950sThe American Board of Opticianry (ABO) was established, setting standards for optician certification.
1970sThe Contact Lens Registry Exam (CLRE) was developed, recognizing specialized skills in contact lens dispensing.
1983QuickBooks software was first released, revolutionizing small business accounting and financial management.
PresentOngoing advancements in optical technology and business practices continue to shape the role of the modern Optical Manager.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, this program is designed for individuals seeking a direct path into the optical field. While a high school diploma or GED is generally recommended for certification exams, a college degree is not required to enroll or pursue this career.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, upon successful completion of this program, you will receive a voucher to sit for the QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) exam. Please note that vouchers for the American Board of Opticianry (ABO) exams (NOCE/CLRE) are not included.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This program is self-paced and includes 322 course hours. The actual time to complete it will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program thoroughly prepares you for the current American Board of Opticianry (ABO) exams, including the National Opticianry Competency Exam (NOCE) and the Contact Lens Registry Exam (CLRE). It also prepares you for the QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Optician, Optical Practice Manager, Contact Lens Dispenser, or Optical Sales Associate. This training equips you with both the technical skills for dispensing and the management skills for office operations.
