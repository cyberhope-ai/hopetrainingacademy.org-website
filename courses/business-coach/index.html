What is this field?

A Business Coach is a strategic partner who guides entrepreneurs and small business owners through their unique challenges, helping them clarify goals, develop actionable strategies, and implement changes to improve performance and achieve their vision. This role involves analyzing business operations, identifying areas for improvement, and providing expert advice on everything from marketing and finance to operational efficiency and client acquisition.

Think of a business coach as a seasoned guide for a mountain climber. While the climber has the ambition and strength, the guide provides the map, points out the safest and most efficient routes, helps navigate obstacles, and ensures the climber has the right tools and mindset to reach the summit. Similarly, a business coach provides the frameworks, insights, and accountability to help business owners navigate the complexities of growth and reach their business goals.

How the field grew

1980sEmergence of executive coaching, laying groundwork for broader business advisory.
1990sExpansion of coaching into diverse fields, including dedicated business coaching for entrepreneurs.
2000sDigital transformation enables remote coaching and broader access to business advisory services.
2010sIncreased demand for specialized coaching, focusing on niche markets and specific business challenges.
2020sPost-pandemic surge in entrepreneurship and the need for agile, results-driven business support.
