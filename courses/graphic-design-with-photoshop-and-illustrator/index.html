What is this field?

Graphic design is the art of visual communication, combining images, typography, and ideas to convey messages to an audience. It's a critical skill in today's digital world, where businesses rely on compelling visuals to build brand identity, engage customers, and drive marketing efforts across web, social media, advertising, and print platforms. Skilled graphic designers are essential for creating everything from logos and websites to marketing materials and digital art.

Think of a graphic designer as a visual storyteller. Just as an author uses words to craft a narrative, a graphic designer uses colors, shapes, fonts, and images to tell a brand's story, explain a product, or evoke an emotion. This program equips you with both the creative principles and the technical tools, like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, to effectively communicate through design and bring your visual ideas to life.

How the field grew

1990sDigital design tools like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator revolutionize visual creation.
2000sExplosion of web design and digital media, expanding the roles of graphic designers.
2010sIncreased focus on user experience (UX) and mobile-first design principles.
2020sIntegration of artificial intelligence and generative design techniques into workflows.

What the career pays

Graphic designers create visual concepts, using computer software or by hand, to communicate ideas that inspire, inform, or captivate consumers. They develop the overall layout and production design for advertisements, brochures, magazines, and corporate reports.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a graphic designer?

No, a degree is not required to enroll in this career training program or to succeed in graphic design. This program focuses on practical skills and portfolio development, which are highly valued by employers.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher is not included as this program focuses on skill development and portfolio creation rather than a specific certification exam.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This program consists of 165 course hours. As it is self-paced, the total time to completion will depend on your individual study schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to build comprehensive graphic design skills and a professional portfolio using industry-standard tools like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, rather than preparing for a single certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Graphic Designer, Visual Designer, Marketing Designer, or Digital Artist, working in agencies, in-house teams, or as a freelancer.
