What is this field?

Technology integration in the classroom is the strategic and thoughtful use of digital tools and resources to enhance learning, improve teaching methods, and foster student engagement. It moves beyond simply using computers to embedding technology seamlessly into the curriculum, making it an integral part of the educational process.

Think of it like a master chef using a new, advanced kitchen appliance. They don't just use it for the sake of it; they integrate it into their cooking process to create better dishes, improve efficiency, and offer new culinary experiences. Similarly, educators learn to leverage technology to create richer, more interactive, and personalized learning environments for their students.

How the field grew

1980sIntroduction of personal computers in schools, primarily for computer literacy.
1990sEmergence of the internet and early educational software, connecting classrooms globally.
2000sWidespread adoption of interactive whiteboards and Learning Management Systems (LMS).
2010sRise of mobile devices, cloud computing, and personalized learning platforms.
2020sFocus on AI, virtual/augmented reality, and data-driven instruction in education.
