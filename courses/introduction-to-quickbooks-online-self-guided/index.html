What is this field?

QuickBooks Online is a powerful, cloud-based accounting software designed to help businesses manage their finances efficiently. It allows users to track income and expenses, pay bills, invoice customers, generate reports, and much more, all from virtually anywhere with an internet connection. Think of it as your digital financial assistant, organizing every transaction and providing a clear picture of your business's health.

This program teaches you how to leverage QuickBooks Online to its full potential, transforming complex financial tasks into manageable steps. You'll learn to navigate the platform, set up your company, process transactions, and generate essential financial statements, equipping you with the skills to maintain accurate records and make informed business decisions.

How the field grew

1983Intuit, the creator of QuickBooks, is founded.
1992The first QuickBooks desktop software is released, revolutionizing small business accounting.
2001QuickBooks Online (QBO) is launched, bringing cloud-based accounting to the forefront.
2013QBO undergoes a major redesign, enhancing user experience and solidifying its cloud-first strategy.
PresentQuickBooks Online continues to evolve, integrating AI and advanced features to meet modern business needs.
