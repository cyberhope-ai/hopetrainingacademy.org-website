What is this field?

Information Systems Auditors are critical guardians of an organization's digital assets, ensuring that IT systems are secure, compliant, and operate effectively. They assess vulnerabilities, manage risks, and implement robust controls to protect sensitive data and prevent security breaches. This specialized role combines IT expertise with auditing principles to safeguard information integrity and business continuity in an increasingly complex digital landscape.

Think of an Information Systems Auditor as a detective for digital infrastructure. Just as a financial auditor examines ledgers and transactions to ensure accuracy and compliance, an IS Auditor scrutinizes IT systems, processes, and data flows to identify weaknesses, ensure regulatory adherence, and verify that security measures are functioning as intended, ultimately protecting the organization from cyber threats and operational failures.

How the field grew

1969The Information Systems Audit and Control Association (ISACA) is founded to address the growing need for IT governance.
1978ISACA introduces the Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) credential, establishing a global standard for IT audit professionals.
OngoingThe CISA certification content is regularly updated to reflect the latest advancements in information technology, security threats, and auditing practices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA)?

While a degree can be beneficial, the CISA certification primarily requires a minimum of five years of professional information systems auditing, control, or security work experience. This program focuses on preparing you for the certification exam, and practical experience is key to earning the credential.

Does this training program include an exam voucher for the CISA certification?

No, an exam voucher for the current ISACA CISA certification exam is not included with this specific training program purchase.

How long does the CISA training program take?

The core training consists of 40 intensive course hours, typically delivered over five days. Additionally, successful preparation for the CISA exam requires dedicated self-study and practice outside of the structured course time.

Which CISA exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to thoroughly prepare you for the current ISACA CISA certification exam, covering the latest domains and knowledge areas required by the industry.

What kind of jobs can I get with CISA certification?

A CISA certification opens doors to roles such as Information Systems Auditor, IT Audit Manager, Information Security Analyst, Risk and Compliance Analyst, and other positions focused on ensuring the security, control, and auditability of an organization's information systems.
