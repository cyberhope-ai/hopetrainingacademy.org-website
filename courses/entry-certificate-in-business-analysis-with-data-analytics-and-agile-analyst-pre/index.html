What is this field?

Business analysis is the critical practice of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. This often involves understanding organizational structure, policies, and operations, and then recommending improvements that leverage technology or process changes. Data analytics complements this by extracting insights from data, helping to inform decisions and validate proposed solutions.

Think of a business analyst as the architect and translator for a company's future. They don't just build; they understand what's needed, design the blueprint, and ensure everyone speaks the same language, from stakeholders to technical teams. Agile analysis then adds the flexibility to adapt these plans quickly, ensuring projects stay relevant and deliver value in fast-changing environments.

How the field grew

1990sEmergence of business analysis as a distinct professional discipline.
2003International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) founded.
2006Publication of the first Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK) Guide.
2011Introduction of the Agile Extension to the BABOK Guide.
2016Release of BABOK Guide v3.0, expanding the scope of business analysis.
