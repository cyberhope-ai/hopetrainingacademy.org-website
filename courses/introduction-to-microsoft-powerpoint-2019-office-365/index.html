What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint is the industry-standard software for creating dynamic and engaging presentations. It allows users to combine text, images, charts, and multimedia into visually appealing slides to convey information, persuade audiences, and support public speaking engagements. From business proposals to educational lectures, PowerPoint is an indispensable tool for effective communication in nearly every professional setting.

Think of PowerPoint as your visual storyteller. Just as a writer uses words to craft a narrative, PowerPoint helps you use slides to build a compelling visual story. This program teaches you how to design, organize, and deliver presentations that captivate your audience and clearly communicate your message, transforming complex ideas into easily digestible visual content.

How the field grew

1987Forethought Inc. develops 'Presenter', later renamed PowerPoint.
1987Microsoft acquires PowerPoint, recognizing its potential.
1990PowerPoint 2.0 for Windows is released as part of Microsoft Office 1.0.
Early 2000sPowerPoint becomes the dominant software for business and educational presentations worldwide.
2019/Office 365Integration with cloud services and advanced features enhance collaboration and design capabilities.
