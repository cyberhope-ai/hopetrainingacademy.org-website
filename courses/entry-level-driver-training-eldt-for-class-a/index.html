What is this field?

Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) for a Class A Commercial Driver's License (CDL) is the federally mandated theoretical instruction required for individuals seeking to operate heavy and tractor-trailer trucks. This program covers the foundational knowledge and regulations essential for safe and compliant commercial driving, serving as the crucial first step before you can begin behind-the-wheel training.

Think of it as the comprehensive classroom instruction you need to understand the rules of the road, vehicle mechanics, and safety protocols for large commercial motor vehicles. This training ensures you meet the minimum standards set by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA), preparing you for the practical skills required to earn your Class A CDL.

How the field grew

2012The Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21) mandates Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) for commercial drivers.
2016The FMCSA publishes the final rule establishing comprehensive ELDT requirements for CDL applicants.
2022ELDT regulations become effective, requiring all new commercial drivers to complete specific training before taking skills tests.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Basic Vehicle Operation

Control systems, pre- and post-trip inspections, shifting, backing, coupling and uncoupling.

02
Safe Operating Procedures

Visual search, communication, distracted driving, speed management, space management, night and extreme weather operation.

03
Advanced Operating Practices

Hazard perception, skid control, jackknifing, emergency maneuvers, and railroad-highway grade crossings.

04
Vehicle Systems & Malfunctions

Identification and diagnosis of malfunctions, roadside inspections, and vehicle maintenance.

05
Non-Driving Activities

Cargo handling, environmental compliance, hours of service, fatigue awareness, post-crash procedures, trip planning, and substance abuse.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a truck driver?

No, a college degree is not required. This program focuses on the specific training and licensing needed to enter the trucking industry.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CDL test?

This program does not include a voucher for the state-administered Class A CDL skills test or any other state exam charges. It covers the required ELDT theory exams.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

This self-paced program consists of 40 course hours, allowing you to complete the federal theory requirements at your own pace.

Does this program prepare me for the current Class A CDL exam?

Yes, this program is designed to meet all current federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) theory requirements established by the FMCSA for the Class A Commercial Driver's License.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

Upon successful completion of this theory training and subsequent behind-the-wheel training and state CDL testing, you can pursue roles such as a Heavy and Tractor-Trailer Truck Driver, Long Haul Truck Driver, or Commercial Driver.
