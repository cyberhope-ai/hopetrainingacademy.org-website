What is this field?

The Certificate in Holistic and Integrative Health: Foundations 2 program delves into diverse healing philosophies, blending ancient wisdom with modern wellness practices. You'll explore the foundational principles of Ayurvedic and Traditional Chinese Medicine, understand the role of spirituality in well-being, and learn practical meditation techniques.

Think of it like learning the foundational ingredients and cooking techniques for a diverse and healthy global cuisine, preparing you to understand and apply various approaches to holistic well-being.

How the field grew

2000 BCEAyurvedic medicine flourishes in ancient India, establishing principles of holistic health.
200 BCEThe Huangdi Neijing (Yellow Emperor's Classic of Internal Medicine) lays the theoretical foundation for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM).
1970sThe holistic health movement gains significant traction in Western medicine, emphasizing mind-body connection.
1998The U.S. National Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine (NCCAM) is established, fostering research into integrative therapies.
2010sIntegrative health models become increasingly adopted in clinical settings, blending conventional and complementary approaches.
