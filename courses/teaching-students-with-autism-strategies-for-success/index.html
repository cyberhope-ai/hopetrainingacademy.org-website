What is this field?

This program is designed for educators seeking to enhance their skills in supporting students with high-functioning autism and Asperger's Syndrome. You will learn evidence-based strategies to create an inclusive and effective learning environment, fostering academic success and social-emotional growth for every student.

Think of it like learning a specialized communication toolkit. Just as a linguist learns new languages to connect with diverse cultures, this program provides you with specific techniques and insights to better understand and interact with students on the autism spectrum, helping them unlock their unique potential in the classroom.

How the field grew

1940sEarly clinical descriptions of autism emerge, laying groundwork for understanding.
1970s-1980sShift towards inclusion in education, emphasizing individualized support and behavioral therapies for students with autism.
1990sIncreased recognition of Asperger's Syndrome and the importance of tailored educational plans.
2000s-PresentFocus on neurodiversity, strengths-based approaches, and comprehensive inclusive classroom strategies.
