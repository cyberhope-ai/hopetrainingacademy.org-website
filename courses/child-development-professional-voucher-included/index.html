What is this field?

A Child Development Professional is dedicated to fostering the holistic growth of young children, from birth through age five. This vital role involves understanding developmental stages, creating safe and stimulating learning environments, and implementing age-appropriate activities that encourage cognitive, linguistic, physical, social, and emotional development. Professionals in this field often collaborate with families and ensure high standards of care and education.

This comprehensive career training program equips you with the essential knowledge for early childhood education, preparing you for the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential. Additionally, it provides in-depth training and certification preparation for Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Think of it like building a strong foundation for a child's future (CDA) while also mastering the essential tools to efficiently manage and administer educational programs (MOS).

How the field grew

1985The Council for Professional Recognition was established, leading to the creation of the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential.
1990Microsoft Office suite was first released, beginning its journey to revolutionize workplace productivity and administration.
Early 2000sMicrosoft Office Specialist (MOS) certifications gained prominence as a globally recognized standard for software proficiency.
PresentBoth the CDA and MOS certifications remain critical credentials for professionals aspiring to leadership roles in early childhood education and administration.
