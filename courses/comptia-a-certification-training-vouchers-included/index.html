What is this field?

The CompTIA A+ certification is the industry standard for establishing a career in IT, validating foundational knowledge across a wide range of hardware and software technologies. It's a vendor-neutral credential that proves your ability to support, troubleshoot, and maintain various computing devices and operating systems, making you a versatile asset in any organization.

Think of CompTIA A+ as your IT driver's license. It proves you understand the rules of the road (operating systems, networking), can fix common car problems (hardware/software troubleshooting), and are ready to safely navigate the digital highway. This program equips you with the practical skills needed to diagnose and resolve technical issues, ensuring smooth operations for businesses and individuals alike.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA, a leading IT industry association, is founded to advance the global technology workforce.
1999The CompTIA A+ certification is introduced, quickly becoming the benchmark for entry-level IT professionals worldwide.
Early 2000sA+ evolves to incorporate new technologies, including networking fundamentals, mobile devices, and enhanced security concepts.
PresentCompTIA A+ continues to be regularly updated, ensuring certified professionals are proficient with the latest hardware, software, and operational procedures in the ever-changing IT landscape.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to get a job with CompTIA A+ certification?

No, a college degree is not required. The CompTIA A+ certification is widely recognized by employers as proof of foundational IT skills, often serving as a direct pathway to entry-level IT roles without the need for a traditional degree.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

Yes, this CompTIA A+ Certification Training program includes exam vouchers for both required certification exams, covering the cost for you to sit for the certifying exams upon eligibility.

How long does it take to complete the CompTIA A+ training?

This program is designed for self-paced learning and includes 200 course hours. The actual time to completion can vary based on your individual study schedule and prior experience.

Which CompTIA A+ exam version does this training prepare me for?

This training program is meticulously designed to prepare you for the current/latest CompTIA A+ certification exam. CompTIA regularly updates its exams to reflect the newest technologies and industry best practices.

What kind of jobs can I get with a CompTIA A+ certification?

With a CompTIA A+ certification, you'll be qualified for various entry-level IT positions such as Help Desk Technician, IT Support Specialist, Field Service Technician, Desktop Support Analyst, and Technical Support Specialist.
