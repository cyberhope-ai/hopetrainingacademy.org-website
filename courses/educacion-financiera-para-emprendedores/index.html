What is this field?

Financial education for entrepreneurs is a vital training program designed to equip small business owners, freelancers, and new entrepreneurs with the knowledge and tools to make smart financial decisions. It covers critical areas such as business budgeting, cash flow management, financial planning, risk prevention, and understanding tax fundamentals in the United States. In today's competitive market, mastering these financial competencies is crucial for sustaining and scaling any business.

Think of your business finances like the engine of a car. Without proper maintenance, fuel, and understanding of its components, even the best car won't run efficiently or reliably. This program teaches you how to be the expert mechanic for your business's financial engine, ensuring it runs smoothly, avoids costly breakdowns, and is ready for growth.

How the field grew

1953The Small Business Administration (SBA) is founded, underscoring the importance of small businesses to the U.S. economy.
1980sThe rise of personal computing and early accounting software begins to democratize financial record-keeping for small businesses.
Early 2000sThe advent of online banking and cloud-based accounting platforms transforms how entrepreneurs manage their finances, making tools more accessible.
2020sIncreased focus on digital payment systems, financial resilience, and strategic planning becomes paramount for entrepreneurs navigating a dynamic global market.
