What is this field?

Pharmacy technicians are vital members of the healthcare team, working under the supervision of a licensed pharmacist to ensure patients receive the correct medications safely and efficiently. They manage prescription orders, prepare medications, handle administrative tasks, and interact directly with patients, providing essential support that allows pharmacists to focus on clinical responsibilities. Think of them as the skilled navigators and organizers behind the scenes, ensuring the right medicine gets to the right person at the right time.

This career training program equips you with the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to thrive in this role. You'll learn medical and pharmaceutical terminology, understand basic anatomy relevant to pharmacology, master pharmaceutical calculations, and gain hands-on experience with retail pharmacy procedures through an online lab. The curriculum also covers ethical practices, patient interaction, and the complexities of prescription processing and reimbursement, preparing you to confidently pursue entry-level positions and contribute meaningfully to patient care.

How the field grew

1995Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB) established to standardize certification.
1996The first Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE) was administered.
2001The PTCB's Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT) credential gained wider recognition across states.
2015PTCB updated eligibility requirements, emphasizing formal education or equivalent work experience.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this career training program does not require a college degree. It is designed to prepare you for entry-level pharmacy technician roles and the certification exam with a high school diploma or equivalent.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, this program includes a voucher that covers the fee for the Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE) offered by the Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB).

How long does it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced program with 400 course hours. The actual completion time varies based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current/latest Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE) to obtain the Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT) credential from the Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for entry-level pharmacy technician positions in various settings like retail pharmacies, hospitals, and other healthcare facilities. Please note that this course does not meet state requirements for students intending to work in Florida, Ohio, North Dakota, Utah, Washington, and West Virginia, and it is designed for the U.S. healthcare system.
