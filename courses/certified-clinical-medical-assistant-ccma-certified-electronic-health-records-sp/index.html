What is this field?

A Clinical Medical Assistant (CCMA) is a versatile healthcare professional who performs a blend of administrative and clinical tasks in various medical settings, from doctor's offices to clinics and hospitals. They are crucial to patient care, handling everything from taking vital signs and assisting with minor procedures to managing patient records and scheduling appointments. An Electronic Health Records Specialist (CEHRS) focuses specifically on the digital management of patient information, ensuring accuracy, security, and accessibility of electronic health records.

Think of a CCMA as the central hub of a healthcare practice, ensuring every patient interaction, from check-in to follow-up, runs smoothly and efficiently. The CEHRS is like the meticulous digital librarian, safeguarding and organizing all patient health data so that care providers have instant, accurate information at their fingertips.

How the field grew

1996HIPAA enacted, increasing the importance of patient data privacy and security.
Early 2000sEmergence of electronic health records (EHR) systems to modernize patient data management.
2009HITECH Act passed, accelerating the widespread adoption and meaningful use of EHR technology across healthcare.
PresentContinued high demand for skilled healthcare support professionals capable of managing both clinical duties and advanced digital record-keeping.
