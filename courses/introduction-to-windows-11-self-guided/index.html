What is this field?

Windows 11 is Microsoft's latest operating system, designed to deliver a more intuitive, powerful, and unique computing experience. This foundational course equips you with the essential knowledge to confidently navigate, customize, and optimize your Windows 11 PC right from the start.

Think of an operating system like the conductor of an orchestra for your computer; it manages all the hardware and software, allowing them to work together harmoniously. Mastering Windows 11 means you'll understand how to direct your digital environment, enhancing your productivity and control.

How the field grew

1995Windows 95 launches, revolutionizing personal computing with the iconic Start menu.
2001Windows XP becomes a global standard, known for its stability and user-friendliness.
2015Windows 10 is released, introducing "Windows as a Service" with continuous updates.
2021Windows 11 debuts with a modern redesign, enhanced performance, and new productivity features.
