What is this field?

American Sign Language (ASL) is a vibrant, visual language used by millions of people in North America. This foundational program provides an immersive, silent learning experience, focusing on proper sign techniques and essential facial expressions to convey meaning effectively. You'll gain the skills to understand and engage in basic conversations, opening doors to new communities and cultural insights.

Just as learning a spoken language connects you to new cultures, mastering ASL allows you to bridge communication gaps and interact with the Deaf and hard-of-hearing communities. Our hands-on approach ensures you don't just memorize signs, but truly understand the nuances of ASL, empowering you to communicate confidently and respectfully.

How the field grew

1817The American School for the Deaf is founded, a pivotal moment in the development and standardization of ASL.
1960sASL is formally recognized as a distinct and complete language, separate from English.
1988The 'Deaf President Now' movement at Gallaudet University raises national awareness and respect for ASL and Deaf culture.
2000sThe rise of online learning platforms significantly expands access to ASL education for a global audience.
