What is this field?

Writing for children is a specialized craft that involves creating stories, poems, and non-fiction works tailored to young audiences, from toddlers to young adults. It requires a unique blend of imagination, empathy, and an understanding of child development to craft narratives that are engaging, age-appropriate, and often carry meaningful messages. This training program guides you through the process of developing compelling characters, imaginative plots, and a distinctive voice that resonates with young readers.

Think of it like building a miniature world: you're not just telling a story, you're designing an entire experience for a child. Every word, every illustration (even if you're not the illustrator), and every plot twist must be carefully considered to capture their attention, spark their curiosity, and leave a lasting impression, much like a master toy designer creates a beloved plaything.

How the field grew

17th CenturyEarly moralistic tales and fables, like those by Aesop, begin to be adapted for children, laying groundwork for distinct children's literature.
19th CenturyThe 'Golden Age' of children's literature emerges with classics like 'Alice's Adventures in Wonderland' and 'Peter Pan,' emphasizing imagination and adventure.
Mid-20th CenturyPicture books gain prominence, and authors like Dr. Seuss revolutionize storytelling with playful language and illustrations.
Late 20th/Early 21st CenturyChildren's literature diversifies, addressing complex themes and featuring a wider range of characters and cultural experiences, reflecting global society.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a children's author?

No, a formal degree is not required to become a successful children's author. This program focuses on practical writing skills and industry knowledge that are essential for creating and publishing children's books.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

No, an exam voucher is not included as this course does not lead to an external certification exam. The focus is on developing your writing craft and understanding the publishing process.

How long does it take to complete the Writing for Children program?

This program is self-paced, but typically involves 24 hours of course material over six weeks. You can progress through the lessons at a pace that fits your schedule.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

No, this program is designed to teach you the skills and knowledge needed to write and publish children's books, rather than preparing for a specific certification exam. Your portfolio of work will be your primary credential.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be equipped to pursue roles as a children's book author, freelance writer for youth publications, or a content creator specializing in children's media. Many graduates also apply these skills to editing or publishing roles within the children's literature industry.
