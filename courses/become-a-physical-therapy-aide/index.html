What is this field?

Physical Therapy Aides are essential support staff in physical therapy settings, assisting physical therapists and physical therapist assistants with patient care and administrative tasks. They help prepare treatment areas, move patients, clean equipment, and manage schedules, ensuring the smooth operation of clinics and the comfort of patients during their rehabilitation journey.

Think of a Physical Therapy Aide as the pit crew for a race car driver. While the physical therapist (the driver) focuses on the high-level strategy and execution of treatment, the aide (the pit crew) ensures all the equipment is ready, the environment is safe, and everything runs efficiently behind the scenes, allowing the therapist to perform at their best and patients to receive optimal care.
