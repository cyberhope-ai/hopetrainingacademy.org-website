What is this field?

WordPress is the most widely used content management system (CMS) globally, powering over 40% of all websites. From personal blogs to major corporate sites, its versatility allows individuals and businesses to create, manage, and publish content with remarkable ease and flexibility. Mastering WordPress opens doors to a vast array of opportunities in web development and digital content management.

Think of WordPress as a comprehensive toolkit for building a house. Instead of needing to be a master carpenter who cuts every piece of wood, WordPress provides the foundational structure, pre-built walls, and a roof. You then choose your architectural style (themes), add specialized rooms (plugins), and furnish it with your unique content. This allows you to construct a professional, functional website without writing complex code from scratch.

How the field grew

2003WordPress 1.0 'Davis' released, establishing its foundation as a blogging platform.
2005Introduction of themes and plugins, significantly expanding customization and functionality.
2010Custom Post Types and Custom Taxonomies added, transforming WordPress into a full-fledged CMS.
2018Gutenberg block editor launched, revolutionizing the content creation experience.
