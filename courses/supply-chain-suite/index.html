What is this field?

Supply chain management is the intricate process of overseeing the entire flow of goods and services, from raw materials to final consumption. It involves planning, executing, and optimizing every step, including sourcing, manufacturing, inventory, logistics, and delivery, to ensure efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Think of it like the conductor of a grand orchestra, where each musician (supplier, manufacturer, distributor, retailer) must play their part in perfect harmony and timing. Our program equips you with the skills to orchestrate this complex process, ensuring products move seamlessly and cost-effectively to meet market demands.

How the field grew

1980sThe term 'Supply Chain Management' gains prominence, emphasizing integration beyond traditional logistics.
1990sGlobalization and advancements in information technology (IT) lead to widespread adoption of Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems.
2000sIncreased focus on sustainability, ethical sourcing, and risk management within global supply chains.
2020sDigital transformation accelerates with AI, automation, and predictive analytics becoming crucial for supply chain resilience.
