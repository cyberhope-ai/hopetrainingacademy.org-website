What is this field?

Catering is the art and business of providing food and beverage services for events, gatherings, and organizations, often off-site. It involves everything from menu creation and food preparation to logistics, presentation, and client management. A successful caterer combines culinary skill with sharp business acumen to deliver memorable experiences.

This online program dives deep into the 'secrets' of professional catering, equipping you with the practical knowledge to not only cook delicious food but also to manage the complexities of a catering business. Think of it like learning to bake a perfect cake (the culinary skill) while also mastering how to run a profitable bakery (the business side) – ensuring every event you cater is a success, both culinarily and financially.

How the field grew

1700sFormal catering services emerge for European aristocracy and elite social events.
1800sExpansion of professional catering as industrialization and urbanization create demand for commercial food services.
1900sCatering evolves into a specialized industry, offering diverse services for corporate, private, and institutional clients.
2000sRise of niche catering, focus on dietary needs, sustainability, and unique culinary experiences.

What the career pays

Caterers plan, prepare, and serve food for various events, from intimate gatherings to large corporate functions. They manage all aspects of food service, including menu design, ingredient sourcing, staff coordination, and client relations, ensuring a seamless culinary experience.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a caterer?

No, formal degrees are not typically required to start a catering business. This program focuses on practical skills and business knowledge, which are often more valuable in this field.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this course does not include an exam voucher as it is a skills-based training program rather than a certification preparation course.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program. While completion times vary, most students dedicate a few hours per week and finish within 6-8 weeks.

Is there a certification exam for this course?

This program does not lead to a specific certification exam. It is designed to provide you with the practical knowledge and skills to successfully operate a catering business.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared to launch your own catering business, work as a catering manager, event chef, or take on roles requiring advanced culinary and business skills in the food service industry.
