What is this field?

QuickBooks Online is a leading cloud-based accounting software designed to help businesses manage their finances efficiently. It allows users to track income and expenses, process invoices, manage payroll, reconcile bank accounts, and generate comprehensive financial reports, all from anywhere with an internet connection. This program equips you with the essential skills to navigate and utilize this powerful tool effectively.

Think of QuickBooks Online as your business's digital financial command center. Just as a pilot uses an aircraft's dashboard to monitor vital systems and navigate, you'll use QuickBooks Online to oversee your company's financial health, ensuring every transaction is recorded, every bill is paid, and every dollar is accounted for, providing a clear picture of where your business stands.

How the field grew

1983Intuit, the creator of QuickBooks, was founded.
1992The first QuickBooks Desktop software was launched, revolutionizing small business accounting.
2001QuickBooks Online (initially 'QuickBooks for the Web') was introduced, bringing accounting to the cloud.
2013QuickBooks Online underwent a significant redesign, enhancing user experience and functionality.
2020sContinued innovation with AI integration, expanded app ecosystem, and focus on cloud-first features.
