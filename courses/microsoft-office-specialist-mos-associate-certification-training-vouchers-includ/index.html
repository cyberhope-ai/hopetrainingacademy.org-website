What is this field?

The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Associate certification validates your proficiency in the core Microsoft Office applications: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. It's a foundational credential that demonstrates to employers you possess the essential digital literacy and productivity skills required in virtually any modern professional environment.

Think of this certification like a universal key to the digital office. Just as a driver's license proves you can operate a vehicle safely and efficiently, the MOS Associate credential confirms you can navigate and expertly utilize the most common office software, making you a reliable and productive contributor from day one in a wide range of roles.

How the field grew

1980sMicrosoft Office applications begin to emerge, revolutionizing workplace productivity.
1990sThe Microsoft Office suite becomes the dominant standard for business software worldwide.
1997Microsoft introduces its first official certification programs to validate user and professional skills.
Early 2000sThe Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification gains widespread recognition as a benchmark for office proficiency.
PresentThe MOS Associate certification remains a vital credential, continuously updated to reflect the latest software versions and workplace demands.
