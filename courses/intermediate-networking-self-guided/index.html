What is this field?

Intermediate Networking builds upon foundational IT knowledge, focusing on the complex systems that allow computers and devices to communicate securely and efficiently across local and global networks. This training delves into crucial topics such as Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), robust network security protocols, and the intricacies of Internet connectivity, preparing individuals to manage and troubleshoot more sophisticated network environments.

Think of intermediate networking as becoming a skilled traffic controller for a bustling city's digital highway system. While basic networking teaches you how to build roads and traffic lights, intermediate networking teaches you how to design secure tunnels (VPNs), set up surveillance and patrol routes to prevent accidents and crime (security), and optimize traffic flow across different cities and countries (Internet connectivity). You learn to keep the data moving safely and reliably.

How the field grew

1969ARPANET, the precursor to the Internet, is established, laying the groundwork for modern networking.
1980sThe widespread adoption of TCP/IP protocols standardizes network communication, enabling global connectivity.
1990sThe commercialization of the Internet and the rise of the World Wide Web drive rapid expansion in networking technologies.
Early 2000sIncreased focus on network security and virtual private networks (VPNs) emerges as data breaches become more prevalent.
TodayNetworking continues to evolve with cloud computing, IoT, and advanced cybersecurity demanding ever more skilled professionals.
