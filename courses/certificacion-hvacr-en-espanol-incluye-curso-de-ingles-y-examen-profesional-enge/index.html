What is this field?

Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration (HVACR) technicians are the skilled professionals who install, maintain, and repair the essential climate control systems that keep our homes, businesses, and industrial facilities comfortable and efficient. They work with complex machinery, electrical components, and refrigerants, ensuring optimal performance and safety.

This unique career training program is designed specifically for Spanish speakers, integrating comprehensive HVACR technical instruction with targeted English language development. You'll gain the specialized vocabulary and communication skills needed to excel in the American workforce, alongside practical, hands-on simulations that prepare you for real-world service calls and industry-recognized certifications.

How the field grew

1902Willis Carrier invents modern air conditioning, laying the foundation for the HVACR industry.
Mid-20th CenturyWidespread adoption of refrigeration and air conditioning creates a growing demand for skilled technicians.
1990sEPA Section 608 certification becomes mandatory for refrigerant handling, emphasizing environmental responsibility.
Early 2000sIndustry certifications like NATE and HVAC Excellence emerge, standardizing technician competency and training.
Present DayFocus on energy efficiency, smart systems, and integrated training solutions for diverse workforces, including English language support.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this is a career training program designed to equip you with the specific skills and certifications needed for an HVACR technician role, without requiring a college degree.

Does this program include exam vouchers for certification?

Yes, this program includes specific exam vouchers for the HVAC Excellence Employment Ready certifications (Electricity and Air Conditioning) and the EPA 608 certification.

How long does it take to complete this career training program?

This program consists of 490 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own speed. An initial English proficiency assessment determines your starting point, with a goal to reach a 'High Intermediate' English level within six months to begin the core HVACR training.

Which certification exams will I be prepared for?

You will be prepared to take the current HVAC Excellence Employment Ready certifications, the NATE Ready to Work certification, and the EPA 608 Refrigerant Handling certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as an HVACR Technician, HVAC Installer, Refrigeration Mechanic, or HVAC/R Service Technician, working with heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems.
