What is this field?

Marketing communications is the strategic process of conveying messages to target audiences to promote products, services, or brands. It encompasses a wide array of channels and techniques, from crafting compelling website content and engaging social media posts to optimizing search engine visibility and running targeted advertising campaigns. Essentially, it's about telling your brand's story effectively to connect with customers and drive action.

Think of marketing communications as a brand's voice and megaphone. Just as a skilled storyteller uses different tones and platforms to reach various listeners, a marketing communications professional leverages tools like email, blogs, social media, and SEO to ensure their message resonates. They ensure that every interaction a potential customer has with a brand, whether it's a search result or an Instagram ad, reinforces a consistent and persuasive narrative.

How the field grew
