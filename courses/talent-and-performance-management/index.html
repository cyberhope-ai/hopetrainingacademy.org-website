What is this field?

Talent and Performance Management is the strategic process of attracting, developing, motivating, and retaining high-performing employees within an organization. It's about ensuring that a company has the right people in the right roles, with the right skills, to achieve its business objectives. This involves everything from initial recruitment and onboarding to ongoing training, performance reviews, career development, and succession planning.

Think of it like a sports team building a championship roster. A Talent and Performance Manager acts as the general manager, scouting new players (talent acquisition), developing their skills (performance management and training), ensuring they work well together (team building), and keeping them motivated and committed to the team's success (retention and engagement). This strategic approach is crucial for organizations to compete effectively in today's dynamic global marketplace.

How the field grew

1900sEmergence of 'Personnel Management' focused on administrative tasks like payroll and compliance.
1970sShift to 'Human Resources' (HR), recognizing employees as valuable assets and strategic partners.
1990sThe concept of 'Talent Management' gains prominence, emphasizing strategic attraction, development, and retention of key employees.
2000sIntegration of talent management systems and data analytics to optimize workforce planning and performance.
2010s-PresentFocus on employee experience, diversity & inclusion, and leveraging technology for global talent pools and continuous performance feedback.
