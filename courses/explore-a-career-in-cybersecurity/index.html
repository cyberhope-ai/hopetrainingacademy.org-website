What is this field?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from digital attacks, damage, or unauthorized access. Think of it like a digital fortress, where skilled professionals act as guardians, constantly defending against intruders and ensuring the safety of valuable information. It's a critical field that touches every aspect of modern life, from personal privacy to national security.

This program is your essential first step into understanding this dynamic world. It's designed to give you a clear overview of what cybersecurity entails, the fundamental concepts involved, and the diverse range of job opportunities available. You'll gain the foundational knowledge needed to decide if a career in protecting digital assets aligns with your interests and skills, setting the stage for further specialized training.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to start a cybersecurity career?

While some roles may prefer a degree, many entry-level cybersecurity positions prioritize practical skills and certifications. This program helps you explore the field and understand alternative pathways, including our United States Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this introductory program is designed for career exploration and does not include an exam voucher for any specific certification.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program offers flexible, self-paced learning, allowing you to explore cybersecurity concepts and career paths at your own speed. It's designed as an introductory overview to help you make informed career decisions.

Is this program for a specific cybersecurity certification exam?

No, this program is not tied to a specific certification exam. Its purpose is to provide a broad introduction to the cybersecurity field and the various job roles within it, helping you decide if it's the right career for you.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program is an exploratory first step. It will equip you with foundational knowledge of cybersecurity and an understanding of diverse job roles, preparing you to pursue further specialized training or entry-level positions like a Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst or Junior Cybersecurity Analyst.
