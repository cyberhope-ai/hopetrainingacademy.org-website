What is this field?

Microsoft Excel is the industry-standard software for organizing, calculating, and presenting data. It's a fundamental tool that empowers individuals and businesses to manage information efficiently, from simple lists to complex financial models. Mastering Excel means gaining the ability to transform raw data into actionable insights.

Think of Excel as a super-powered digital ledger and calculator combined, allowing you to not just store numbers but also see patterns, predict outcomes, and present information clearly. It's an indispensable skill for anyone working with data, budgets, schedules, or reports across virtually every industry.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel 1.0 released for Macintosh, challenging existing spreadsheet software.
1987Excel 2.0 launched for Windows, marking its entry into the dominant PC market.
1993Excel 5.0 introduced Visual Basic for Applications (VBA), enabling powerful automation.
2007Major interface overhaul with the introduction of the Ribbon, enhancing user experience.
2016Microsoft Excel 2016 released, offering new features for data analysis and collaboration, integrated with Office 365.
