What is this field?

This online training focuses on intermediate techniques for Microsoft Word 2019, the world's leading word processing software. You'll move beyond basic document creation to explore powerful features often overlooked, such as sophisticated formatting, advanced layout tools, and efficient communication strategies. It's about transforming simple text into polished, professional-grade documents and streamlining your workflow.

Think of it like upgrading from driving a car to knowing how to fine-tune its engine and use all its advanced features. You're not just typing; you're learning to design newsletters, create personalized mass mailings, and manage complex documents with ease. This proficiency makes you an invaluable asset in any role requiring strong organizational and communication skills.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Mastering Document Structure

Advanced formatting techniques, applying and managing styles, working with themes, creating and manipulating sections, headers, and footers.

02
Module 2: Enhancing Visual Communication

Integrating and formatting graphics, SmartArt, and charts; utilizing text boxes and columns for layout design.

03
Module 3: Desktop Publishing Fundamentals

Designing professional page layouts, creating brochures and newsletters, and mastering advanced document design principles.

04
Module 4: Streamlining Communication with Mail Merge

Setting up data sources, performing mail merges for letters, creating labels, and generating envelopes efficiently.

05
Module 5: Collaborating and Securing Documents

Using track changes and comments for collaboration, protecting documents, and managing different document versions.

06
Module 6: Advanced Productivity Features

Automating tasks with macros, customizing shortcuts, and creating effective document templates for repeated use.
