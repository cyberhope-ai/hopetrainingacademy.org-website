What is this field?

Six Sigma is a data-driven methodology designed to eliminate defects, reduce variation, and improve processes within any organization. Its core objective is to achieve near-perfect quality by identifying and removing the causes of errors, thereby enhancing efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

Think of Six Sigma like a master chef perfecting a signature dish. They meticulously measure every ingredient, control cooking temperatures precisely, and taste-test rigorously to ensure every plate is consistently exceptional. This systematic approach minimizes waste, ensures consistent quality, and delights every customer, much like Six Sigma optimizes business processes to deliver flawless results.

How the field grew

1980sMotorola develops Six Sigma as a robust methodology for quality improvement.
1990sGeneral Electric widely adopts and champions Six Sigma, popularizing its use across diverse industries.
2000sSix Sigma becomes a globally recognized standard for process excellence and operational efficiency.
2010s-PresentThe methodology evolves, integrating with Lean principles to form Lean Six Sigma, emphasizing both speed and quality.
