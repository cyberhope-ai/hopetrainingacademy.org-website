What is this field?

The Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP) Collaboration certification validates your expert-level skills in designing, implementing, configuring, and troubleshooting Cisco collaboration and unified communications applications and devices. This advanced program covers core collaboration technologies and specialized applications, preparing you to manage complex voice, video, messaging, and presence solutions that are critical for modern businesses.

Imagine you're the architect and lead engineer for a global company's communication system. You're not just making sure phones ring; you're building a seamless digital environment where teams can connect instantly via video, share screens, send messages, and collaborate from anywhere in the world. The CCNP Collaboration program gives you the blueprint and tools to construct and maintain this sophisticated digital nervous system.

How the field grew

1998Cisco introduces the Certified Network Professional (CCNP) certification, establishing a professional-level credential for network engineers.
Early 2000sCisco begins to develop specialized tracks within the CCNP program, recognizing the growing complexity of network technologies like voice and video.
2010sAs unified communications gain prominence, Cisco's collaboration certifications evolve to cover Voice over IP (VoIP), video conferencing, and integrated messaging.
2020Cisco revamps its certification program, consolidating tracks and introducing the current CCNP Collaboration certification, requiring a core exam (CLCOR) and a concentration exam (like CLICA).
