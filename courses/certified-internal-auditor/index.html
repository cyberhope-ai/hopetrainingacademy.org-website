What is this field?

Internal auditing is a critical function within any organization, acting as an independent assurance and consulting activity designed to add value and improve operations. Internal auditors help an organization accomplish its objectives by bringing a systematic, disciplined approach to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of risk management, control, and governance processes. They are the eyes and ears of an organization, ensuring compliance, efficiency, and ethical conduct.

Hope Training Academy's Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) training program equips you with the essential knowledge and practical skills needed to excel in this vital role. You'll delve into the IIA's Global Internal Audit Standards and gain proficiency in advanced Microsoft Excel techniques, preparing you not only for the rigorous CIA exam but also for the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert Excel certification. Think of it as learning to be a master detective for business operations, armed with the best analytical tools.

How the field grew

1941The Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) is founded, establishing a professional body for internal auditors.
1974The IIA introduces the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) designation, becoming the globally recognized certification for internal audit professionals.
2000sThe IIA's International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF) is developed and continually updated, providing mandatory guidance for internal audit.
PresentInternal auditing evolves to address complex risks like cybersecurity, data analytics, and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) factors, requiring continuous skill development.
