What the career pays

Computer and Information Systems Managers plan, coordinate, and direct computer-related activities within an organization. They manage IT departments, oversee technology projects, and ensure that systems align with business goals.

$109,200entry
$157,640median
$208,000+experienced

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) / O*NET OnLine (SOC 11-3021.00)

Jobs it opens

Computer and Information Systems Manager

Oversees an organization's IT operations and strategy.

IT Project Manager

Leads and manages technology projects from conception to completion.

Technology Director

Guides the technological direction and innovation within a company.

IT Department Lead

Manages a specific team or section within an IT department.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of IT Management

Introduction to IT leadership, organizational culture, managing risk and perceptions.

02
Collaborative Problem Solving

Understanding management backgrounds, effective communication, motivation, and team development.

03
Strategic Operations & Best Practices

Business models, strategic planning, financial accounting, legal issues, and ethical considerations.

04
Organizational Leadership & Decision Making

Leadership theories, sources of power, decision-making frameworks, and fostering accountability.

05
Organizational Development & Change

Exploring organizational culture, managing change, and addressing resistance within teams.

06
Negotiating & Persuasive Communication

Negotiation strategies, tools, personality types, and fundamentals of persuasive communication.

07
Core Management Disciplines & Budgeting

Strategy, HR, technology, operations, sales, customer, finance, risk management, and budgeting essentials.

Registered Apprenticeship — via the United States Department of Labor

Hope Training Academy proudly offers online Registered Apprenticeship pathways, recognized by the United States Department of Labor, for aspiring IT managers. This unique opportunity allows you to gain invaluable on-the-job experience while completing your training, significantly de-risking your career transition by combining structured learning with practical application.

Hope Training Academy Online · a United States Department of Labor (USDOL) Registered Apprenticeship pathway

If you're in Indiana: hands-on labs and in-person apprenticeship components run from our downtown Indianapolis center. (Online coursework and the registered apprenticeship pathway are available more broadly.)

Why Hope Training Academy

As a program of Video Game Palooza Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Hope Training Academy reinvests all proceeds back into our mission. This allows us to offer free training programs to underserved communities, fostering a more inclusive and skilled workforce.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific academic prerequisites for this program. Basic computer skills, internet navigation, and an email account are all that's required to get started.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This program focuses on developing management skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long will it take to complete the Management for IT Professionals program?

This self-paced program includes 390 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own pace and fit it into your schedule.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Computer and Information Systems Manager, IT Project Manager, Technology Director, or IT Department Lead, among others.

What makes Hope Training Academy's approach to IT management unique?

Our program grounds you in practical leadership, strategic planning, and financial acumen, all within the context of a United States Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship pathway, providing real-world experience alongside your studies.
