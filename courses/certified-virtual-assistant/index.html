What is this field?

A Certified Virtual Assistant (CVA) is a highly skilled independent professional who provides administrative, technical, or creative assistance to clients remotely. Unlike traditional employees, CVAs operate their own businesses, offering specialized services to multiple clients from anywhere in the world.

Think of a CVA as a freelance business partner for busy entrepreneurs and companies. Instead of hiring a full-time assistant, clients contract with a CVA for specific tasks or projects, gaining flexible support without the overhead of an employee. This program equips you to not just perform tasks, but to build and manage your own successful virtual assistant enterprise.
