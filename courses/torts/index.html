What is this field?

Torts law is a fundamental area of civil law that deals with civil wrongs, or torts, which cause someone else to suffer loss or harm, resulting in legal liability for the person who commits the tortious act. Unlike criminal law, which focuses on offenses against the state, tort law addresses injuries against an individual's person or property, allowing the injured party to seek compensation for their damages.

Think of Torts law like the rules of fair play in everyday life. If someone's actions cause you harm, whether it's a physical injury from a car accident (negligence) or damage to your reputation (defamation), Torts law provides a framework for you to seek justice and recover losses. This course lays the groundwork for understanding these crucial legal principles, preparing you for roles where you'll assist in navigating such cases.
