What is this field?

Reading strategies are structured approaches and techniques that educators use to help students comprehend, analyze, and engage with text more effectively. These strategies move beyond basic decoding, focusing instead on building critical thinking, vocabulary, and comprehension skills necessary for academic success across all subjects. By implementing a variety of strategies, teachers can address diverse learning styles and ensure every student has the tools to become a confident reader.

Think of reading strategies like a chef's toolkit. A good chef doesn't just know how to cook; they have a range of specialized tools and techniques for different ingredients and dishes – a sharp knife for slicing, a whisk for blending, a specific method for roasting. Similarly, an effective educator uses a diverse toolkit of reading strategies – like predicting, questioning, visualizing, or summarizing – to help students 'cook up' meaning from different types of texts, ensuring every learner can digest and understand the material, no matter their starting point.
