What is this field?

Productivity and benchmarking are essential business practices focused on optimizing performance and efficiency. Productivity measures how effectively resources are converted into outputs, while benchmarking involves comparing an organization's processes and performance metrics to industry best practices or competitors to identify areas for improvement. Together, they provide a data-driven approach to continuous enhancement.

Think of it like training for a marathon. Productivity is how fast you run a mile with the least effort. Benchmarking is comparing your mile time and training methods to those of elite runners or even your past self, identifying what they do better or where you've improved, and then adapting your strategy to achieve your personal best.

How the field grew

1911Frederick Winslow Taylor's 'Scientific Management' lays foundation for productivity analysis.
1979Xerox pioneers competitive benchmarking, formalizing the practice of comparing performance.
1980sTotal Quality Management (TQM) and Six Sigma integrate benchmarking into continuous improvement.
PresentDigital tools and AI revolutionize data collection and analysis for advanced performance insights.
