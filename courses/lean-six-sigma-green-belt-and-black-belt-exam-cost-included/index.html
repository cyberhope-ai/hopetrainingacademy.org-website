What is this field?

Lean Six Sigma Green and Black Belt training equips professionals with advanced methodologies to identify and eliminate waste, reduce defects, and streamline operations within any organization. You'll learn to lead complex projects, apply statistical analysis, and drive significant improvements that boost efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

Think of Lean Six Sigma as the ultimate toolkit for making things run better. Just like a master mechanic diagnoses and fine-tunes an engine for peak performance, or a chef optimizes a kitchen workflow to deliver perfect dishes consistently, you'll learn to analyze business processes, pinpoint inefficiencies, and implement data-driven solutions to achieve measurable, sustainable results.

How the field grew

1980sMotorola develops Six Sigma to reduce manufacturing defects to near perfection.
1990sGeneral Electric widely adopts Six Sigma, popularizing its use across diverse industries.
Early 2000sLean principles, focused on waste reduction, merge with Six Sigma's defect reduction, forming Lean Six Sigma.
PresentLean Six Sigma is a globally recognized methodology for continuous improvement and operational excellence.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Introduction to Lean Six Sigma

Overview of Lean Six Sigma, its origins, benefits, and the 8 Wastes (DOWNTIME).

02
Green Belt: Define Phase

Creating project charters, gathering customer voice, and mapping current processes.

03
Green Belt: Measure Phase

Selecting measures, planning data collection, and establishing baseline process data.

04
Green Belt: Analyze Phase

Conducting process and data analysis to identify and validate root causes of problems.

05
Green Belt: Improve & Control Phases

Crafting and implementing solutions, risk management, monitoring plans, and project documentation.

06
Black Belt: Advanced Project Selection & Leadership

Refining project selection, conducting process walks, and strengthening leadership skills.

07
Black Belt: Advanced Statistical Analysis

Laying statistical groundwork, testing measurement systems, and assessing process capability.

08
Black Belt: Design Experiments & Control Charts

Designing experiments, selecting appropriate control charts, and implementing visual management.
