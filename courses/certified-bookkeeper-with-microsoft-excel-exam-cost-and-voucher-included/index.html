What is this field?

A Certified Bookkeeper (CB) is a highly skilled professional responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, ensuring compliance, and providing essential data for business decisions. This program elevates your expertise by combining the rigorous standards of the American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) certification with advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, a critical tool for financial analysis and reporting.

Think of a Certified Bookkeeper as the financial guardian and data architect of an organization. They meticulously track every transaction, like a skilled librarian organizing a vast collection, while also using Excel to build powerful reports and insights, much like an engineer constructing a detailed blueprint from raw data.

How the field grew

1987American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) established, setting standards for the profession.
Early 2000sMicrosoft introduces the Office Specialist (MOS) program, validating software proficiency.
2010sIncreased demand for data analytics solidifies Microsoft Excel as an indispensable tool for bookkeepers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Certified Bookkeeper?

No, a degree is not required. However, the American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) certification requires two years (3,000 hours) of practical bookkeeping experience.

Does this program include exam vouchers?

Yes, this program includes vouchers for both the AIPB Certified Bookkeeper exam and the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Excel Expert certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and includes 210 course hours. Completion time varies based on individual study habits and schedule.

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) Certified Bookkeeper exam and the current Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Excel Expert certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Certified Bookkeeper, Accounting Assistant, Payroll Specialist, or Financial Data Analyst, utilizing their combined bookkeeping and advanced Excel skills.
