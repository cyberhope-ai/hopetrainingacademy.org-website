What is this field?

Talent and Performance Management is the strategic process of attracting, developing, motivating, and retaining high-performing employees within an organization. It's about ensuring that the right people are in the right roles, equipped with the skills and support they need to succeed, and continuously evaluated to foster growth and achieve business objectives.

Think of it like building and maintaining a championship sports team. You need to scout and recruit the best players (talent acquisition), train them to improve their skills (talent development), keep them motivated and engaged (performance management), and create an environment where they want to stay and contribute long-term (talent retention). This program equips you with the strategies to build such a winning workforce.

How the field grew

1980Personnel Management focused on administrative tasks and compliance
1990Emergence of Human Resources as a strategic partner in organizations
2000Rise of Talent Management, emphasizing a holistic approach to the employee lifecycle
2010Increased focus on Performance Management with continuous feedback and data-driven insights
2020Integration of technology and analytics for an optimized talent experience
