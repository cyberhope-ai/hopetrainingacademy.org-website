What is this field?

Genealogy is the fascinating study of family lineage, tracing ancestral lines through generations to discover personal history and heritage. It involves meticulous research, piecing together historical records, and often connecting with living relatives to reconstruct the narrative of a family's past.

Think of it like being a historical detective for your own family. You gather clues from various sources—old documents, photographs, and oral traditions—to solve the mystery of your origins, connecting disparate pieces of information to form a complete and compelling story.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesOral traditions and tribal histories preserved lineage through storytelling.
11th-16th CenturyRise of heraldry and noble family trees, documenting aristocratic lineages.
17th-19th CenturyIncreased availability of parish registers, census records, and civil documents for broader populations.
Late 20th CenturyEmergence of genetic genealogy and the digitization of records, making research more accessible.
21st CenturyGlobal online databases and collaborative platforms revolutionize family history research.
