What is this field?

A Certified Six Sigma Green Belt is a professional who plays a crucial role in improving business processes by identifying and solving quality problems. This methodology is a data-driven approach focused on removing defects and minimizing variations in any process, leading to enhanced efficiency, reduced costs, and improved customer satisfaction. Green Belts are integral members of process improvement teams, applying statistical analysis and problem-solving tools to achieve measurable results.

Think of a Six Sigma Green Belt as a skilled detective for business operations. Just as a detective uses evidence to uncover the root cause of a mystery, a Green Belt uses data and analytical tools to pinpoint inefficiencies and defects within a company's processes. They then implement targeted solutions to make those processes smoother, faster, and more reliable, much like a detective closing a case by bringing order to chaos.

How the field grew

1986Motorola develops Six Sigma as a data-driven method for reducing defects in manufacturing.
1995General Electric adopts Six Sigma, leading to its widespread recognition and application across various industries.
Early 2000sIntegration of Lean principles with Six Sigma to create Lean Six Sigma, emphasizing waste reduction alongside defect elimination.
