What is this field?

Windows 11 is the latest operating system from Microsoft, serving as the digital foundation for most modern computers. It's the environment where you interact with software, manage your files, and connect to the internet. Understanding Windows 11 is crucial for virtually any professional role today, providing the bedrock for productivity and digital communication.

Think of Windows 11 as the control center of your computer, much like the dashboard of a car. Just as you need to know how to use the steering wheel, pedals, and various controls to drive effectively, you need to understand Windows 11 to efficiently operate your computer. This program teaches you how to master its features, from organizing your digital workspace to securely browsing the web, ensuring you're in full control of your digital tasks.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft introduces Windows 1.0, a graphical user interface for MS-DOS.
1995Windows 95 launches, bringing the iconic Start button and a major interface overhaul.
2001Windows XP is released, becoming one of the longest-supported and most popular versions.
2009Windows 7 arrives, praised for its stability and user-friendly design.
2021Windows 11 is released, featuring a redesigned interface and enhanced productivity tools.
