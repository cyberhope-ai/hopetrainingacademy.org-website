What is this field?

High Speed Project Management is the art and science of guiding complex initiatives to completion under conditions of rapid change, tight deadlines, and often, constrained resources. Unlike traditional project management, which might allow for more deliberate planning phases, high-speed approaches demand quick decision-making, adaptive strategies, and efficient resource allocation to meet aggressive timelines without compromising quality.

Imagine you're building a custom race car, but the competition is next week, you have a smaller team than usual, and your budget is limited. High-speed project management equips you with the skills to rapidly design, source parts, assemble, and test that car, ensuring it's race-ready and competitive, despite all the constraints. It's about optimizing every step to accelerate delivery and achieve critical objectives.

How the field grew

1950sEarly project management techniques emerge with complex defense and aerospace projects, focusing on structured planning.
1980sThe rise of information technology introduces new complexities, pushing for more flexible and iterative approaches.
2001The Agile Manifesto is published, emphasizing adaptive planning, evolutionary development, and rapid delivery.
2010sIncreased market volatility and technological advancements drive demand for 'high-speed' or 'accelerated' project methodologies.
PresentModern project management integrates lean, agile, and hybrid strategies to navigate fast-paced, resource-constrained environments.
