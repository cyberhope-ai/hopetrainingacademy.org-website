What is this field?

Financial analysis and business accounting are critical disciplines that empower organizations to make informed decisions about their financial health and future investments. This program provides comprehensive training in evaluating financial statements, assessing market trends, and developing strategic financial plans to drive business growth and stability.

Think of a financial analyst as a company's financial detective. They sift through vast amounts of numerical data, like a detective examining clues, to uncover patterns, predict future outcomes, and advise on the best course of action for financial success, ensuring every dollar works efficiently.

How the field grew

1920sEmergence of modern financial analysis with the growth of stock markets and corporate finance.
1970sIncreased adoption of quantitative analysis and computer modeling in financial decision-making.
2000sHeightened focus on data analytics, risk management, and regulatory compliance in finance.
2020sIntegration of artificial intelligence and advanced predictive analytics reshaping financial strategies.

What the career pays

Financial analysts evaluate investment opportunities and assess the performance of stocks, bonds, and other financial instruments. They provide critical insights to businesses and individuals, guiding strategic financial decisions.
