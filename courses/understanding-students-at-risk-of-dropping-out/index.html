What is this field?

This online program equips educators and support staff with foundational knowledge to understand and effectively intervene with students at risk of disengaging from their education. You will explore the complex factors contributing to student vulnerability, from early warning signs to underlying root causes, and learn actionable strategies to promote a positive learning environment.

Think of this training like becoming a skilled detective for student success. Just as a detective gathers clues to solve a mystery, you'll learn to identify subtle indicators of distress, understand the 'why' behind challenging behaviors, and apply targeted interventions to help students overcome obstacles and thrive academically and personally.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Understanding the At-Risk Student

Defining vulnerability, identifying student populations at risk, and the scope of the challenge.

02
Early Warning Indicators

Recognizing academic, behavioral, and social signs of disengagement and potential dropout.

03
Root Causes of Disengagement

Exploring factors like family issues, mental health, socioeconomic challenges, and learning disabilities.

04
Building Supportive Relationships

Strategies for effective communication, trust-building, and collaboration with students and families.

05
Intervention and Support Strategies

Implementing academic, social-emotional, and community-based interventions for student success.

06
Fostering Student Resilience and Success

Empowering students to overcome obstacles, develop coping mechanisms, and thrive in their education.

Frequently asked questions

Is a specific degree required to enroll?

No specific degree is required to enroll in this professional development program. It is designed to enhance the skills of current or aspiring educators and support staff.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

An exam voucher is not included with this course, as it is a professional development program and not tied to a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn at your own convenience. While the content is designed for flexible learning, typical completion times vary depending on individual study habits.

Is this program tied to a current certification exam?

This program is a professional development course focused on strategies for supporting at-risk students, not a certification exam preparation course. Therefore, there is no external exam associated with it.

What kind of job can I get with this training?

This program enhances skills for roles such as school counselors, educational advisors, student support specialists, or any professional working directly with students who may be at risk of disengaging from their education.
