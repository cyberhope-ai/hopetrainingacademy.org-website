What is this field?

A Residential Electrician is a skilled tradesperson responsible for installing, maintaining, and repairing electrical power, communications, lighting, and control systems in homes and other residential structures. This critical role ensures that buildings are safely and efficiently powered, adhering to strict electrical codes and safety standards. From new construction wiring to troubleshooting existing systems and installing modern smart home technology, residential electricians are vital to modern living.

Think of a residential electrician as the architect of a home's nervous system. Just as nerves carry signals throughout the body, an electrician designs and installs the pathways (wires) and control centers (panels, switches) that carry electricity, allowing lights to turn on, appliances to run, and devices to charge. They diagnose issues when a 'nerve' misfires and ensure the entire system operates safely and reliably, bringing power and convenience to every corner of a home.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals with no prior experience. It provides the foundational knowledge and skills needed for an entry-level career as a residential electrician.

Does this program include an exam voucher for state licensing?

This program does not include an exam voucher for state licensing. However, it does include a valuable 19-piece tool kit from IDEAL Electrical, shipped directly to you, to help you start your career with essential equipment.

How long does it take to complete the Residential Electrician training program?

This is a self-paced program totaling 125 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Does this program prepare me for a specific electrician licensing exam?

This program prepares you with the comprehensive knowledge required for electrician licensing tests, which are generally mandated by most states and localities. You will learn about the National Electrical Code® and common electrical work situations, but you should always check your specific state, county, or city requirements for licensing.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for entry-level positions such as a Residential Electrician or Electrician Apprentice. The skills you gain are highly sought after in the construction and maintenance industries.
