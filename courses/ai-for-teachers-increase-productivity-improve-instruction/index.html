What is this field?

This program equips educators with the knowledge and tools to effectively integrate artificial intelligence into their daily teaching practices. You'll explore how AI can streamline administrative tasks, personalize learning experiences, and foster more engaging classroom environments, moving beyond basic tools to truly leverage AI's potential.

Think of AI in teaching like a smart assistant that helps you prepare lessons, grade assignments, and understand student needs more efficiently. Instead of replacing the teacher, AI empowers you to focus more on direct student interaction and creative instruction, much like a GPS helps a driver navigate complex routes, allowing them to concentrate on the journey itself.

How the field grew

2010sEarly adoption of AI in adaptive learning platforms and educational software.
