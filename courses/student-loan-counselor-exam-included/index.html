What is this field?

With over 43 million Americans owing $1.5 trillion in federal student loan debt, the need for knowledgeable and compassionate student loan counselors has never been greater. These professionals serve as vital guides, helping individuals understand complex loan systems, navigate repayment options, and find solutions for financial stability. They provide crucial support to those struggling with the intricacies of student debt, offering clarity and actionable strategies.

Think of a student loan counselor as a financial GPS for education debt. Just as a GPS guides you through unfamiliar roads to your destination, a student loan counselor helps clients chart a clear path through the often-confusing landscape of student loans, ensuring they reach their financial goals with confidence and peace of mind.

How the field grew

Early 2000sStudent loan debt begins its significant rise, increasing the complexity of repayment for borrowers.
2008-2010The Great Recession exacerbates financial struggles, leading to increased defaults and a greater demand for debt counseling services.
2010sFederal student loan programs undergo significant changes, introducing new repayment plans and forgiveness options, requiring specialized expertise to advise borrowers.
PresentThe student loan crisis continues, solidifying the critical role of certified student loan counselors in helping millions manage their financial futures.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this career?

No, this program focuses on practical skills and prepares you for the NACCC certification. However, enrollment requires prior certification as a Certified Credit Counselor or Financial Health Counselor through NACCC.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher is not included with this program.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced program designed for 60 course hours, with six months of access to complete your studies at your own pace.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program specifically prepares you for the National Association of Certified Credit Counselors (NACCC) Certified Student Loan Counselor exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Student Loan Counselor, Financial Counselor, Debt Management Specialist, or Financial Wellness Advisor.
