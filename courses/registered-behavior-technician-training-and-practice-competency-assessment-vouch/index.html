What is this field?

A Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) is a paraprofessional who practices under the close, ongoing supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). RBTs are crucial members of a behavioral healthcare team, directly implementing behavior-analytic services to support individuals, often those with autism spectrum disorder or other developmental disabilities. They work in various settings, including homes, schools, and clinics, helping clients develop new skills and reduce challenging behaviors through interventions based on the principles of Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA).

Think of an RBT as a skilled coach who helps individuals learn and grow by breaking down complex tasks into manageable steps. Just as a sports coach uses specific drills and positive reinforcement to improve an athlete's performance, an RBT uses evidence-based techniques to teach communication, social, and daily living skills, while carefully tracking progress and adapting strategies to meet each person's unique needs.

How the field grew

1998The Behavior Analyst Certification Board (BACB) was established to credential behavior analysis professionals.
2013The BACB introduced the Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) credential to standardize and professionalize direct care providers.
2019The RBT Task List was updated to its 2nd Edition, refining the competencies required for the certification.
2024The BACB implemented new requirements for the Initial Competency Assessment, emphasizing employer-led evaluations.
