What is this field?

A Cloud and AI Security Engineer is a specialized professional who designs, implements, and maintains robust security measures for cloud-based infrastructures and artificial intelligence workloads. Imagine them as the digital guardians protecting your company's most valuable assets – its data and intelligent systems – from cyber threats, much like a fortress architect builds defenses around a castle.

As organizations increasingly rely on cloud platforms and integrate AI services, the demand for experts who can secure these complex environments has skyrocketed. This role is crucial for preventing data breaches, ensuring compliance, and maintaining the integrity and privacy of information across hybrid and AI-enabled ecosystems.

How the field grew

Early 2000sInitial rise of cloud computing concepts, laying groundwork for services like Azure.
2010sRapid adoption of public cloud platforms, creating urgent demand for specialized cloud security.
Mid-2010sIncreased integration of AI and machine learning into enterprise applications.
Late 2010s-PresentConvergence of cloud and AI, highlighting the critical need for integrated security strategies.
