What is this field?

Legal research is the systematic process of identifying and retrieving information to support legal decision-making. This involves meticulously examining statutes, regulations, judicial opinions, and other legal authorities to understand the law, find precedents, and build compelling arguments for cases.

Think of legal research as being a skilled detective in the world of law. You're given a case or a legal question, and your mission is to uncover all the relevant clues—laws, past court decisions, and expert interpretations—to piece together the most accurate and effective legal strategy, much like a detective gathers evidence to solve a mystery.

How the field grew

1800sEarly systematic compilation of legal texts and case reporters began, laying foundational access to legal precedents.
1970sThe advent of computerized legal research platforms like LexisNexis and Westlaw revolutionized information retrieval.
2000sWidespread internet access led to the proliferation of online legal databases and digital research tools, enhancing accessibility.
2020sIntegration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools began to assist in predictive analysis and efficiency within legal research.
