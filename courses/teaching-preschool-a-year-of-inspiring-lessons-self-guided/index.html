What is this field?

Teaching preschool involves guiding young children through their crucial early developmental stages, fostering their social, emotional, cognitive, and physical growth. It's about creating a nurturing and stimulating environment where curiosity is encouraged, and learning happens through play, exploration, and structured activities. This course focuses on equipping you with the skills to design engaging educational experiences that captivate preschoolers' imaginations.

Think of a preschool teacher as a master gardener for young minds. Just as a gardener carefully plans the soil, sunlight, and water for each plant to thrive, a preschool teacher meticulously designs daily activities and monthly themes to ensure every child receives the right nourishment for their unique growth. This program teaches you how to cultivate that perfect learning garden, month by month.

How the field grew

Early 1900sMaria Montessori introduces her child-centered educational method, focusing on self-directed activity.
1920s-1930sNursery schools gain popularity in the U.S., focusing on early social and emotional development.
1965Project Head Start is launched in the U.S., providing comprehensive early childhood education for low-income children.
PresentModern preschool education emphasizes play-based learning, social-emotional intelligence, and individualized instruction.

What the career pays

Preschool teachers educate and care for children typically aged 3 to 5, preparing them for kindergarten. They develop and implement engaging lesson plans that foster cognitive, social, and emotional development.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a preschool teacher?

While some positions may prefer a degree, many entry-level preschool teaching roles or assistant positions require a postsecondary nondegree award, such as a Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, or relevant experience. This course provides foundational knowledge for the field.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses on skill development for creating lesson plans and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-guided online course, allowing you to learn at your own pace. The time to complete it will vary based on your schedule and dedication.

Is there a certification exam for this course?

This course is designed to equip you with practical skills for developing preschool lesson plans and does not culminate in a formal certification exam. It enhances your professional development in early childhood education.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

Completing this course will enhance your skills for roles such as a Preschool Teacher, Assistant Preschool Teacher, or Childcare Worker. It provides valuable knowledge for anyone working in early childhood education settings.
