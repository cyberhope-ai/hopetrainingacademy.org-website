What is this field?

The Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) credential is a globally recognized certification for experienced business analysts who excel at tackling complex projects and driving significant organizational change. It validates your advanced expertise in business analysis principles and practices, as defined by the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) BABOK Guide v3.0. Earning your CBAP demonstrates your capability to lead initiatives, manage stakeholders, and deliver impactful solutions.

Think of a CBAP as the master architect of business solutions. Just as an architect designs a building from concept to completion, ensuring structural integrity and meeting client needs, a CBAP designs and refines business processes and systems. They translate strategic goals into actionable plans, identifying requirements, mitigating risks, and ensuring the final 'structure' (solution) is robust, efficient, and delivers maximum value to the organization.

How the field grew

2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) was founded to develop and maintain standards for the business analysis profession.
2006The first version of the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK Guide) was released, establishing a common framework for business analysis practices.
2008The Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) certification was launched, providing a globally recognized credential for experienced business analysts.
2015The BABOK Guide v3.0 was released, becoming the definitive resource that underpins the current CBAP certification exam.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to enroll in this program?

While a bachelor's degree is common for senior business analyst roles, the CBAP certification itself primarily requires significant professional experience in business analysis, not a specific academic degree. This program focuses on preparing you for the certification exam based on your practical experience.
