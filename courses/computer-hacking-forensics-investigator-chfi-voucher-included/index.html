What is this field?

Digital forensics is a specialized branch of forensic science focused on identifying, acquiring, processing, analyzing, and reporting data stored electronically. In today's interconnected world, electronic evidence is a critical component of nearly all investigations, from cybercrime to corporate espionage. This program equips you with the essential skills to meticulously investigate digital incidents, uncover intruder traces, collect evidence for potential prosecution, and implement measures to prevent future attacks.

Think of a Computer Hacking Forensics Investigator as a digital detective. Just as a traditional detective examines a crime scene for clues, a CHFI systematically investigates digital environments—computers, networks, mobile devices, and cloud systems—to piece together what happened, who was involved, and how it occurred. You'll learn to recover hidden data, analyze system logs, and maintain the chain of custody, ensuring digital evidence is admissible and reliable.

How the field grew

Early 2000sEmergence of specialized digital forensics as a critical field due to rising cybercrime.
Mid-2000sDevelopment of vendor-neutral certifications like CHFI to standardize investigator skills.
Recent YearsThe CHFI certification achieves ANSI 17024 accreditation, signifying global recognition for quality.
TodayCHFI is mapped to the NICE 2.0 framework and recognized by the DoD under Directive 8570, affirming its industry relevance.
