What is this field?

Statistics is the science of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, presenting, and organizing data. In today's data-driven world, understanding statistics is crucial for making informed decisions, identifying trends, and solving complex problems across virtually every industry, from business and healthcare to science and technology.

Think of statistics as a powerful lens that allows you to see patterns and draw conclusions from a vast sea of information. Just as a detective uses clues to solve a mystery, statistics provides the tools to uncover hidden insights and make sense of the data that surrounds us, turning raw numbers into meaningful stories.

How the field grew

1654Blaise Pascal and Pierre de Fermat lay the mathematical groundwork for probability theory.
1763Thomas Bayes' work on conditional probability, known as Bayes' Theorem, is published posthumously.
1880sFrancis Galton introduces concepts of regression and correlation, fundamental to modern statistical analysis.
1920sRonald Fisher develops modern statistical inference and experimental design, shaping contemporary research methods.
