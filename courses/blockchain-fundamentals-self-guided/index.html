What is this field?

Blockchain Fundamentals introduces you to the core principles of blockchain technology, a revolutionary system for creating secure, transparent, and immutable digital records. This foundational understanding covers the underlying methodology, including how data is structured, secured, and validated across a distributed network.

Think of blockchain like a digital ledger that's shared across many computers, where every new entry (a 'block') is cryptographically linked to the previous one. Once an entry is made, it's nearly impossible to change, creating a highly trustworthy and verifiable record for transactions, assets, or any digital information.

How the field grew

1991Stuart Haber and W. Scott Stornetta describe a cryptographically secured chain of blocks.
2008Satoshi Nakamoto publishes the Bitcoin whitepaper, outlining the first practical blockchain.
2009The Bitcoin network is launched, marking the first real-world implementation of blockchain technology.
2015Ethereum is launched, introducing smart contracts and expanding blockchain's applications beyond cryptocurrency.
