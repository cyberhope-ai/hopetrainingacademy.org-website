What is this field?

An Electrical and Electronics Technician is a skilled professional responsible for installing, testing, maintaining, and repairing electronic components and electrical systems across various industries. This hands-on training program equips you with the essential knowledge and practical skills to diagnose issues, interpret schematics, and ensure the safe and efficient operation of complex equipment.

Think of an Electrical and Electronics Technician as a detective for circuits and systems. Just as a detective uses clues to solve a mystery, a technician uses specialized tools and knowledge to pinpoint and resolve electrical malfunctions, ensuring everything from industrial machinery to smart home devices operates flawlessly.

How the field grew

1978ETA International, a leading certifying body for electronics professionals, is founded.
1980sThe widespread adoption of personal computers and integrated circuits expands the demand for electronics technicians.
2000sThe proliferation of wireless technologies and smart devices creates new specializations within the field.
PresentOngoing advancements in automation, IoT, and advanced manufacturing continue to drive innovation and demand for skilled technicians.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Basic Electrical Theory & Safety

Fundamentals of electricity, electromagnetism, basic circuit building, and essential safety practices.

02
Circuit Analysis & Measurement

Ohm's Law, basic mathematics for electronics, interpreting electrical drawings, and using testing devices like multimeters.

03
DC & AC Circuits

Understanding series and parallel circuits, power calculations, DC troubleshooting, and an introduction to alternating current (AC) principles.

04
Advanced Components & Systems

In-depth study of capacitors, inductors, transformers, reactive circuits, semiconductors, and electronic interfacing.

05
Practical Application & Troubleshooting

Hands-on lab experiences, real-world workplace scenarios, diagnostic assessments, and systematic repair techniques.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become an Electrical and Electronics Technician?

No, a college degree is not typically required to start a career as an Electrical and Electronics Technician. This program provides the foundational knowledge and hands-on skills needed for entry-level positions and prepares you for industry certification.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

This program prepares you thoroughly for the Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) exam developed by the Electronics Technicians Association, International, Inc. However, an exam voucher is not included with this specific training program.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

This is a self-paced program designed for flexible learning, requiring approximately 160 course hours to complete. You can progress through the material at a speed that fits your schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) certification exam, offered by the Electronics Technicians Association, International, Inc. (ETA International).

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for various roles such as Electronics Repair Technician, Industrial Electronics Technician, Field Service Technician, and positions specializing in areas like avionics, telecommunications, or smart technologies.
