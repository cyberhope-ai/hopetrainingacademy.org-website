What is this field?

Business analysis is the discipline of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. This often involves understanding organizational structures, policies, and operations, then recommending changes that deliver value to stakeholders. It acts as a crucial bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring that projects align with strategic goals.

An Agile analyst applies these principles within dynamic, iterative development environments. Think of it like a skilled architect who not only designs a building (the business solution) but also continuously collaborates with the construction crew and future occupants, adapting plans as needed to ensure the final structure perfectly meets evolving needs and delivers maximum utility. This program equips you with both the foundational business analysis knowledge and the specialized Agile techniques to thrive in modern project settings.

How the field grew

1990sEmergence of dedicated business analysis roles within organizations.
2003Founding of the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) to standardize the profession.
2005Release of the inaugural Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK) Guide.
2015Publication of BABOK Guide v3.0, expanding the scope and practices of business analysis.
2020sRapid integration of Agile methodologies, leading to specialized Agile analysis roles.
