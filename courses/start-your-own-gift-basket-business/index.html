What is this field?

A gift basket business allows you to curate and design themed collections of products, presenting them beautifully as gifts for various occasions. It's a creative venture where you select items, arrange them aesthetically, and package them for sale, often from a home-based studio.

This program empowers you to become an entrepreneur, turning your passion for thoughtful giving into a source of income. Think of yourself as a personal curator of joy, crafting unique experiences for customers to share with their loved ones, all while building your own brand and business.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesTraditions of gift-giving and presenting goods in decorative containers or woven baskets date back centuries across various cultures.
18th-19th CenturyEuropean 'hampers' became popular, filled with provisions for travel or special occasions, evolving into a form of curated gift.
Early 20th CenturyDepartment stores began offering pre-assembled gift sets and themed baskets, making curated gifting more accessible to the public.
Late 20th CenturyThe rise of specialized gift basket companies, often home-based, catering to corporate clients and individual consumers seeking personalized options.
21st CenturyE-commerce revolutionizes the industry, enabling global reach for small businesses and a focus on highly personalized, niche, and sustainable gift basket offerings.
