What is this field?

Complementary and Integrative Health (CIH) represents a holistic approach to wellness, combining conventional medical treatments with evidence-informed complementary therapies. This field focuses on the whole person, considering physical, mental, emotional, spiritual, and social aspects of health to achieve optimal well-being. It moves beyond simply treating symptoms to fostering a deeper understanding of health and illness, empowering individuals to take an active role in their healing journey.

Think of it like a master chef who not only knows how to cook traditional dishes but also expertly incorporates unique, flavorful ingredients and techniques from around the world to create a more complete and satisfying dining experience. This program teaches you to blend conventional healthcare knowledge with a broader palette of wellness strategies, offering more comprehensive, patient-centered support to individuals seeking improved health.

How the field grew

1970sGrowing public interest in holistic health and alternative medicine practices.
1992National Institutes of Health (NIH) establishes the Office of Alternative Medicine, signaling federal recognition and research focus.
Early 2000sThe term 'integrative health' gains prominence, emphasizing coordinated, whole-person care.
2010sIncreased scientific research and evidence-based approaches validate many complementary therapies.
2020sHealthcare systems increasingly adopt integrative models, driving demand for knowledgeable professionals.
