What is this field?

Depression is more than just feeling sad; it's a complex mood disorder that can significantly impact daily life, affecting thoughts, feelings, and physical health. It manifests in various forms, from persistent low mood to severe clinical depression, each with unique characteristics and challenges.

An integrative approach to depression acknowledges that mental health is influenced by a combination of biological, psychological, and social factors. Instead of relying on a single treatment, it combines conventional medical therapies with complementary practices like nutrition, mindfulness, and lifestyle adjustments to foster holistic well-being.
