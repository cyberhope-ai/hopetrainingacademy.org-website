What is this field?

Perinatal care encompasses the critical period surrounding pregnancy, from conception through the first year after birth. It's a holistic approach to ensuring the well-being of both the birthing parent and the infant, addressing physical health, mental health, and social support needs during this transformative time. Understanding perinatal issues is vital for healthcare professionals and support staff who aim to provide comprehensive, compassionate care.

This training program equips you with the knowledge to address common perinatal challenges, such as gestational diabetes, hypertension, and postpartum depression, through a multidisciplinary lens. Think of it like being a specialized guide for new families, helping them navigate the unique health landscape from pregnancy through early parenthood, ensuring they receive the best possible support and resources.

How the field grew

1970sGrowing recognition of postpartum mental health challenges and the need for specialized support.
1990sEmphasis on integrated, family-centered care models for maternal and infant well-being.
2000sExpansion of global health initiatives addressing maternal and infant mortality and morbidity worldwide.
2010sIncreased focus on social determinants of health and their impact on perinatal outcomes.
