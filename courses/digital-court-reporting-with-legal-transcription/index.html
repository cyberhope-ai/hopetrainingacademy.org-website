What is this field?

Digital court reporting and legal transcription are critical roles within the legal system, responsible for creating accurate, verbatim records of legal proceedings. Digital court reporters utilize advanced audio and video recording equipment to capture testimony, while legal transcriptionists convert these recordings into precise written transcripts. These professionals ensure the integrity of legal documentation, supporting attorneys, judges, and the public.

Think of a digital court reporter as the 'eyes and ears' of the court, meticulously recording every word and action during a trial or deposition, much like a meticulous historian documenting an important event. A legal transcriptionist then takes that raw historical record and transforms it into a perfectly formatted, searchable, and official written document, ensuring every detail is preserved for future reference.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyStenography machines become standard for court reporting, revolutionizing speed and accuracy.
Late 20th CenturyIntroduction of digital audio recording technology begins to supplement traditional stenography.
Early 21st CenturyDigital court reporting and legal transcription emerge as distinct, technology-driven fields, addressing growing demand and evolving legal tech.
Present DayRemote work capabilities and advanced software define modern digital reporting and transcription practices.
