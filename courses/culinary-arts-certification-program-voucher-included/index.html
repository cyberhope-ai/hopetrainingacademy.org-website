What is this field?

The Culinary Arts Certification Program is a comprehensive career training program designed to equip you with the essential skills for a professional kitchen. You'll delve into advanced cooking techniques, master kitchen management principles, and gain critical knowledge in food safety, preparing you for leadership roles in the culinary world.

Think of this program like building a house from the ground up. You'll learn the foundational carpentry (basic cooking), then move to plumbing and electrical (advanced techniques), and finally, interior design and project management (menu creation and kitchen operations), resulting in a complete, functional, and beautiful structure ready for use.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this program?

No, this career training program is designed to equip you with the practical skills and certifications needed for a culinary career, without requiring a college degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, an exam voucher for the World Food Safety Organization (WFSO) Food Protection Manager certification is included with your enrollment.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This self-paced program is designed for completion within 485 hours, with 12-month access to all course materials.

Which certification exams will I be prepared for?

You will be prepared for the current/latest World Food Safety Organization (WFSO) Food Protection Manager certification exam, and the program aligns with the American Culinary Federation (ACF) Certified Culinarian (CC) pathway (ACF exams are completed separately).

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Chef, Sous Chef, Kitchen Manager, Line Cook, and other professional culinary positions in various food service establishments.
