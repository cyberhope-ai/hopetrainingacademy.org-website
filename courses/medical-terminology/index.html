What is this field?

Medical terminology is the specialized language used by healthcare professionals to describe the human body, its conditions, procedures, and treatments. It's like learning a universal code that allows doctors, nurses, and administrative staff to communicate precisely and efficiently, ensuring patient safety and effective care. Without this foundational knowledge, understanding medical reports, communicating with colleagues, or even comprehending health information can be challenging.

This hands-on career training program provides a comprehensive introduction to this essential language, focusing on word parts, body systems, and common medical abbreviations. You'll gain a deep understanding of how various systems—from musculoskeletal to nervous—function in health and disease, preparing you for success in further healthcare career training programs such as medical assisting, medical coding, or health information technology.

Foundations of Medical Language

Introduction to terminology, word parts, and human body systems.

Musculoskeletal & Cardiovascular Systems

Anatomy, physiology, and related medical terms for bones, muscles, heart, and blood vessels.
