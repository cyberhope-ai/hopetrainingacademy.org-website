What is this field?

The ABC Method is a powerful framework used by educators to understand and manage student behavior. It focuses on identifying the Antecedent (what happens before a behavior), the Behavior itself (the action), and the Consequence (what happens immediately after). By systematically analyzing these three components, you can uncover the underlying function or purpose of a student's actions, moving beyond simply reacting to behavior.

Think of it like being a detective for student behavior. Instead of just seeing a student act out, you learn to look for the clues that led up to it and what the student gains from it. This allows you to develop targeted strategies that address the root cause, much like a skilled mechanic fixing an engine by understanding its parts, rather than just painting over a warning light.
