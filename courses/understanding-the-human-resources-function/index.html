What is this field?

Human Resources (HR) is the backbone of any successful organization, focusing on managing the most valuable asset: its people. HR professionals are responsible for attracting, developing, and retaining talent, ensuring a productive and positive work environment, and navigating complex employment laws. This vital function impacts everything from company culture to bottom-line performance.

Think of HR as the central nervous system of a company. Just as your nervous system coordinates all your body's functions to keep you healthy and performing, HR coordinates all the 'people' functions within an organization. From the moment someone considers joining, through their growth and development, to their eventual departure, HR ensures fair treatment, clear communication, and strategic alignment of human capital with business goals.

How the field grew

Early 1900sEmergence of 'Personnel Management' focused on efficiency and welfare.
1930sHuman Relations Movement emphasizes employee motivation and social factors.
1960sCivil Rights Act and EEO laws transform HR into a compliance-focused field.
1980sShift to 'Strategic Human Resource Management' aligning HR with business strategy.
2000sRise of HR technology, data analytics, and global workforce management.
