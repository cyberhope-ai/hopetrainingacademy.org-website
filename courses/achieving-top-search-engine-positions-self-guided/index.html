What is this field?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of increasing the quantity and quality of traffic to your website through organic search engine results. It's about understanding how search engines work and what people are searching for, then optimizing your website and content to appear prominently in those results. This course provides a comprehensive, step-by-step guide to mastering these crucial techniques.

Think of a search engine like a massive digital library. When you search for a book, you want the most relevant and authoritative one to appear first. SEO is like being the librarian who meticulously organizes and categorizes books, ensuring that when someone searches for a specific topic, your book (website) is not only found but also recognized as the best resource, leading to more readers (visitors) discovering your content naturally.

How the field grew

1990sEarly search engines like AltaVista and Yahoo emerge, laying the groundwork for web discovery.
1998Google is founded, introducing sophisticated algorithms that prioritize relevance and authority, fundamentally changing SEO.
Early 2000sThe rise of 'black hat' SEO tactics leads to major algorithm updates (e.g., Florida, Panda, Penguin) focusing on quality and user experience.
2010sSEO evolves to emphasize mobile optimization, local search, and high-quality, user-centric content strategies.
PresentIntegration of artificial intelligence, voice search optimization, and the importance of E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) shape modern SEO practices.
