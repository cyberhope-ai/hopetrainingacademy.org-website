What is this field?

Digital painting with Photoshop is the art of creating visual works directly on a computer using software like Adobe Photoshop, mimicking traditional painting techniques with digital tools. This intermediate program builds on your existing Photoshop knowledge, teaching you to color, paint, and enhance photos or create entirely new digital artworks from scratch. It's about leveraging powerful software to bring imaginative concepts to life with precision and flexibility.

Think of it like moving from sketching with a pencil to painting with a full palette of oil paints, but all within a digital canvas. You'll learn to manipulate light, shadow, texture, and color to produce professional-grade illustrations, concept art, or visually compelling photo manipulations, opening up a world of creative possibilities without the mess of physical art supplies.

How the field grew

1987Thomas and John Knoll begin developing ImagePro, the precursor to Photoshop.
1990Adobe releases Photoshop 1.0, revolutionizing digital image editing.
1995Photoshop 3.0 introduces layers, a fundamental feature for complex digital compositions.
2003Adobe launches Creative Suite (CS), bundling Photoshop with other creative applications.
2013Adobe transitions to the Creative Cloud (CC) subscription model, offering continuous software updates.
