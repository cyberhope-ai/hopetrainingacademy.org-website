What is this field?

Project management is the strategic application of processes, methods, skills, knowledge, and experience to achieve specific project objectives within agreed parameters. It involves meticulous planning, efficient execution, and effective closing of projects to deliver desired outcomes.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. They don't play every instrument, but they ensure each section plays in harmony, on time, and to the right tempo, bringing a complex piece of music (the project) to a successful, unified performance.

How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded, establishing a professional body for the discipline.
1984The Project Management Professional (PMP) certification exam was first offered, setting a global standard for project management expertise.
1987The first edition of the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) was published, providing a foundational framework for the profession.
2002The Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) certification was introduced, offering an entry-level credential for aspiring project managers.
OngoingThe PMBOK® Guide and associated certifications are continuously updated to reflect evolving industry standards and best practices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a Project Manager?

While many project managers hold degrees, this program prepares you for certifications like CAPM and PMP, which can qualify you for roles based on education and experience, not solely a degree. The CAPM requires a secondary degree and 23 hours of project management education (met by this course). The PMP has varying experience requirements depending on your educational background.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included. You will need to schedule and pay for your CAPM or PMP certification exam separately after completing the program.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and comprises 250 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Which CAPM and PMP exams does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current/latest CAPM and PMP certification exams, based on the most recent Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide).

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Project Manager, Project Coordinator, Assistant Project Manager, or even specialized roles like Scrum Master, depending on your experience and further specialization.
