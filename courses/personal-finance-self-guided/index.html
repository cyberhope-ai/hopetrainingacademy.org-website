What is this field?

Personal finance is the art and science of managing your money. It encompasses everything from budgeting and saving to investing, debt management, and planning for retirement. Mastering personal finance means understanding how to make informed decisions about your income and expenses to achieve your unique financial goals, whether that's buying a home, saving for college, or simply building a robust emergency fund.

Think of personal finance as your financial GPS. Just as a GPS guides you to a destination by planning routes and avoiding obstacles, personal finance provides the tools and strategies to navigate your economic landscape. It helps you set clear financial destinations, map out a path to get there, and adjust your course when unexpected detours arise, ensuring you stay on track towards prosperity.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of financial planning as a distinct profession, moving beyond traditional banking and insurance.
1980sGrowth of diverse investment products and increased public interest in personal wealth management.
2000sRise of digital tools, online banking, and early robo-advisors, making financial management more accessible.
2020sIncreased focus on holistic financial wellness, digital financial literacy, and personalized advice.
