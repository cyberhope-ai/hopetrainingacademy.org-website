What is this field?

Teaching high school students involves more than just delivering subject matter; it's about inspiring young adults, fostering critical thinking, and preparing them for their futures. This program equips educators with the latest pedagogical strategies to engage diverse learners, create inclusive environments, and manage classroom dynamics effectively, ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed.

Think of a high school classroom like a complex orchestra: each student is a unique instrument with their own strengths and needs. You, the teacher, are the conductor, guiding them to play in harmony while nurturing their individual talents, ultimately creating a cohesive and impactful learning experience for all.

How the field grew

1800sEarly public high schools emerge, focusing on basic literacy, vocational skills, and preparing students for civic life.
Early 1900sThe Progressive Education movement gains traction, advocating for student-centered learning, critical thinking, and practical application over rote memorization.
Mid-20th CenturyExpansion of comprehensive high schools, with an increased emphasis on college preparatory curricula and a broader range of academic and extracurricular offerings.
Late 20th/Early 21st CenturyIntegration of technology, rise of standardized testing, and a growing focus on personalized learning, equity, and social-emotional development in education.

What the career pays

High school teachers educate students typically aged 14-18, guiding them through complex subjects and preparing them for higher education or careers. They design engaging lessons, manage classroom dynamics, and foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to take this course?

While a bachelor's degree is typically required to become a certified high school teacher, this course focuses on enhancing teaching strategies and classroom management skills. It is designed for both aspiring and current educators looking to improve their practice, regardless of their current degree status.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, this course is focused on professional development and skill enhancement, not preparation for a specific certification exam. Therefore, an exam voucher is not included.

How long does it take to complete this online course?

This is a flexible, self-paced online course, meaning the completion time can vary based on your schedule and learning pace. While typical sessions might run for several weeks, you can progress at your own speed to master the material. Contact us for specific session start dates if you prefer a structured schedule.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

No, this program is designed to equip you with practical teaching strategies and classroom management techniques for high school environments. It does not lead to a specific certification or prepare you for a state licensure exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This training enhances your qualifications for roles such as a high school teacher, substitute teacher, instructional assistant, or an educational support specialist. It provides valuable skills applicable across various educational settings and can be a strong foundation for a teaching career.
