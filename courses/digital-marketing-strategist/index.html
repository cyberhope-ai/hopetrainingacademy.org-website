How the field grew

1990sEmergence of early web advertising and basic search engine optimization.
2000sGoogle Ads (formerly AdWords) and the rise of social media platforms transform online advertising.
2010sMobile-first strategies, content marketing, and advanced analytics become central to digital campaigns.
2020sIntegration of AI for personalization, automation, and campaign optimization, alongside new platforms like TikTok.

What the career pays

A Digital Marketing Strategist designs and executes comprehensive online campaigns, leveraging various digital channels to achieve business objectives. They are responsible for aligning SEO, paid ads, social media, and email marketing into a unified strategy that drives measurable results.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a Digital Marketing Strategist?

No, this program requires no prior experience. Success in digital marketing is often driven by practical skills and a strong portfolio, which this program helps you build.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this comprehensive training program does not include an exam voucher as it focuses on practical skills and portfolio development rather than a single certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the Digital Marketing Strategist program?

This program involves 410 course hours of self-paced study. You will have 12 months of access to complete all modules and your capstone project.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to provide broad, practical skills across the digital marketing landscape and does not specifically prepare you for a single external certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for high-demand roles such as Digital Marketing Specialist, Paid Ads Manager, SEO Specialist, Social Media Strategist, and Content Marketing Manager.
