What is this field?

A Certified Bookkeeper is a highly skilled financial professional, often considered the elite in their field, much like CPAs are in accounting. They are responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, managing transactions, reconciling accounts, and ensuring compliance with financial regulations. This crucial role supports the financial health and operational efficiency of businesses across all industries, providing essential data for decision-making and strategic planning.

Through this comprehensive program, you'll gain the expertise to handle complex financial scenarios, from adjusting entries and payroll to inventory management and fraud prevention. You'll also master Microsoft Excel at an expert level, transforming raw data into actionable insights. Think of it as becoming the financial architect for an organization, building and maintaining the robust systems that track every dollar, ensuring everything is in its right place and ready for analysis.

How the field grew

1987The American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) was founded, setting standards for professional bookkeeping.
1987Microsoft Excel was first released for Windows, revolutionizing spreadsheet software and data analysis.
Early 2000sThe Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program gained widespread recognition, validating proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. This program is designed for experienced bookkeepers with at least two years of full-time (or 3,000 hours part-time) experience who wish to achieve national certification.

Are the certification exam fees included with the program?

This program prepares you thoroughly for both the AIPB Certified Bookkeeper and Microsoft MOS Excel Expert certification exams. Candidates are responsible for registering and paying for their certification exams separately.

How long does it take to complete this career training program?

This is a self-paced program totaling 210 course hours. While you can progress at your own speed, successful completion also requires meeting the AIPB's prerequisite of two years of bookkeeping experience.

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) Certified Bookkeeper certification exam and the current Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification exam for Microsoft Excel.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Certified Bookkeeper, Accounting Clerk, Financial Data Analyst, or Payroll Specialist, equipped with advanced bookkeeping knowledge and expert Excel skills.
