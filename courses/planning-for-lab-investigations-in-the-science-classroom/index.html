What is this field?

This online course provides educators with a deep dive into the scientific investigation process, focusing on how to effectively plan and execute lab activities within a science classroom. You will learn to construct comprehensive pre- and post-lab exercises that seamlessly integrate with experiments following the scientific method, ensuring a cohesive and enriching learning experience for students.

Think of it like being an architect for a hands-on learning experience. Instead of just giving students a blueprint (the lab instructions), you'll learn to design the entire building process, from laying the foundation (pre-lab preparation) to evaluating the finished structure (post-lab analysis), ensuring every step contributes to a strong understanding of scientific principles.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to take this course?

While this course enhances skills for educators, becoming a certified teacher typically requires a bachelor's degree in education or a related field, along with state-specific licensure.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this professional development course does not include an exam voucher, as it is not tied to a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this online course?

This is a 100% online, self-paced course, allowing you to complete the modules on your schedule. The total duration will depend on your individual pace and dedication.

Is there a certification exam associated with this course?

No, this course is designed for professional development and skill enhancement in planning lab investigations, rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This course is ideal for current or aspiring science teachers at the middle or secondary school level, enhancing your ability to design and lead effective lab investigations in your classroom.
