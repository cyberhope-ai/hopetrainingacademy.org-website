What is this field?

The CompTIA TECH+ certification training program is your gateway to a dynamic career in information technology. Designed for individuals without prior IT experience, this program provides a comprehensive introduction to the core concepts and skills needed in today's tech-driven world. You'll gain proficiency in essential areas like computer hardware, operating systems, networking, programming, databases, and cybersecurity, establishing a solid foundation for further specialization and career growth.

Think of CompTIA TECH+ as your IT 'starter kit.' Just as a builder needs to understand basic tools and materials before constructing a house, an aspiring IT professional needs to grasp the fundamental components and principles that make technology work. This program equips you with that essential knowledge, preparing you for entry-level roles and setting the stage for advanced certifications like CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+.

How the field grew

Early 2000sCompTIA establishes itself as a leading vendor-neutral IT certification body, offering foundational credentials.
2018CompTIA introduces the ITF+ (IT Fundamentals) certification, providing a comprehensive entry point for aspiring IT professionals.
2023The ITF+ certification evolves into the current CompTIA TECH+ certification, updating content to reflect the latest industry demands.
