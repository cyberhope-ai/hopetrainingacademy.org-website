What is this field?

Depression is a serious mood disorder that causes a persistent feeling of sadness and loss of interest. It can affect how you feel, think, and behave and can lead to a variety of emotional and physical problems. More than just a bout of the blues, depression requires understanding and often professional support to manage effectively.

This program provides a comprehensive exploration of depression, covering its various forms, underlying causes, and how it's diagnosed. You'll learn about different treatment modalities, from therapy to medication, and gain crucial insights into suicide prevention strategies, enabling you to better understand and support those affected by this widespread condition.

How the field grew

1950sFirst antidepressant medications developed, marking a shift in treatment approaches.
1980DSM-III published, standardizing diagnostic criteria for mental health disorders, including depression.
Early 2000sIncreased public awareness campaigns and efforts to destigmatize mental health conditions.
PresentGrowing focus on integrated mental health care, digital therapeutics, and personalized treatment plans.
