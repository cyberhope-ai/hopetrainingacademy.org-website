What is this field?

Meditation is a practice rooted in diverse cultures that involves training attention and awareness to achieve a mentally clear and emotionally calm state. It encompasses various techniques, from focused concentration to open monitoring, all designed to cultivate inner peace, reduce stress, and improve mental clarity.

Think of meditation like training a muscle in your mind. Just as you'd exercise your body to build strength and endurance, meditation exercises your attention and awareness, making them stronger and more resilient. This mental workout leads to documented health benefits and a deeper connection to oneself.
