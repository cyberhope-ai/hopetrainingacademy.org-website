What is this field?

Nonprofit marketing is the strategic effort to communicate your organization's mission and impact to a diverse audience, including potential donors, volunteers, beneficiaries, and the wider community. It's about building awareness, fostering engagement, and inspiring action to support your cause, ensuring your vital work can continue and grow.

Think of it like a lighthouse for a good cause. Just as a lighthouse guides ships safely to shore, effective nonprofit marketing guides people to your mission, showing them how they can contribute to positive change and helping your organization navigate towards its goals.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of 'social marketing' as a distinct field, applying commercial marketing principles to social causes.
1990sNonprofits increasingly adopt professional marketing and fundraising techniques to compete for resources.
2000sDigital revolution opens new avenues for online fundraising, advocacy, and global outreach.
2010sSocial media platforms become indispensable tools for storytelling, community building, and rapid response campaigns.
