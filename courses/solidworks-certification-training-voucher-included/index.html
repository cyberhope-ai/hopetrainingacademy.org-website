What is this field?

SOLIDWORKS is the industry-standard software for 3D computer-aided design (CAD) and engineering. It empowers designers and engineers to create, simulate, and manage product designs from concept to manufacturing. From intricate mechanical parts to complex assemblies, SOLIDWORKS provides a robust platform for visualizing and validating designs in a virtual environment before they are ever built physically.

Think of SOLIDWORKS as a master builder's digital workshop. Instead of sketching on paper, you're constructing precise 3D models with virtual tools, testing their strength, movement, and fit, and then generating detailed blueprints for construction. It's the essential toolkit for anyone looking to bring innovative products to life, ensuring functionality and efficiency at every stage of the design process.

How the field grew

1993SOLIDWORKS Corporation founded by Jon Hirschtick.
1995First product, SolidWorks 95, released, revolutionizing 3D CAD for Windows.
1997Acquired by Dassault Systèmes, a global leader in 3D design software.
Early 2000sGained significant market share, becoming a dominant force in mechanical design.
PresentContinues to be a widely adopted industry standard for product design and engineering.
