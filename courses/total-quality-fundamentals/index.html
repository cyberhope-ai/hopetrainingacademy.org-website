What is this field?

Total Quality Fundamentals introduces you to the core principles and practices of Total Quality Management (TQM). This foundational training equips you with the knowledge to identify inefficiencies, improve processes, and foster a culture of continuous improvement within any organization. You'll learn how to implement quality systems that enhance customer satisfaction and operational effectiveness.

Think of it like learning to cook. Instead of just following a recipe, Total Quality Fundamentals teaches you the science behind why certain ingredients and techniques work best. You learn how to consistently produce delicious meals, identify when something goes wrong, and adjust your methods to make every dish perfect, every time, ensuring your diners are always delighted.

How the field grew

1950sW. Edwards Deming and Joseph Juran introduce modern quality control methods in Japan.
1980sTotal Quality Management (TQM) principles gain widespread adoption in Western industries.
1987The U.S. establishes the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award to recognize quality excellence.
1990sISO 9000 series of international quality management standards become globally recognized.

02
Core Principles of TQM

Customer focus, leadership, process approach, and continuous improvement methodologies.

03
Quality Tools and Techniques

Introduction to data analysis, problem-solving tools like Pareto charts and Ishikawa diagrams.

04
Process Improvement Methodologies

Understanding the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle and Kaizen for ongoing enhancements.

05
Quality Standards and Frameworks

Overview of ISO 9000 and the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award.

06
Implementing TQM

Strategies for fostering a quality culture and leadership's role in TQM success.
