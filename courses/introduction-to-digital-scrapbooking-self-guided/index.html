What is this field?

Digital scrapbooking is the art of creating beautiful, personalized layouts and albums using computer software and digital tools, blending the tactile charm of traditional scrapbooking with the flexibility and efficiency of modern technology. It allows you to design intricate pages with photos, text, and embellishments without the need for physical paper, scissors, or glue.

Think of it as having an endless craft store at your fingertips, combined with the precision of a graphic design studio. Instead of cutting out paper shapes, you're dragging and dropping digital elements; instead of gluing photos, you're placing them perfectly with a click. This method offers unparalleled creative freedom, easy sharing, and the ability to endlessly revise your artistic vision.

How the field grew

1990sPersonal computers and image editing software make digital art accessible to a wider audience.
Early 2000sWidespread adoption of digital cameras fuels interest in digital photo organization and creative applications.
Mid-2000sDedicated digital scrapbooking software and online communities emerge, offering specialized tools and resources.
2010sAdvancements in cloud storage and social media platforms enable easy sharing and collaboration on digital creative projects.
