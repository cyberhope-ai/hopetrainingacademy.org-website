What is this field?

A Chartered Tax Professional specializes in preparing individual income tax returns, ensuring accuracy and compliance with federal and state tax laws. This career training program equips you with the practical skills to navigate complex tax regulations, identify deductions, and assist clients in fulfilling their tax obligations.

Think of it like being a financial detective: you meticulously examine financial records, interpret tax codes, and piece together the puzzle of an individual's financial year to file their taxes correctly and efficiently. Our hands-on approach ensures you gain real-world competence from day one.

How the field grew

191316th Amendment ratified, establishing federal income tax in the U.S.
1943Introduction of 'pay-as-you-go' income tax withholding for employees.
1954The Bureau of Internal Revenue was reorganized and renamed the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).
1986The Tax Reform Act of 1986 significantly simplified the tax code for many individuals.
2000sWidespread adoption of electronic filing (e-filing) for tax returns.
