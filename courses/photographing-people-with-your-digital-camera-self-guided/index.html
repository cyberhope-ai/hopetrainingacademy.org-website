What is this field?

This online program teaches you the art and science of photographing people using your digital camera. You'll move beyond basic snapshots to understand lighting, composition, and posing techniques that bring out the best in your subjects, whether they are adults, children, or newborns. The goal is to equip you with the skills to create beautiful, memorable images that tell a story.

Think of it like learning to cook a gourmet meal. Anyone can follow a recipe, but a chef understands the nuances of ingredients, temperature, and presentation to create something truly special. This program gives you the 'chef's knowledge' for photography, transforming your ability to capture compelling images of people.

How the field grew

1826First permanent photograph created by Nicéphore Niépce.
1975Kodak engineer Steven Sasson invents the first self-contained digital camera.
1990sDigital photography begins to gain traction with consumer cameras and early image editing software.
2000sDigital Single-Lens Reflex (DSLR) cameras become widely accessible, revolutionizing professional and amateur photography.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program does not require a degree. It is designed for individuals eager to learn digital photography skills, regardless of their prior educational background.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this program is a skill-building course focused on practical photography techniques and does not lead to an external certification or include an exam voucher.

How long does it take to complete this program?

As a self-paced online program, you can complete the material at your own speed, fitting it into your schedule. The flexibility allows you to learn effectively without strict deadlines.

Is there a certification exam for this course?

This program focuses on developing hands-on photography skills rather than preparing for a specific certification exam. Upon completion, you will have a strong portfolio of work.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program equips you with skills for roles such as a portrait photographer, event photographer, or a lifestyle photographer, enabling you to capture compelling images of people for various clients.
