What is this field?

Human Resources (HR) professionals are the backbone of any successful organization, acting as the strategic link between employees and management. They are responsible for attracting, developing, and retaining talent, ensuring fair employment practices, and fostering a positive and productive work environment. From navigating complex labor laws to designing impactful training programs, HR specialists play a crucial role in shaping company culture and driving business success.

Think of an HR professional as the architect of a company's human capital. Just as an architect designs a building to be functional, safe, and inspiring, HR designs and maintains the systems that allow people to thrive within an organization. They ensure everyone has the right tools, support, and environment to do their best work, making them indispensable to any modern enterprise.

How the field grew

1910sPersonnel Management emerges, focusing on employee welfare and efficiency.
1964Civil Rights Act transforms workplace practices, emphasizing equal opportunity.
1980sStrategic HR gains prominence, aligning human capital with business goals.
2000sDigital transformation and globalization reshape HR functions and talent management.
2020sIncreased focus on diversity, equity, inclusion (DEI), and remote work strategies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program or work in HR?

While a bachelor's degree is often preferred for many HR roles, this training program prepares you for certifications like the aPHR and SHRM-CP, which do not strictly require a degree to sit for the exam. The PHR exam has specific experience and education requirements, which are detailed in the course.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

An exam voucher is not included with this training program.

How long does it take to complete the Human Resources Professional program?

This self-paced training program is designed for approximately 150 course hours, allowing you to learn at a pace that fits your schedule.

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for current versions of industry-recognized certifications, including the aPHR (Associate Professional in Human Resources), PHR (Professional in Human Resources), and SHRM-CP (Society for Human Resource Management's Certified Professional) exams.

What types of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates of this program are prepared for various entry-level to mid-level human resources roles such as HR Generalist, Recruiter, HR Representative, HR Assistant, and HR Specialist.
