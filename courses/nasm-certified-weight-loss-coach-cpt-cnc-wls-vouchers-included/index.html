What is this field?

A Certified Weight Loss Coach and Personal Trainer empowers individuals to improve their health through structured exercise, personalized nutrition guidance, and effective behavior change strategies. This comprehensive program prepares you to serve as a trusted expert, helping clients navigate the complexities of fitness and diet to achieve sustainable results.

Think of it like being a personal architect for someone's health journey. You don't just hand them blueprints; you help them lay the foundation, build the structure, and ensure it stands strong against challenges, adapting the plan as their needs evolve to create a healthier, more vibrant life.

How the field grew

1980sFormalization of personal training as a recognized health and fitness profession.
1990sGrowing understanding of the critical link between nutrition and exercise for optimal health.
2000sIncreased demand for specialized expertise in areas like weight management and behavioral coaching.
TodayIntegration of fitness, nutrition, and psychological strategies for holistic client well-being.

What the career pays

Certified Weight Loss Coaches and Personal Trainers design and implement safe, effective exercise programs while providing evidence-based nutrition and behavior change coaching. They guide clients through personalized plans to achieve specific health, fitness, and weight management objectives.

Inside the course, module by module

01
NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT)

Professional development, client relations, behavioral coaching, human movement science, exercise metabolism, assessments, and integrated training concepts.

02
NASM Certified Nutrition Coach (CNC)

Nutritional science, energy balance, macronutrients, micronutrients, hydration, supplements, and behavior change strategies.

03
NASM Weight Loss Specialist (WLS)

The obesity epidemic, physiology of obesity, science of weight loss, metabolism, nutrition, and managing plateaus.

04
Assessment & Program Design

Conducting comprehensive client assessments, body composition testing, and creating customized, evidence-based programs.

05
Coaching & Communication

Motivational interviewing, goal setting, navigating real-world challenges, and effective client communication.
