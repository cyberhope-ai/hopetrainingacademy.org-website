What is this field?

AutoCAD is the industry-standard computer-aided design (CAD) software used by professionals across architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) to create precise 2D and 3D drawings. It's the foundational tool for designing everything from building blueprints and infrastructure layouts to mechanical parts and electrical schematics, enabling accurate visualization and documentation of complex projects.

Think of AutoCAD as the digital blueprint paper and advanced drafting tools combined. Instead of using rulers, compasses, and pencils on a large sheet of paper, you use a computer to draw, measure, and modify designs with unparalleled accuracy and efficiency. It allows designers to create detailed plans that can be easily shared, revised, and used for construction or manufacturing.

How the field grew

1982AutoCAD is first released, revolutionizing the drafting industry by bringing design to personal computers.
1986AutoCAD becomes the most widely used CAD program globally, setting a new standard for design and documentation.
Late 1990sSignificant enhancements in 3D modeling capabilities expand AutoCAD's utility beyond 2D drafting.
2010sIntegration with cloud services and mobile platforms allows for greater collaboration and accessibility in design workflows.
