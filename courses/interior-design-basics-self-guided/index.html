What is this field?

Interior design is more than just decorating; it's the art and science of understanding people's behavior to create functional, safe, and aesthetically pleasing indoor spaces. It involves careful planning, research, coordination, and management of projects to enhance the quality of life and productivity of occupants.

Think of an interior designer as a skilled conductor for a living or working space. They orchestrate various elements like light, color, texture, and furniture to create a harmonious and purposeful environment. This foundational course provides you with the essential 'sheet music' to begin composing beautiful and functional spaces.

How the field grew

18th CenturyEarly forms of interior decoration emerge, often managed by architects or skilled artisans for affluent clients.
Late 19th CenturyThe profession of 'interior decorator' begins to formalize, with pioneers like Elsie de Wolfe emphasizing comfort and practicality.
Mid-20th CenturyThe field evolves into 'interior design,' focusing on functional planning, safety regulations, and the psychological impact of spaces.
21st CenturyEmphasis on sustainability, smart home technology integration, and specialized design areas like healthcare and hospitality.
