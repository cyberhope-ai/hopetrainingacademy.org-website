What is this field?

Adobe Illustrator CC is the industry-standard vector graphics software, essential for creating scalable artwork such as logos, icons, illustrations, and intricate typography. Unlike pixel-based images that can blur when resized, Illustrator's vector art remains crisp and clear at any dimension, from a business card to a billboard.

Think of Adobe Illustrator as your digital sculpting studio for graphics. Instead of painting with pixels, you're precisely shaping lines and curves to build perfect, adaptable designs. This allows you to craft visuals that maintain their quality and impact across all platforms and sizes, making your work consistently professional.

How the field grew

1987First released for Apple Macintosh, revolutionizing desktop publishing.
1989Introduced to Windows, expanding its reach to a broader user base.
2003Integrated into Adobe Creative Suite (CS), offering a unified design ecosystem.
2013Transitioned to Creative Cloud (CC), providing continuous updates and cloud integration.
2020sContinues to evolve with new features, including AI enhancements and collaborative tools.
