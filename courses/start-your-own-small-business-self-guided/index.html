What is this field?

This comprehensive online program guides you through every critical step of launching your own small business. From refining your initial idea and conducting market research to developing a robust business plan and securing funding, you'll gain the foundational knowledge needed to build a sustainable enterprise.

Think of it like planting a garden: you need to prepare the soil, choose the right seeds, understand the climate, and nurture it carefully to see it grow and bear fruit. This program provides the essential tools and knowledge to cultivate your business idea into a flourishing venture.

Registered Apprenticeship — via the United States Department of Labor

While this course provides foundational knowledge for entrepreneurship, Hope Training Academy also offers United States Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship programs, providing structured, online pathways to in-demand careers. These apprenticeships combine paid on-the-job training with related instruction, significantly de-risking your career transition by ensuring you earn while you learn and gain recognized credentials.

Why Hope Training Academy

Hope Training Academy is a program of Video Game Palooza Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to workforce development. Your enrollment directly supports our mission to reinvest in the community, funding free tech training and creating pathways to living-wage careers for others.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for aspiring entrepreneurs regardless of their prior educational background or experience.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this self-guided program focuses on practical business skills and does not lead to an external certification or include an exam voucher.

How long does it take to complete this course?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn at your own speed and schedule, fitting seamlessly into your life.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program equips you with the knowledge and skills to launch and manage your own small business, rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program empowers you to create your own job by launching and operating your own small business, becoming an entrepreneur or proprietor in any industry you choose.
