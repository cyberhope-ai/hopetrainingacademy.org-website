What is this field?

The GED Mathematical Reasoning test assesses your ability to solve quantitative problems and apply mathematical concepts in real-world scenarios. It covers fundamental areas like number operations, algebra, geometry, and data analysis, ensuring you possess the essential math literacy required for everyday life and further education. This program is designed to build your confidence and competence in these areas, preparing you thoroughly for success on exam day.

Think of the GED Math test as the final puzzle piece in completing your foundational education. Just as a strong foundation is crucial for building a sturdy house, mastering these mathematical skills provides the bedrock for pursuing higher education, entering a skilled trade, or advancing in your chosen career path. It's about proving you have the essential tools to tackle challenges and understand the world around you.

How the field grew

1942The first General Educational Development (GED) tests were developed and administered to returning World War II veterans, providing a pathway to civilian employment and education.
1971All U.S. states and territories officially adopted the GED as the standard for high school equivalency, solidifying its role in adult education.
2002A significant revision updated the GED tests to align with evolving high school curriculum standards and better reflect the skills needed for college and careers.
2014The GED underwent a major overhaul, transitioning to computer-based testing and focusing more on critical thinking, problem-solving, and college and career readiness skills.
2024The GED continues to evolve, ensuring its relevance to current educational requirements and workforce demands, providing a vital credential for adult learners.
