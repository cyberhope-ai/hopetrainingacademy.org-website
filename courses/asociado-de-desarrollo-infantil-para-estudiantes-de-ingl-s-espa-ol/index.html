What is this field?

This career training program is specifically designed for English Language Learners seeking to enter or advance in the early childhood education field. It focuses on developing the English proficiency and academic vocabulary necessary to successfully pursue the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, a nationally recognized certification for early childhood educators. You'll gain foundational knowledge in child development, learning strategies, and creating supportive environments, all while enhancing your language skills.

Think of it as building a strong linguistic bridge to a fulfilling career. Just as a construction worker needs the right tools and blueprints to build a house, an aspiring early childhood educator needs the right language skills and knowledge to nurture young minds. This program provides both, ensuring you're well-equipped to understand, communicate, and excel in a daycare or preschool setting.

How the field grew

1971The concept of the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential was first developed to professionalize early childhood education.
1975The very first CDA credential was awarded, marking a significant step in recognizing early childhood educators' expertise.
1985The Council for Professional Recognition was established to administer and oversee the CDA credentialing process nationally.
Early 2000sIncreased national focus on the importance of early childhood education led to greater demand for qualified professionals and robust training programs.
PresentOngoing efforts continue to support diverse individuals in achieving professional credentials like the CDA, ensuring high-quality care for children.

What the career pays

Child care workers nurture and supervise infants, toddlers, and young children in various settings, fostering their social, emotional, physical, and intellectual development. They create safe, engaging, and educational environments, often assisting with daily routines and implementing age-appropriate activities.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed to prepare you for the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, which does not require a college degree. It focuses on practical skills and English language proficiency for early childhood education.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CDA credential?

This program focuses on building your English language skills and foundational knowledge for the CDA credential process. Please note that an exam voucher for the CDA credential is not included with this course purchase.

How long does it take to complete this career training program?

This program consists of 160 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own speed. The time it takes to complete will depend on your individual study habits and English language proficiency progression.

Does this program prepare me for a specific version of the CDA exam?

This program is designed to equip you with the necessary English language skills and foundational knowledge to pursue the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, which has a standardized process rather than frequently versioned exams. It prepares you for the overall CDA accreditation requirements, including the portfolio, verification visit, and exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and obtaining your CDA credential, you'll be prepared for roles such as a Child Care Worker, Preschool Teacher Assistant, Daycare Assistant, or Early Childhood Educator Aide in various early learning environments.
