What is this field?

This online professional development course equips educators with a diverse toolkit of strategies to effectively gauge student understanding at every stage of a lesson. You'll learn how to proactively assess comprehension before new material is introduced, monitor learning in real-time during instruction, and evaluate retention and application after a lesson concludes. The goal is to empower you to make data-driven instructional decisions that lead to improved student outcomes.

Think of it like a skilled chef tasting their dish throughout the cooking process. They don't just wait until the end to see if it's good; they constantly check seasonings, textures, and flavors to ensure a perfect result. Similarly, this course teaches you how to continuously 'taste' your students' understanding, allowing you to adjust your teaching approach precisely when and where it's needed most, preventing learning gaps before they widen.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Introduction to Formative Assessment

Understanding the 'why' and 'what' of checking for understanding, its role in effective instruction, and foundational principles.

02
Module 2: Strategies Before the Lesson

Techniques to activate prior knowledge, assess readiness, and identify misconceptions before new content is introduced.

03
Module 3: Strategies During the Lesson

Real-time methods to monitor student engagement, gauge comprehension, and provide immediate feedback during instruction.

04
Module 4: Strategies After the Lesson

Effective ways to assess learning retention, application of concepts, and identify areas for re-teaching or further practice.

05
Module 5: Implementing and Adapting Strategies

Choosing the right strategy for different learning objectives, adapting techniques for diverse learners, and integrating them seamlessly into your teaching practice.
