What is this field?

Real estate investing involves purchasing, managing, and selling properties for profit. It's a powerful strategy for building long-term wealth, generating passive income, and achieving financial independence. This program equips you with the knowledge to identify profitable opportunities, understand market dynamics, and make informed investment decisions.

Think of it like planting a tree: you invest time and resources upfront (buying and improving a property), nurture it (manage it), and over time, it grows in value and provides fruit (rental income, appreciation). This course teaches you how to select the right 'seeds' and cultivate your 'orchard' for maximum financial yield.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyRise of individual homeownership and rental property markets as viable investment vehicles.
Mid-20th CenturyPost-WWII economic boom fuels suburban growth, increasing demand for residential and commercial real estate.
1960Introduction of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), democratizing access to large-scale property investments.
21st CenturyDigital platforms and fractional ownership models expand accessibility and sophistication of real estate investing.
