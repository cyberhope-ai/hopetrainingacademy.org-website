What is this field?

Administrative professionals are the backbone of any successful organization, ensuring smooth operations, managing communications, and supporting leadership. They are critical problem-solvers, organizers, and communicators who handle a diverse range of tasks from scheduling and record-keeping to event planning and technology utilization. This program equips you with the comprehensive skills needed to excel in this vital role.

Think of an administrative professional as the conductor of an orchestra. While they may not play every instrument, they ensure every section is in sync, every note is played on time, and the entire performance runs flawlessly. This training provides you with the baton and the sheet music to lead your own administrative symphony, mastering both the strategic and technical aspects of modern office management.

How the field grew

1942The International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) is founded, establishing a professional community for administrative roles.
1983IAAP introduces its first certification, the Certified Professional Secretary (CPS), a precursor to the modern CAP credential.
1990sMicrosoft Office applications become the industry standard for productivity, necessitating specialized skills for administrative roles.
1997The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program is launched, validating proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
2001The Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) credential is introduced, reflecting the evolving demands and strategic importance of administrative roles.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no degree prerequisites to enroll in our training program. However, to be eligible to sit for the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam, the IAAP requires specific work experience, which varies based on your highest level of education (e.g., 2-4 years of relevant experience).

Are exam vouchers included with this training?

Yes, this program includes vouchers for both the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam and the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Associate certification exams, along with an IAAP membership and CAP study guide.

How long will it take to complete this career training program?

This program is designed for self-paced learning and includes 345 course hours. The actual time to completion will depend on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Which certification exams will I be prepared for?

You will be thoroughly prepared for the current Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam offered by the IAAP and the current Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Associate certification exams.

What types of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be qualified for roles such as Administrative Assistant, Office Manager, Executive Assistant, Administrative Coordinator, and other essential support positions across various industries.
