What is this field?

This specialized training helps educators seamlessly weave engaging STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) activities into Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) for students with diverse learning needs. You'll discover practical, hands-on methods to make complex concepts accessible, fostering critical thinking and problem-solving skills in every student.

Think of it like building a custom roadmap for each student, where every STEM activity is a carefully chosen step designed to meet their unique IEP goals. This course provides the tools to create those personalized, impactful learning journeys, ensuring special education students not only participate but thrive in STEM.

How the field grew

1975Landmark legislation establishes educational rights for students with disabilities.
1990Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) reinforces individualized learning plans.
2000sIncreased national emphasis on STEM education across all learning environments.
2010sGrowing recognition of the benefits of hands-on STEM for diverse learners in special education.
TodayFocus on practical strategies to align engaging STEM activities with specific IEP objectives.
