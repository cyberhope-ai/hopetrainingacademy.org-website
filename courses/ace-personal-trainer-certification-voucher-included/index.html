What is this field?

A certified personal trainer guides individuals through personalized exercise programs, helping them improve their health, strength, and overall well-being. This involves understanding human anatomy, exercise physiology, and the psychology of behavior change to create effective and safe routines tailored to each client's unique needs and goals.

Think of a personal trainer as a highly skilled architect for the human body. Just as an architect designs a building based on its purpose, location, and the client's vision, a personal trainer designs a fitness plan based on a client's current physical condition, health history, and desired outcomes, ensuring a strong foundation and sustainable progress.

How the field grew

1985American Council on Exercise (ACE) founded, establishing standards for fitness professionals.
1987ACE administers its first certification exam, formalizing the personal training profession.
Early 2000sACE champions behavior change science, integrating it into fitness programming.
2010sFocus on comprehensive fitness models like the ACE Integrated Fitness Training (ACE IFT) Model.

What the career pays

Personal trainers design and implement safe and effective exercise programs for individuals and groups, motivating clients to achieve their health and fitness objectives. They assess client fitness levels, provide instruction on proper exercise techniques, and offer guidance on nutrition and healthy lifestyle choices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become an ACE Personal Trainer?

No, a college degree is not required to take this course or to sit for the ACE Personal Trainer Certification Exam. You must be at least 18 years old and have a high school diploma or equivalent.

Does this program include the ACE Personal Trainer exam voucher?

Yes, this career training program includes a voucher for the ACE Personal Trainer certification exam, covering the cost of your official certification attempt.

How long does it take to complete the ACE Personal Trainer program?

This program is self-paced and includes 50 course hours. Most students complete it within a few months, but you can study at your own speed to fit your schedule.

Which ACE Personal Trainer exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current ACE Personal Trainer certification exam, ensuring you are ready for the latest industry standards and content.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon certification, you can pursue roles such as a Personal Trainer, Fitness Coach, Group Exercise Instructor, or Wellness Coordinator in gyms, health clubs, corporate wellness programs, or as an independent contractor.
