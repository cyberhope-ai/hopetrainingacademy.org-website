What is this field?

This comprehensive online program is designed for aspiring fantasy fiction writers, guiding you through every stage of crafting a compelling novel. You'll explore the fundamental elements of world-building, character development, intricate plot structures, and the unique stylistic demands of the fantasy genre, all from the comfort of your home.

Think of it like learning to build a magnificent castle; you'll master the blueprints, choose your materials, and construct a world that stands tall and captivating for your readers. Our program provides the tools and techniques to bring your most imaginative stories to life, transforming raw ideas into polished manuscripts.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this program focuses on practical skills and portfolio development, making it accessible regardless of your academic background.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course is focused on skill development in creative writing and does not lead to an external certification exam, so no voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This self-guided online program is designed for flexible learning, typically completed in approximately 24 course hours at your own pace.

What job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles as fantasy authors, content writers, editors, or copywriters, applying their enhanced storytelling skills across various industries.

What is a Registered Apprenticeship?

A Registered Apprenticeship is a proven model of job preparation that combines paid on-the-job learning with related instruction, leading to a national credential recognized by the United States Department of Labor.
