What is this field?

Energy medicine explores the body's subtle energy fields to promote healing and well-being. It encompasses a range of practices, including meditation, reiki, and acupuncture, which are often used to complement conventional medical treatments, focusing on the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit to support overall health.

Think of it like tending a garden. Just as a gardener ensures the soil, water, and sunlight are balanced for plants to thrive, energy medicine aims to balance your body's internal 'ecosystem.' By addressing energetic imbalances, these practices help create an optimal environment for your natural healing processes to flourish, leading to enhanced health outcomes.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesPractices like acupuncture and meditation have roots in traditional healing systems spanning thousands of years.
1920sModern reiki, a hands-on healing technique, was developed in Japan by Mikao Usui, gaining popularity worldwide.
1970sThe term 'energy medicine' gained prominence as a field of study, exploring the body's subtle energy systems.
Present DayEnergy healing methods are increasingly recognized for their potential to complement conventional healthcare and enhance overall wellness.
