What is this field?

Photoshop Masking and Selection are fundamental skills for digital artists and graphic designers, enabling precise control over image elements. These techniques allow you to isolate specific parts of an image, make targeted edits, and seamlessly combine various components without permanently altering your original pixels. Mastering these tools is crucial for creating professional-quality composites, performing advanced photo retouching, and designing intricate layouts with unparalleled flexibility.

Think of masking like using a sophisticated stencil. You apply the mask over your artwork, and only the areas not covered by the mask can be painted or modified, leaving the rest untouched. Selection, on the other hand, is akin to carefully cutting out a specific shape from a piece of paper; once selected, you can move, resize, or change only that particular shape. Together, these tools provide incredible power and precision in your creative process, transforming your digital art capabilities.

How the field grew

1987Thomas and John Knoll begin developing 'Display', the precursor to Photoshop.
1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 is officially released, revolutionizing digital image manipulation.
1994Photoshop 3.0 introduces layers, a foundational feature critical for advanced masking techniques.
2003Adobe Creative Suite (CS) launches, integrating Photoshop with a suite of professional design tools.
2013Adobe Creative Cloud (CC) introduces a subscription model, ensuring continuous access to the latest masking and selection innovations.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Introduction to Selections

Explore basic selection tools like Marquee, Lasso, and Magic Wand, and understand their applications.

02
Advanced Selection Techniques

Master tools such as the Pen Tool for precise paths, Color Range, Focus Area, and the powerful Select and Mask workspace.

03
Understanding Layer Masks

Learn to create and apply layer masks, paint with black, white, and gray, and perform non-destructive image editing.

04
Vector Masks and Clipping Masks

Utilize vector shapes for crisp masks and create clipping masks for text, shapes, and image integration.

05
Channel Masks and Luminosity Masks

Discover how to isolate complex elements using color channels and create advanced tonal selections for intricate adjustments.

06
Refining and Outputting Masks

Adjust feathering, density, and contrast of masks, and learn to save selections for future use.

07
Practical Applications

Apply masking and selection skills to real-world scenarios like image compositing, photo retouching, and background removal.
