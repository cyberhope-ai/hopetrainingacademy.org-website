What is this field?

The Certified Penetration Testing Professional (CPENT) program at Hope Training Academy is a comprehensive training pathway designed to elevate your penetration testing skills to an expert level. This program delves into advanced techniques for attacking, exploiting, evading, and defending enterprise network environments, covering everything from writing custom exploits to securing complex IoT, OT, and Cloud systems.

Think of it like a highly skilled detective hired to break into a secure vault, not to steal anything, but to find every possible weakness before a real thief does. You're paid to think like the bad guys to protect the good guys, meticulously identifying vulnerabilities and providing critical insights to fortify an organization's digital defenses.

How the field grew

Early 2000sEmergence of ethical hacking as a recognized discipline to proactively identify security weaknesses.
Mid-2010sGrowing complexity of enterprise networks and targeted attacks necessitate more sophisticated penetration testing methodologies.
Late 2010sPenetration testing expands to include specialized domains like IoT, Operational Technology (OT), and cloud environments due to increasing interconnectedness.
PresentContinuous adaptation of penetration testing techniques and tools to counter rapidly evolving cyber threats and advanced persistent adversaries.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. While prior experience with ethical hacking (like the Certified Ethical Hacker certification) is strongly recommended, your practical skills and dedication are what truly matter for success in this field.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, an exam voucher for the current Certified Penetration Testing Professional (CPENT) certification exam is included with your enrollment, covering the cost of your certification attempt.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

The program is self-paced and designed for flexible learning, requiring approximately 200 course hours to complete. You can progress at a speed that fits your schedule.

Which version of the CPENT exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is continuously updated to prepare you for the current Certified Penetration Testing Professional (CPENT) certification exam, ensuring you learn the most relevant and up-to-date techniques.

What job roles can I pursue after completing this program?

Graduates are well-prepared for roles such as Penetration Tester, Information Security Analyst, Red Team Operator, and Vulnerability Management Specialist, among others in the cybersecurity domain.
