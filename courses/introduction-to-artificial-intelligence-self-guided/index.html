What is this field?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a rapidly evolving field focused on developing computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. This includes learning from data, understanding natural language, recognizing patterns, and making decisions. It's the technology behind self-driving cars, personalized recommendations, and sophisticated data analysis.

Think of AI like teaching a child to recognize different animals. Instead of explicitly programming every single feature of every animal, you show the child many examples, and they learn to identify new animals on their own. Similarly, AI systems are trained with vast amounts of data to learn patterns and make intelligent predictions or actions without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.

How the field grew

1950Alan Turing proposes the 'Turing Test' for machine intelligence.
1956The Dartmouth Workshop officially coins the term 'Artificial Intelligence'.
1997IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
2012Breakthroughs in deep learning, notably AlexNet, revolutionize image recognition.
2022OpenAI's ChatGPT sparks widespread public awareness and adoption of generative AI.
