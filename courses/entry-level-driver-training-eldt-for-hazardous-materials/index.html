What is this field?

Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) for Hazardous Materials is a federally mandated program designed to equip commercial drivers with the essential knowledge for transporting dangerous goods. This specialized training is a prerequisite to taking the state-administered knowledge test for the Hazardous Materials (H) endorsement, which is required for operating vehicles carrying hazardous cargo.

Think of it like getting a special license to carry certain types of cargo. Just as a pilot needs specific training for different aircraft, a commercial driver needs specialized knowledge to handle hazardous materials, ensuring safety for everyone on the road and compliance with stringent federal regulations.

How the field grew

1975The Hazardous Materials Transportation Act (HMTA) establishes federal regulations for the safe transport of dangerous goods.
2012The Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21) mandates new Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements for commercial drivers.
2016The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) publishes the final ELDT rule, setting comprehensive standards for HazMat endorsement training.
2022ELDT regulations become fully effective, requiring approved training programs for all new commercial driver's license (CDL) applicants and those seeking specific endorsements, including HazMat.

Proper procedures for loading, unloading, placarding, marking, and shipping papers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this training?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our Entry-Level Driver Training for Hazardous Materials. This program focuses on the specific knowledge needed for the HazMat endorsement.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

An exam voucher is not included with this training program. The program prepares you for the state-administered knowledge test for the hazardous materials endorsement.

How long does it take to complete the HazMat ELDT program?

This is a self-paced, 16-hour online program. You will have 12 months of access to complete the training at your convenience.

Does this training prepare me for a specific exam version?

This training prepares you to fulfill the federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirement for the hazardous materials (H) endorsement. It covers the foundational knowledge needed for the state-administered test, which does not have version numbers.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

After completing this training and obtaining your hazardous materials endorsement, you can pursue specialized roles as a Hazardous Materials Truck Driver, Tanker Truck Driver, or other commercial driving positions requiring the HazMat endorsement.
