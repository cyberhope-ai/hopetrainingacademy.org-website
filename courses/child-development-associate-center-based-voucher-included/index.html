What is this field?

A Child Development Associate (CDA) credential signifies a professional's expertise in nurturing the growth and development of children from birth through age five. This career training program prepares you to meet the rigorous standards set by the Council for Professional Recognition, covering essential areas like child safety, health, learning environments, and family engagement.

Think of a CDA as the foundational architect for a child's early learning journey. Just as an architect designs a safe and stimulating building, a CDA designs and implements developmentally appropriate activities and environments that foster a child's physical, cognitive, social, and emotional well-being, laying the groundwork for future success.

How the field grew

1975The Council for Professional Recognition established the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential.
1985The first CDA credentials were awarded, recognizing early childhood educators nationwide.
OngoingThe CDA credential continuously evolves to reflect best practices and research in early childhood education.

What the career pays

Child Development Associates provide direct care and educational support to young children in various settings, fostering their holistic development. They create safe, engaging, and stimulating environments that promote learning and well-being for infants, toddlers, and preschoolers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites to enroll in this career training program. However, to be eligible for the Child Development Associate (CDA) certification, you must have a high school diploma or GED, or be a junior or senior in a high school career and technical program.

Does this program include the CDA exam voucher?

Yes, this program includes a voucher that covers the fee for the Child Development Associate (CDA) certification exam once you are eligible to sit for it.

How long does it take to complete this career training program?

This is a self-paced program designed for flexible learning, requiring 120 course hours. The actual time to complete it depends on your individual study schedule.

Which CDA certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the Child Development Associate (CDA) certification process of the Council for Professional Recognition, covering both infant/toddler and preschool-age pathways. You'll receive assistance for your second pathway certification within two years of completion.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon earning your CDA credential, you can pursue roles such as Child Care Provider, Day Care Teacher, Preschool Teacher Assistant, or Family Child Care Home Provider, working with children from birth through age five.
