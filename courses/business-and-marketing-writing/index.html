What is this field?

Business and marketing writing is the art of crafting clear, persuasive, and engaging text designed to achieve specific organizational goals. This includes everything from website content and social media posts to professional reports, sales proposals, and advertising copy. It's about understanding your audience, conveying value, and motivating action, whether that's a purchase, a sign-up, or a deeper understanding of a product or service.

Think of it like building a bridge: business writing creates a sturdy, functional path for information to travel, ensuring clarity and accuracy. Marketing writing, on the other hand, adds the aesthetic appeal and directional signs, making the journey inviting and guiding people exactly where you want them to go. Mastering both allows you to communicate effectively and persuasively in any professional context.

How the field grew

Early 1900sFoundations of modern advertising copywriting and direct mail emerge, focusing on persuasive language.
1960sThe rise of mass media (TV, radio) expands the need for skilled copywriters to craft messages for broader audiences.
1990sThe internet revolutionizes content, introducing web writing, early SEO, and the need for digital communication strategies.
2000sThe era of content marketing takes hold, emphasizing valuable, relevant content through blogs, social media, and email.
2020sAI tools begin to integrate into content creation workflows, enhancing efficiency and enabling new forms of personalized communication.
