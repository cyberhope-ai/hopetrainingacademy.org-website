What is this field?

A Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) is a paraprofessional who practices under the close, ongoing supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). RBTs are crucial in implementing behavior-analytic services, working directly with individuals to help them acquire new skills and reduce challenging behaviors, often in settings like schools, homes, and clinics.

Imagine you're a coach for someone learning a new skill. You break down the steps, celebrate progress, and adjust your approach based on how they're doing. That's similar to what an RBT does, applying structured methods from Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) to help individuals develop crucial life skills and positive behaviors, making a tangible difference in their daily lives.

How the field grew

1998The Behavior Analyst Certification Board (BACB) is established to credential behavior analysts.
Early 2010sRapid growth in demand for Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) services, particularly for individuals with autism spectrum disorder.
2014The BACB introduces the Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) credential to standardize the training and supervision of direct-care staff.
OngoingThe RBT credential becomes a widely recognized and respected standard for paraprofessionals delivering direct behavior intervention services.

What the career pays

Registered Behavior Technicians implement behavior intervention plans developed by BCBAs, helping individuals achieve their behavioral goals. They collect data, assist with assessments, and provide direct support in various therapeutic and educational environments.
