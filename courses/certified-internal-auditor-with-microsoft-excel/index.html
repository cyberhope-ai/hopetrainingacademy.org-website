What is this field?

A Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) is a highly respected professional responsible for evaluating an organization's operations, financial reporting, and compliance with laws and regulations. They act as an independent voice, providing objective assessments to improve efficiency and mitigate risks. This program also equips you with Microsoft Excel Expert skills, essential for advanced data analysis, financial modeling, and reporting in any auditing role.

Think of a CIA as a company's internal detective and problem-solver, ensuring everything runs smoothly and ethically, while the Excel Expert certification is like having a super-powered calculator and data organizer at your fingertips, allowing you to quickly uncover insights and present findings with clarity.

How the field grew

1941The Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) was founded, establishing professional standards for the internal audit profession.
1974The IIA launched the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) designation, becoming the only globally accepted certification for internal auditors.
Early 2000sMicrosoft introduced its Office Specialist (MOS) program, providing industry-recognized validation of proficiency in its software, including Excel.
PresentBoth the CIA and MOS Excel Expert certifications remain highly valued credentials for career advancement in auditing and business analysis.
