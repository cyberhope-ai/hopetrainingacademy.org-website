What is this field?

An Automotive Service Technician is the expert who keeps our vehicles running safely and efficiently. From routine oil changes and tire rotations to complex engine diagnostics and brake repairs, these professionals are the backbone of vehicle reliability. They understand the intricate systems of modern cars, using specialized tools and diagnostic equipment to identify and fix problems.

Think of them as the 'doctors' for your car. Just as a doctor diagnoses and treats human ailments, an automotive technician diagnoses and repairs vehicle issues. They ensure every component, from the electrical system to the engine, functions correctly, providing peace of mind to drivers and extending the life of their vehicles.

How the field grew

1972The National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE) was established to professionalize the industry through standardized certification.
1980sThe introduction of sophisticated electronic systems and computer diagnostics began to transform vehicle repair and maintenance.
1996On-Board Diagnostics, Second Generation (OBD-II) was mandated, standardizing vehicle diagnostic procedures and data access.
2000sThe rise of hybrid and electric vehicles introduced new technologies and specialized skill requirements for technicians.
PresentModern vehicles integrate advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and complex software, demanding continuous learning and diagnostic expertise.
