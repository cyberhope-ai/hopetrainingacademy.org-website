What is this field?

This program is your essential guide to the software side of IT support, focusing on the operating systems and applications that power today's technology. You'll dive deep into managing and troubleshooting various platforms, from the ubiquitous Windows environment to the unique characteristics of macOS and the open-source flexibility of Linux. It's about understanding the digital brain of a computer, ensuring it runs smoothly and securely.

Think of it like learning to be a master mechanic for software. Just as a car mechanic understands engines, transmissions, and electrical systems, you'll learn the inner workings of operating systems, how applications interact with hardware, and how to diagnose and fix software-related issues. This foundational knowledge is critical for anyone aiming to provide effective technical support and maintain reliable computer systems.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA founded to advance the global IT industry.
1999CompTIA A+ certification launched, establishing a vendor-neutral standard for entry-level IT professionals.
Early 2000sA+ evolves to include broader operating system support beyond just Windows.
2010sFocus expands to include mobile devices, cloud, and virtualization concepts.
PresentContinually updated to reflect the latest technologies in hardware, software, and cybersecurity.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll or get certified?

No, a college degree is not required. The CompTIA A+ certification is an industry-recognized credential that validates your skills regardless of your academic background.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This specific course does not include an exam voucher. Vouchers for the CompTIA A+ certification exams must be purchased separately.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is the second of three courses in our A+ certification prep series. Completion time varies based on individual study pace, as it is a self-guided online program.

Which CompTIA A+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the software-related objectives of the current CompTIA A+ certification exam, which typically requires passing two exams (Core 1 and Core 2).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon achieving CompTIA A+ certification, you'll be qualified for entry-level IT roles such as Help Desk Technician, IT Support Specialist, Desktop Support Technician, and Technical Support Analyst.
