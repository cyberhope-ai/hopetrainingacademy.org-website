What is this field?

Homeschooling With Success is a comprehensive training program designed for parents and guardians who wish to provide a robust and enriching education for their children at home. This program equips you with the knowledge and tools to create a dynamic learning environment, ensuring your children thrive academically and socially.

Think of it like becoming the architect and builder of your child's educational journey. Instead of simply following a blueprint, you learn to design and construct a personalized learning experience, tailored to their unique needs and interests, fostering both intellectual curiosity and well-rounded development.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of the modern homeschooling movement in the U.S.
1980sIncreased legal recognition and growth of homeschooling nationwide.
1990sDiversification of homeschooling philosophies, curricula, and support networks.
2000sIntegration of digital resources and online learning into homeschooling practices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for parents and guardians regardless of their prior educational background. It focuses on practical skills for effective homeschooling.

Is an exam voucher included with this purchase?

No, this program does not include an exam voucher as it is a skill-building course, not a certification preparation program.

How long does it take to complete the Homeschool With Success program?

This online program is designed for flexible learning. While it offers approximately 24 course hours of content, you can progress at your own pace, typically completing it within 6 weeks.

Is there a certification exam for this program?

No, this program is focused on practical skills and knowledge for homeschooling and does not conclude with a certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

While primarily for personal development, the skills you gain in curriculum, instruction, and child development are highly transferable. They can enhance your capabilities for roles such as a private tutor, childcare assistant, or educational program aide, or even lead to starting your own educational support services.
