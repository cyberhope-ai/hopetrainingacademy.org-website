What is this field?

This specialized online training program is designed for current or aspiring educators who wish to significantly enhance their effectiveness in teaching science to students in grades 4 through 6. You'll delve into modern pedagogical approaches, curriculum development, and engaging classroom strategies tailored specifically for the unique learning needs and curiosities of upper elementary students. The focus is on fostering a deep understanding of scientific concepts through inquiry-based learning and hands-on activities.

Think of it like a masterclass for a chef who wants to specialize in creating nutritious and exciting meals for a particular age group. Instead of just following a recipe, you'll learn the science behind the ingredients, how to adapt for different tastes, and present dishes in a way that makes everyone eager to try them. This program equips you with the tools to transform your science classroom into a dynamic environment where young minds can explore, question, and discover the wonders of the natural world.

How the field grew

1980sEmphasis on 'hands-on' and experiential learning in science education grows.
1996National Science Education Standards (NSES) provide a framework for K-12 science content.
2013Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) introduce a new vision for K-12 science education, focusing on practices, crosscutting concepts, and disciplinary core ideas.
PresentOngoing evolution towards inquiry-based learning, STEM integration, and digital literacy in elementary science classrooms.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Elementary Science Education

Understanding pedagogical theories, national/state science standards, and the role of science in elementary curriculum.

02
Designing Engaging Science Lessons

Strategies for inquiry-based learning, hands-on experiments, and fostering scientific curiosity in grades 4-6.

03
Life Sciences for Grades 4-6

Effective teaching methods for topics like ecosystems, human body systems, and biodiversity.

04
Physical Sciences for Grades 4-6

Approaches to teaching concepts such as matter, energy, forces, and motion.

05
Earth and Space Sciences for Grades 4-6

Engaging students with topics like geology, weather patterns, and the solar system.

06
Assessment and Differentiation

Formative and summative assessment techniques, and adapting instruction for diverse learners in science.

07
Integrating Technology and STEM

Utilizing digital tools, virtual labs, and incorporating engineering design principles into science lessons.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a teaching degree to enroll in this program?

While this program is designed to enhance the skills of current educators, individuals aspiring to teach science in grades 4-6 may also benefit. Please check your state's specific requirements for elementary school teacher certification.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this program is a professional development course focused on enhancing teaching skills and does not conclude with a certification exam or include an exam voucher.

How long does it take to complete this online training?

This is a self-paced online training program. While typical ed2go courses are designed to be completed in about six weeks with a few hours of study per week, you have flexible access to complete the material at your own pace.

Is there a specific exam associated with this course?

This program is a professional development course and does not lead to a specific certification exam. Its purpose is to provide you with enhanced knowledge and practical strategies for teaching science.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This training is ideal for current elementary school teachers looking to specialize or improve their science instruction for grades 4-6. It can also support roles such as a science curriculum developer or an instructional coach focused on elementary science.
