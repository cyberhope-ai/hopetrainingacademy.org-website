What is this field?

The Cisco CCNP Enterprise program is designed for networking professionals ready to elevate their skills in implementing core enterprise network technologies. This comprehensive program covers the intricacies of dual-stack architecture, virtualization, network assurance, robust security protocols, and automation within complex enterprise environments. You'll gain the expertise needed to manage and administer sophisticated network infrastructures, preparing you for critical roles in today's digital landscape.

Think of it as moving from building a sturdy house to designing and constructing an entire smart city infrastructure. While foundational certifications teach you the basics of connecting devices, CCNP Enterprise training empowers you to architect, secure, and automate vast, interconnected systems, ensuring seamless and resilient operation for large organizations. You'll learn to anticipate challenges, optimize performance, and implement cutting-edge solutions across diverse network domains.

How the field grew

1984Cisco Systems founded, pioneering the development of multi-protocol routers and laying the groundwork for modern internetworking.
1998Cisco introduces the CCIE (Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert) certification, establishing a benchmark for advanced networking proficiency.
Early 2000sRapid expansion of enterprise networks and the internet drives increasing demand for skilled professionals capable of designing and managing complex infrastructures.
2020Cisco revamps its certification program, consolidating CCNP tracks and introducing the current Enterprise certification path, emphasizing automation and software-defined networking.

What the career pays

Computer Network Architects design and implement data communication networks, including local area networks (LANs), wide area networks (WANs), and intranets. They are responsible for ensuring network security, efficiency, and reliability across an organization's infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a formal college degree is not required to enroll. While there are no strict prerequisites, a strong understanding of networking fundamentals, such as that gained from a Cisco CCNA certification or equivalent experience, is highly recommended.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher for the Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification exams is not included with this program offering. You will need to purchase exam vouchers separately when you are ready to test.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This program consists of 105 course hours. It is self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own speed, so the total completion time will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Does this program prepare me for the current Cisco CCNP Enterprise exams?

Yes, this program is specifically designed to prepare you for the current Cisco CCNP Enterprise core exam (350-401 ENCOR) and the Cisco CCNP Enterprise concentration exam (300-420 ENSLD).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you'll be prepared for advanced networking roles such as Network Architect, Senior Network Engineer, Enterprise Network Administrator, or Solutions Architect specializing in networking.
