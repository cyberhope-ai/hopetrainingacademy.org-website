How the field grew

1954Bell Labs develops the first practical silicon solar cell, marking the dawn of modern PV technology.
1970sEarly commercial solar panels become available, primarily for niche applications like satellites and remote power.
2000sGrid-tied solar systems gain traction, increasing the demand for skilled design and installation professionals.
2010sSignificant advancements in PV efficiency and cost reduction drive widespread residential and commercial adoption.
PresentFocus shifts to integrating smart technologies, energy storage solutions, and advanced design tools for optimized performance.

What the career pays

A Solar Design Specialist engineers custom photovoltaic (PV) systems, ensuring optimal energy production and seamless integration. They are responsible for assessing client needs, creating detailed system layouts, and navigating regulatory requirements.

Jobs it opens

Solar PV Designer

Creates detailed plans for residential and commercial solar installations.

Renewable Energy Project Coordinator

Manages the lifecycle of solar projects from conception to completion.

Electrical Design Engineer (PV Systems)

Specializes in the electrical schematics and grid interconnection of solar arrays.

Energy Consultant

Advises clients on the best solar solutions to meet their energy and financial goals.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Customer Expectations & Project Criteria

Assessing client energy needs, preferences, and defining project scope, budget, and timeline.

02
Project Site Assessment

Evaluating location options, shading analysis, and structural considerations for installation.

03
Mechanical Design Principles

Selecting solar panels, mounting structures, and optimizing system layout for energy generation.

04
Electrical Design Principles

Choosing inverters, wiring, and ensuring efficient energy production and system reliability.

05
System Monitoring & Communications

Implementing real-time tracking and analysis for proactive maintenance and optimization.

06
Permitting & Approvals

Navigating local building codes, utility regulations, and interconnection requirements.

07
Project Documentation & Adaptation

Preparing detailed specifications and adjusting designs to meet evolving requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program focuses on practical skills and industry knowledge essential for a career in solar design. While a foundational understanding of solar concepts is encouraged, a college degree is not a prerequisite.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

This program provides comprehensive training in solar design principles. Exam vouchers for external certifications are not included with this specific program purchase.

How long does it take to complete the Solar Design Training program?

This self-paced program is designed to be completed in approximately 20 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own convenience and schedule.

What certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program provides foundational knowledge aligned with the competencies required for roles in solar PV design. It helps prepare you for advanced certification paths, such as the current NABCEP PV Design Specialist certification exam, which typically requires additional training and experience.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Solar PV Designer, Renewable Energy Project Coordinator, or an Electrical Design Engineer specializing in PV systems, contributing to the growing renewable energy sector.
