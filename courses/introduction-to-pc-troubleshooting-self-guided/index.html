What is this field?

PC troubleshooting is the systematic process of identifying, diagnosing, and resolving issues that prevent a personal computer from functioning correctly. This foundational skill is crucial for maintaining system performance, ensuring data integrity, and extending the lifespan of hardware and software. It encompasses everything from fixing a slow internet connection to resolving software conflicts or hardware malfunctions.

Think of PC troubleshooting like being a detective for your computer. When something goes wrong, you gather clues (error messages, symptoms), use your knowledge to form hypotheses (potential causes), and then test solutions until the problem is solved. This course equips you with the tools and techniques to become that effective digital detective.

How the field grew

1980sEmergence of personal computers, requiring basic command-line diagnostics.
1990sRise of graphical user interfaces (GUIs) and the internet, introducing new software and connectivity challenges.
2000sIncreased hardware complexity, widespread malware, and the demand for remote support solutions.
2010sProliferation of mobile devices, cloud computing, and advanced networking, expanding the scope of technical issues.
PresentIntegration of AI-assisted diagnostics and the need to troubleshoot complex interconnected systems and cybersecurity threats.
