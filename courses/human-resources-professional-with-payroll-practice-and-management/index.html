What is this field?

Human Resources (HR) professionals are the backbone of any organization, managing everything from recruitment and employee development to compensation and workplace culture. They ensure a healthy, productive environment where employees thrive and business objectives are met. Payroll, a critical extension of HR, focuses on the accurate and timely payment of wages, taxes, and benefits, ensuring compliance with complex federal and state regulations.

This program equips you with expertise in both vital fields, transforming you into a versatile professional capable of navigating the intricate landscape where people management meets financial administration. Think of it like being the architect and the builder of a company's most valuable asset: its people and their financial well-being.

How the field grew

1976Human Resources Certification Institute (HRCI) founded, establishing a standard for HR professionalism.
1982American Payroll Association (now PayrollOrg) established to advance the payroll profession.
1993HRCI launched the Professional in Human Resources (PHR) certification.
2000PayrollOrg introduced the Fundamental Payroll Certification (FPC) exam.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll or get certified?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this program or to pursue the PHR or FPC certifications. These certifications are based on demonstrating professional experience and knowledge.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

No, an exam voucher for the PHR or FPC certification exam is not included with the purchase of this training program. You will need to purchase your exam voucher separately.

How long will it take to complete this career training program?

This program is self-paced and designed to be completed in approximately 250 course hours, allowing you to learn at a schedule that fits your life.

This program thoroughly prepares you for the current Professional in Human Resources (PHR) certification exam offered by HRCI and the Fundamental Payroll Certification (FPC) exam offered by PayrollOrg. It also provides useful information for the current SHRM-CP and aPHR exams.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are well-prepared for roles such as HR Generalist, Payroll Specialist, HR Specialist, Compensation and Benefits Manager, HR Representative, and Payroll Manager, among others.
