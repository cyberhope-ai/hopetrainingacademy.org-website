What is this field?

A Personal Trainer is a fitness professional dedicated to guiding individuals toward their health and wellness goals. They design and implement safe and effective exercise programs, provide nutritional guidance, and offer motivational support to clients of all ages and fitness levels. This role requires a deep understanding of human anatomy, exercise science, and behavioral coaching principles to help clients achieve sustainable results.

Think of a Personal Trainer as a personalized GPS for your fitness journey. Just as a GPS helps you navigate to a specific destination by providing turn-by-turn directions, a Personal Trainer helps you reach your fitness goals by providing step-by-step exercise plans, nutritional advice, and encouragement, ensuring you stay on track and overcome obstacles along the way.

How the field grew

1950sRise of fitness culture and early forms of personal training emerge, often informally.
1987The National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) is founded, establishing science-based training methodologies.
1990sPersonal training gains widespread recognition as a legitimate profession, with certifications becoming standard.
2000sExpansion of specialized certifications, including sports nutrition and youth exercise, reflecting diverse client needs.
PresentContinued evolution with online training, advanced biomechanics, and integrated wellness approaches.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this program or to sit for the NASM Certified Personal Trainer certification exam. A high school diploma or equivalent is typically sufficient.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, this program includes a voucher that covers the cost of sitting for the NASM Certified Personal Trainer certification exam upon eligibility.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This program is self-paced and designed for completion within 80 course hours. Many students complete it within a few months, depending on their study schedule.

Which NASM certification exam does this prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) certification exam, and also includes preparation for the NASM Certified Sports Nutrition Coach (CSNC) and NASM Youth Exercise Specialist (YES) credentials.

What kind of jobs can I get with these certifications?

With these certifications, you can pursue roles such as a Certified Personal Trainer in gyms or independently, a Sports Nutrition Coach, or a Youth Exercise Specialist. You'll be qualified to work with diverse client populations, from general fitness enthusiasts to athletes and young people.
