How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded, establishing a professional body for project management.
1984PMI introduces the first Project Management Professional (PMP) certification exam, setting a global standard.
1996The Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) is first published, standardizing project management practices worldwide.
2021PMP exam content updated to reflect agile, hybrid, and predictive approaches across People, Process, and Business Environment domains.

What the career pays

Project Managers are the orchestrators of success, responsible for planning, executing, and closing projects while managing resources, timelines, and budgets. They lead diverse teams, communicate with stakeholders, and navigate challenges to achieve specific goals and deliver value.

Jobs it opens

Project Manager

Oversees all aspects of a project from conception to completion.

Program Manager

Manages multiple related projects to achieve strategic business objectives.

Portfolio Manager

Aligns projects and programs with organizational strategy and investment.

Senior Project Lead

Leads complex projects and mentors junior project management staff.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for PMP certification?

While not always a degree, PMI requires specific educational and project management experience hours to sit for the PMP exam. Our program helps you meet the 35 contact hours of project management education.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses solely on preparing you for the PMP certification exam; an exam voucher is not included. The exam fee is paid directly to PMI.

How long will it take to complete this PMP prep program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to study at your own convenience. The time to complete depends on your prior knowledge and study habits.

Which PMP exam version does this course prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Project Management Professional (PMP) certification exam, covering the latest content outline from PMI.

What kind of jobs can I get with a PMP certification?

PMP certification opens doors to roles like Project Manager, Program Manager, Project Lead, and Portfolio Manager across diverse industries such as IT, construction, healthcare, and finance.
