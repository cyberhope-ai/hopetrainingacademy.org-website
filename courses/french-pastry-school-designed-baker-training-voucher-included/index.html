What is this field?

Baking and pastry arts combine scientific precision with artistic flair to create delicious and visually stunning edible masterpieces. It's a field that demands meticulous attention to detail, a deep understanding of ingredients, and the creativity to innovate. From the foundational chemistry of dough fermentation to the intricate decoration of a multi-tiered cake, a professional baker or pastry chef crafts experiences that delight the senses and bring joy to countless occasions.

Imagine a baker as an edible architect and chemist, meticulously measuring ingredients and understanding their reactions to build a perfect loaf of bread or a delicate pastry. Just as a builder uses blueprints and materials to construct a house, a baker uses recipes and ingredients to craft a product, ensuring each component plays its part in achieving the desired texture, flavor, and appearance. This program equips you with both the scientific knowledge and the practical skills to master this delicious craft.

How the field grew

Ancient EgyptFirst evidence of leavened bread, a staple food and early form of baking.
17th CenturyDevelopment of modern pastry techniques in France, elevating baking to an art form.
19th CenturyIndustrialization of baking, with new equipment making production more efficient.
20th CenturyRise of specialized pastry shops and culinary schools, professionalizing the craft.
Present DayEmphasis on artisan, healthy, and globally-inspired baking, alongside food safety standards.
