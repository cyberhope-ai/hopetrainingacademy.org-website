What is this field?

Hemodialysis Technicians, often called Dialysis Technicians, are vital members of the healthcare team, working closely with doctors and nurses to deliver critical care to individuals suffering from permanent kidney failure. They operate complex dialysis machines, monitor patients during treatment, and ensure a safe and sterile environment, directly impacting the quality of life for those undergoing regular dialysis.

Imagine your kidneys as the body's natural filters, removing waste and excess fluids. When kidneys fail, a hemodialysis machine steps in to perform this essential function. As a Hemodialysis Technician, you are the expert who sets up, operates, and maintains this life-sustaining equipment, ensuring each patient receives precise and effective treatment, much like a skilled pilot meticulously managing an aircraft's systems for a safe journey.

How the field grew

1940sFirst successful human dialysis procedures, laying the foundation for modern kidney treatment.
1960sDevelopment of long-term dialysis, creating a critical need for specialized care providers to manage ongoing treatments.
1970sMedicare coverage for End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) significantly expanded access to dialysis, driving demand for trained technicians.
1980s-PresentContinuous advancements in dialysis technology and techniques, emphasizing the need for highly trained and certified hemodialysis technicians.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this career training program is designed to prepare you for an entry-level Hemodialysis Technician role and the BONENT CHT certification without requiring a college degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the certification?

An exam voucher is not included with this program. You will be responsible for registering and paying for the BONENT CHT certification exam separately.

How long does it take to complete the Hemodialysis Technician training?

This program consists of 205 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete the material on a schedule that fits your life.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you to sit for the Certified Hemodialysis Technician (CHT) certification exam offered by the Board of Nephrology Examiners Nursing and Technology (BONENT).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for an entry-level position as a Hemodialysis Technician or Dialysis Technician in hospitals, clinics, or specialized dialysis centers.
