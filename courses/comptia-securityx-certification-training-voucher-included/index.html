What is this field?

The CompTIA SecurityX certification, formerly known as CASP+, is designed for experienced cybersecurity professionals who want to validate their advanced-level skills in enterprise security architecture, operations, and risk management. This program delves into complex topics like integrating security across diverse IT environments, advanced authentication, cryptographic techniques, and incident response, preparing you to tackle the most sophisticated cyber threats facing organizations today.

Think of it like becoming a master architect for a city's defense system. While others might build walls or patrol streets, you're designing the entire strategic framework—ensuring all systems work together, anticipating future threats, and leading the response when an attack occurs. This training equips you to be that strategic leader, capable of designing and implementing robust security solutions across an entire enterprise.

How the field grew

1982CompTIA founded, laying the groundwork for IT professional standards.
1993Launched the CompTIA A+ certification, establishing a benchmark for IT skills.
Early 2000sExpanded into specialized cybersecurity certifications to address evolving digital threats.
Recent YearsIntroduced and evolved advanced credentials like SecurityX (formerly CASP+), reflecting the dynamic cybersecurity landscape.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, a degree is not required to enroll. While there are no formal prerequisites for this course, CompTIA recommends a minimum of ten years of IT administration experience, with at least five years in hands-on technical security.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, this program includes a prepaid voucher for the current CompTIA SecurityX certification exam. Please note that separate proctor fees may apply and are not included.

How long will it take to complete this training?

This program consists of 75 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on a schedule that fits your life. Most students complete the material within a few months.

Which version of the CompTIA SecurityX exam does this course prepare me for?

This course is designed to prepare you for the current CompTIA SecurityX certification exam. CompTIA regularly updates its exams to reflect the latest industry standards and threats.

What kind of jobs can I get with a CompTIA SecurityX certification?

The CompTIA SecurityX certification prepares you for advanced roles such as Security Architect, Security Engineer, Technical Lead Analyst, and Application Security Engineer, among others.
