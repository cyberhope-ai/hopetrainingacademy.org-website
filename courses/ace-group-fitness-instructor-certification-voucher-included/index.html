What is this field?

A Group Fitness Instructor is a dynamic leader who designs and delivers engaging exercise classes for groups of people. This role goes beyond just demonstrating movements; it involves motivating participants, ensuring proper form for safety, adapting exercises for various fitness levels, and fostering a positive, energetic environment. You become the conductor of a fitness orchestra, guiding individuals through a harmonious workout experience.

Imagine you're leading a high-energy dance fitness class, a calming yoga session, or an intense boot camp. As an ACE Group Fitness Instructor, you're not just teaching exercises; you're creating an experience that inspires health and wellness. You'll learn to craft effective workouts, manage diverse groups, and become a trusted guide on your participants' fitness journeys, all while building a fulfilling career in the health and fitness industry.

How the field grew

1985The American Council on Exercise (ACE) was founded, establishing professional standards and certifications for fitness professionals.
1990sGroup fitness expanded rapidly beyond traditional aerobics, with ACE developing certifications for diverse new modalities like step and indoor cycling.
2000sACE integrated evidence-based practices and a holistic approach to health coaching into its certification programs, emphasizing comprehensive wellness.
TodayACE remains a globally recognized leader, continually evolving its certifications to reflect the latest science and industry trends in group fitness instruction.
