What is this field?

Natural health and healing is a holistic approach to well-being, focusing on the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit to achieve optimal health. It emphasizes prevention, self-care, and the body's innate ability to heal, utilizing natural remedies, lifestyle adjustments, and a balanced perspective on health and illness.

Think of it like learning the fundamental ingredients and basic cooking techniques for a healthy, balanced diet. This program provides the core understanding of how various elements contribute to overall wellness, preparing you to make informed choices and advocate for a natural approach to health.
