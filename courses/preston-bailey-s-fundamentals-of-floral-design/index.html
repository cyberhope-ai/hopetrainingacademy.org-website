What is this field?

Floral design is the art of arranging flowers and other plant materials to create beautiful and harmonious compositions. It blends creativity, an understanding of color theory, texture, and form with practical skills in flower care and arrangement techniques. Floral designers craft arrangements for a wide array of occasions, from intimate personal gifts to grand event installations, transforming spaces and conveying emotions through natural beauty.

Think of a floral designer as a living sculptor, using nature's palette to create temporary masterpieces that evoke emotion and enhance environments, much like a chef crafts a dish from raw ingredients. They select, prepare, and arrange fresh blooms to tell a story or set a mood, making every petal and leaf contribute to the overall artistic vision.
