What is this field?

The Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized exam designed to assess a student's preparedness for entry into healthcare programs, including nursing, dental hygiene, and medical assisting. It evaluates fundamental academic knowledge in Reading, Math, Science, and English and Language Usage. Passing the TEAS exam is often a critical prerequisite for admission, serving as a benchmark to ensure applicants possess the foundational skills necessary for success in rigorous healthcare curricula.

This specialized training program focuses specifically on equipping you with the practical skills and strategic approaches needed to excel in the Math and Science sections of the TEAS exam. Think of it as a focused training regimen for a crucial academic hurdle. By honing your understanding of key concepts and developing effective test-taking strategies, you'll gain the confidence and competence to perform your best, significantly enhancing your chances of acceptance into your desired healthcare education program.

How the field grew

1990sDevelopment of standardized entrance exams for healthcare programs to ensure student readiness.
Early 2000sThe TEAS exam emerges as a widely adopted assessment tool for allied health and nursing school admissions.
OngoingContinuous updates and revisions to the TEAS exam content to align with evolving healthcare education standards.
