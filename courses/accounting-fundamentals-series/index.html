What is this field?

Accounting Fundamentals training equips you with the core principles of financial record-keeping, essential for any organization. This program introduces you to the systematic process of identifying, recording, and summarizing financial transactions, providing the bedrock knowledge for managing a business's economic health.

Think of it like learning the basic rules of a game: you'll understand how money moves in and out, how to keep score, and how to present that score clearly. You'll master double-entry bookkeeping, analyze financial data, and prepare reports that tell the story of a company's financial performance.

How the field grew

Ancient CivilizationsEarly forms of record-keeping emerged to track trade and taxes.
1494Luca Pacioli published 'Summa de arithmetica,' detailing double-entry bookkeeping.
1800sIndustrial Revolution spurred complex accounting systems for growing businesses.
Mid-20th CenturyStandardization of accounting principles and the rise of professional bodies.
Present DayDigital transformation and automation continue to reshape accounting practices.

Process invoices and ensure timely payment to vendors.

Accounts Receivable Clerk

Manage incoming payments and ensure customer accounts are current.
