What is this field?

Stock trading involves the buying and selling of shares in publicly traded companies, with the goal of profiting from price fluctuations. It's a dynamic and analytical field that demands a deep understanding of market trends, company performance, and economic indicators. Mastering stock trading empowers individuals to make informed investment decisions, manage personal wealth, or pursue professional roles in the financial sector.

Think of stock trading like being a skilled detective in a vast marketplace. You learn to meticulously investigate companies, analyze clues from financial reports, and interpret market signals to predict future movements. Just as a detective gathers evidence to solve a case, a successful stock trader collects and processes information to make strategic decisions about when to buy or sell, aiming to uncover profitable opportunities.

How the field grew

1602The Dutch East India Company issues the first publicly traded shares.
1792The Buttonwood Agreement establishes the New York Stock Exchange.
1971NASDAQ launches as the world's first electronic stock market.
2008The Global Financial Crisis underscores the need for robust financial analysis and risk management.
