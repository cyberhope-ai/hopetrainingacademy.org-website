What is this field?

An Eyelash Stylist is a skilled beauty professional specializing in enhancing natural eyelashes through various techniques like extensions, lifting, and tinting. This role combines artistic precision with client care, transforming appearances and boosting confidence. Eyelash stylists work in salons, spas, or as independent contractors, offering personalized services to a diverse clientele seeking to elevate their look.

Think of an Eyelash Stylist as a meticulous artist for the eyes. Just as a painter uses brushes and colors to create a masterpiece, a lash stylist uses specialized tools and products to craft beautiful, customized lash designs. This program equips you with both the artistic techniques and the business acumen to build a successful career in the booming beauty industry.

How the field grew

1940sEarly forms of false eyelashes gain popularity in Hollywood, setting the stage for lash enhancement.
2000sIndividual lash extensions emerge from Asia, revolutionizing the beauty industry globally.
2010sAdvanced techniques like Volume Lashing are introduced, offering fuller, more dramatic results.
2020sLash lifts and tints become mainstream, expanding the range of services offered by lash professionals.

What the career pays

Eyelash Stylists apply extensions, perform lifts, and tint lashes to enhance clients' natural beauty. They also manage client consultations, maintain hygiene standards, and often handle business operations like marketing and scheduling.
