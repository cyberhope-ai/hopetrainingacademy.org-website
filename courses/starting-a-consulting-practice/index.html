What is this field?

Consulting involves sharing your specialized knowledge or skills with individuals or organizations to help them solve problems, improve performance, or achieve specific goals. It's about leveraging your expertise to provide valuable insights and actionable strategies, often on a project-by-project basis.

This program empowers you to formalize your expertise into a viable business. Think of it like building a custom house: you have the skills (carpentry, plumbing, electrical), but this course teaches you how to become the general contractor—managing clients, pricing your work, marketing your services, and ensuring your business foundation is solid.

How the field grew

1886Arthur D. Little establishes one of the first modern management consulting firms, focusing on technical research.
1914Booz Allen Hamilton founded, pioneering management consulting for businesses and government.
1926McKinsey & Company established, formalizing strategic consulting methodologies.
1980sRise of specialized consulting, driven by technology and globalization, expanding opportunities for individual experts.
2000s-PresentDigital transformation and remote work accelerate the growth of independent consultants and online consulting practices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to start a consulting practice?

No, a specific degree is not required. Success in consulting is primarily based on your expertise, experience, and ability to deliver value to clients. This program focuses on equipping you with the business skills needed to launch and manage your practice.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this program focuses on practical business skills and does not lead to a certification exam, therefore an exam voucher is not applicable or included.

How long does it take to complete this online program?

This is a flexible online learning program, allowing you to progress at your own pace. While there are no fixed hours, it's designed to provide comprehensive guidance for launching your consulting business effectively.

Is there a certification exam associated with this course?

This program is designed to provide you with the knowledge and skills to start your own consulting practice, rather than preparing you for a specific industry certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completing this program, you'll be prepared to launch your own independent consulting practice, work as a freelance business advisor, or apply your entrepreneurial skills within an existing organization as a specialized consultant or management analyst.
