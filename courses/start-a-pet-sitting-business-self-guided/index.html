What is this field?

A pet sitting business offers professional care for pets in their owners' homes, providing services like feeding, walking, playtime, and medication administration while owners are away. It's an ideal venture for animal lovers seeking flexibility and independence, allowing you to build a career around your passion.

Think of it like being a trusted, temporary guardian for beloved family members. Instead of a traditional office, your 'workplace' is often a client's home, ensuring pets receive personalized attention and maintain their routines in a familiar environment, providing peace of mind for pet parents.

How the field grew

1970s-1980sInformal pet care evolves into recognized professional services
1990sEstablishment of national pet sitting associations and industry standards
2000sIncreased demand for in-home pet care and specialized services
2010s-PresentIntegration of technology, online platforms, and further professionalization of the industry

What the career pays

Pet sitting business owners provide compassionate care for animals in their clients' homes, managing all aspects from client acquisition to service delivery. They ensure pets are safe, happy, and well-cared for, offering peace of mind to owners.
