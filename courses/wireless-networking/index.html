What is this field?

Wireless networking allows devices to connect and communicate without physical cables, using radio waves. From your home Wi-Fi to vast corporate networks and public hotspots, wireless technology is fundamental to modern connectivity. This program demystifies the principles behind these invisible connections, equipping you with the expertise to design, implement, and maintain reliable wireless infrastructures.

Think of wireless networking like building a sophisticated, invisible highway system for data. Instead of laying down asphalt (cables), you're setting up signals and access points to guide information seamlessly from one point to another, ensuring fast, secure, and efficient travel for all connected devices.

How the field grew

1997First Wi-Fi standard (802.11) released, laying groundwork for modern wireless LANs.
2000sRapid expansion of Wi-Fi hotspots and widespread adoption in homes and businesses.
2010sEmergence of advanced wireless technologies like Wi-Fi 6 and 5G, enabling faster speeds and greater capacity.
TodayWireless networking is critical for IoT, cloud computing, and ubiquitous connectivity across all industries.
