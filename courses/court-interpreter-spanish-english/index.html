What is this field?

A court interpreter is a vital linguistic professional who facilitates communication between individuals who speak different languages within legal proceedings. Their role is to accurately and impartially convey spoken language, ensuring that all parties, including defendants, witnesses, and legal professionals, fully understand the proceedings, testimony, and instructions.

Think of a court interpreter as a crucial bridge in the justice system. Just as a physical bridge connects two separate landmasses, an interpreter connects two distinct languages, ensuring that every word, nuance, and legal concept is precisely transferred between English and Spanish speakers, upholding the principle of fair and accessible justice for everyone involved.

How the field grew

1978Federal Court Interpreters Act established, mandating certified interpreters in federal courts.
1990sGrowing recognition of the need for standardized state-level court interpreter certification.
Early 2000sExpansion of training methodologies, including online platforms, to meet demand for qualified interpreters.
2010sIncreased focus on practical, hands-on simulation in training programs to mirror real-world courtroom environments.
