What is this field?

Business Data Analytics is the process of examining large datasets to discover patterns, correlations, and other insights that can help organizations make better decisions. It involves using statistical analysis, data mining, and predictive modeling to transform raw data into actionable intelligence, guiding strategic planning and operational improvements across various business functions.

Think of a business data analyst as a detective for an organization's performance. Just as a detective sifts through clues to understand past events and predict future outcomes, a data analyst sifts through sales figures, customer feedback, and market trends to uncover hidden opportunities, identify risks, and recommend strategies that drive growth and efficiency.

How the field grew

2003International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) founded to advance the business analysis profession.
2010sRapid growth in big data and analytics drives demand for specialized business analysis skills.
2018IIBA launches the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) to validate expertise in this critical domain.
PresentBusiness data analytics becomes a cornerstone for strategic decision-making across all industries.
