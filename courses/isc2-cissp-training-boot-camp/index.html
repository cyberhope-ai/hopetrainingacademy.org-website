What is this field?

The Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) credential is a globally recognized certification for information security professionals. It validates an individual's expertise in designing, implementing, and managing best-in-class cybersecurity programs. Earning your CISSP demonstrates a deep understanding across a broad spectrum of security domains, from risk management to software development security, positioning you as a leader in the field.

Think of a CISSP as the architect and chief engineer of a fortress, but for digital information. Just as an architect designs robust physical defenses, a CISSP designs and oversees the comprehensive security framework for an organization's critical data and systems. They don't just patch vulnerabilities; they strategically build and maintain an entire security ecosystem to protect against evolving threats.

How the field grew

1989The International Information Systems Security Certification Consortium, or (ISC)², was formed.
1994The CISSP certification was officially launched, becoming one of the first vendor-neutral security credentials.
OngoingThe CISSP Common Body of Knowledge (CBK) is regularly updated to reflect the latest cybersecurity threats and technologies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

While a degree can be beneficial, it is not a strict prerequisite for enrolling in this training program. The CISSP certification itself requires specific professional experience, but you can become an Associate of (ISC)² by passing the exam without meeting the full experience requirements first.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program provides comprehensive preparation materials, boot camp sessions, and a Certification Success Program designed to support your exam journey, including eligibility for a second exam attempt if certain conditions are met after an unsuccessful first attempt.

How long does it take to complete the CISSP training program?

The program is self-paced and flexible, allowing you to learn at a speed that fits your schedule. The intensive boot camp format is designed to efficiently cover the broad range of knowledge required for the CISSP exam.

Which CISSP exam version does this training prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current CISSP certification exam, covering the latest Common Body of Knowledge (CBK) domains established by (ISC)².

What kind of job can I get after earning my CISSP?

Earning your CISSP opens doors to advanced roles such as Information Security Analyst, Security Architect, Security Consultant, and even Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), making you a highly sought-after professional in the cybersecurity industry.
