What is this field?

A Veterinary Assistant is a vital member of any animal care team, providing essential support to veterinarians and veterinary technicians. They are often the first and last point of contact for pet owners, playing a crucial role in client communication, patient intake, and maintaining a clean, organized clinic environment. Their duties range from assisting with examinations and procedures to preparing surgical suites, performing laboratory tests, and ensuring the comfort and well-being of animal patients.

Think of a Veterinary Assistant as the indispensable 'right hand' in a veterinary practice. Just as a medical assistant supports a doctor in a human hospital, a vet assistant ensures the smooth operation of an animal clinic, allowing veterinarians and technicians to focus on advanced medical care. They handle many of the day-to-day tasks that keep an animal hospital running efficiently and compassionately.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyInformal roles supporting veterinarians begin to emerge as animal medicine professionalizes.
1960sFormalized training programs for veterinary technicians and assistants start developing across North America.
1980s-PresentIncreased specialization in veterinary medicine drives demand for skilled support staff, expanding the scope of the veterinary assistant role.
2000s-PresentGrowth of online learning platforms makes veterinary assistant training more accessible to a wider audience.

Is there a certification exam for Veterinary Assistants?
