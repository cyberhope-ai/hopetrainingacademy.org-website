What is this field?

Investing is the act of allocating resources, usually money, with the expectation of generating income or profit. It's about making your money work for you, rather than just saving it. This course demystifies the world of personal finance, guiding you through the fundamentals of growing your wealth to achieve long-term financial security, especially for retirement.

Think of investing like planting a garden. Instead of just storing your seeds (money) in a shed, you plant them in fertile soil (investments) and nurture them. Over time, these seeds grow into plants that yield fruits and vegetables (returns), providing sustenance for your future. This program teaches you how to choose the right 'soil' and 'seeds' for your financial garden.

How the field grew

1970sIntroduction of index funds, democratizing diversified investing.
1980sRise of 401(k) plans, shifting retirement planning to individuals.
1990sEmergence of online brokerage platforms, making investing more accessible.
2000sGrowth of passive investing strategies and robo-advisors.
2020sIncreased focus on financial literacy and personalized investment strategies.
