What is this field?

Teaching Proper Text Annotations & Evidence is a professional development program designed for educators to enhance their instructional methods for reading comprehension. It focuses on equipping teachers with strategies to guide students in actively engaging with texts, identifying key information, and using textual evidence to support their understanding. This involves techniques like highlighting, note-taking, and questioning directly within the reading material.

Think of it like teaching a detective to meticulously examine a crime scene. Just as a detective marks clues, identifies patterns, and gathers evidence to solve a case, students learn to 'investigate' a text. By annotating and finding evidence, they develop deeper understanding, improve critical thinking, and can articulate their insights more effectively, moving beyond surface-level reading to true comprehension.

How the field grew

1980sGrowing academic focus on 'reading to learn' strategies beyond basic decoding.
2000sIncreased emphasis on critical thinking and analytical skills in K-12 education.
2010sCommon Core State Standards highlight the crucial role of text evidence in comprehension.
2020sDigital literacy and the ability to discern credible evidence become paramount in a complex information landscape.
