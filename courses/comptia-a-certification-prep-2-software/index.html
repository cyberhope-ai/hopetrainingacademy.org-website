What is this field?

This program focuses on the critical software components of IT support, preparing you for a significant portion of the current CompTIA A+ certification exam. You'll dive deep into operating systems like Windows, macOS, and Linux, understanding their installation, configuration, and troubleshooting. The curriculum also covers virtualization concepts and the management of multifunction devices, equipping you with the skills to diagnose and resolve common software-related issues.

Think of it like becoming a master chef for computer systems. While the first part of A+ might teach you about the kitchen appliances (hardware), this course teaches you how to prepare the ingredients, follow recipes, and fix any cooking mishaps (software issues) to create a perfect meal (a smoothly running computer). You'll learn to manage the 'brain' of the computer, ensuring all programs and functions work harmoniously.

How the field grew

1982CompTIA (then the Association of Better Computer Dealers) is founded.
1993The first CompTIA A+ certification is introduced, setting a benchmark for PC technicians.
Early 2000sCompTIA A+ becomes a widely recognized and respected entry-level IT certification globally.
PresentThe CompTIA A+ certification continuously evolves to cover new technologies like cloud, virtualization, and mobile devices.
