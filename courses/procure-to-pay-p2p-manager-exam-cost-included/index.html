What is this field?

Procure-to-Pay (P2P) is the end-to-end process that encompasses everything from requisitioning goods or services to paying the supplier. It's a critical business function that ensures an organization acquires necessary resources efficiently, manages cash flow effectively, and maintains strong supplier relationships. By integrating procurement and accounts payable, P2P streamlines operations, reduces costs, and minimizes risk.

Think of P2P as the central nervous system for your organization's spending. Just as a nervous system coordinates various body parts to achieve a goal, a well-managed P2P process coordinates purchasing, receiving, and payment functions to ensure every dollar spent delivers maximum value. This training program equips you with the leadership and technical skills to optimize this vital process, transforming it from a cost center into a strategic advantage.

How the field grew

1980sEarly enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems begin to integrate disparate business functions, laying groundwork for process-centric views.
1990sThe advent of e-procurement tools starts to digitize purchasing, highlighting the potential for end-to-end process efficiency.
2000sIncreased focus on supply chain optimization and cost control leads to the formalization of the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) concept.
2010sWidespread adoption of cloud-based P2P platforms and automation technologies transforms how organizations manage expenditures.
PresentAdvanced analytics, artificial intelligence, and machine learning further refine P2P processes, emphasizing strategic value and resilience.

No, there are no prerequisites to take this course. However, to earn the Accredited Procure-to-Pay Manager (APPM) certification, the Institute of Finance and Management (IOFM) recommends varying levels of related work experience depending on your educational background, ranging from 1 to 5 years.

Is the certification exam fee included with the program?

Yes, the cost of the certification exam for the Accredited Procure-to-Pay Manager (APPM) designation from IOFM is included with your program enrollment.

How long will it take to complete this career training program?

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you to take the 90-minute, 100-question multiple-choice exam to earn the Accredited Procure-to-Pay Manager (APPM) designation from the Institute of Finance and Management (IOFM).
