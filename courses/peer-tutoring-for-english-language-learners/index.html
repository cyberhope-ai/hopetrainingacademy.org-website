What is this field?

Peer tutoring for English Language Learners (ELLs) is a powerful instructional strategy where students work together to support each other's language acquisition and academic growth. It involves pairing students, often with varying levels of English proficiency, to engage in collaborative learning activities, practice language skills, and reinforce academic concepts. This approach fosters a supportive classroom environment, builds confidence, and provides personalized attention that can significantly accelerate an ELL's progress.

Think of it like a study buddy system, but with a clear educational purpose. Just as an athlete benefits from practicing with a teammate who can offer immediate feedback and encouragement, an ELL student gains immensely from working alongside a peer. This course teaches educators how to design, implement, and manage such a system effectively, ensuring both the tutor and tutee benefit from the experience and contribute to a more inclusive and successful learning environment.

How the field grew

1960sEmergence of English as a Second Language (ESL) as a distinct field of study and practice.
1970sGrowing research and recognition of the academic and social benefits of peer learning and collaborative instruction.
1990sIncreased focus on communicative language teaching and student-centered approaches, integrating peer interaction into language learning.
2000sDiversification of student populations and a greater demand for differentiated instruction and structured support for English Language Learners.
PresentIntegration of technology and refined methodologies for implementing effective peer tutoring programs to support diverse student needs.

Understanding English Language Learners

Foundations of Peer Tutoring

Learn the principles of effective peer learning, its benefits for both tutors and tutees, and various peer tutoring models.

03
Designing a Peer Tutoring Program

Set clear program goals, identify student needs, and structure engaging and productive tutoring sessions.

Develop strategies for selecting suitable student tutors and equipping them with essential skills and resources.
