What is this field?

Digital Court Reporters are essential professionals who accurately record and transcribe legal proceedings using specialized digital audio and video equipment and software. They ensure every word spoken in court, depositions, or administrative hearings is precisely captured, creating official records vital for legal processes. This role is critical for maintaining the integrity of the justice system, providing an impartial and complete account of events.

Think of a Digital Court Reporter as the unbiased historian of the courtroom. Just as a historian meticulously documents events for future reference, a digital reporter uses advanced technology to create a precise, verifiable record of legal proceedings, capturing every detail for attorneys, judges, and future appeals.

How the field grew

1970sIntroduction of early audio recording devices in legal settings.
1990sEmergence of digital audio recording technology, improving clarity and storage.
2000sDevelopment of specialized digital reporting software with annotation capabilities.
2010sIncreased adoption of digital reporting methods to address court reporter shortages.
2020sExpansion of remote and hybrid digital reporting for virtual proceedings.
