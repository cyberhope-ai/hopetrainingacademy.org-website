What is this field?

The GED Mathematical Reasoning test is a crucial component of the General Educational Development (GED) exam, designed to assess your ability to solve quantitative problems and understand mathematical concepts typically learned in high school. Passing this test demonstrates a foundational level of mathematical literacy, opening doors to higher education and a broader range of employment opportunities.

Think of preparing for the GED Math test like learning to read a map before a big journey. Just as a map helps you navigate unfamiliar territory, mastering these math skills provides you with the essential tools to understand and solve real-world problems, manage personal finances, and succeed in academic or vocational training programs.

How the field grew

1942The GED test is first offered to help returning WWII veterans earn high school equivalency.
1971The first major revision to the GED test is introduced, updating content and format.
2002A significant update aligns the test with modern educational standards and introduces computer-based testing options.
2014The latest major revision is launched, focusing on college and career readiness standards across all subject areas.
