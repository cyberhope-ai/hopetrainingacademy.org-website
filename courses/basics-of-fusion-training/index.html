What is this field?

Basics of Fusion Training equips you with the essential skills to master Autodesk Fusion, a powerful cloud-based 3D CAD/CAM software. This program guides you through creating parametric models, complex assemblies, and technical drawings, preparing you to design real-world products from concept to manufacturing.

Think of Autodesk Fusion as your digital workshop, allowing you to sculpt, assemble, and refine virtual prototypes with precision. Just as a chef uses various tools to create a culinary masterpiece, Fusion provides the tools to bring your product ideas to life, ensuring they are functional, durable, and ready for production.

How the field grew

2013Autodesk Fusion 360 launched as a cloud-based CAD/CAM platform.
2015Expanded capabilities to include integrated CAM, rendering, and simulation tools.
2020sEvolved into 'Autodesk Fusion,' continuously integrating advanced manufacturing and design technologies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for beginners and has no prerequisites. Anyone eager to learn 3D modeling and product design with Autodesk Fusion is welcome.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

This program focuses on building practical skills in Autodesk Fusion and does not lead to a specific certification or include an exam voucher.

How long will it take to complete the Basics of Fusion Training program?

You will have six months of access to complete the 80 course hours at your own pace. This timeframe is calculated to allow ample time for thorough learning.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is focused on hands-on skill development in Autodesk Fusion and does not directly prepare you for a certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Product Designer, CAD Designer, Industrial Designer, or Mechanical Design Engineer, applying their skills in various manufacturing and design industries.
