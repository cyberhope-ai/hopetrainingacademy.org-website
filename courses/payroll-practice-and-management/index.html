What is this field?

Payroll is the essential process of managing employee compensation, including wages, salaries, bonuses, and deductions. It involves calculating gross pay, withholding taxes, administering benefits, and ensuring compliance with a myriad of federal, state, and local regulations. A robust payroll system is critical for any organization to maintain financial health and employee satisfaction.

Think of payroll as the financial heartbeat of any organization. Just as a heart ensures blood flows correctly throughout the body, payroll ensures that money flows accurately and on time to employees, while also managing critical tax obligations and benefits, keeping the entire operation healthy and compliant.

How the field grew

1938The Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) established national minimum wage, overtime pay, recordkeeping, and child labor standards.
1982PayrollOrg (formerly the American Payroll Association) was founded, becoming the leading professional organization for payroll professionals in the U.S.
2000PayrollOrg launched the Fundamental Payroll Certification (FPC) program, providing a nationally recognized credential for payroll professionals.
