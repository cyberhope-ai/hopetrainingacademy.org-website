What is this field?

Business and marketing writing is the strategic craft of creating clear, concise, and compelling text designed to inform, persuade, and engage specific audiences. It encompasses everything from internal communications and reports to external marketing materials like website copy, advertisements, and social media posts, all aimed at achieving organizational goals and enhancing brand perception.

Think of it like a master chef crafting a menu. Just as a chef carefully selects ingredients and prepares dishes to delight customers and reflect the restaurant's brand, a skilled writer chooses words and structures content to resonate with readers, convey a specific message, and ultimately drive desired actions, whether it's a purchase, an inquiry, or a deeper connection with a brand.

How the field grew

1950sRise of mass media advertising and professional copywriting for print and broadcast.
1990sEmergence of digital content, web writing, and early forms of online marketing.
2010sExplosion of content marketing, SEO-driven writing, and social media communication strategies.
2020sIntegration of AI tools for content generation and optimization, focus on personalized and data-driven writing.
