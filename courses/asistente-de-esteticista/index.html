What is this field?

An Esthetician Assistant is a skilled professional who specializes in enhancing skin health and appearance through various treatments. Think of them as the architects of radiant skin, using their expertise to perform facials, body treatments, hair removal, and provide personalized skincare advice. They help clients look and feel their best, often working in spas, salons, or even independently.

Our career training program equips you with both the hands-on techniques for advanced skincare and the crucial business acumen needed in today's market. Beyond mastering treatments like anti-aging facials or body contouring, you'll learn digital marketing, client communication, and professional development, ensuring you're not just skilled but also business-savvy and ready for success.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become an Esthetician Assistant?

No, a postsecondary nondegree award is typically the entry-level education for this field. Our program provides the comprehensive training you need to succeed without requiring a traditional college degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on providing hands-on skills and professional development for a career as an Esthetician Assistant. It does not lead to a specific certification exam, and therefore, no exam voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete the Esthetician Assistant training?

This self-paced program includes 243 course hours. The total time to complete it will depend on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to equip you with practical skills and professional knowledge to work as an Esthetician Assistant. It does not specifically prepare you for a single, standardized certification exam, but rather a broad range of industry competencies.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates of this program are prepared for roles such as Skincare Specialist, Spa Esthetician, or even to work independently as a beauty consultant. You'll be equipped for positions in salons, spas, wellness centers, and private practices.
