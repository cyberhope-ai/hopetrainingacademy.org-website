What is this field?

The Basic Housekeeping Career Training Program equips you with the comprehensive skills needed to excel as a full-charge housekeeper in diverse environments, from private residences to bustling hospitality groups. You'll gain expertise in meticulous cleaning techniques, efficient laundry management, and effective home organization, ensuring every space you manage is pristine and welcoming.

Think of a professional housekeeper as the meticulous director of a household's daily operations. Just as a conductor ensures every instrument plays its part in harmony, you'll orchestrate cleanliness, order, and comfort, making sure every detail, from sparkling surfaces to freshly laundered linens, contributes to a perfectly maintained environment.

How the field grew

1990sThe demand for skilled household professionals grows, leading to a focus on structured training.
Early 2000sIndustry experts begin developing comprehensive curricula to standardize professional housekeeping practices.
2010sTraining programs expand to include advanced techniques, career development, and specialized skills like childcare.

What the career pays

A full-charge housekeeper is responsible for maintaining the cleanliness, order, and overall upkeep of a home or facility. This includes managing daily cleaning tasks, laundry, organization, and sometimes basic childcare or kitchen duties, ensuring a comfortable and well-maintained environment.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this program?

No, there are no prerequisites or prior degrees required to enroll in the Basic Housekeeping Career Training Program. It's designed for anyone eager to start or advance their career.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This program focuses on skill development and awards a Certificate of Completion upon successful achievement; it does not include an external certification exam or voucher.

How long will it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced program with 50 course hours. You will have six months of access to complete the training at your convenience.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program provides comprehensive training for a career in housekeeping and awards a Certificate of Completion, but it does not lead to an external certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as a full-charge housekeeper in private residences, a hotel housekeeper, a household assistant, or a residential cleaning specialist.
