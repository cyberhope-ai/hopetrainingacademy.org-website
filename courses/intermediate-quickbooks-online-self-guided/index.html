What is this field?

QuickBooks Online is a leading cloud-based accounting software designed to help businesses manage their finances efficiently. This intermediate program builds upon foundational knowledge, teaching you how to handle more complex financial tasks such as advanced payroll, detailed reporting, and sophisticated expense tracking, all within the secure, accessible online platform.

Think of it like upgrading from driving a car to navigating a complex vehicle with advanced features. You've mastered the basics, and now you're learning to use GPS, cruise control, and advanced diagnostics to get to your destination more efficiently and with greater control. This program equips you with the skills to confidently steer your business's financial health.

How the field grew

1983Intuit, the creator of QuickBooks, is founded.
1992The first QuickBooks desktop software is released, revolutionizing small business accounting.
2001QuickBooks Online launches, bringing cloud-based financial management to businesses.
2014QuickBooks Online undergoes a significant redesign, enhancing user experience and features.
