What is this field?

A Medical Secretary, often known as a Medical Administrative Assistant, is the backbone of any healthcare facility, ensuring smooth daily operations. This vital role involves managing patient records, scheduling appointments, handling correspondence, and supporting medical staff. With the increasing reliance on digital systems, proficiency in office software, especially Microsoft Excel, is crucial for tasks like data organization, reporting, and financial tracking.

Think of a Medical Secretary as the central nervous system of a medical office. Just as the nervous system coordinates all bodily functions, a Medical Secretary coordinates the flow of information, appointments, and administrative tasks, keeping the entire practice running efficiently and allowing healthcare providers to focus on patient care.

How the field grew

1980Our lead instructor, Douglas Best, began his career as a field medical instructor in the US Army, laying the groundwork for his extensive healthcare and training expertise.
1990sThe widespread adoption of personal computers began to transform medical administrative roles, introducing digital tools for record-keeping and scheduling.
2000sThe advent of Electronic Health Records (EHR) systems revolutionized medical office management, demanding advanced technical skills from administrative professionals.
PresentModern Medical Secretaries are indispensable for navigating complex digital workflows, managing patient data, and leveraging software like Microsoft Excel for operational efficiency and reporting.
