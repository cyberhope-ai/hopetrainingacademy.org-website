What is this field?

Nature photography is the art of capturing elements of the natural world, including landscapes, wildlife, plants, and natural phenomena. It encompasses a wide range of styles, from grand scenic vistas to intimate close-ups of flora and fauna, all aimed at showcasing the beauty and wonder of our environment. This course focuses on leveraging your digital camera's capabilities to achieve professional-looking results in diverse natural settings.

Think of your digital camera as a versatile paintbrush, and nature as your canvas. This training program teaches you how to master the 'strokes' – understanding light, composition, and camera settings – to paint vivid, compelling images of the world around you. Whether you're documenting a majestic mountain range or the delicate details of a dew-kissed flower, you'll gain the technical and artistic skills to bring your vision to life.

How the field grew

1975The first digital camera prototype was developed, laying the groundwork for electronic image capture.
1990sDigital cameras began to enter the consumer market, making photography more accessible to enthusiasts.
Early 2000sDigital Single-Lens Reflex (DSLR) cameras became dominant, offering professional-grade image quality and control, revolutionizing nature photography.
PresentMirrorless cameras, advanced sensors, and computational photography tools continue to expand creative possibilities for nature photographers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this nature photography training. This program is designed to equip individuals with practical skills regardless of their prior academic background.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, an exam voucher is not included as this course focuses on skill development and does not lead to an external certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this course?

This is a self-paced online course. While you can progress at your own speed, it typically involves approximately 24 hours of instruction and is often completed over a six-week period.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This course is designed to build foundational and advanced skills in nature photography, not to prepare for a specific certification exam. It focuses on practical application and artistic development.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

While this course enhances a hobby, the skills learned can be applied to various roles such as a freelance nature photographer, a photography assistant, or contributing to stock photography agencies. It also provides a strong foundation for further professional photography training.
