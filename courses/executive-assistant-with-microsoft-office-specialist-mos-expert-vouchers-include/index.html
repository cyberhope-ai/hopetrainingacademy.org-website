What is this field?

Executive Assistants are indispensable professionals who provide high-level administrative support directly to senior leadership, ensuring the smooth operation of an executive's office and overall organizational efficiency. Their responsibilities extend beyond traditional administrative tasks to include strategic communication, project coordination, and managing complex schedules, making them vital partners in executive decision-making.

Think of an Executive Assistant as the conductor of an executive's professional life. Just as a conductor orchestrates every instrument to create a harmonious performance, an Executive Assistant meticulously manages schedules, communications, and projects, ensuring all elements align perfectly to support their executive's goals and the organization's success.

How the field grew

1942International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) founded.
1970sCertified Administrative Professional (CAP) certification introduced by IAAP.
Late 1990sMicrosoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program launched, validating software proficiency.

What the career pays
