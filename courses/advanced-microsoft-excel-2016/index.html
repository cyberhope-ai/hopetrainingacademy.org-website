What is this field?

Advanced Microsoft Excel 2016 training moves beyond basic spreadsheet operations, equipping you with powerful tools to analyze, manage, and visualize complex datasets. You'll master sophisticated functions, data modeling techniques, and dynamic reporting features essential for informed decision-making in any professional setting.

Think of it like upgrading from a basic calculator to a high-powered scientific instrument for data. While a calculator handles simple arithmetic, advanced Excel allows you to build intricate models, automate complex calculations, and uncover hidden patterns within vast amounts of information, turning raw numbers into strategic business intelligence.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel first launched for the Apple Macintosh, laying the groundwork for its future dominance.
1987Excel for Windows was released, quickly becoming a formidable competitor in the spreadsheet market.
1993Excel 5.0 introduced revolutionary features like VBA macros and PivotTables, fundamentally changing data analysis.
2007A major interface overhaul with the Ribbon and significant increases in row/column capacity enhanced its power.
2016Microsoft Excel 2016 brought further enhancements to data analysis tools, forecasting capabilities, and charting options.
