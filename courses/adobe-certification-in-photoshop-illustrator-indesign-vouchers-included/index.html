What is this field?

Visual design is the art of communicating ideas through images, typography, and layout, shaping everything from logos and websites to magazines and marketing materials. This program equips you with proficiency in the industry-standard Adobe Creative Cloud applications: Photoshop for image manipulation, Illustrator for vector graphics, and InDesign for page layout.

Think of these three powerful tools as a specialized toolkit for a digital artist. Photoshop is your digital darkroom for editing photos and creating raster art, Illustrator is your precision drawing board for scalable logos and illustrations, and InDesign is your publishing studio for assembling professional documents and layouts. Together, they empower you to bring any visual concept to life.

How the field grew

1987Adobe Illustrator, a revolutionary vector graphics editor, is first released.
1990Adobe Photoshop, the industry-standard image manipulation software, makes its debut.
1999Adobe InDesign is introduced, quickly becoming a leading desktop publishing application.
2013Adobe Creative Cloud launches, offering these powerful tools as an integrated subscription service.
