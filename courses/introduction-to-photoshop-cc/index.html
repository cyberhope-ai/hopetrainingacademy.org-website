What is this field?

Adobe Photoshop CC is the industry-standard software for image manipulation, graphic design, and digital art. This powerful tool allows you to enhance photographs, create stunning visual compositions, and bring your creative visions to life. From correcting flaws in a picture to designing complex digital artwork, Photoshop provides an extensive suite of features for professionals and enthusiasts alike.

Think of Photoshop as a digital darkroom combined with an artist's studio. It enables you to precisely adjust colors, remove unwanted elements, combine multiple images seamlessly, and add special effects, much like a chef uses various ingredients and tools to craft a culinary masterpiece. This course provides a hands-on introduction to navigating its interface and utilizing its core functionalities.

How the field grew

1987Thomas and John Knoll begin developing 'ImagePro', the precursor to Photoshop.
1990Adobe Systems releases Photoshop 1.0 for Macintosh.
1994Photoshop 3.0 introduces layers, a foundational feature for complex image editing.
2003Adobe bundles Photoshop into its Creative Suite (CS) collection.
2013Photoshop transitions to a subscription-based model as part of Adobe Creative Cloud (CC).
