What is this field?

Outdoor education is an innovative pedagogical approach that moves learning beyond traditional classroom walls, utilizing natural environments as dynamic teaching spaces. It emphasizes hands-on, experiential learning, where students engage directly with their surroundings to explore concepts in science, history, art, and more.

This method fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, and a deeper connection to the natural world. Think of it like swapping a textbook for a living, breathing laboratory, where lessons come alive through direct interaction with the environment, promoting holistic development and environmental stewardship.

How the field grew

Early 1900sEmergence of nature study movements and scouting organizations, laying groundwork for outdoor learning.
Mid-20th CenturyDevelopment of experiential education philosophies, notably with Kurt Hahn's Outward Bound, emphasizing character building through outdoor challenges.
Late 20th CenturyGrowth of environmental education, focusing on ecological literacy and conservation, often delivered through outdoor programs.
21st CenturyIncreased integration of outdoor learning into mainstream curricula, recognizing its benefits for academic achievement, well-being, and skill development.
