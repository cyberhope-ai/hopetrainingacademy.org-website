What is this field?

Data analytics and visualization is the process of inspecting, cleansing, transforming, and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, informing conclusions, and supporting decision-making. It's about taking raw numbers and turning them into a clear story that helps businesses understand their past performance and predict future trends.

Think of it like being a detective for a business. Instead of clues, you have data – sales figures, customer feedback, website traffic. You gather these clues, organize them, look for patterns, and then present your findings in an easy-to-understand way, like charts and dashboards, so everyone can see what's happening and what steps to take next.

How the field grew

1960sEarly data processing systems emerge, laying groundwork for structured data management.
1980sSpreadsheet software like Microsoft Excel becomes widely adopted for data organization and basic analysis.
2000sBusiness Intelligence (BI) tools gain prominence, enabling interactive reporting and dashboard creation.
2010sThe rise of Big Data, Python, and advanced visualization platforms like Power BI revolutionize data analysis capabilities.
