What is this field?

Agile Analysis is the practice of applying business analysis principles within an Agile framework, focusing on delivering value iteratively and adaptively. It involves understanding stakeholder needs, defining requirements, and facilitating communication to guide development teams in fast-paced, evolving project environments.

Think of an Agile Analyst as a skilled navigator on a fast-moving ship. Instead of following a rigid, pre-defined map, they constantly assess the environment, gather new information, and adjust the course to ensure the team builds the right features efficiently, delivering maximum value to the customer with each iteration.

How the field grew

Early 2000sThe Agile Manifesto formalized principles for iterative and collaborative software development.
2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) was founded to advance the global business analysis profession.
Mid-2010sRecognizing the growing adoption of Agile, IIBA introduced its first certification specifically for Agile analysis practitioners.
