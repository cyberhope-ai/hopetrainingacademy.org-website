What is this field?

Accounts Payable Specialists are vital to any organization's financial health, ensuring that all bills and financial commitments are paid accurately and on time. They manage invoices, process payments, and maintain meticulous financial records, often serving as the backbone of a company's cash flow management. This role demands precision, organizational skills, and a strong understanding of financial principles.

Think of an Accounts Payable Specialist as the 'financial gatekeeper' for outgoing money. Just as a librarian meticulously tracks books borrowed and returned, an AP Specialist tracks every invoice received and every payment made. They ensure that only legitimate expenses are paid, that payments are made on schedule, and that all transactions are recorded correctly, preventing financial errors and maintaining strong vendor relationships.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel's initial release, revolutionizing spreadsheet-based financial management.
1990sSpreadsheet software becomes indispensable for businesses, driving demand for proficient users in financial departments.
Early 2000sThe Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program gains prominence, validating essential software skills globally.
2000s-PresentThe Institute of Finance & Management (IOFM) establishes professional certifications like Accounts Payable Specialist (APS), standardizing industry best practices.
OngoingContinuous evolution of financial software and automation tools necessitates updated skills and certifications for AP professionals.

Do I need a degree to become an Accounts Payable Specialist?

While a degree can be helpful, it's not strictly required. The Institute of Finance & Management (IOFM) allows experience to substitute for a degree for their APS certification, such as 3 years of AP experience without a degree, or 1-2 years with an associate's or bachelor's degree.
