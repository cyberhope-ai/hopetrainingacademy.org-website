What is this field?

A Healthcare and Eldercare Mediator is a neutral third party who facilitates communication and negotiation between individuals or groups involved in disputes within medical, long-term care, or family eldercare contexts. These professionals help navigate complex issues like treatment decisions, end-of-life planning, family disagreements over care, and billing conflicts, aiming for mutually agreeable solutions.

Think of a mediator as a skilled guide helping two people find common ground when they're lost in a dense forest of disagreement. Instead of telling them which path to take, the mediator helps them communicate effectively, understand each other's perspectives, and ultimately discover a path they can both agree on, preserving relationships and achieving resolution.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of formal mediation as an alternative dispute resolution method.
1990sGrowing recognition of mediation's value in family and community disputes.
2000sIncreased demand for specialized mediation in complex fields like healthcare and eldercare.
2010sDevelopment of structured models like INACCORD for specific conflict types.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Healthcare and Eldercare Mediator?

While many mediators have backgrounds in law, social work, or healthcare, this program focuses on practical skills and specialized knowledge. No specific degree is required to enroll, though a bachelor's degree is common for the general mediator profession.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included. Upon successful completion, you will receive four professional certificates directly from Mediators Without Borders.

How long does it take to complete the Healthcare and Eldercare Mediator training program?

This program is self-paced and designed for flexible learning, totaling 240 course hours. You can progress at your own speed, allowing you to balance training with your existing commitments.

What certifications will I earn upon completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will receive four professional certificates from Mediators Without Borders: Mediation, Applied Mediation Practice, Healthcare and Eldercare Mediation, and Professional Healthcare and Eldercare Mediation.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Healthcare Mediator, Eldercare Conflict Specialist, Bioethics Committee Facilitator, or Insurance Dispute Resolution Professional in various settings including hospitals, nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and private practice.
