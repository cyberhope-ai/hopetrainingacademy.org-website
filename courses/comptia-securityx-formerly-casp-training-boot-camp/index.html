What is this field?

A CompTIA SecurityX professional is an elite cybersecurity architect and engineer, moving beyond foundational defense to proactively design, implement, and manage complex security solutions across entire enterprise environments. They are the strategic thinkers who identify vulnerabilities, assess risks, and build resilient systems to protect an organization's most critical assets from sophisticated threats.

If a cybersecurity analyst is like a skilled security guard protecting a building, a CompTIA SecurityX professional is the master architect who designs the entire security system, from the blueprints of network infrastructure to surveillance, access control, and comprehensive incident response protocols, ensuring the whole structure is resilient against the most advanced and persistent attacks.

How the field grew

2011CompTIA launches the CASP+ certification for advanced security professionals.
OngoingRegular updates ensure CASP+ (now SecurityX) remains current with evolving cyber threats and technologies.
RecentThe CASP+ certification is rebranded as CompTIA SecurityX, reflecting its comprehensive scope in enterprise security.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

While a degree can be beneficial, it is not a strict requirement for enrollment. This program focuses on practical skills and industry certifications, though significant prior IT and security experience (10+ years IT, 5+ years hands-on security) is strongly recommended.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CompTIA SecurityX certification?

An exam voucher is not included with this specific training program. You will need to purchase your exam voucher separately to take the current CompTIA SecurityX certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the CompTIA SecurityX training?

This is an intensive 5-day boot camp designed for experienced professionals. You will also have access to course materials for three months following completion.

Which CompTIA SecurityX exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program is meticulously designed to prepare you for the current CompTIA SecurityX certification exam, ensuring your knowledge is up-to-date with the latest industry standards.

What kind of jobs can I get after earning my CompTIA SecurityX certification?

With a CompTIA SecurityX certification, you'll be qualified for advanced roles such as Security Architect, Security Engineer, Technical Lead Analyst, and Application Security Engineer, among others.
