What is this field?

Holistic and integrative health is an approach to wellness that considers the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—and seeks to understand the root causes of imbalance rather than just treating symptoms. It blends conventional medicine with complementary therapies, emphasizing prevention, self-care, and the body's innate healing capabilities. This field recognizes that all aspects of an individual's life are interconnected and contribute to their overall health.

Our Holistic and Integrative Health: Foundations 2 program builds on core principles by diving into specific ancient healing systems and practices. You'll explore the foundational tenets of Ayurvedic and Traditional Chinese Medicine, understanding their unique perspectives on health, energy, and balance. The program also integrates the vital roles of spirituality and meditation, providing practical insights into how these practices can foster mental clarity, emotional resilience, and physical harmony for a comprehensive approach to well-being.
