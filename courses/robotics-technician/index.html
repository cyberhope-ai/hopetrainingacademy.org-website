What is this field?

A Robotics Technician is a skilled professional responsible for the installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and programming of robotic systems used across various industries. They ensure that automated machinery operates efficiently and safely, playing a critical role in modern manufacturing, logistics, and beyond. Their expertise keeps production lines running smoothly and integrates new robotic solutions into existing infrastructure.

Think of a Robotics Technician as a specialized mechanic and programmer for advanced automated systems. Just as a car mechanic diagnoses engine issues and performs routine maintenance, a Robotics Technician diagnoses malfunctions in robotic arms or automated assembly lines, performs preventive care, and fine-tunes their programming to optimize performance. They are the frontline experts who keep the gears of automation turning.

How the field grew

1950sFirst industrial robots developed, laying the groundwork for factory automation.
1980sRobotics adoption expands significantly, particularly in the automotive manufacturing sector.
2000sAdvancements in sensors, vision systems, and collaborative robots (cobots) emerge.
2010sRobotics becomes more accessible, integrating into diverse fields like healthcare and logistics.
2020sIncreased focus on AI-driven robotics, machine learning, and human-robot interaction.
