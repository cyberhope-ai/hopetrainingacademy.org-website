What is this field?

Adobe Photoshop Elements for the Digital Photographer II is an advanced training program designed to elevate your image editing capabilities. Building on foundational skills, this course dives deep into sophisticated techniques for enhancing, restoring, and creatively manipulating digital images. You'll learn to harness powerful tools to achieve professional-grade results, making your photographs truly stand out.

Think of Photoshop Elements as your comprehensive digital darkroom and creative studio rolled into one. This advanced course teaches you how to move beyond basic adjustments, enabling you to tackle complex tasks like intricate photo restoration, seamless image merging, and precise color correction. It's the essential toolkit for anyone serious about mastering their digital photography workflow and artistic expression.

How the field grew

2001Adobe Photoshop Elements first released, bringing powerful image editing to a broader audience.
2004Significant updates introduced RAW file support and advanced healing brush tools.
2007The Guided Edits feature debuted, simplifying complex tasks for users with step-by-step instructions.
2010Content-Aware Fill technology brought revolutionary object removal capabilities to Elements.
2023Latest versions integrate AI-powered features for enhanced automation and creative possibilities.

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in the Photoshop Elements for the Digital Photographer II program. This course focuses on practical skills and hands-on application.

No, this course does not include an exam voucher. Photoshop Elements is a software skill, and there is no specific certification exam associated with it.

This program includes 24 hours of instruction, designed to be completed at your own pace online. Most students finish within six weeks.

This course is designed to teach advanced skills in Adobe Photoshop Elements, not to prepare for a specific certification exam. It focuses on practical application and mastery of the software.
