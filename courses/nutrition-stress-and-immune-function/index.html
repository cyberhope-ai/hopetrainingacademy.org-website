What is this field?

This program explores the profound connection between what you eat, how you feel, and your body's ability to fight off illness. You'll delve into the science behind the stress response, its impact on your nutritional needs, and practical strategies for building a robust immune system through diet and mindful practices.

Think of your body as a high-performance vehicle. Just as the right fuel and regular maintenance keep a car running smoothly, proper nutrition and stress management are crucial for your body's optimal function and resilience. This training provides the owner's manual for maintaining your internal systems.

How the field grew

1970sEarly research highlights the physiological effects of chronic stress on the body.
1990sNutritional immunology emerges, linking specific nutrients to immune system function.
2000sMind-body connection gains scientific traction, emphasizing mindful eating and stress reduction.
2010sHolistic wellness becomes a mainstream focus for preventative health and resilience.
2020sGlobal events underscore the critical importance of immune health and stress coping mechanisms.
