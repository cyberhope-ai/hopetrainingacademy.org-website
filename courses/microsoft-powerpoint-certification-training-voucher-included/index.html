How the field grew

1987PowerPoint 1.0 is released, revolutionizing visual communication.
1990Microsoft acquires PowerPoint, integrating it into the Microsoft Office suite.
1997The Microsoft Office User Specialist (MOUS) program launches, offering formal certification for Office applications.
Early 2000sMOUS evolves into the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) program, becoming the global standard for validating Office proficiency.
PresentMOS certifications continue to adapt with new software versions, ensuring professionals demonstrate up-to-date skills in the latest Microsoft Office applications.

Inside the course, module by module

01
PowerPoint Fundamentals

Navigating the interface, creating basic presentations, and utilizing core tools like the Ribbon and Backstage View.

02
Content & Formatting Essentials

Adding and formatting text, incorporating images, and working with tables and charts to enhance clarity.

03
Presentation Management & Delivery

Organizing slides with the Outline and Slide Sorter, preparing for print, and confidently running slideshows.

Advanced Customization & Design

Applying themes, utilizing Presentation Masters, and integrating special effects and SmartArt for visual impact.

05
Multimedia & Collaborative Features

Working with graphic elements, embedding multimedia, merging presentations, and reusing slides efficiently.

06
Finalizing & Security

Setting up slideshows, managing multiple presentations, ensuring compatibility, and protecting your work with security features.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no formal educational prerequisites for this program. While familiarity with using a personal computer and Microsoft Office is helpful, a college degree is not required to enroll or to earn your Microsoft PowerPoint certification.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, this program includes a voucher for the Microsoft Office Specialist PowerPoint Certification exam. This voucher covers the cost of sitting for your certifying exam once you are eligible.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

This is a self-paced program designed for 60 course hours of instruction. You can start anytime and progress at your own speed, allowing you to fit the training into your schedule.

Which Microsoft PowerPoint certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Microsoft Office Specialist PowerPoint Certification exam. It covers the skills and knowledge required to demonstrate proficiency in the latest versions of PowerPoint.

What kind of jobs can I get with a Microsoft PowerPoint certification?

A Microsoft PowerPoint certification enhances your employability across a wide range of roles that require strong presentation and communication skills. This includes positions such as Management Consultant, Marketing Specialist, Business Analyst, Project Coordinator, Administrative Assistant, and many others where creating compelling visual content is key.
