What is this field?

A Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) is a highly skilled professional who optimizes and innovates production processes, ensuring efficiency, quality, and cost-effectiveness in manufacturing operations. They are the architects of how products are made, from initial design concepts to final assembly, integrating cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to streamline workflows and enhance productivity.

Think of a manufacturing engineer as the conductor of an orchestra, where each instrument is a machine or process. They don't just play one instrument; they understand how every part works together to create a harmonious and efficient production line. They design the score, manage the musicians, and ensure the performance (production) is flawless, constantly seeking ways to improve the symphony's sound and speed.

How the field grew

Late 19th CenturyEmergence of Scientific Management and Industrial Engineering principles, focusing on efficiency and process optimization in factories.
Mid-20th CenturyPost-WWII boom in manufacturing, leading to advancements in automation, quality control (e.g., Statistical Process Control), and lean production concepts.
Late 20th CenturyRise of Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM), revolutionizing product development and production.
Early 21st CenturyIntroduction of Industry 4.0, integrating digital tools, robotics, IoT, and smart manufacturing for highly interconnected and adaptive production systems.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

While a degree can contribute to the experience requirements for CMfgE certification, it is not strictly mandatory for program enrollment. The CMfgE credential requires a minimum of 4 years of related work experience and 8 years of total experience and education, allowing various paths to eligibility.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CMfgE certification?

This career training program does not include an exam voucher. You will need to purchase the exam voucher separately when you are ready to sit for the CMfgE certification exam.

How long will it take to complete this career training program?

This self-paced program consists of 160 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and prior experience.

Which CMfgE exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) certification exam offered by SME, ensuring your knowledge aligns with the latest industry standards.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and certification, you'll be prepared for roles such as Manufacturing Engineer, Process Engineer, Production Engineer, or Quality Engineer, contributing to the optimization and innovation of manufacturing processes across various industries.
