What is this field?

A Legal Nurse Consultant (LNC) is a specialized registered nurse who applies their extensive medical knowledge and clinical experience to the legal process. They serve as a crucial link between the medical and legal fields, helping attorneys understand complex medical issues, identify key facts, and develop compelling cases. LNCs are indispensable in various legal matters, including personal injury, medical malpractice, workers' compensation, and criminal cases.

Think of an LNC as a medical detective for the legal world. Just as a detective uncovers clues to solve a mystery, an LNC meticulously reviews medical records, identifies deviations from standards of care, and translates intricate medical jargon into understandable terms for legal teams. They provide the expert medical foundation necessary for attorneys to build strong arguments and achieve justice for their clients.

How the field grew

1970sNurses begin informally assisting attorneys with medical cases, laying the groundwork for the LNC role.
1989The American Association of Legal Nurse Consultants (AALNC) is founded, formalizing the profession and setting standards.
1990sLegal Nurse Consulting gains widespread recognition as a distinct and valuable nursing specialty.
2000sProfessional certification for Legal Nurse Consultants (e.g., LNCC) becomes available, further validating expertise.
