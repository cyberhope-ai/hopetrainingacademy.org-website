What is this field?

Business analysis is the critical practice of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, skilled business analysts are indispensable for bridging the gap between business objectives and technical implementation. Think of a business analyst as an architect for a company's future, designing the blueprints for projects that drive growth and efficiency.

This comprehensive program prepares you for two highly respected credentials: the Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP®) and the Agile Analysis Certification (IIBA®-AAC). Earning these certifications from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA®) validates your expertise, enhances your professional credibility, and positions you for significant career advancement and higher earning potential in a field increasingly reliant on Agile practices.

How the field grew

1990sBusiness analysis emerges as a distinct professional discipline.
2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) is founded.
2006IIBA publishes the first Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide).
2013IIBA introduces the Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP®) certification.
Late 2010sGrowing emphasis on Agile methodologies leads to specialized Agile analysis certifications.
