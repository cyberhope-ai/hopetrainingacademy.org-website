What is this field?

A freight broker acts as an intermediary, connecting businesses (shippers) that need goods transported with qualified carriers (trucking companies, rail, air, or sea cargo). They don't own the trucks or cargo themselves but manage the logistics, negotiation, and paperwork to ensure efficient and cost-effective movement of freight. Freight agents often work under a broker, focusing on specific tasks like finding loads or carriers.

Think of a freight broker like a travel agent for goods. Just as a travel agent finds the best flights and hotels for a vacationer, a freight broker finds the most suitable transportation solutions for a company's products, handling all the complex arrangements to get items from point A to point B smoothly and on time.
