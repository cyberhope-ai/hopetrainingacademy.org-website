What is this field?

Instructional design is the systematic process of creating effective and engaging learning experiences. It involves analyzing learning needs, designing course content, developing materials, and evaluating their impact to ensure learners acquire new knowledge and skills efficiently.

Think of an instructional designer as an architect for learning. Just as an architect plans every detail of a building to ensure it's functional, safe, and aesthetically pleasing, an instructional designer meticulously plans a learning journey, ensuring it's logical, engaging, and achieves specific educational goals.

How the field grew

1940sSystematic training methods emerge from WWII military and industrial needs.
1960sRobert Gagné's 'Conditions of Learning' and behavioral objectives shape educational design.
1970sThe ADDIE model (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation) becomes a foundational framework.
1990sThe rise of personal computers and the internet sparks the growth of e-learning.
