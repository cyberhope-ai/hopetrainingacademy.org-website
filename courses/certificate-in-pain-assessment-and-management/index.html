What is this field?

Pain assessment and management involves understanding, evaluating, and treating various types of pain in patients. It's a critical skill for healthcare professionals, enabling them to improve patient quality of life, reduce suffering, and contribute to more effective recovery processes across diverse medical settings.

Think of it like a detective solving a mystery. Just as a detective gathers clues, interviews witnesses, and analyzes evidence to understand a crime, a healthcare professional uses specific tools and techniques to identify the source, intensity, and impact of a patient's pain, then develops a targeted plan to alleviate it.

How the field grew

1960sModern pain clinics emerge, emphasizing multidisciplinary approaches.
1990sSignificant advancements in pharmacological and interventional pain therapies.
2000sIncreased focus on patient-centered care and quality of life in pain management.
2010sGreater integration of psychological and physical therapies for chronic pain.
