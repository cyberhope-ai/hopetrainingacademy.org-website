What is this field?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of increasing the quantity and quality of traffic to your website through organic search engine results. In today's digital landscape, a strong online presence is crucial for any business or individual looking to connect with their audience, and SEO is the primary driver of that visibility.

This program provides a practical, step-by-step roadmap to understanding and implementing effective SEO strategies. Think of it like learning to navigate a complex city: we'll provide you with the best maps, traffic rules, and shortcuts to ensure your website reaches its destination – the top of search engine results – efficiently and effectively.

How the field grew

2000Google's PageRank algorithm gains dominance, shifting focus to link building and content relevance.
2005The rise of Web 2.0 and user-generated content makes content quality and user experience increasingly crucial for rankings.
2010Google's Panda and Penguin updates penalize low-quality content and spammy links, emphasizing ethical, white-hat SEO practices.
2015Mobile-first indexing and the growth of voice search emerge, broadening the scope of SEO to include diverse user behaviors.
2020Artificial intelligence and machine learning profoundly influence search algorithms, demanding sophisticated content strategies and a focus on user intent.
