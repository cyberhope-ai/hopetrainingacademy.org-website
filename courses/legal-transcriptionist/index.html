What is this field?

A Legal Transcriptionist is a highly skilled professional responsible for accurately converting spoken words from legal proceedings, depositions, and other judicial events into written text. This critical role ensures that every word is meticulously documented, punctuated, and formatted according to strict legal standards, creating the official verbatim record essential for court cases, appeals, and legal research.

Imagine being the silent, precise architect of legal history. Just as a court reporter captures every utterance in real-time, a legal transcriptionist takes recorded audio and transforms it into an unassailable written document. This requires not only exceptional listening skills but also a deep understanding of legal terminology, grammar, and ethical considerations, making you an indispensable part of the justice system's administrative backbone.

How the field grew

Late 19th CenturyEmergence of stenography and early court reporting, laying the foundation for verbatim legal record-keeping.
Mid-20th CenturyIntroduction of electronic recording devices in legal settings, creating a new demand for professionals to transcribe audio into written transcripts.
1978Founding of the American Association of Electronic Reporters and Transcribers (AAERT), establishing professional standards and certification for the field.
Early 21st CenturyAdvancements in digital audio technology and internet connectivity enable remote work, expanding opportunities for legal transcriptionists to work from anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a Legal Transcriptionist?

No, a college degree is not required. This program is designed for individuals with a high school diploma or equivalent who possess basic computer skills and a strong command of the English language.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the certification?

An exam voucher is not included with this program. The AAERT Certified Electronic Transcriber (CET) exam has a separate registration fee, which you will pay directly to AAERT when you are ready to test.

How long does it take to complete the Legal Transcriptionist training?

This is a self-paced program with 100 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program thoroughly prepares you to sit for and pass the current AAERT Certified Electronic Transcriber (CET) certification exam, which is widely recognized and respected in the legal field.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Legal Transcriptionist, Court Transcriptionist, Deposition Transcriber, or a Freelance Legal Transcriber, often with opportunities for remote work.
