What is this field?

An Engineering Technician is a vital problem-solver and team player in the manufacturing and engineering sectors. They bridge the gap between theoretical engineering concepts and practical application, assisting engineers and technologists in critical projects spanning research, development, implementation, and operation. This role demands a practical understanding of various engineering principles to ensure efficient and high-quality production.

Think of an Engineering Technician as the skilled craftsperson who brings an architect's blueprint to life, but for complex industrial processes. They translate designs into tangible products, troubleshoot machinery, ensure quality control, and implement advanced manufacturing techniques, making them indispensable to any modern production environment.

How the field grew

1950sPost-WWII industrial expansion creates demand for specialized technical support in manufacturing.
1980sEmergence of Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and Manufacturing (CAM) revolutionizes production, requiring new technical proficiencies.
2000sGlobalized manufacturing and stringent quality standards (Six Sigma, Lean) increase the complexity of production processes.
2010sWidespread adoption of advanced manufacturing techniques like additive manufacturing (3D printing) and sophisticated automation.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Manufacturing Fundamentals

Safety, inspection methods, quality systems (Six Sigma, Lean, 5S), metallurgy, and materials science.

02
Machining and Grinding Processes

Basic grinding theory, surface and cylindrical grinder operation, grinding safety, and material considerations.

03
CNC Programming and Operations

Introduction to CNC lathes and mills, G-code programming, CAD/CAM basics, and control panel functions for Fanuc, Haas, and Mazak systems.

04
Additive Manufacturing

Introduction to 3D printing, safety, basic processes, methods, materials science, and integration with traditional manufacturing.

05
Electrical Systems and Automation

Electrical units, circuit fundamentals, DC/AC power sources, NEC® overview, electrical print reading, and motor controls.

06
Composites and Adhesives

Safety for composite processing, overview of composite types, advanced materials, molding techniques, inspection, and adhesive bonding principles.

07
Product Design and Management

Fabrication and assembly processes, product/process/production design, and management principles.

This program focuses on building comprehensive skills for the Engineering Technician role. It does not include a specific certification exam voucher, but the curriculum covers many topics relevant to industry-recognized credentials.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Engineering Technician, Manufacturing Technician, Tool and Die Maker, CNC Machinist, and other advanced manufacturing positions requiring hands-on technical expertise.
