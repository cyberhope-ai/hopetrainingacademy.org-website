What is this field?

Buying and selling on eBay is like running your own digital storefront in a bustling global marketplace. Instead of a physical shop, you list items online, connecting with millions of potential buyers worldwide. This program teaches you the strategies and tools to effectively navigate this dynamic environment, whether you're clearing out clutter, finding hidden gems to resell, or building a dedicated online business.

Think of it as learning to be a savvy treasure hunter and a skilled merchant all in one. You'll discover how to identify valuable items, craft compelling listings, manage transactions, and ensure customer satisfaction, turning your home into a hub for commerce. This course equips you with the practical knowledge to generate income from your computer, transforming your entrepreneurial aspirations into tangible results.

How the field grew

1995eBay (originally AuctionWeb) is founded by Pierre Omidyar, pioneering online auctions.
1997The company officially renames itself eBay Inc., reflecting its growing scope.
2000seBay expands globally, introducing 'Buy It Now' features and becoming a dominant force in online retail.
TodayeBay continues to evolve as a major e-commerce platform, supporting millions of sellers worldwide.

What the career pays

An eBay seller manages an online storefront, sourcing products, creating listings, fulfilling orders, and providing customer service. They leverage the platform to generate income, either as a side hustle or a full-time entrepreneurial venture.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become an eBay seller?

No, formal degrees are not required to succeed as an eBay seller. This program focuses on practical skills and strategies for entrepreneurial success.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, this course does not include an exam voucher as it leads to practical skills for online selling rather than a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-guided online program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. Completion time will vary based on your schedule and dedication.

Is there a certification exam for eBay selling?

There is no single, industry-standard certification exam specifically for general eBay selling. This course focuses on practical skills for success on the platform.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

This course primarily prepares you for entrepreneurial roles as an online seller or to generate supplemental income. The skills learned are also valuable for e-commerce assistant or general retail roles.
