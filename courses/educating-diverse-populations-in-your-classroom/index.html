What is this field?

"Educating Diverse Populations in Your Classroom" focuses on equipping educators with the essential skills to support every student, regardless of their background or learning needs. This includes understanding the unique challenges faced by educationally disadvantaged students and those identified as at-risk, ensuring all learners have an equitable opportunity to succeed.

Think of your classroom as a garden, and each student as a unique plant. Some thrive in direct sunlight, others prefer shade, and some need specific soil or more water. This program teaches you how to identify each plant's needs and provide the right environment and care, so every student can blossom to their full potential.

How the field grew

1975Education for All Handicapped Children Act (EAHCA) established the right to free, appropriate public education for children with disabilities.
1990EAHCA was reauthorized and renamed the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), emphasizing individualized education programs.
2001No Child Left Behind Act (NCLB) mandated accountability for all student subgroups, including those from diverse backgrounds.
2015The Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA) replaced NCLB, continuing the focus on equity and support for diverse learners.
