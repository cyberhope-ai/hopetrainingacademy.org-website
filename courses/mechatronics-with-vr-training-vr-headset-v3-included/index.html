What is this field?

Mechatronics is a dynamic, interdisciplinary field that combines mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science, and control systems to design, build, and maintain intelligent machines and automated systems. This program provides comprehensive training in these essential areas, enhanced by cutting-edge virtual reality (VR) simulations that offer a hands-on learning experience without the need for physical equipment.

Think of mechatronics as the brain and body of modern technology. Just as a self-driving car integrates mechanical parts, electrical sensors, and computer algorithms to navigate, mechatronics professionals bring together diverse disciplines to create and manage complex automated systems, from robotic assembly lines to smart home devices.

How the field grew

1960sThe term 'mechatronics' was coined in Japan, marking the initial convergence of mechanical and electronic engineering disciplines.
1980sRapid expansion of industrial robotics and automation drove significant demand for integrated system expertise in manufacturing.
2000sThe emergence of smart manufacturing and Industry 4.0 emphasized interconnected, intelligent systems, further blending physical and digital domains.
2010sGrowth of advanced sensor technology, AI, and the Internet of Things (IoT) led to more sophisticated mechatronic applications.
2020sIntegration of virtual and augmented reality technologies began to revolutionize training, design, and maintenance practices within mechatronics.

Essential math, workplace safety, basic measurement techniques, and quality standards like Six Sigma and Lean.

Circuits, magnetism, AC/DC fundamentals, motor controls, and an introduction to PLC basics.

03

Forces, power transmission components, lubrication, bearings, and gear applications.

04
Fluid Power & Automation

Hydraulics, pneumatics, fluid system maintenance, and actuator applications.

05
Robotics & Advanced Control

Robot components, programming concepts, maintenance, and vision systems for automation.

06
Inspection & Troubleshooting

Blueprint reading, Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T), and systematic approaches to problem-solving.

OSHA standards, Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), lockout/tagout procedures, ISO 9001, and continuous improvement methodologies.
