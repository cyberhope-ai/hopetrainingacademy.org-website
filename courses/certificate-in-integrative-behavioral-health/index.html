What is this field?

Integrative Behavioral Health is a holistic approach to mental well-being, focusing on the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit. It combines conventional mental health practices with complementary therapies to provide comprehensive support, addressing not just symptoms but also underlying lifestyle, environmental, and social factors that influence an individual's health.

This program equips you with the knowledge to understand and apply these complementary methods of care, enabling you to effectively support individuals facing a variety of mental health conditions. Think of it like learning to be a master gardener who not only addresses plant diseases but also nurtures the soil, sunlight, and water conditions for overall plant vitality.
