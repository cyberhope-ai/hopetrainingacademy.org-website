What is this field?

Real estate law governs the rights and interests related to land, buildings, and other immovable property. It encompasses a vast array of topics, from buying and selling property to landlord-tenant relations, property development, and environmental regulations. Understanding these legal principles is crucial for anyone involved in the property market, whether as an investor, agent, or legal support professional.

This course demystifies the complexities of real estate law, providing you with a solid understanding of its core components. Think of it like learning the rules of the road before driving a car: you'll grasp the essential legal framework that ensures smooth and lawful property dealings, helping you avoid common pitfalls and make informed decisions in the real estate world.

How the field grew

1600sEnglish common law principles of property rights adopted in American colonies.
1787U.S. Constitution's Fifth Amendment establishes the 'takings clause' for private property.
1862Homestead Act encourages land ownership and westward expansion across the United States.
1968Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in housing sales, rentals, and financing.
2010Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act introduces significant mortgage and real estate finance regulations.
