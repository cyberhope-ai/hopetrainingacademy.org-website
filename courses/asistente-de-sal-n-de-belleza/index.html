What is this field?

A Professional Hair Stylist and Cosmetologist is a creative artist who transforms hair into stunning looks, offering services from cutting and coloring to styling and treatments. Beyond the technical skills, today's beauty professionals also master client communication, digital presence, and business acumen to thrive in a dynamic industry.

This program equips you with the comprehensive skills needed to excel in the beauty sector. Think of it like a master workshop where you learn not just how to paint a beautiful picture, but also how to choose the right brushes, mix custom colors, and even market your art to a wide audience. You'll gain practical expertise in hair care, cutting, styling, and coloring, alongside essential digital and marketing strategies to build your personal brand and attract clients.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyThe rise of professional hairstyling, driven by cinematic glamour and evolving fashion trends.
Mid-20th CenturyInnovation in hair care products and styling tools, making diverse looks accessible to a broader public.
Late 20th CenturyThe salon experience evolves into a holistic beauty and wellness destination, emphasizing client relationships.
2000sDigital platforms and social media revolutionize how stylists showcase work and connect with clients globally.
Present DayHope Training Academy launches hands-on programs to meet the demand for skilled, digitally-savvy beauty professionals.

What the career pays

Professional Hair Stylists and Cosmetologists provide a wide range of hair care services, including cutting, coloring, styling, and treatments, to enhance clients' appearances. They also advise on hair health, manage client relationships, and often leverage digital tools for marketing and business growth.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. This program is designed for individuals eager to gain practical, hands-on skills to launch a career in professional hair styling and cosmetology.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher is not included as this program focuses on developing practical skills and a portfolio rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

How long will it take to complete the Professional Hair Styling & Cosmetology program?

This self-paced program consists of 224 course hours. The actual completion time will depend on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program focuses on building practical skills in hair styling, coloring, and business acumen. While it provides a strong foundation, it does not directly prepare you for a specific external certification exam; state licensing requirements vary and are separate.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Hair Stylist, Cosmetologist, Salon Assistant, or even to launch your own independent beauty professional business.
