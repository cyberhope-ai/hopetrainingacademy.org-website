What is this field?

Divergent thinking is a cognitive process used to generate creative ideas by exploring many possible solutions. Unlike convergent thinking, which focuses on finding a single, correct answer, divergent thinking encourages broad exploration, brainstorming, and the production of multiple, unique ideas. It's a cornerstone of innovation and problem-solving, essential for students to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Think of it like brainstorming: instead of searching for the single 'right' answer, you're generating as many different ideas as possible, like casting a wide net before picking the best fish. This course empowers educators to cultivate an environment where students feel confident exploring new ideas, making connections, and developing original solutions across all subjects.
