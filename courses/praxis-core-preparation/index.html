What is this field?

The Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators (Praxis Core) exam is a fundamental step for individuals aspiring to become certified teachers in many states. It assesses your foundational academic skills in reading, writing, and mathematics, ensuring you possess the necessary abilities to succeed in a teacher preparation program and ultimately, in the classroom.

Think of the Praxis Core as the academic foundation upon which your teaching career is built. Just as a strong building needs a solid blueprint and basic construction skills, a successful teacher needs a firm grasp of core academic concepts to effectively educate students across various subjects and grade levels.

How the field grew

Early 1990sThe Praxis Series of exams, developed by ETS, is introduced to assess the knowledge and skills of prospective educators.
2014Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators (CASE) replaces the Praxis I (PPST) exam, updating the assessment to reflect current educational standards.
OngoingThe Praxis Core exam continues to be a widely adopted standard for teacher licensure in numerous states across the U.S.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to take this course?

While this course prepares you for the Praxis Core exam, most states require a bachelor's degree to become a certified teacher. This exam is typically a prerequisite for entry into a teacher preparation program or for state licensure.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

An exam voucher for the Praxis Core test is not included with this course purchase. You will need to register and pay for the exam separately through the official testing service.

How long does it take to complete this Praxis Core preparation program?

The course is self-paced and 100% online, allowing you to study at your convenience. The time required varies based on your existing knowledge and study habits, but consistent engagement will help you prepare efficiently.

Which Praxis Core exam version does this course prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators exam, covering the essential reading, writing, and mathematics skills required for teacher certification.

What kind of job can I get after passing the Praxis Core exam?

Passing the Praxis Core exam is a crucial step towards becoming a certified teacher. It qualifies you to pursue various teaching roles, such as an elementary, middle, or high school teacher, depending on your further education and specialization.
