What is this field?

The Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) credential is a globally recognized certification for professionals who audit, monitor, control, and assess an organization's information technology and business systems. It demonstrates your expertise in applying a risk-based approach to planning, executing, and reporting on audit engagements, ensuring the integrity and security of information assets.

Think of a CISA professional as a highly skilled detective for an organization's digital infrastructure. They meticulously examine IT systems, processes, and controls to identify vulnerabilities, ensure compliance with regulations, and verify that information assets are protected, much like a financial auditor scrutinizes financial records to prevent fraud and ensure accuracy.

How the field grew

1978ISACA (Information Systems Audit and Control Association) is founded, recognizing the growing need for IT governance and audit.
1990The Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) certification is established by ISACA, becoming the first certification for IT audit professionals.
Early 2000sCISA gains significant global recognition as IT systems become central to business operations and regulatory compliance.
PresentCISA remains a premier certification, continuously updated to reflect the latest in IT audit, governance, and security practices.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for CISA certification?

While a degree is not strictly required to take the CISA exam, ISACA mandates five years of professional work experience in information systems auditing, control, or security for certification.
