What is this field?

The Health and Well-Being Bundle provides health professionals with a comprehensive understanding of Complementary, Holistic, and Integrative Health. This field explores diverse therapies and approaches that consider the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—to promote optimal well-being, moving beyond conventional medicine to address underlying causes and enhance overall health.

Think of it like a master chef learning about global cuisines. While they might specialize in one type of cooking, understanding a wide array of ingredients, techniques, and cultural approaches allows them to create more innovative, balanced, and satisfying dishes. Similarly, this training equips health professionals with a broader 'menu' of wellness strategies to better serve their diverse clients.

How the field grew

1970sGrowing interest in 'alternative medicine' as people sought more personalized and natural health solutions.
1990sEstablishment of the National Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine (NCCAM) by the NIH, signaling increasing scientific inquiry.
2000sShift towards 'integrative health,' emphasizing evidence-based complementary therapies alongside conventional care for a holistic approach.
TodayWidespread recognition of the value of mind-body practices, nutrition, and lifestyle interventions in comprehensive patient care.
