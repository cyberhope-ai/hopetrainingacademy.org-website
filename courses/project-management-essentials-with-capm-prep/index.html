What is this field?

Project management is the art and science of planning, executing, and closing projects to achieve specific goals within a defined timeframe and budget. This training program provides a robust foundation in the core principles of project management, covering essential concepts from initiation to completion, and specifically prepares you for the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® certification exam.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. Each musician (team member) has their part, but the conductor ensures everyone plays in harmony, on time, and delivers a beautiful symphony (successful project) to the audience (stakeholders). This program equips you with the baton and the score to lead your own projects to a standing ovation.

How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded, establishing a global standard for the profession.
1984PMI launched its first certification, the Project Management Professional (PMP)®, setting a benchmark for project management expertise.
2003The Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® certification was introduced by PMI, offering an entry-level credential for aspiring project managers.
PresentThe CAPM® certification continues to evolve, aligning with the latest project management standards and agile methodologies to meet industry demands.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program or take the CAPM® exam?

You do not need a degree to enroll in this training program. However, to be eligible for the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® exam, you must possess a secondary degree (high school diploma, associate degree, or global equivalent).

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

No, an exam voucher for the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® certification exam is not included with this training program.

How long does it take to complete the Project Management Essentials program?

This program consists of 100 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on a schedule that fits your life and learning style.

Which CAPM® exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® certification exam offered by the Project Management Institute (PMI)®, ensuring you're ready for the latest industry standards.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for entry-level project management roles such as Project Coordinator, Associate Project Manager, or Project Management Specialist, and can also enhance your skills for roles like Business Analyst.
