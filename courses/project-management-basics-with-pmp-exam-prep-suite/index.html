What is this field?

Project management is the art and science of guiding a project from its beginning to its successful completion. It involves applying specific knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet the project requirements. From launching a new product to constructing a building, project managers are the orchestrators who ensure everything runs smoothly, on time, and within budget.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. Just as a conductor brings together various musicians and instruments to create a harmonious performance, a project manager coordinates diverse teams, resources, and tasks to achieve a specific goal. They define the melody (scope), set the tempo (schedule), manage the instruments (resources), and ensure the final piece is a masterpiece.

How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded to advance the profession.
1984First Project Management Professional (PMP) certification exam administered.
1996First edition of the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) published.
2021PMP exam content updated to reflect agile and hybrid project management approaches.
