What is this field?

This online course equips educators, parents, and support staff with essential strategies to design and implement highly predictable and structured learning environments for students with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). By understanding the unique needs of these learners, you will gain practical tools to foster independence, reduce anxiety, and promote positive behavior, creating a more effective and inclusive educational experience.

Think of it like building a clear, custom-designed roadmap for a journey; for students with autism, a structured classroom provides that essential, predictable map, making their educational path clear, less overwhelming, and significantly more navigable.

How the field grew

1975Education for All Handicapped Children Act (EHA) mandates free, appropriate public education for children with disabilities.
1990Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) replaces EHA, emphasizing individualized education programs (IEPs) and inclusion.
Early 2000sIncreased focus on evidence-based practices and structured teaching methodologies specifically for autism spectrum disorder (ASD).
PresentOngoing research and development of specialized strategies continue to refine support for students with autism in diverse educational settings.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Understanding Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

Explore the core characteristics of ASD, sensory sensitivities, communication styles, and their impact on learning.

02
Principles of Structured Teaching

Learn the foundational concepts of predictability, visual supports, clear expectations, and environmental organization.

03
Designing the Structured Classroom Environment

Master techniques for physical classroom layout, developing work systems, implementing visual schedules, and creating independent work stations.

04
Strategies for Behavior Support

Discover proactive interventions, positive reinforcement techniques, methods for managing anxiety, and communication-based strategies.

05
Individualized Support & Collaboration

Understand how to tailor strategies to individual needs, collect data effectively, and collaborate with families and support staff.
