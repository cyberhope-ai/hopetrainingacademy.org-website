What is this field?

Clinical Medical Assistants are the backbone of clinical operations, ensuring patient care runs smoothly from intake to follow-up. They perform vital administrative and clinical tasks, acting as a crucial link between patients and medical providers in various healthcare settings.

Imagine a busy doctor's office: the CCMA is the skilled professional who greets you, takes your vital signs, prepares you for your exam, and even assists with minor procedures. They handle everything from managing patient records to performing basic lab tests, making them indispensable to the healthcare team.

How the field grew

1960sMedical assisting emerges as a distinct support role in physician offices.
1989National Healthcareer Association (NHA) founded, establishing national certification standards.
Early 2000sIncreased demand for certified professionals as healthcare regulations and technology evolved.
PresentCertified Clinical Medical Assistants are recognized as vital, multi-skilled members of the modern healthcare team.

Manages both administrative and clinical tasks in a medical office setting.

Phlebotomist

Specializes in drawing blood for tests, a key skill for CCMAs.
