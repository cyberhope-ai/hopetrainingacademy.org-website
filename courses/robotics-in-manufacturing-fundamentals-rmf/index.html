What is this field?

The Robotics in Manufacturing Fundamentals (RMF) program is a comprehensive career training program designed to equip individuals with the foundational knowledge and skills necessary for a successful career in advanced manufacturing, specifically focusing on industrial robotics. This program covers essential topics from the basics of manufacturing and applied mathematics to the intricacies of robot systems, control, and safety, preparing you for a high-demand field where automation drives innovation.

Think of it like learning the alphabet and basic grammar before writing a novel. The RMF program provides the fundamental 'alphabet' and 'grammar' of robotics, giving you the essential building blocks to understand, operate, and eventually contribute to the design and maintenance of robotic systems in a manufacturing environment. It's the critical first step towards becoming a skilled professional in an automated world.

How the field grew

1932SME (Society of Manufacturing Engineers) is founded, establishing a legacy of advancing manufacturing knowledge and standards.
1989FIRST® (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is established, inspiring young people in science and technology, including robotics.
2009The Robotics Education & Competition (REC) Foundation is formed, expanding access to robotics education and competitive programs.
PresentThese leading organizations collaborate to develop the Robotics in Manufacturing Fundamentals (RMF) credential, setting industry benchmarks for entry-level robotics proficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals without a college degree. It focuses on providing practical, industry-recognized skills for entry-level roles in advanced manufacturing.

Is the RMF certification exam voucher included with the program?

The program price does not include the RMF certification exam voucher. You will need to purchase the exam voucher separately when you are ready to sit for the certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the Robotics in Manufacturing Fundamentals program?

This self-paced program consists of 30 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Which RMF certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the Robotics in Manufacturing Fundamentals (RMF) credential exam, developed by SME, the Robotics Education & Competition (REC) Foundation, and FIRST®.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for entry-level positions such as a Robotics Technician, Robot Operator, or an Automated Assembly Operator in the advanced manufacturing sector.
