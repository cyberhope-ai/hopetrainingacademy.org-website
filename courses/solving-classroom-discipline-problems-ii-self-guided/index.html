What is this field?

This professional development course equips educators with practical strategies to navigate and resolve serious classroom discipline challenges. You'll learn to identify root causes of disruptive behavior and implement effective interventions that promote a positive and productive learning environment for all students.

Consider it like learning to be a master gardener for your classroom. Just as a gardener understands different plant needs and how to address pests or poor soil, this training teaches you to understand diverse student behaviors and cultivate a thriving, well-managed educational space where every student can grow.

How the field grew

1950sBehaviorist approaches emphasized rewards and punishments for compliance in classrooms.
1970sShift towards humanistic methods, focusing on student self-esteem and intrinsic motivation in education.
1990sEmergence of Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports (PBIS) for systemic, proactive strategies in schools.
2000sIncreased focus on restorative justice and trauma-informed practices to address student behavior.
