What is this field?

An Administrative Professional is the cornerstone of any successful organization, managing critical office functions, streamlining communication, and ensuring smooth daily operations. This comprehensive career training program equips you with the advanced skills needed to excel in this vital role, preparing you for both the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) credential and the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certifications.

Think of an Administrative Professional as the conductor of an orchestra: they ensure every section plays in harmony, managing schedules, resources, and communications to produce a seamless performance. Our program transforms you into that skilled conductor, proficient in both strategic office management and the advanced technical tools of Microsoft Office.

How the field grew

Early 1900sThe rise of 'secretarial' roles, primarily focused on shorthand, typing, and basic correspondence, laying the groundwork for modern administrative support.
Mid-20th CenturyTechnological advancements like electric typewriters and early office machines begin to enhance productivity and expand administrative responsibilities.
Late 1980sThe advent of personal computers and early word processing software revolutionizes office work, demanding new digital literacy from administrative staff.
Early 2000sMicrosoft Office becomes the industry standard, making advanced proficiency in applications like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint crucial for administrative professionals.
Present DayAdministrative roles evolve into strategic partnerships, requiring expertise in project management, human resources, and advanced technology, validated by certifications like CAP and MOS Expert.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program or get certified?

No degree is required to enroll in our training program. However, to be eligible for the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam, you will need relevant work experience (e.g., 2 years with a Bachelor's degree, 3 years with an Associate's, or 4 years without a degree). There are no experience prerequisites for the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification exams.

Are exam vouchers included with this program?

Yes, this program includes vouchers that cover the fees for the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam and the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification exams upon eligibility. Please note that proctor fees, if applicable, are not included.

How long does it take to complete this career training program?

This program is designed for self-paced learning and consists of 435 course hours. The total time to complete will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Which version of the certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current/latest Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam offered by the International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) and the current/latest Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification exams.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion of this program and earning your certifications, you will be prepared for roles such as Administrative Assistant, Office Manager, Executive Assistant, Project Coordinator, and other essential administrative support positions across various industries.
