What is this field?

The CompTIA A+ certification is the industry standard for establishing a career in IT. Think of it as your foundational blueprint for understanding how computers, networks, and mobile devices work, from the inside out. It covers everything from assembling a PC and troubleshooting software glitches to securing systems and supporting end-users, making you a versatile problem-solver in the digital world.

This program is designed to transform you into a skilled computer support specialist, much like a master mechanic for digital systems. You'll learn to diagnose and fix issues, install and configure essential technology, and provide crucial technical assistance. It's the essential first step for anyone looking to enter the dynamic field of information technology, providing the core knowledge employers demand.

How the field grew

1990sCompTIA A+ certification introduced, quickly becoming an industry benchmark for PC technicians.
Early 2000sThe certification evolves to include broader operating system and hardware support, reflecting technological advancements.
Mid-2010sCurriculum expands to cover mobile devices, cloud concepts, and basic security, adapting to modern IT infrastructure.
PresentContinuously updated to ensure technicians are equipped with the latest skills for today's diverse IT environments.

What the career pays

Computer Support Specialists provide technical assistance to computer users, helping them with hardware, software, and network issues. They troubleshoot problems, install applications, and ensure systems run smoothly for individuals and organizations.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll or get a job?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our CompTIA A+ program or to pursue many entry-level IT support roles. This certification is widely recognized as a strong credential for starting your career.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program does not include an exam voucher for the CompTIA A+ certification exams. Exam fees are a separate cost.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is an intensive 5-day boot camp designed for rapid skill acquisition. While the core instruction is completed in five days, individual study time may vary.

Which CompTIA A+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current CompTIA A+ certification exam, ensuring your skills are up-to-date with the latest industry standards.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for entry-level roles such as Help Desk Technician, IT Support Specialist, Desktop Support Technician, or Computer User Support Specialist.
