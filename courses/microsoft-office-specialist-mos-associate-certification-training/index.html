What is this field?

The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Associate Certification Training program is designed to transform you from a basic user into a highly proficient expert across the core Microsoft Office applications: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Think of it like learning to be a master chef, not just someone who can follow a recipe. You'll gain a deep understanding of each tool's capabilities, allowing you to create, manage, and analyze information with efficiency and precision.

This program goes beyond simple feature tutorials, focusing on practical application and advanced techniques. You'll engage in hands-on exercises and in-depth course material to build the skills necessary to earn the prestigious MOS Associate certification, a widely recognized credential that validates your expertise to employers.

How the field grew

1983Microsoft Word's initial release, laying the foundation for modern word processing.
1985Microsoft Excel is introduced, revolutionizing spreadsheet management and data analysis.
1990The first Microsoft Office suite is released, bundling key applications together.
1997The Microsoft Office User Specialist (MOUS) program launches, later rebranded as MOS.
OngoingMicrosoft Office and MOS certifications continually evolve to reflect the latest software versions and industry demands.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this career training program. Our focus is on practical skill development and industry certification.

This program prepares you for the current Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Associate certification exams for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
