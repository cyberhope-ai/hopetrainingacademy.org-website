What is this field?

An Engineering Technician is a vital problem-solver and team player, bridging the gap between theoretical engineering concepts and practical application. They assist engineers in critical projects spanning research, development, implementation, and operation, ensuring precision and efficiency in advanced manufacturing environments.

Think of an Engineering Technician as the skilled craftsperson who brings an architect's blueprint to life. While the engineer designs the complex structure, the technician understands the materials, tools, and processes required to build it, troubleshoot issues, and ensure every component functions perfectly, often using cutting-edge technologies like virtual reality for training and execution.

How the field grew

1950sPost-war industrial expansion creates a significant demand for skilled technical support, formalizing the role of engineering technicians in manufacturing.
1980sThe digital revolution introduces Computer Numerical Control (CNC) and Computer-Aided Design/Manufacturing (CAD/CAM), fundamentally transforming precision machining and production.
2000sAdditive Manufacturing (3D printing) emerges, expanding production capabilities and requiring technicians with new expertise in digital fabrication.
2020sVirtual Reality (VR) technology begins to revolutionize technical training, offering immersive, hands-on learning experiences for complex machinery and processes.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Manufacturing Fundamentals

Math for manufacturing, inspection methods, safety protocols, materials science, quality systems (Six Sigma, Lean, TPM).

02
Precision Machining & Grinding

Grinding processes, surface/cylindrical/centerless grinding operations, grinding fluids, ferrous/nonferrous materials, grinding wheel geometry.

03
Additive Manufacturing

Introduction to 3D printing, safety, basic processes, materials, design for additive manufacturing, integration with traditional methods.

04
CNC Machining & Programming

Basics of CNC lathes and mills, coordinate systems, G-code programming, CAD/CAM introduction, control panel functions, offsets, program creation.

05
Advanced CNC Controls

In-depth operation and programming for Fanuc, Haas, and Mazak CNC systems, including control panel overview, offset entry, program execution, and storage.

06
Composites & Adhesives

Safety for composite processing, overview of composite types, advanced resins and materials, molding techniques, surface finishing, inspection, and repair methods; adhesive bonding basics.

07
Electrical Systems & Automation

Electrical units, safety for electrical work, introduction to circuits, automation principles, and motor controls.
