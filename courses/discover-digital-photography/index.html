What is this field?

Digital photography is the art and science of capturing images using electronic sensors instead of film. It encompasses everything from understanding your camera's settings to composing compelling shots and refining them through post-processing. This training introduces you to the fundamental techniques and creative principles needed to produce high-quality photographs, whether for personal enjoyment or professional pursuits.

Think of learning digital photography like learning to play a musical instrument; you start with the basics of chords and scales, then move to composing your own melodies. This program provides the foundational 'notes' and 'techniques' to help you create your unique visual 'symphonies'.

How the field grew

1975First digital camera prototype developed by Kodak engineer Steven Sasson.
1990First commercially available digital camera, the Dycam Model 1, hits the market.
2000sDigital Single-Lens Reflex (DSLR) cameras become widely accessible and popular among enthusiasts.
2010sSmartphone cameras revolutionize photography, making high-quality image capture ubiquitous.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this digital photography training?

No, a degree is not required. This program focuses on practical skills and hands-on learning to prepare you for real-world photography opportunities.

Does this course include an exam voucher for a certification?

This introductory course does not include an exam voucher, as there is no external certification exam directly associated with it.

How long does it take to complete the Discover Digital Photography training?

The course is 100% online and self-paced. While completion times vary, most students typically complete the material over several weeks, dedicating a few hours per week.

Is there a certification exam for this course?

There is no external certification exam associated with this introductory digital photography course. The focus is on building foundational skills and practical knowledge.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This training provides foundational skills for roles such as Photographer, Photojournalist, Commercial Photographer, or Event Photographer, helping you build a portfolio and pursue various visual career paths.
