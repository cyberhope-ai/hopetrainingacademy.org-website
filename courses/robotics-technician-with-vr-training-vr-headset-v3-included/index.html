What is this field?

A Robotics Technician is a highly skilled professional responsible for the installation, maintenance, programming, and repair of automated machinery and robotic systems. These technicians are crucial in modern manufacturing and industrial settings, ensuring that robots operate efficiently, safely, and accurately to boost production and minimize downtime. They diagnose complex issues, perform preventive maintenance, and integrate new robotic technologies into existing workflows, keeping the wheels of innovation turning.

Our comprehensive program equips you with the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to excel in this dynamic field. You'll delve into essential areas like math, safety protocols, quality control, electrical and mechanical systems, fluid power, and rigging. The program uniquely integrates virtual reality (VR) simulations, providing immersive, hands-on experience with a Meta Quest 3 VR Headset Kit included, allowing you to practice complex procedures in a safe, virtual environment – much like a pilot uses a flight simulator to master controls before taking to the skies.

How the field grew

1954George Devol patents the first programmable robot, 'Programmed Article Transfer'.
1961UNIMATE, the first industrial robot, is installed on a General Motors assembly line.
1980sRobotics expands beyond automotive into electronics, food processing, and other industries.
2000sEmergence of collaborative robots (cobots) and advanced sensor integration.
PresentGrowing integration of AI, machine learning, and VR/AR for robotics development and training.
