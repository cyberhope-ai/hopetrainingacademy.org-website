What is this field?

This online professional development course is designed to significantly increase your effectiveness as a science educator for children in fourth through sixth grades. You'll explore modern pedagogical approaches and practical strategies to make science engaging, understandable, and exciting for elementary and middle school students, fostering a lifelong love for discovery.

Think of it like a masterclass for a chef who already knows how to cook: you'll refine your techniques, learn new recipes for scientific inquiry, and discover innovative ways to present complex concepts. This program equips you with the tools to transform your science classroom into a dynamic lab where curiosity thrives and learning is an adventure.

How the field grew

1960sRise of inquiry-based science education, emphasizing student-led discovery.
1996Publication of the National Science Education Standards (NSES) by the National Research Council.
2013Introduction of the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS), focusing on practices, crosscutting concepts, and disciplinary core ideas.
