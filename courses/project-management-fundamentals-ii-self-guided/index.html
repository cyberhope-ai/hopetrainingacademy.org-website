What is this field?

Project management is the application of processes, methods, skills, knowledge, and experience to achieve specific project objectives according to the project acceptance criteria within agreed parameters. It involves planning, organizing, motivating, and controlling resources to achieve all the goals of a project.

Think of it like orchestrating a complex event, such as building a house or launching a new product. A project manager is the conductor, ensuring all the different musicians (teams, resources, tasks) play their part in harmony, on time, and within budget, to deliver a successful performance (the completed project).

How the field grew

1950sDevelopment of PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique) and CPM (Critical Path Method) for complex defense projects.
1969Founding of the Project Management Institute (PMI), establishing a professional body for the field.
1980sWidespread adoption of personal computers and specialized project management software, making PM accessible to more industries.
1990sEmergence of Agile methodologies, emphasizing iterative development and flexibility, particularly in software development.
2000s-PresentIncreased globalization, technological advancements, and complex business environments drive demand for skilled project managers across all sectors.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program focuses on practical, hands-on skills essential for project management roles. While a degree can be beneficial, it is not a prerequisite for enrollment or for starting a career in project management.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

No, this course is designed to build foundational project management skills and does not include an exam voucher for a specific certification. It provides the essential knowledge to pursue certifications independently.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn at your own speed. While specific hours are not defined, most learners can complete the foundational skills within a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on their dedication.

Does this program prepare me for a specific project management exam?

This program focuses on teaching the fundamental principles and essential skills of project management. While it provides a strong knowledge base, it is not specifically designed as a test preparation course for any particular certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue entry-level roles such as Project Coordinator, Junior Project Manager, Project Assistant, or Project Analyst. These foundational skills are valuable across various industries and serve as a stepping stone for career advancement in project management.
