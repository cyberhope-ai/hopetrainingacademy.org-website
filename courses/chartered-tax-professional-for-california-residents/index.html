What is this field?

A Chartered Tax Professional for California Residents is an expert in both federal and California state tax laws, qualified to prepare a wide range of tax returns for individuals and businesses. This career training program is designed to equip you with the knowledge needed to meet California Tax Education Council (CTEC) requirements and to prepare for the IRS Special Enrollment Examination (SEE), which leads to the prestigious Enrolled Agent (EA) credential.

Think of a tax professional as a financial navigator, guiding individuals and businesses through the complex waters of tax codes to ensure compliance, minimize liabilities, and optimize their financial outcomes. They are trusted advisors who help clients understand their tax obligations and plan for future financial health.

How the field grew

1977The Taxpayer Relief Act introduces significant changes, increasing the complexity of tax law.
1997California establishes the California Tax Education Council (CTEC) to regulate non-attorney/CPA tax preparers.
Early 2000sWidespread adoption of electronic filing (e-file) transforms the tax preparation landscape.
TodayThe IRS Enrolled Agent (EA) credential is a highly respected federal designation for tax professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Chartered Tax Professional?

No, a college degree is not required to become a CTEC-registered tax preparer or to pursue the Enrolled Agent credential.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CTEC or Enrolled Agent exam?

This training program provides comprehensive preparation for the CTEC registration requirements and the IRS Special Enrollment Examination (SEE) for Enrolled Agents, but an exam voucher is not included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This self-paced program includes 200 course hours, allowing you to complete it according to your schedule and commitment.

Which specific tax exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you to meet the California Tax Education Council (CTEC) requirements for tax preparers and provides thorough preparation for the IRS Special Enrollment Examination (SEE) to become an Enrolled Agent.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as individual tax preparer, small business tax specialist, or, upon passing the SEE, a federally licensed Enrolled Agent representing clients before the IRS.
