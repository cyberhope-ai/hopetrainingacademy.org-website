What is this field?

The Certificate in Medical Cannabis program offers a comprehensive understanding of cannabis, from its ancient history to its modern applications in healthcare, business, and policy. This program is designed for individuals seeking to navigate the complex landscape of medical cannabis, providing insights into its therapeutic potential, legal frameworks, and market dynamics. It's an essential foundation for anyone looking to specialize or advance their career in this rapidly evolving sector.

Think of this program like learning the full instruction manual for a complex, beneficial plant. You'll understand not just how it works, but also its historical journey, the rules governing its use, and how it fits into both wellness practices and the modern economy. This knowledge empowers you to speak confidently and accurately about medical cannabis in various professional settings.

How the field grew

Ancient CivilizationsCannabis used for millennia in traditional medicine and spiritual practices across various cultures.
Early 20th CenturyWidespread global prohibition and stigmatization of cannabis, limiting research and medical use.
Late 20th Century RevivalRenewed scientific interest and patient advocacy lead to initial medical cannabis legalization efforts.
Modern EraIncreasing legalization for medical use across many regions, fostering rapid industry growth and research.
