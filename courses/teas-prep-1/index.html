What is this field?

The Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized, multiple-choice exam for students applying to health science programs. It assesses your foundational academic knowledge in reading, mathematics, science, and English and language usage. Passing the TEAS is a critical step for aspiring healthcare professionals, demonstrating readiness for rigorous academic and clinical training.

Our TEAS Prep training program focuses specifically on the Reading and English and Language Usage sections, equipping you with targeted strategies and content review. Think of it like a specialized coaching session for a crucial academic hurdle: we help you understand the test format, identify your learning style, and build confidence to perform your best, paving the way for your entry into a fulfilling healthcare career.

How the field grew

2007Assessment Technologies Institute (ATI) releases TEAS V, an updated version of the exam.
2016The TEAS VI exam is introduced, refining content and question formats.
2022ATI launches the current TEAS 7, reflecting contemporary healthcare education needs.

An exam voucher is not included with this TEAS Prep training program. You will need to register and pay for the official TEAS exam separately through ATI.

Does this training program prepare me for the current TEAS exam?

Yes, this training program is designed to prepare you for the current version of the Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) exam, focusing on the Reading and English and Language Usage sections.
