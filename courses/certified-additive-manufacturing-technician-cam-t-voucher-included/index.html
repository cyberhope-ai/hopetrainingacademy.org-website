How the field grew

1980sFirst patents for stereolithography (SLA) and early commercial 3D printing systems.
1990sIntroduction of Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) and broader material applications.
2000sEmergence of open-source 3D printing and desktop 3D printers, expanding accessibility.
2010sSignificant industrial adoption, development of advanced materials, and increased investment.
2020sIntegration into mainstream manufacturing, focus on supply chain resilience, and hybrid processes.

What the career pays

An Additive Manufacturing Technician operates and maintains 3D printing equipment, ensuring the efficient production of high-quality components. They are responsible for process optimization, material selection, and integrating additive techniques into broader manufacturing operations.

$62,010median

Jobs it opens

Additive Manufacturing Technician

Operates and maintains 3D printing systems for production.

3D Printing Specialist

Focuses on design, material, and process optimization for 3D printed parts.

Manufacturing Production Technician

Applies additive manufacturing skills within broader production environments.

Rapid Prototyping Technician

Utilizes 3D printing for quick creation of product prototypes and models.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Additive Manufacturing

Introduction to AM, safety guidelines, and the basic 3D printing process.

02
Methods & Materials

Exploring various AM technologies, material science, and rapid prototyping techniques.

03
Design & Quality Assurance

Principles of design for AM, metrology, and nondestructive testing methods.

04
Integration & Advanced Concepts

Hybrid manufacturing, integrating AM with traditional processes, and reverse engineering.

05
Supply Chain & Specialized Applications

Managing the AM supply chain, lightweighting, qualification, and specific design considerations for FDM, Jetting, and other methods.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, there are no specific degree prerequisites for this program. Basic computer skills are recommended to ensure a smooth learning experience.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, your enrollment in this career training program includes a voucher to cover the fee for the Certified Additive Manufacturing - Technician (CAM-T) certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the training?

This program is self-paced and designed for completion in approximately 30 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Certified Additive Manufacturing - Technician (CAM-T) certification exam, validating your skills in this rapidly growing field.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Additive Manufacturing Technician, 3D Printing Specialist, Manufacturing Production Technician, or Rapid Prototyping Technician.
