What is this field?

This professional development program equips educators with the skills to design and implement a range of assessment methods specifically tailored for gifted students. You'll explore pre-assessment to gauge prior knowledge, formative assessment to monitor ongoing learning, and summative assessment to measure overall growth. The goal is to ensure that your teaching strategies are informed by precise data, allowing you to adjust the level of rigor and provide more effective learning experiences.

Think of it like a master chef creating a custom meal. Just as a chef considers individual dietary needs and preferences to craft a perfect dish, this program teaches you to 'custom-fit' assessments for your gifted learners. You'll learn to use various 'ingredients' (assessment types) to gather the right 'feedback' (data), ensuring each student receives the optimal 'nourishment' (instruction) for their unique intellectual appetite.

How the field grew

1920sEarly psychometric testing began to identify students with exceptional intellectual abilities, laying the groundwork for gifted education.
1972The Marland Report to Congress broadened the definition of giftedness beyond just IQ, emphasizing diverse talents and the need for tailored educational approaches.
2000sEducational focus shifted towards differentiated instruction and the use of ongoing, varied assessments to support continuous growth and challenge for gifted learners.
