What is this field?

This hands-on program is specifically crafted for individuals who have a foundational understanding of Excel and are eager to elevate their proficiency. You will move beyond basic data entry to master advanced tools like sophisticated sorting and filtering, create compelling visual reports, efficiently clean and combine diverse datasets, and collaborate securely on complex projects.

Think of it like upgrading from driving a car on local roads to navigating complex highways with advanced GPS and performance tuning. You're not just getting from point A to point B; you're optimizing the journey, understanding traffic patterns, and using all the vehicle's capabilities for efficiency and speed in your data management.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel 1.0 released for Macintosh, pioneering spreadsheet innovation.
1987Excel 2.0 launched for Windows, beginning its dominance in the spreadsheet market.
1993Excel 5.0 introduced Visual Basic for Applications (VBA), enabling powerful automation.
2007Excel 2007 revolutionized the user interface with the introduction of the Ribbon.
2020Microsoft 365 (formerly Office 365) became the primary delivery model, emphasizing cloud and collaboration features.
