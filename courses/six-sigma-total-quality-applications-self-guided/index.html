What is this field?

Six Sigma is a data-driven methodology focused on eliminating defects and improving processes within any organization. It employs a set of quality management methods, primarily empirical and statistical, to identify and remove the causes of errors, minimize variability in manufacturing and business processes, and achieve near-perfect results. This systematic approach ensures consistent quality and efficiency across all operations, leading to significant cost savings and enhanced customer satisfaction.

Think of Six Sigma like a master chef meticulously refining a recipe. They don't just guess; they measure every ingredient, test different cooking times, and analyze feedback to ensure every dish is consistently perfect, every single time. Six Sigma applies this same rigorous, data-backed approach to business processes, making sure every 'dish' (product or service) meets the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

How the field grew

1980sMotorola develops Six Sigma as a data-driven approach to reduce defects and improve quality.
1990sGeneral Electric, under Jack Welch, widely adopts Six Sigma, significantly boosting its global recognition and application.
2000sSix Sigma evolves and integrates with Lean principles, becoming a standard for process improvement across diverse industries worldwide.
