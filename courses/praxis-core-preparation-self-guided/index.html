What is this field?

The Praxis Core Exam assesses fundamental academic skills in reading, writing, and mathematics, serving as a critical prerequisite for individuals aspiring to become licensed educators in many U.S. states. It ensures that prospective teachers possess the foundational knowledge necessary to succeed in a classroom environment and effectively guide student learning.

Think of the Praxis Core as the academic fitness test for future teachers. Just as a pilot needs to pass a basic flight knowledge exam before advanced training, aspiring educators need to demonstrate proficiency in core subjects before they can lead a classroom and embark on their professional teaching journey.

How the field grew

1947Educational Testing Service (ETS), the developer of the Praxis series, is founded.
1993The Praxis Series of exams is introduced to standardize teacher licensure assessments.
2014The Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators exam replaces the former Praxis I, updating the assessment of foundational skills.

High School Teacher

Specializes in a subject area, preparing older students for higher education or careers (grades 9-12).

Substitute Teacher

Fills in for absent teachers across various grade levels and subject areas.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll in this program?

While this program does not require a degree, passing the Praxis Core Exam is often a prerequisite for entry into teacher licensure programs or certification pathways, which typically require a bachelor's degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher for the Praxis Core Exam is not included with this self-guided preparation program.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-guided online program, allowing you the flexibility to study at your own pace and complete the material according to your schedule and learning needs.

Which version of the Praxis Core exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators exam, covering the essential reading, writing, and mathematics sections.

What kind of job can I get after passing the Praxis Core Exam?

Passing the Praxis Core Exam is a foundational step towards various K-12 teaching roles, including elementary, middle, and high school teacher positions, as well as substitute teaching and other educational support roles, depending on your state's licensure requirements.
