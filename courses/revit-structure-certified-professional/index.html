What is this field?

This career training program immerses you in Autodesk Revit, the leading Building Information Modeling (BIM) software for structural design. You'll learn to create detailed 3D structural models, generate precise construction documents, and integrate designs with other disciplines, preparing you for a vital role in modern construction projects.

Think of Revit as the ultimate digital blueprint for buildings. Instead of just drawing lines, you're building a virtual structure with intelligent components, allowing engineers to visualize, analyze, and document every beam, column, and foundation. This program teaches you to master this powerful tool, making you an invaluable asset in any structural engineering firm.

How the field grew

1982Charles River Software (later Revit Technology Corporation) is founded, laying groundwork for parametric building modeling.
2000Revit 1.0 is released, introducing a revolutionary approach to building design with parametric change management.
2002Autodesk acquires Revit Technology Corporation, integrating Revit into its suite of design and construction software.
2000s-PresentBuilding Information Modeling (BIM) gains widespread adoption, with Revit becoming a cornerstone for collaborative project delivery in structural engineering.

01
Introduction to Revit & Basics

User interface, project setup, fundamental modeling elements, graphics, and sheets.

02
Intermediate Revit Design

Design options, working with families, tagging, scheduling components, and building areas.

Revit Structure I: Foundations & Framing

Modeling foundation walls, basement columns, beams, slabs, and roof framing.

Revit Structure II: Documentation & Details

Framing drawings, dimensioning, tagging elements, assembling detail sheets, and construction documents.

05

Interface, tools, exporting/importing models, animator, scripter, and clash detection.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites or specific degree requirements to enroll in the Revit Structural Design Professional training program. It is designed for individuals seeking to enter or advance in the structural engineering field.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher for the Autodesk Certified Professional (ACP) in Revit for Structural Design certification exam is not included with this program.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced program with 140 course hours. While you can work at your own speed, most students complete it within a few months, depending on their study schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Autodesk Certified Professional (ACP) in Revit for Structural Design certification exam, validating your expertise in structural BIM.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Structural Designer, BIM Modeler (Structural), Revit Technician, or Junior Structural Engineer, contributing to various construction and engineering projects.
