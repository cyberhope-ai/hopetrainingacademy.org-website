What is this field?

Criminal law is the body of rules that defines crimes, regulates the apprehension, charging, and trying of suspected persons, and sets punishments for convicted offenders. This program provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental principles, procedures, and key players within the criminal justice system, offering a crucial starting point for anyone considering a career in the legal field.

Think of the criminal justice system as a complex game with very strict rules. This course teaches you the rulebook, explaining who the players are (prosecutors, defense attorneys, paralegals), what their roles entail, and how the game is played from investigation to sentencing. It's your essential guide to navigating this intricate landscape.
