What is this field?

Starting a small business involves identifying a market need, developing a product or service to meet it, and then building and managing an organization to deliver it. This program equips aspiring entrepreneurs with the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to navigate the complexities of launching and growing a successful enterprise, from initial concept to sustainable operations.

Think of starting a small business like planting a garden. You begin with a seed of an idea, but success requires careful planning (choosing the right soil and location), consistent nurturing (marketing and customer service), and understanding the environment (market analysis and legal compliance). This course provides the essential tools and knowledge to cultivate your business idea into a flourishing venture.

How the field grew

1953The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) is established, providing vital resources and support for entrepreneurs.
1980sThe rise of personal computing and accessible software begins to democratize business management for small enterprises.
2000sE-commerce platforms emerge, significantly lowering barriers to entry for small businesses to reach global markets.
2020sDigital tools and online learning platforms expand access to comprehensive entrepreneurship education and mentorship.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this program or to successfully start your own business. Success hinges on your vision, dedication, and the practical skills you'll gain through this training.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses on practical business skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore an exam voucher is not included.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The online program is designed to be completed over six weeks, with approximately 24 hours of instruction, allowing you to learn at a flexible pace that fits your schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is a skill-building course for aspiring entrepreneurs and small business owners, and it does not prepare you for or lead to a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program prepares you to become a small business owner, entrepreneur, or startup founder, enabling you to create your own job and build your own enterprise across various industries.
