What is this field?

Supply chain management is the intricate process of overseeing the entire flow of goods and services, from raw materials to the final consumer. It involves coordinating and integrating all activities, from sourcing and procurement to production, logistics, and delivery, ensuring efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Think of it like conducting a symphony orchestra. Each section—sourcing, manufacturing, warehousing, transportation—must play in perfect harmony, guided by the conductor (the supply chain manager), to deliver a flawless performance (the product) to the audience (the customer). This coordination minimizes costs, reduces waste, and maximizes value.

How the field grew

1980sThe term 'supply chain management' gains prominence, emphasizing integration beyond traditional logistics.
1990sGlobalization and information technology drive increased complexity and the need for sophisticated SCM strategies.
2000sFocus shifts to lean principles, sustainability, and risk management within global supply chains.
2010sDigital transformation, big data analytics, and e-commerce revolutionize supply chain visibility and responsiveness.
2020sResilience, ethical sourcing, and advanced automation become critical in navigating disruptions and meeting consumer demands.
