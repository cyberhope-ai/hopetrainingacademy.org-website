What is this field?

A TEFL (Teaching English as a Foreign Language) certification equips you with the essential methodologies and practical skills to teach English to non-native speakers. This program covers everything from grammar fundamentals and lesson planning to classroom management and specialized teaching techniques for various age groups and contexts. It's your passport to a rewarding career, whether you aspire to teach online from home or immerse yourself in a new culture abroad.

Think of it like learning to drive a car. You might know how to operate a vehicle, but a driving instructor teaches you the rules of the road, defensive driving techniques, and how to handle different situations. Similarly, a TEFL certification teaches you the 'rules of the classroom,' effective teaching strategies, and how to navigate the diverse needs of English language learners, transforming you from an English speaker into a confident and capable English teacher.

How the field grew

1980sEarly pioneers like Denise begin teaching English to new immigrants, laying groundwork for formal TEFL training.
1992Formal teacher training, such as the CELTA, becomes a recognized standard for English language educators.
2000sThe advent of online learning and blended courses begins to transform TEFL training accessibility and delivery.
2008Experienced educators like Alex and Thomas contribute to the development of comprehensive TEFL curricula.
PresentTEFL certification evolves to meet the demands of a globalized world, including remote and specialized teaching.
