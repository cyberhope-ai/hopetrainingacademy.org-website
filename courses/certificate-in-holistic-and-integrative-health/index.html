What is this field?

Holistic and integrative health is an approach to wellness that considers the whole person — mind, body, spirit, and environment — in the pursuit of optimal health. Rather than focusing solely on symptoms, it seeks to understand and address the root causes of imbalance, drawing from both conventional medicine and complementary therapies to create a personalized path to healing and well-being.

Think of it like tending a garden. Instead of just pulling weeds (treating symptoms), a holistic gardener examines the soil, sunlight, water, and surrounding ecosystem to ensure the entire garden thrives. This program equips you with the knowledge to cultivate health in the same comprehensive way, blending ancient wisdom with modern scientific understanding to support individuals in achieving lasting vitality.

How the field grew

1970sThe 'holistic health' movement gains prominence, emphasizing the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit.
1992The U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH) establishes the Office of Alternative Medicine, acknowledging the growing interest in non-conventional therapies.
1998The NIH office is renamed the National Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine (NCCAM), signaling a shift towards research and integration.
2014NCCAM becomes the National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH), reflecting a focus on integrating these approaches with conventional care.
PresentIntegrative health principles are increasingly adopted in healthcare settings, academic programs, and wellness coaching worldwide.
