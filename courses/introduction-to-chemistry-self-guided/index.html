What is this field?

Chemistry is the scientific study of matter—its properties, how it's structured, how it interacts, and the changes it undergoes. It's the fundamental science that explains everything from why food cooks the way it does to how medications work in your body, providing the bedrock for understanding the natural world around us.

Think of chemistry as the 'instruction manual' for the universe. This program teaches you to read that manual, revealing the basic building blocks (atoms), how they connect (bonds), and how they transform into new substances (reactions). It's the essential first step to understanding complex scientific processes and pursuing a wide range of science-related careers.

How the field grew

17th CenturyModern chemistry emerges, shifting from alchemy to rigorous scientific inquiry based on observation and experimentation.
19th CenturyThe Industrial Revolution highlights the critical role of chemical understanding in manufacturing, agriculture, and public health, driving demand for formal chemistry education.
20th CenturyChemistry education expands globally, becoming a cornerstone for advancements in medicine, technology, materials science, and environmental protection.
TodayFoundational chemistry knowledge remains indispensable for innovation, problem-solving, and career success across all STEM disciplines.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: The Nature of Matter

Exploring states of matter, physical vs. chemical properties, elements, compounds, and mixtures.

02
Module 2: Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table

Understanding atoms, subatomic particles, isotopes, and the organization of the periodic table.

03
Module 3: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

Delving into how atoms connect through ionic and covalent bonds, and basic molecular shapes.

04
Module 4: Chemical Reactions and Stoichiometry

Learning about types of chemical changes, balancing equations, and quantitative relationships in reactions.

05
Module 5: Solutions, Acids, and Bases

Investigating properties of solutions, concentration, the pH scale, and neutralization reactions.

06
Module 6: Introduction to Organic Chemistry

Basic concepts of carbon-based compounds, hydrocarbons, and their importance in living systems.
