What is this field?

Guided Reading and Writing are powerful instructional approaches designed to foster literacy development by providing targeted support to students. These strategies involve educators working with small groups of students who share similar learning needs, offering tailored instruction that helps them develop independent reading and writing skills.

Think of it like a personal trainer for literacy. Just as a trainer customizes exercises to help athletes build specific muscles, guided reading and writing allow educators to focus on particular skills, helping students overcome challenges and build confidence in their ability to comprehend, analyze, and express themselves effectively through text.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this professional development program is designed for current or aspiring educators looking to enhance their literacy instruction skills, regardless of their current degree status.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this program is a professional development course focused on skill enhancement and does not include an exam voucher for any external certification.

How long does it take to complete this program?

The program is 100% online and self-paced, allowing you to learn at your convenience. Specific course hours are not provided in the description, but it's designed for flexible completion over several weeks.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program focuses on enhancing your practical teaching skills in guided reading and writing strategies, not on preparing for a specific professional certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program enhances the skills of educators, making them more effective in roles such as Elementary School Teacher, Middle School Teacher, or Reading Specialist, by improving their ability to teach reading and writing effectively.
