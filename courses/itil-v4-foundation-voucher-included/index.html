What is this field?

The ITIL 4 Foundation certification provides a comprehensive framework for managing IT-enabled services, focusing on value co-creation, collaboration, and continual improvement. It's designed for anyone involved in IT service delivery, offering a common language and set of best practices to align IT with business objectives, optimize resources, and enhance service quality.

Think of ITIL 4 as the blueprint for running a highly efficient and customer-focused IT department. Just as a well-designed blueprint ensures a building is sturdy and functional, ITIL 4 provides the structure and guidance to ensure your organization's IT services are reliable, cost-effective, and directly support business goals, making IT a strategic asset rather than just a cost center.

How the field grew

Late 1980sThe UK government's CCTA develops the IT Infrastructure Library (ITIL v1) to standardize IT practices.
2000ITIL v2 is released, consolidating practices into a more structured framework.
2007ITIL v3 (ITIL 2007) introduces a lifecycle approach to service management.
2019ITIL 4 is launched, integrating modern practices like Agile, DevOps, and Lean into the service value system.
