What is this field?

Blogging and podcasting are powerful digital mediums that allow individuals and organizations to publish written articles (blogs) or audio episodes (podcasts) on the internet. They serve as platforms for sharing information, expressing opinions, telling stories, and building communities around specific topics or interests.

Think of blogging as having your own online newspaper column or magazine, where you control the content and publication schedule. Podcasting is like hosting your own radio show or talk program, accessible on-demand to listeners worldwide. Both empower you to become a publisher and broadcaster from your own home.

How the field grew

1994The first personal online journals, precursors to modern blogs, begin to appear.
1997The term 'weblog' is coined to describe these online journals.
2004The term 'podcast' is coined, and Apple introduces podcasting support to iTunes, significantly boosting its mainstream adoption.
2006Apple releases GarageBand 3, including dedicated podcasting tools, making audio production more accessible.
2010s-PresentExplosive growth in content creation tools, hosting platforms, and monetization opportunities for bloggers and podcasters.
