What is this field?

Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL) certification equips you with the essential skills and knowledge to teach English to non-native speakers around the world. This program focuses on practical methodologies, grammar instruction, and classroom management techniques, preparing you for diverse teaching environments, whether online, abroad, or in your local community.

Think of it like learning to drive a car. While you might know how to operate a vehicle, a driving instructor teaches you the rules of the road, defensive driving, and how to handle various situations safely and effectively. Similarly, a TEFL certification teaches you the "rules of the language road" and the best pedagogical practices to guide students efficiently and confidently toward English fluency.

How the field grew

1980sCommunicative Language Teaching (CLT) gains prominence, shifting focus to practical communication.
1990sStandardized TEFL certifications emerge, offering structured training for aspiring English teachers.
2000sThe internet revolutionizes TEFL, enabling online certification programs and remote teaching opportunities.
2010sIncreased demand for specialized TEFL skills, including Business English and Young Learner instruction.
2020sRemote and hybrid teaching models become widespread, expanding global access to English education.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to become a TEFL instructor?

While many TEFL positions, especially abroad, prefer or require a bachelor's degree, it is not always mandatory. This certification can open doors to teaching opportunities even without a degree, depending on the employer and location.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification exam?

This program provides a comprehensive 220-hour Advanced TEFL Certification upon completion of the course material. There is no separate external exam voucher included or required for this specific certification.

How long does it take to complete the 220-hour Advanced TEFL Certification?

What specific TEFL certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program awards you the 220-hour Advanced TEFL Certification directly upon successful completion of all course modules. It does not prepare you for a separate external certification exam, as the course itself is the certification.

What kind of jobs can I get with this TEFL certification?

With this certification, you can pursue roles such as an online ESL instructor, an English language teacher abroad, a business English trainer, or an educator specializing in teaching English to young learners.
