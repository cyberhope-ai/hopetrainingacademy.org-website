How the field grew

1965Melzack and Wall introduce the Gate Control Theory of Pain, revolutionizing understanding.
1970sThe first interdisciplinary pain clinics emerge, emphasizing holistic treatment.
1990sPain is recognized as the 'fifth vital sign,' increasing its prominence in assessment.
2000sGrowing awareness of the opioid crisis drives innovation in non-pharmacological pain therapies.

What the career pays

Professionals skilled in pain assessment and management accurately evaluate patient pain levels and characteristics, then develop and implement comprehensive treatment plans. They utilize a variety of techniques to alleviate suffering, improve function, and enhance patient well-being.

$81,220median

The skills the work is built on

  • Active Listening
  • Critical Thinking
  • Patient Monitoring
  • Social Perceptiveness
  • Judgment and Decision Making
  • Treatment Planning

Jobs it opens

Registered Nurse

Applies pain management protocols in direct patient care.

Physical Therapist

Designs therapeutic exercises and modalities for pain relief.

Occupational Therapist

Helps patients adapt activities to manage chronic pain.

Medical Assistant

Assists with patient intake, documentation, and comfort measures.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this certificate program is designed to enhance skills for current healthcare professionals or those entering allied health fields, and does not require a prior degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This self-guided certificate focuses on skill development and does not include an external certification exam or voucher.

How long will it take to complete this training?

This program consists of 14 contact hours, allowing you to complete it at your own pace online.

Is this course for a specific version of a pain management certification?

This program is a skill-building certificate in pain assessment and management, not tied to a specific external certification exam version.

What kind of job can I get with this certificate?

This certificate enhances skills for various healthcare roles such as Registered Nurses, Physical Therapists, Occupational Therapists, and Medical Assistants, allowing you to specialize in pain care.
