What is this field?

Adobe Animate CC is a powerful software used to create vector graphics, animation, and interactive content for web, television, and games. This training program teaches you how to bring static images to life, design engaging user interfaces, and develop dynamic multimedia experiences.

Think of it like being a digital puppeteer or a comic book artist who can make their drawings move and respond to touch. You'll learn to craft everything from simple animated banners to complex interactive games, giving you the skills to tell stories and engage audiences in new, dynamic ways.

How the field grew

1996FutureSplash Animator launches, pioneering web animation.
1996Macromedia acquires FutureSplash, rebranding it as Macromedia Flash.
2005Adobe acquires Macromedia, integrating Flash into its Creative Suite.
2015Adobe Flash Professional is rebranded as Adobe Animate CC, emphasizing its role in creating content for modern web standards and platforms.
