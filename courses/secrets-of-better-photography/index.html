What is this field?

Photography is more than just pointing and shooting; it's the art of capturing light to create lasting images. It involves understanding your camera's capabilities, mastering composition, and manipulating light to tell a story or convey emotion. From documenting life's moments to crafting visual narratives, photography is a powerful medium for expression and communication.

Think of photography like painting with light. Just as a painter uses brushes and colors, a photographer uses a camera, lenses, and various techniques to 'paint' a scene. This program equips you with the fundamental skills to control your camera and environment, allowing you to move beyond snapshots and create truly impactful photographs that reflect your unique vision.

How the field grew

1826First permanent photograph by Nicéphore Niépce
1888Kodak introduces the first easy-to-use camera, making photography accessible
1975First digital camera prototype developed by Kodak engineer Steven Sasson
2000sRise of digital photography and smartphone cameras revolutionizes image capture

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this photography program?

No, this program is designed for individuals eager to learn photography skills, regardless of prior academic degrees. Our focus is on practical, hands-on training.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

This course focuses on practical photography skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, so an exam voucher is not applicable or included.

How long does it take to complete the 'Secrets of Better Photography' program?

This is a self-paced online program. While completion times vary, most dedicated learners can finish the material within 6 to 8 weeks.

Does this program prepare me for a specific photography certification exam?

This program is designed to build foundational and advanced photography skills, not to prepare for a specific industry certification exam. It focuses on practical application and artistic development.

What kind of job can I get after completing this photography program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as portrait photographer, event photographer, photojournalist, commercial photographer, or freelance photographer, applying their enhanced skills in various creative fields.
