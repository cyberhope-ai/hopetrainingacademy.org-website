What is this field?

Floral design is more than just arranging flowers; it's an art form that transforms spaces and emotions. From elegant bouquets to grand event installations, floral designers craft beauty for every occasion. Think of a floral designer as a visual storyteller, using nature's palette to create unforgettable experiences and enhance significant moments.

This program dives into the foundational principles of floral design, guided by the expertise of industry icon Preston Bailey. You'll learn the essential tools, processing techniques, and wholesale purchasing strategies needed to create stunning arrangements and build a successful career, even if you're starting with no prior experience in floristry.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyThe rise of professional floristry as a distinct trade, moving beyond simple garden arrangements to structured, artistic compositions for events and homes.
Mid-20th CenturyIncreased demand for elaborate event decor and specialized floral services, leading to the development of more complex design techniques and business practices.
Late 20th CenturyPreston Bailey emerges as a leading figure, revolutionizing event floral design with his signature opulent and imaginative creations, inspiring a new generation of designers.
PresentThe industry continues to evolve with sustainable practices and online learning, making professional floral design accessible to aspiring artists worldwide.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a floral designer?

No, a formal degree is not typically required. This program is designed for beginners and focuses on practical skills and industry knowledge to prepare you for a professional career.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher is not included with this specific program. The program culminates in a final project and exam to assess your acquired skills.

How long does it take to complete this floral design program?

This program is self-paced and includes 240 course hours. The actual completion time will vary based on your individual study schedule and dedication.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Floral Designer, Event Florist, Freelance Floral Artist, or Floral Assistant, working in flower shops, event companies, or starting their own businesses.

Does Hope Training Academy offer internships for this program?

While this specific program does not include an externship through Hope Training Academy, it provides the foundational skills to pursue internship opportunities independently, including potential applications with Preston Bailey Designs. Hope Training Academy does offer United States Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship training programs more broadly.
