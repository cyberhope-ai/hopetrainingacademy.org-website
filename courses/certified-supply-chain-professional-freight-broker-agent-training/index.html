What is this field?

A Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) is a highly skilled expert who orchestrates the entire flow of goods and services, from raw materials to final delivery. This critical role involves optimizing planning, sourcing, manufacturing, and logistics to ensure efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance customer satisfaction. Coupled with freight broker/agent training, you'll also learn to connect shippers with carriers, managing the transportation of goods and potentially building your own independent business in the dynamic freight industry.

Think of a Certified Supply Chain Professional as the conductor of a complex orchestra, ensuring every instrument (supplier, manufacturer, distributor, transporter) plays in perfect harmony to deliver a seamless performance (product to customer). Adding freight brokering skills means you also become the expert travel agent for goods, finding the best routes and carriers to get products where they need to go, efficiently and reliably.

How the field grew

1957APICS (now ASCM), the certifying body for CSCP, is founded, laying groundwork for supply chain professionalism.
1990sGlobalization drives the expansion and complexity of supply chains, increasing demand for skilled professionals.
2006The Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) certification is launched by APICS, setting a global standard.
2020sSupply chain resilience, sustainability, and digital transformation become paramount, shaping modern logistics.
