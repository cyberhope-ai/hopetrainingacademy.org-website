What is this field?

The Medical Cannabis Program provides comprehensive insight into the rapidly expanding medical cannabis industry. You'll explore its historical context, understand its diverse healthcare applications, and navigate the complex social, legal, and business landscapes surrounding it. This training is designed for professionals seeking to enter or advance within this evolving field, offering a foundational understanding of its critical facets.

Think of it like learning to navigate a new, bustling city. Instead of just knowing a few streets, you'll understand the city's history, its major districts (healthcare, legal, business), its traffic laws, and how different areas connect. This holistic view prepares you to confidently move through and contribute to the medical cannabis environment.
