What is this field?

HIPAA Compliance refers to adhering to the regulations set forth by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), along with subsequent amendments like the HITECH Act. These laws establish national standards for protecting sensitive patient health information (PHI) from being disclosed without the patient's consent or knowledge. For anyone working with health data, understanding and implementing these rules is not just good practice—it's a legal necessity.

Think of HIPAA compliance like a secure lock and key system for all patient health information. Just as you wouldn't leave your personal financial documents exposed, healthcare providers and related organizations must ensure that medical records, billing information, and even conversations about a patient's health are protected. This training teaches you how to properly use and maintain that 'lock and key' system, ensuring privacy, security, and accountability across the healthcare landscape.

How the field grew

1996Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) enacted, establishing national standards for protecting patient health information.
2009Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act passed as part of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA), strengthening HIPAA's enforcement and expanding its scope.
2013HIPAA Omnibus Rule implemented, further enhancing patient privacy protections and strengthening the enforcement provisions of HIPAA and HITECH.
