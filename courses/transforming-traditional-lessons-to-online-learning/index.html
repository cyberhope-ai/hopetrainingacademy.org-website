What is this field?

This training program equips educators and trainers with the essential skills to adapt traditional lessons into dynamic, effective online learning experiences. You'll learn to leverage digital tools and pedagogical strategies to engage learners in virtual environments, ensuring high-quality instruction regardless of the setting.

At its core, the program focuses on the SAMR model (Substitution, Augmentation, Modification, and Redefinition), a framework for integrating technology into teaching. Think of it like upgrading from a basic flip phone to a smartphone: you start by simply replacing old functions with digital ones (Substitution), then enhance them (Augmentation), fundamentally change how tasks are done (Modification), and finally create entirely new learning possibilities (Redefinition).
