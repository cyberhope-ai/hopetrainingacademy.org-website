What is this field?

Nonprofit marketing is the strategic effort to promote a nonprofit organization's mission, programs, and services to its target audience, including donors, volunteers, beneficiaries, and the general public. Unlike commercial marketing focused on sales, nonprofit marketing aims to generate support, raise awareness, and inspire action for a social cause. It encompasses a wide range of activities from public relations and fundraising to community engagement and advocacy.

Think of your nonprofit's mission as a powerful engine, and marketing as the fuel and navigation system. Without effective marketing, even the most impactful mission struggles to gain traction, attract resources, or reach those it aims to serve. This program equips you with the tools to not only fuel your mission but also steer it towards maximum impact and visibility.

How the field grew

1980sRise of direct mail and telemarketing as primary fundraising and awareness tools for nonprofits.
1990sEmergence of the internet, leading to early nonprofit websites for information dissemination and basic online donations.
2000sSocial media platforms begin to transform how nonprofits engage with supporters, advocate for causes, and recruit volunteers.
2010sData-driven marketing, digital storytelling, and sophisticated online fundraising campaigns become standard practice in the nonprofit sector.
2020sIntegration of AI for personalized outreach, donor segmentation, and optimized communication strategies to enhance impact.
