What is this field?

Digital marketing encompasses all marketing efforts that use an electronic device or the internet. Businesses leverage digital channels such as search engines, social media, email, and other websites to connect with current and prospective customers. It's about reaching your audience where they spend their time, building brand awareness, and driving sales through measurable online campaigns.

Think of digital marketing as your business's online megaphone and GPS system combined. Instead of shouting into a crowd hoping someone hears, you're using precise tools to speak directly to your ideal customers, guide them to your virtual storefront, and track exactly how they interact with your message. This allows for incredible efficiency and the ability to adapt your strategy in real-time for maximum impact.

How the field grew

1990sEmergence of search engines and the first clickable banner ads, marking the dawn of digital advertising.
2000sRise of social media platforms like MySpace and Facebook, alongside Google AdWords, revolutionizing online targeting.
2010sMobile marketing, content marketing, and sophisticated data analytics become central to digital strategies.
2020sIncreased focus on personalization, video content, AI-driven marketing, and privacy-first approaches.
