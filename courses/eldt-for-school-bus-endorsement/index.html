What is this field?

The Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) for School Bus Endorsement program is a crucial online theory course designed for individuals seeking to add a School Bus (S) endorsement to their existing Commercial Driver's License (CDL). This endorsement is federally mandated for new school bus drivers and ensures you possess the foundational knowledge required to safely operate a school bus and manage student passengers. It covers critical aspects from vehicle inspections and emergency procedures to student management techniques, all essential for becoming a responsible and effective school bus driver.

Think of it like getting your pilot's license for a specific type of aircraft. You already have your basic driver's license (CDL), but to fly a school bus (a specialized vehicle with unique responsibilities), you need additional, specific training. This program provides the theoretical knowledge for that 'specialized license,' preparing you for the written portion of the S endorsement exam and laying the groundwork for the required behind-the-wheel training.

How the field grew

1970sFederal regulations for school bus safety began to be established by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA).
1986The Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Act (CMVSA) was enacted, leading to the creation of the Commercial Driver's License (CDL) program.
2012The Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21) mandated the development of Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) standards.
2022The ELDT final rule became effective, requiring specific training for new CDL applicants and those seeking certain endorsements, including the School Bus (S) endorsement.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a school bus driver?

No, a college degree is not required. Most employers look for a high school diploma or equivalent, a valid CDL with the necessary endorsements, and a clean driving record.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the S endorsement?

This program provides the required theoretical training for the School Bus (S) endorsement. Information regarding exam vouchers is not included with this course.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This self-paced online program is designed to be completed in approximately 10 course hours. You will have three months of access to the course materials from your registration date.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

Yes, this program prepares you for the theory portion of the federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements for obtaining a School Bus (S) endorsement on your Commercial Driver's License (CDL).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion of this program and subsequent behind-the-wheel training and testing, you will be qualified to work as a school bus driver for school districts, private transportation companies, or other organizations requiring school bus operation.
