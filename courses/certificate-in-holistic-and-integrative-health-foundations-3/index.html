What is this field?

Holistic and integrative health is an approach to well-being that considers the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—and integrates conventional medicine with complementary therapies. Instead of focusing solely on symptoms, it seeks to understand and address the root causes of imbalance, promoting overall health and vitality through a broad spectrum of practices.

Think of it like a master chef creating a balanced meal: they don't just focus on one ingredient, but carefully combine various flavors, textures, and nutrients to create a harmonious and nourishing experience. Similarly, holistic health combines diverse healing modalities like humor, music, energy work, and therapeutic touch to support an individual's complete well-being.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesTraditional healing systems like Ayurveda and Traditional Chinese Medicine emphasize holistic principles, viewing health as a balance of interconnected elements.
1970sThe rise of 'alternative medicine' gains traction, with increased interest in non-conventional therapies and a focus on natural healing.
1990s-2000sThe term 'integrative medicine' emerges, signaling a shift towards combining evidence-based complementary therapies with conventional medical treatments for comprehensive care.
PresentHolistic and integrative health continues to gain acceptance, with growing research supporting its benefits and increased integration into mainstream healthcare settings.
