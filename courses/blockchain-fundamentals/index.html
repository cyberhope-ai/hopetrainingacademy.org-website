What is this field?

Blockchain Fundamentals introduces you to the revolutionary distributed ledger technology that underpins cryptocurrencies and countless other secure digital applications. You'll explore how blockchain creates an immutable, transparent, and decentralized record of transactions, eliminating the need for intermediaries and enhancing trust across networks.

Imagine a digital ledger, like a shared, unchangeable notebook, where every new entry (a 'block') is cryptographically linked to the previous one, forming an unbreakable chain. This course demystifies how these blocks are created through processes like 'mining' and 'minting,' and how complex algorithms achieve 'consensus' among network participants to validate and secure every transaction.

How the field grew

1991The concept of a cryptographically secured chain of blocks is first described.
2008Satoshi Nakamoto publishes the Bitcoin whitepaper, outlining a decentralized digital currency.
2015Ethereum launches, introducing smart contracts and programmable blockchain applications.
PresentBlockchain technology sees widespread adoption in finance, supply chain, healthcare, and more.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Introduction to Blockchain

Explore the foundational concepts of blockchain, its history, and the principles of distributed ledger technology (DLT).

02
Module 2: Cryptography and Security

Understand the cryptographic primitives that secure blockchain, including hashing, digital signatures, and immutability.

03
Module 3: Consensus Mechanisms

Learn about various algorithms like Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS) that enable network consensus and transaction validation.

04
Module 4: Transactions and Blocks

Delve into the lifecycle of a blockchain transaction, from initiation to its inclusion in a block through mining or minting.

05
Module 5: Blockchain Applications and Use Cases

Discover real-world applications of blockchain beyond cryptocurrencies, including smart contracts, supply chain, and identity management.
