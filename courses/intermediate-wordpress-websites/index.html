What is this field?

WordPress is the world's most popular content management system, powering over 40% of all websites. While many can build a basic site, mastering intermediate WordPress skills allows you to create truly unique and powerful online experiences. This goes beyond simple blogs to include complex functionalities like user accounts, custom content types, and advanced design elements.

Imagine a website not just as a static brochure, but as a vibrant digital hub where visitors can actively participate. This training empowers you to build sites where users can respond to messages, fill out custom forms, securely purchase products, book appointments, and much more. It's about turning a passive viewer into an active participant, making your websites functional and engaging.

How the field grew

2003WordPress 0.7 released, evolving from b2/cafelog
2005Introduction of Pages, Themes, and the Akismet spam filter
2010Launch of custom post types, enabling greater content flexibility
2018Introduction of the Gutenberg block editor, revolutionizing content creation
2023Continued evolution with full site editing and advanced customization tools
