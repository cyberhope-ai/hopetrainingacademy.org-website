What is this field?

Product Ownership Analysis (POA) is a critical discipline for professionals who guide the development of successful products and services. In today's product-centric business landscape, POA practitioners bridge the gap between business strategy and product execution, ensuring that what gets built truly delivers value to customers and the organization. This involves understanding market needs, defining product vision, managing backlogs, and collaborating across teams to bring innovative solutions to life.

Think of a POA practitioner as the architect and conductor of a product's journey. They don't just build; they envision, plan, and steer the entire process from concept to launch and beyond. Just as a skilled architect ensures a building is structurally sound and meets its purpose, a POA expert ensures a product is valuable, viable, and desirable, transforming complex ideas into tangible, successful outcomes.

How the field grew

2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) is founded, establishing a global standard for business analysis professionals.
2010sThe rise of Agile methodologies and product-centric organizational models shifts focus from project delivery to continuous product value creation.
Recent YearsIIBA introduces the Certificate in Product Ownership Analysis (CPOA) to validate specialized skills in this evolving and in-demand field.
