What is this field?

Behavior contract design is a structured approach to improving specific behaviors in students by clearly outlining expectations, rewards, and consequences. It's a collaborative agreement between a student and an educator, designed to foster self-management and positive behavioral change within the learning environment.

Think of it like a fitness plan for behavior. Just as a personal trainer helps you set clear exercise goals, track progress, and earn rewards for reaching milestones, a behavior contract provides a similar framework for students to achieve their behavioral goals, making the journey clear and motivating.

How the field grew

1960sEarly applications of behavioral principles in educational settings.
1970sFormalization of behavior modification techniques in classrooms.
1990sEmphasis on positive behavior interventions and supports (PBIS).
2000sIntegration of individualized behavior plans and contracts in education.

Module 3: Choosing Effective Reinforcers

Explore various types of reinforcers, conduct preference assessments, and create a reinforcement menu.

Module 4: Designing the Behavior Contract

Learn the essential components of a behavior contract, ensuring clear language and student involvement.
