What is this field?

"Explore a Career in Nursing" is your essential first step into the healthcare profession, designed to help you understand the diverse roles and responsibilities of both Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs) and Registered Nurses (RNs). This program provides a comprehensive overview of the daily life, educational requirements, and career opportunities within nursing, helping you determine if this vital field is the right path for you.

Think of this program as a detailed roadmap for a new journey. Just as a map shows you different routes, landmarks, and destinations, this course guides you through the various specializations and pathways in nursing, allowing you to visualize your future career before committing to a specific educational program.

How the field grew

1860Florence Nightingale establishes the first secular nursing school.
1899American Nurses Association (ANA) founded, advancing nursing standards.
1950sIntroduction of associate degree nursing programs, expanding access to the profession.
1980sRise of advanced practice nursing roles, enhancing specialized patient care.
2020sIncreased demand for nurses in specialized and remote care, driven by global health needs.

What the career pays

Nurses are frontline healthcare professionals dedicated to patient care, advocacy, and education. They play a critical role in promoting health, preventing disease, and helping patients cope with illness.

Jobs it opens

Registered Nurse (RN)

Provides direct patient care, administers medication, educates patients and families.

Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)

Works under RN supervision, performs basic nursing care, monitors patient health.

Clinical Nurse Specialist

Advanced practice nurse focusing on specific patient populations or conditions.

Nurse Educator

Teaches and trains future nurses in academic or clinical settings.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to enroll in this "Explore a Career in Nursing" program?

No, this introductory program has no specific degree prerequisites. It's designed for anyone interested in learning more about the nursing profession.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

An exam voucher is not included with this exploratory course, as it is designed to introduce you to nursing careers, not prepare you for a specific certification exam.

How long will it take me to complete this program?

This is a self-guided program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. The time to completion will vary based on your individual schedule and learning speed.

Does this course prepare me for a specific nursing certification exam?

This course is an introductory exploration of nursing careers (LPN and RN), not a preparation course for a specific certification or licensing exam. It helps you understand what those paths entail.

What kind of job can I get after completing this "Explore a Career in Nursing" course?

This course is designed to help you decide if a career in nursing is right for you. Upon completion, you'll have a clearer understanding of the roles of LPNs and RNs, which will inform your next steps towards formal nursing education and employment.
