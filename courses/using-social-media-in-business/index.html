What is this field?

The Using Social Media in Business program equips you with the essential skills to leverage popular social media platforms for commercial success. You'll learn how to craft compelling online strategies, engage target audiences, and measure the impact of your digital efforts across platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Google Business Profile, Twitter, and Pinterest.

Think of social media for business like having a vibrant, global town square for your brand. Instead of just a storefront, you gain a dynamic space to connect directly with customers, showcase your products or services, listen to feedback, and build a thriving community around your business, all while reaching an unprecedented audience.

How the field grew

2004Facebook launched, revolutionizing online social interaction and setting the stage for business engagement.
2006Twitter launched, introducing microblogging and real-time communication for brands.
2010Instagram launched, popularizing visual content sharing and influencer marketing.
2012Google Business Profile (then Google+ Local) began integrating business listings with social features, enhancing local search.
2020sThe rise of short-form video content and advanced analytics further shaped social media marketing strategies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in the Using Social Media in Business program. Our focus is on practical skills and career readiness, making it accessible to a wide range of learners.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This program is a skill-building course focused on the practical application of social media strategies for business and does not lead to an external certification exam. Therefore, no exam voucher is included.

How long will it take to complete the Using Social Media in Business program?

This online program is self-paced and designed to be completed in approximately 24 course hours. You can learn at a schedule that fits your life and commitments.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to equip you with practical, immediately applicable skills for social media marketing in business, rather than preparing you for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Social Media Specialist, Digital Marketing Coordinator, Content Creator, or contribute significantly to marketing efforts within various businesses looking to enhance their online presence and customer engagement.
