What is this field?

A pet sitting business offers professional care for pets in their own homes while their owners are away. This can include feeding, walking, administering medication, and providing companionship. It's a highly flexible and fulfilling career path for animal lovers, allowing you to be your own boss and build a service that caters to the growing demand for personalized pet care.

Think of it like being a personal concierge for pets. Instead of a one-size-fits-all kennel, you provide tailored, in-home services that ensure pets remain comfortable and happy in their familiar surroundings, reducing stress for both the animals and their owners.

How the field grew

1980sEmergence of professional pet sitting as a recognized service.
1990sFormation of national and international pet sitting associations, standardizing practices.
2000sRise of online platforms connecting pet owners with local sitters, expanding market reach.
2020sIncreased demand for specialized and luxury pet care services, driving business growth.
