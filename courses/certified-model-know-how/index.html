What is this field?

Modeling is more than just looking good; it's a demanding profession that requires business acumen, self-promotion, and a deep understanding of visual communication. This program equips you with the essential skills and insider knowledge to navigate the competitive world of modeling, from perfecting your pose to building a powerful personal brand.

Think of it like becoming a visual entrepreneur. You'll learn to market yourself, manage your image, and create compelling content, transforming your natural talents into a sustainable and rewarding career. This program provides the mentorship and practical training needed to stand out and succeed.

How the field grew

1960sThe rise of iconic supermodels like Twiggy and Jean Shrimpton, defining new beauty standards.
1980s-90sThe 'supermodel era' with figures like Naomi Campbell and Cindy Crawford achieving global celebrity status.
2004Naima Mora wins America's Next Top Model, becoming a household name and an inspiration for aspiring models.
2010s-PresentThe digital age transforms modeling, emphasizing social media presence, personal branding, and content creation.

What the career pays

Models represent brands, products, and ideas through visual presentation in various media. They utilize posing, expression, and movement to convey messages and evoke emotions, often working closely with photographers, designers, and creative directors.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a model?

No, a formal degree is not required to pursue a modeling career. This program focuses on practical skills, industry knowledge, and professional development to prepare you for success.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Information regarding an exam voucher is not available for this specific program. Please contact our admissions team for the most current details.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This self-paced program provides 240 course hours, and you will have 9 months of access to complete all modules and the final exam.

What certification will I earn upon completion?

Upon successful completion of the program and final exam, you will earn the Model Know How Certification offered by Lovegevity.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue various roles such as fashion models, commercial models, runway models, print models, and even develop their own personal brand as social media influencers.
