What is this field?

Catering is the art and business of providing food and beverage services for events, ranging from intimate gatherings to large corporate functions and weddings. It combines culinary creativity with meticulous planning, logistics, and customer service to deliver a memorable dining experience outside of a traditional restaurant setting.

Think of it like being the director of a delicious play, where you're not only crafting the menu (the script) but also managing the cast, crew, and venue to ensure a flawless performance. A successful caterer masters both the kitchen and the boardroom, transforming culinary visions into profitable and perfectly executed events.

How the field grew

1700sEmergence of formal culinary arts and professional chefs in European households.
1800sFirst dedicated catering services appear, serving large social events and travel.
1950sPost-war economic boom leads to widespread growth in event and corporate catering.
2000sRise of specialized catering, focusing on diverse cuisines, dietary needs, and sustainability.

What the career pays

Caterers are culinary entrepreneurs who plan, prepare, and deliver food and beverage services for a wide range of events. They manage everything from menu design and food preparation to logistics, staffing, and client relations, ensuring a memorable dining experience.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a caterer?

No, a college degree is not typically required. Practical culinary skills, business acumen, and hands-on experience are far more valuable in the catering industry.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses on practical skills and business knowledge for catering and does not lead to an external certification exam, so an exam voucher is not applicable.

How long does it take to complete this training?

This is a self-guided online training program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. The total time required will depend on your individual study habits and schedule.

Is there a specific certification exam associated with this training?

This training program is designed to equip you with the essential skills and knowledge to succeed as a caterer, rather than preparing you for a specific external certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Catering Manager, Event Chef, or even to launch and operate your own catering business.
