What is this field?

Power plant operations involve the critical task of monitoring, controlling, and maintaining the complex machinery and systems that generate electricity. Operators ensure the safe and efficient production of power, responding to real-time data and making precise adjustments to keep energy flowing.

Think of a power plant operator as the conductor of a massive, intricate orchestra. They ensure every instrument—from turbines and boilers to generators—plays in perfect harmony, producing the steady, reliable energy that powers our homes and industries.

How the field grew

1882Thomas Edison opens the first central power station in New York City, marking the dawn of commercial electricity generation.
Early 1900sRapid expansion of power grids and large-scale power plants creates a growing demand for skilled operators to manage complex systems.
Mid-20th CenturyAdvancements in automation and control systems begin to transform operator roles, emphasizing monitoring and troubleshooting expertise.
2000sIncreased focus on environmental regulations, renewable energy integration, and digital control systems further evolves the power plant operator profession.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Basic Safety & Foundational Skills

Hazard communication, lockout-tagout, essential mathematics, and effective communication strategies.

02
Applied Physics & Electrical Principles

Fundamental physics, heat exchangers, lubrication, bearings, and electrical safety for operators.

03
Industrial Systems & Controls

Understanding industrial valves, compressed air systems, process drawings, and basic instrumentation and control.

04
Pump Operations

Fundamentals of various pump types, with a detailed focus on the operation of centrifugal pumps.

05
Steam Generation & Boiler Systems

Principles of steam generation, basic boiler systems, fuels, combustion processes, and boiler auxiliaries.

06
Advanced Power Generation

In-depth study of advanced boiler auxiliaries, cogeneration, and Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSG).
