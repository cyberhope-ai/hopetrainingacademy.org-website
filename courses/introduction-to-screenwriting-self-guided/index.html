What is this field?

Screenwriting is the art and craft of writing scripts for films, television shows, video games, or other visual media. It involves creating compelling narratives, developing memorable characters, crafting authentic dialogue, and structuring the story in a way that translates effectively to the screen. A well-written screenplay serves as the blueprint for an entire production, guiding directors, actors, and crew in bringing a vision to life.

Think of a screenplay as a detailed architectural plan for a building. Just as an architect designs every room, hallway, and structural element with purpose, a screenwriter meticulously designs every scene, character interaction, and plot point. The goal is to create a sturdy, engaging framework that allows the final product – whether a blockbuster film or an indie short – to stand tall and captivate its audience.

How the field grew

1890sEmergence of early silent films with basic narrative outlines.
1920sRise of Hollywood studios, establishing screenwriting as a specialized craft.
1940sGolden Age of Hollywood solidifies screenwriting as a foundational element of filmmaking.
1970sNew Hollywood era emphasizes writer-directors and character-driven narratives.
2000sDigital revolution and streaming platforms expand opportunities for screenwriters globally.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a screenwriter?

No, a formal degree is not typically required to become a successful screenwriter. The industry values strong storytelling skills, a compelling portfolio, and a deep understanding of the craft, which this program helps you develop.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This Introduction to Screenwriting program does not include an exam voucher, as it focuses on skill development rather than a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn on your schedule. You will have access to the course content for three months to complete it at your convenience.

Is there a certification exam for screenwriting?

There isn't a single, universally recognized certification exam for screenwriting. Success in this field is typically measured by the quality of your portfolio (your scripts) and your ability to sell or get your work produced.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completing this program, you will have the foundational skills to pursue roles such as a freelance screenwriter, staff writer for television, or to develop your own projects. It prepares you to craft compelling narratives for film, TV, and other media.
