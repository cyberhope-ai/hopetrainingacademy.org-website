What is this field?

English Language Learners (ELLs) are students whose native language is not English and who are developing their English proficiency. Effectively grouping these students is crucial for providing targeted instruction that meets their diverse linguistic and academic needs. This approach ensures all students receive appropriate support, fostering both language acquisition and content mastery.

Flexible grouping involves strategically organizing students into small, temporary groups based on their current English proficiency, learning objectives, or specific skill gaps. Unlike static groups, flexible grouping allows educators to adapt quickly to student progress, ensuring that instruction is always relevant and engaging. This dynamic method maximizes learning opportunities and promotes a more inclusive and effective classroom environment for all ELLs.
