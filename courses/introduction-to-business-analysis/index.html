What is this field?

Business analysis is the discipline of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. This involves understanding an organization's structure, policies, and operations, then recommending solutions that enable the organization to achieve its goals. It's a critical bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring that projects deliver real value.

Think of a business analyst as a detective for an organization. They investigate challenges, gather clues (requirements), and then propose the best solutions to help the business run more smoothly and achieve its objectives, much like a detective solves a case by understanding all the evidence and recommending a course of action.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyRoots in scientific management and efficiency studies, focusing on optimizing workflows.
1970s-1980sEmergence of IT projects highlighted the need for professionals to bridge the gap between business requirements and technical development.
2003Formalization of the Business Analyst role with the founding of the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).
2010s-PresentIncreased demand for BAs in agile environments, data analytics, and digital transformation initiatives across all industries.
