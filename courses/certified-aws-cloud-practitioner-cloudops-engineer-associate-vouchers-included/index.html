What is this field?

A CloudOps Engineer is a vital professional who ensures an organization's cloud infrastructure runs smoothly, securely, and efficiently. They are responsible for deploying, managing, and optimizing cloud-based systems, focusing on automation, monitoring, and maintaining high availability. This role bridges the gap between development and operations, applying best practices to keep cloud services reliable and cost-effective.

Think of a CloudOps Engineer as the air traffic controller for your company's digital infrastructure. Just as an air traffic controller manages flights to ensure safe takeoffs, smooth journeys, and secure landings, a CloudOps Engineer manages cloud workloads, ensuring applications are deployed correctly, monitored for performance, secured against threats, and can recover quickly from any issues, all within the vast and dynamic cloud environment.

How the field grew

1994Amazon.com founded, laying groundwork for future cloud services.
2006Amazon Web Services (AWS) officially launches with foundational services like S3 and EC2.
2013AWS introduces its first certification programs to validate cloud expertise.
2018AWS significantly expands its certification offerings, including associate-level operational roles.
2020sCloudOps emerges as a critical discipline for managing complex, scalable cloud environments.
