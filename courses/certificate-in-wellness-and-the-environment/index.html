What is this field?

Environmental wellness is about understanding and promoting the health of both individuals and the planet. It recognizes that our well-being is deeply intertwined with the quality of our surroundings—from the air we breathe and the water we drink to the global climate patterns. This program delves into how environmental factors directly impact human health and what steps can be taken to mitigate risks and foster a healthier living space and world.

Think of it like tending a garden: just as a gardener understands that healthy soil, clean water, and proper sunlight are essential for plants to thrive, environmental wellness teaches us that a healthy environment is fundamental for human flourishing. This program equips you with the knowledge to identify environmental 'weeds' (hazards) and cultivate a more sustainable and health-supportive 'garden' for yourself and your community.
