What is this field?

Grant writing is the art of crafting compelling proposals to secure funding from foundations, corporations, or government agencies. It's about clearly articulating a project's needs, goals, and impact to persuade funders to invest in a cause.

Think of a grant writer as a storyteller and strategist, translating an organization's vision into a persuasive narrative that aligns with a funder's mission. This course moves beyond the basics, equipping you with advanced techniques to navigate complex grant landscapes and maximize funding success.

How the field grew

1950sPost-WWII era sees growth in philanthropic foundations and government funding, increasing demand for structured proposals.
1970sProfessionalization of fundraising and grant writing begins, with specialized roles emerging in non-profits and educational institutions.
1990sRise of the internet streamlines grant research and application processes, making information more accessible and increasing competition.
2000sIncreased emphasis on data-driven outcomes and measurable impact in grant proposals, requiring more sophisticated evaluation plans.
TodayGrant writing continues to evolve with digital tools, strategic partnerships, and a focus on sustainable impact and social justice initiatives.
