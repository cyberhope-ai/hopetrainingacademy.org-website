What is this field?

"Understanding Adolescents" is a comprehensive online training program designed to equip you with profound insights into the unique world of teenage development. This program delves into the intricate psychological, emotional, and social changes that define adolescence, helping you grasp how young people perceive themselves, interact with their environment, and navigate the journey to adulthood. It's like having a detailed map and compass for the often-uncharted territory of the teenage years, guiding you to understand their behaviors and needs more effectively.

This training goes beyond surface-level observations, exploring the science behind adolescent feelings, the critical process of identity formation, and practical strategies for providing optimal support. Whether you're a parent, educator, social worker, or simply someone who interacts with young people, this program offers the knowledge and tools to foster positive relationships and contribute meaningfully to their well-being and success.

How the field grew

1904G. Stanley Hall's seminal work, "Adolescence," establishes the field of adolescent psychology.
1950sErik Erikson's theory of psychosocial development highlights identity formation as a central adolescent task.
1980sResearch expands to emphasize the diverse social and cultural contexts influencing youth development.
2000sAdvances in neuroscience provide new insights into the adolescent brain's unique structure and function.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific degree requirements to enroll in the Understanding Adolescents training program. It is designed for anyone seeking to deepen their knowledge of youth development.

Is an exam voucher included with this training?

This program is a professional development course focused on knowledge acquisition and skill enhancement, not a certification. Therefore, no exam or exam voucher is included or required.

How long does it take to complete this training?

The program is self-paced and typically takes approximately 24 course hours to complete. You can progress through the material at your own convenience.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

No, this program is designed to provide comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in adolescent development, rather than preparing you for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

While this program doesn't lead to a single job title, it significantly enhances skills for roles such as school counselors, youth program coordinators, social workers, educators, and anyone working directly with young people.
