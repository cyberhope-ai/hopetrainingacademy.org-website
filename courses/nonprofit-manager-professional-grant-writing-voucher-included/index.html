What is this field?

A Nonprofit Manager is a visionary leader who guides organizations dedicated to social good, overseeing everything from mission definition and governance to financial health and community impact. They ensure operations run smoothly, volunteers are engaged, and the organization remains compliant and effective in achieving its goals. This role requires a blend of strategic thinking, operational expertise, and a deep commitment to the cause.

Professional Grant Writing is the art and science of securing funding for these vital organizations. A skilled grant writer crafts compelling proposals that articulate an organization's mission, projects, and needs to potential funders, such as foundations, corporations, and government agencies. Think of it like a puzzle solver who connects a community's needs with the resources available to make a difference, transforming good intentions into actionable, funded programs.

How the field grew

1998The Grant Professionals Association (GPA) is founded, establishing a professional community for grant seekers.
2007The Grant Professionals Certification Institute (GPCI) is established, launching the Grant Professional Certified (GPC) credential to recognize expert grant professionals.
PresentThe GPC certification remains a globally recognized standard for excellence in the grant profession, validating expertise and ethical practice.
