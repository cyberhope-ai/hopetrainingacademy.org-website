What is this field?

Infectious diseases, caused by pathogens like bacteria, viruses, fungi, or parasites, are responsible for over a quarter of global deaths. Understanding how these diseases spread, how to identify them, and how to prevent their transmission is crucial for public health and safety. This program equips you with the foundational knowledge to contribute to these vital efforts.

Think of infection control like a highly effective shield for communities and healthcare settings. Just as a strong defense protects a castle from invaders, robust infection control practices safeguard individuals from harmful pathogens. This training provides the strategies and techniques to build and maintain that shield, from recognizing early warning signs to implementing prevention protocols.
