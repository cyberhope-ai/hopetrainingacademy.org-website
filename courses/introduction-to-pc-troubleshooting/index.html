What is this field?

PC troubleshooting is the essential skill of diagnosing and resolving technical issues that arise with personal computers. It's akin to being a digital detective, meticulously examining symptoms, identifying root causes—whether software glitches, hardware malfunctions, or network problems—and implementing effective solutions to restore optimal system performance.

This introductory course empowers you with a systematic approach to tackle common PC problems. You'll learn to understand computer components, utilize diagnostic tools, and apply logical steps to fix issues, making you a go-to resource for maintaining healthy and efficient computer systems for yourself and others.

How the field grew

1980sRise of personal computers creates initial demand for user support.
1990sInternet boom and complex software drive the need for specialized troubleshooting skills.
2000sEmergence of remote support tools expands the reach and efficiency of PC troubleshooters.
2010sProliferation of mobile devices and cloud computing adds new layers of complexity to support roles.
2020sAI-powered tools assist in diagnostics, but human expertise remains critical for complex and nuanced issues.
