What is this field?

Solar panel installers are the hands-on heroes of the clean energy revolution, responsible for assembling, installing, and maintaining photovoltaic (PV) systems that convert sunlight into electricity. This vital role involves everything from mounting panels on rooftops or ground arrays to wiring electrical components, ensuring systems are safely and efficiently connected to power homes and businesses.

Think of a solar panel installer as a skilled puzzle solver and electrician rolled into one. They carefully piece together the solar array, connect the electrical pathways, and ensure every component works in harmony, much like building a complex LEGO set that then powers your entire house with sunshine.

How the field grew

1954Bell Labs develops the first practical silicon solar cell, marking a significant step towards modern solar technology.
1970sThe oil crisis sparks renewed interest in renewable energy, including solar, leading to early government incentives and research.
2006The Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC) is first enacted in the U.S., significantly boosting solar adoption and industry growth.
2022The Inflation Reduction Act allocates substantial funding for clean energy, including solar, accelerating demand for skilled installers.
