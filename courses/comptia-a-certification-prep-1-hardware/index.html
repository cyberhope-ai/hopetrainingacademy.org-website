What is this field?

CompTIA A+ certification is the industry standard for launching a career in IT support. This foundational program, CompTIA A+ Prep 1: Hardware, focuses specifically on the physical components of computers, teaching you how to build, configure, and troubleshoot the essential elements that make a system run. You'll gain practical skills in identifying hardware, installing components, and resolving common issues, preparing you for entry-level technical roles.

Think of it like learning to be a car mechanic, but for computers. Before you can diagnose complex engine problems or install new software, you need to understand how all the physical parts—the engine, the wheels, the electrical system—work together. This program gives you that fundamental understanding of the computer's 'engine' and 'chassis'.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Computer System Components

Identify and explain the function of internal PC components and form factors.

02
Motherboards, CPUs, and RAM

Understand types, selection, installation, and configuration of core processing components.

03
Storage Devices

Explore various storage technologies, including HDDs, SSDs, and RAID, along with installation.

04
Power Supplies and Cooling Systems

Select appropriate power supply units and implement effective cooling solutions for PCs.

05
Peripheral Devices and Expansion

Connect and configure external input/output devices and internal expansion cards.

06
PC Assembly and Custom Builds

Learn the process of building a computer system from individual components.

07
Hardware Troubleshooting

Diagnose and resolve common hardware issues, failures, and performance problems.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, this program focuses on practical skills and industry certification, not a college degree. It's designed for anyone eager to start an IT career.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher for the CompTIA A+ certification exam is not included with this specific program. Vouchers must be purchased separately.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This self-paced program is estimated to take 24 course hours to complete, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

Which CompTIA A+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the hardware-related objectives of the current/latest CompTIA A+ certification exam (Core 1). It is the first of three courses needed for full A+ preparation.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and earning your CompTIA A+ certification (after completing all three prep courses), you'll be qualified for entry-level roles such as IT Support Specialist, Help Desk Technician, or Computer Technician.
