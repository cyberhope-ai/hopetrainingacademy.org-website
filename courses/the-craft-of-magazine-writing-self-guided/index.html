What is this field?

Magazine writing is the art of crafting compelling articles, features, and stories for various publications, both in print and online. It involves researching topics, conducting interviews, and structuring narratives that inform, entertain, or persuade specific audiences. This craft is essential for communicating ideas effectively and engaging readers in a dynamic and ever-evolving media landscape.

At its core, magazine writing is about storytelling with a purpose, tailored to a publication's unique voice and readership. Think of it like a chef creating a signature dish for a specific restaurant: you learn to select the right ingredients (facts, interviews), apply precise techniques (writing style, structure), and present it beautifully (editing, formatting) to satisfy a particular palate (the magazine's audience and editorial board). This program equips you with the practical skills to master this craft and see your work in print or online.
