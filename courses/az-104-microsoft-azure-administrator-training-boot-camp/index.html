What is this field?

An Azure Administrator is a crucial IT professional responsible for implementing, monitoring, and maintaining Microsoft Azure solutions within an organization. This role involves managing virtual networks, storage, compute resources, and ensuring the security and governance of cloud environments. They collaborate with various IT teams to deploy and optimize cloud services, guaranteeing that an organization's digital infrastructure operates smoothly and efficiently in the cloud.

Imagine an Azure Administrator as the architect and builder of a digital city in the cloud. They design the layout (networking), construct the buildings (virtual machines and applications), manage the utilities (storage and databases), and ensure everything is secure and running perfectly. Their work ensures that the organization's online services are always available, secure, and performant.

How the field grew

2008Microsoft announces its ambitious cloud computing platform, initially known as Windows Azure.
2010Microsoft Azure officially launches, providing a comprehensive suite of cloud services to businesses worldwide.
2014The platform is rebranded as Microsoft Azure, signifying its expanded capabilities beyond Windows-centric services.
2018Microsoft introduces a new generation of role-based certifications, including specialized paths for cloud administrators, to validate specific industry skills.
