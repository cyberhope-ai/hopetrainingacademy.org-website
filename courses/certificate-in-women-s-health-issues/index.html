What is this field?

A Certificate in Women's Health Issues provides a comprehensive overview of the unique health challenges and considerations women face throughout their lives. This program delves into the biological, psychological, and social factors that influence women's well-being, from reproductive health and chronic diseases to mental health and the impact of societal determinants.

Think of it like learning the intricate blueprint of a specialized building. Instead of just seeing the exterior, you gain insight into the structural components, the internal systems, and how various external forces can affect its integrity. This knowledge is crucial for anyone looking to support, educate, or work within the broader healthcare and wellness sectors, ensuring a more informed and empathetic approach to women's health.

How the field grew

1960FDA approves the birth control pill, sparking a new era of reproductive health focus.
1990The National Institutes of Health (NIH) establishes the Office of Research on Women's Health, emphasizing dedicated research.
2010The Affordable Care Act expands access to preventive services crucial for women's health.
