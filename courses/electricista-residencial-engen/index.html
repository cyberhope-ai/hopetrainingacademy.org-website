What is this field?

A residential electrician is a skilled professional who installs, maintains, and repairs electrical systems in homes. This comprehensive training program equips you with the fundamental electrical theory, practical wiring techniques, and crucial customer service skills needed to excel in this in-demand trade, with integrated career-aligned English language instruction to ensure your success.

Think of it like learning to build with LEGOs, but for electricity in homes. You'll learn the 'blocks' (components), the 'instructions' (codes and blueprints), and how to put them together safely and effectively, all while building your confidence in communicating clearly on the job.

How the field grew

1879Thomas Edison invents the practical incandescent light bulb, sparking demand for electrical systems.
1897The first National Electrical Code (NEC) is published, establishing foundational safety standards for wiring.
1970sGrowing recognition of the need for standardized vocational training and apprenticeship programs in skilled trades.
2020sIncreased focus on accessible, career-aligned language instruction to address skilled trade shortages and support diverse learners.
PresentHope Training Academy partners with EnGen to offer specialized residential electrician training for non-native English speakers.

Residential electricians are vital to modern living, ensuring safe and functional electrical systems in homes. They install new wiring, troubleshoot issues, and maintain existing infrastructure, adhering to strict safety codes and providing excellent customer service.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a residential electrician?

No, a college degree is not typically required. This program focuses on practical skills and knowledge needed for state licensing exams and entry-level positions.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher is not included with this program. You will need to arrange for your state's electrical licensing exam separately.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

This self-paced program totals 285 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own speed and fit it into your schedule.

What specific licensing exam does this program prepare me for?

This program provides comprehensive preparation for general state electrical licensing exams, covering common electrical situations and codes. However, specific exam requirements vary by state and locality, so always check with your local licensing board.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for entry-level roles such as a Residential Electrician or Electrician Apprentice, working on electrical systems in homes and residential properties.
