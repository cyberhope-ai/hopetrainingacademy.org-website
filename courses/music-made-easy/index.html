What is this field?

Music Made Easy is a hands-on training program designed to demystify the fundamentals of music theory. You'll gain the essential knowledge to understand how music is constructed, enabling you to interpret musical notation, comprehend rhythmic structures, and grasp the basics of melody and harmony. This program provides a solid base for anyone looking to engage with music on a deeper level, whether for personal enrichment or as a stepping stone toward a creative career.

Think of music theory as the grammar of music. Just as understanding grammar allows you to read, write, and speak a language effectively, mastering music theory equips you with the rules and structures needed to read sheet music, compose your own simple tunes, and even play basic pieces. It transforms abstract sounds into an organized, understandable system, making the world of music accessible and enjoyable.

How the field grew

Ancient GreeceEarly philosophical inquiries into harmony and musical intervals laid foundational concepts.
Medieval EraThe development of staff notation began, allowing for the written preservation and transmission of music.
17th CenturyModern musical notation, including time signatures and key signatures, became largely standardized.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: The Language of Music

Understanding the staff, clefs, notes, and their placement on the musical ledger.

02
Module 2: Rhythm and Meter

Exploring note values, rests, time signatures, and how they define musical pulse.

03
Module 3: Scales and Keys

Learning major scales, key signatures, and the concept of tonality in music.

04
Module 4: Intervals and Chords

Identifying basic intervals and constructing fundamental triads to build harmonic understanding.

05
Module 5: Melody and Harmony

Developing skills in constructing simple melodies and understanding basic chord progressions.

06
Module 6: Musical Forms and Performance

Introduction to basic musical structures and applying learned theory to play simple musical pieces.
