What is this field?

The General Educational Development (GED) test is a nationally recognized examination that certifies an individual has academic skills equivalent to a high school graduate. Earning your GED is a crucial step for many, opening doors to higher education, vocational training, and a broader range of employment opportunities that require a high school diploma or its equivalent.

Think of the GED as your universal key to unlock the next level of your career and educational journey. It's not just a piece of paper; it's a proven credential that demonstrates your foundational knowledge and readiness for future challenges, providing the essential stepping stone for personal and professional growth.

How the field grew

1942The GED test was initially developed for returning World War II veterans to help them transition into civilian life and further their education.
1970sThe GED program became widely adopted by states and provinces as the standard for high school equivalency for the general public.
2002A significant revision updated the test content to align more closely with contemporary high school curriculum standards.
2014The latest major revision introduced computer-based testing and updated content to reflect Common Core State Standards and career readiness skills.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals seeking to earn their high school equivalency, making it accessible regardless of prior academic background.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

The course fee covers comprehensive preparation materials, but an exam voucher for the official GED test is not included.

How long does it take to complete the GED preparation course?

This is a self-paced program, allowing you to study at your own speed. Your individual timeline will depend on your prior knowledge and study habits, but the comprehensive content ensures thorough preparation.

Which GED exam version does this course prepare me for?

This course is designed to prepare you for the current official GED test, covering all four subject areas as administered by the GED Testing Service.

What kind of jobs can I get after earning my GED?

Earning your GED opens doors to a wide range of entry-level positions that require a high school diploma, such as administrative assistant, customer service representative, retail sales associate, and many manufacturing roles, as well as providing a foundation for further education or vocational training.
