What is this field?

The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification validates advanced proficiency in key Microsoft Office applications like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access, and Outlook. It's a globally recognized credential that signals to employers you possess the in-depth skills needed to maximize productivity and efficiency in a modern workplace.

Think of it like a master craftsman's seal of approval for digital tools. Just as a skilled carpenter expertly uses various tools to build a house, an MOS Expert proficiently wields the Office suite to construct reports, analyze data, manage projects, and communicate effectively, making them an indispensable asset to any team.

How the field grew

1990Microsoft Office 1.0 is first released, bundling popular productivity applications.
1997The Microsoft Office User Specialist (MOUS) program is launched, offering the first official certifications for Office proficiency.
2003The MOUS program is rebranded as Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS), expanding its global recognition and scope.
OngoingThe MOS program continuously evolves, offering certifications aligned with the latest versions of Microsoft Office to ensure relevance in a dynamic professional landscape.
