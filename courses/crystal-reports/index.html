What is this field?

Crystal Reports is a powerful business intelligence application used to design, generate, and analyze complex reports from various data sources. It empowers professionals to transform raw data into meaningful insights, visualize trends, and make informed business decisions. This program teaches you to master this essential tool, along with custom SQL statements, to create dynamic and shareable reports.

Think of it like this: if your business data is a vast, unorganized library of information, Crystal Reports is the expert librarian who can quickly find, categorize, and present exactly the books (data) you need in a beautifully organized, easy-to-read summary (report). You'll learn to ask the right questions (SQL) and display the answers clearly.

How the field grew

1991First version released by Crystal Services Inc., quickly becoming a leading reporting tool.
1994Acquired by Seagate Software, expanding its market reach and integration capabilities.
2007Became part of SAP's business intelligence portfolio through the acquisition of Business Objects.
PresentContinues to be a robust and widely utilized solution for data reporting and analysis across various industries.
