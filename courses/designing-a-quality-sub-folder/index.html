What is this field?

A quality substitute teacher folder is your blueprint for ensuring continuity in the classroom when you're away. It's a meticulously organized collection of vital information, emergency contacts, class schedules, student needs, and lesson plans that empowers any substitute to step in confidently and maintain an effective learning environment.

Think of it like a pilot's pre-flight checklist and flight plan. Just as a pilot wouldn't take off without every detail accounted for, a prepared teacher provides a comprehensive guide for their substitute. This course equips you with the strategies and tools, including a critical checklist, to construct such a folder, minimizing disruption and maximizing learning for your students.
