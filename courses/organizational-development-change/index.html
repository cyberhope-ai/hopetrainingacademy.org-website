What is this field?

Organizational Development & Change (OD&C) is a strategic approach focused on improving an organization's effectiveness and health through planned interventions. It involves analyzing and enhancing corporate culture, structure, and processes to enable businesses to adapt, innovate, and thrive in dynamic environments. OD&C professionals work to align an organization's people, processes, and strategy to achieve its goals.

Think of an organization like a garden. Organizational Development is like a skilled gardener who understands the soil (culture), the plants (people), and the ecosystem (processes). They don't just pull weeds; they nurture growth, prune for health, and adapt the garden to changing seasons (market shifts, new technology) so it can flourish and produce its best yield. This program equips you with the tools to be that skilled gardener for any business.

How the field grew

1930s-40sKurt Lewin's work on group dynamics and action research lays foundational concepts for change management.
1950s-60sEmergence of T-groups (sensitivity training) and survey feedback formalizes Organizational Development as a distinct field.
1970s-80sIncreased focus on strategic change, organizational culture, and fostering employee involvement in transformation.
1990s-2000sGlobalization, rapid technological advancements, and market shifts accelerate the demand for agile OD practices.
2010s-PresentDigital transformation, remote work, diversity & inclusion, and continuous learning drive modern OD strategies.
