What is this field?

Stock analysis and valuation is the process of determining a company's true worth and whether its stock is a good investment. It involves digging into financial statements, industry trends, and economic indicators to make educated predictions about a stock's future performance. This skill is crucial for anyone looking to build wealth through the stock market or pursue a career in finance.

Think of it like buying a house: you wouldn't just pay the asking price without looking at comparable sales, inspecting the property, and understanding the neighborhood. Similarly, stock analysis and valuation provides the tools to 'inspect' a company, 'compare' it to its peers, and 'understand' its market, ensuring you make smart investment decisions rather than just guessing.

How the field grew

1934The Securities Exchange Act established the SEC, bringing regulation to financial markets.
1949Benjamin Graham and David Dodd published 'Security Analysis,' foundational for value investing.
1970sModern Portfolio Theory gained prominence, emphasizing diversification and risk management.
1990sThe rise of the internet democratized access to financial data, empowering individual investors.
2008The global financial crisis highlighted the critical need for rigorous risk assessment and valuation.
