What is this field?

Manual machining involves precisely shaping metal parts using traditional hand-operated machines like lathes, mills, and grinders. It's a foundational skill for manufacturing, producing everything from aerospace components to medical devices. This program equips you with the precision and expertise needed to operate these machines, understand materials, and ensure quality.

Our program elevates this traditional craft with cutting-edge virtual reality (VR) training. Imagine stepping into a virtual workshop, practicing complex machine setups, and performing intricate operations without the need for physical equipment. This immersive experience, complete with a Meta Quest 3 headset, allows you to build confidence and muscle memory in a safe, simulated environment, preparing you for real-world production floors.

How the field grew

1770sJames Watt's steam engine drives early advancements in machining, requiring more precise metal parts.
1800sHenry Maudslay invents the first industrial screw-cutting lathe, standardizing screw threads and enabling mass production.
1950sIntroduction of Computer Numerical Control (CNC) revolutionizes machining, automating complex operations.
2020sVirtual Reality (VR) emerges as a powerful tool for hands-on technical training, offering immersive and safe learning environments.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Machining & Safety

Production floor safety, basic math, materials, and workholding principles.

02
Grinding Processes & Operations

Grinding theory, safety, surface, cylindrical, and centerless grinder setup and operation.

CNC Introduction & Programming

Basics of CNC lathes and mills, G-code programming, CAD/CAM introduction, and calculations.

04
Advanced CNC Controls

Detailed control panel functions, offsets, program execution, and storage for Fanuc, Haas, and Mazak systems.

05
Precision Inspection & Quality

Basic measurement, calibration, blueprint reading, GD&T, and advanced inspection methods.

06
Lean Manufacturing & Continuous Improvement

Overview of Lean, Six Sigma, 5S, Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), and troubleshooting.

07
Fasteners & Electrical Safety

Understanding fastener types, threads, torque, selection, and safety protocols for electrical work.
