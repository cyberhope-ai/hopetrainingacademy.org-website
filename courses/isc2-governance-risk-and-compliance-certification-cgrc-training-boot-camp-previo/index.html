What is this field?

The ISC2 Certified in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (CGRC) program equips professionals with the critical skills to manage information system security and compliance. This program focuses on the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Risk Management Framework (RMF), a structured approach for integrating security and risk management activities into the system development life cycle. You'll learn to assess, authorize, and continuously monitor information systems to meet legal, regulatory, and organizational security requirements.

Think of the CGRC as becoming a skilled architect and inspector for digital fortresses. You're not just building walls (implementing security controls); you're also designing the blueprints (categorizing systems, selecting controls), ensuring they meet strict building codes (compliance), getting official approval for occupancy (authorization), and continuously checking for weaknesses or new threats after the fortress is operational (continuous monitoring). This ensures the digital assets within are protected and compliant with all necessary regulations.

How the field grew

2002The Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA) established requirements for federal agencies to develop, document, and implement information security programs.
2010NIST Special Publication 800-37, Revision 1, formally introduced the Risk Management Framework (RMF), providing a comprehensive approach for managing security and privacy risk.
2014(ISC)² launched the Certified Authorization Professional (CAP) certification, aligning with the RMF to validate expertise in information system authorization.
2018NIST released SP 800-37, Revision 2, updating the RMF to integrate privacy risk management and improve cybersecurity practices.
2024The (ISC)² CAP certification was rebranded as the Certified in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (CGRC), reflecting an expanded scope and continued relevance in the evolving GRC landscape.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Risk Management Framework (RMF) Overview

Understanding the RMF process, categorization, selection, implementation, assessment, authorization, and monitoring.

02
Categorize Information Systems

Defining system boundaries, security categorization, and system security plan development.

03
Implement Security Controls

Tailoring and documenting selected security controls for various information systems.

04
Assess Security Controls

Preparing for assessments, establishing assessment plans, determining control effectiveness, and reporting findings.

05
Authorize Information Systems

Developing Plans of Action and Milestones (POAMs), assembling authorization packages, and obtaining authorization decisions.

06
Monitor Security State

Performing ongoing assessments, managing changes, conducting remediation, and continuous reporting.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program or get the CGRC certification?

While a degree can be beneficial, it is not strictly required for enrollment. (ISC)² typically requires relevant professional experience for full certification, which can often substitute for a degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CGRC certification?

We do not include an exam voucher with this program. You will need to purchase your exam voucher directly from (ISC)² when you are ready to take the certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the CGRC training program?

This is an intensive boot camp format, typically delivered over three days, designed to quickly prepare you for the certification exam. The self-paced study materials allow for flexible learning beyond the live sessions.

Which version of the CGRC exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is meticulously designed to prepare you for the current (ISC)² CGRC certification exam, covering the latest body of knowledge and best practices.

What kind of job can I get after earning the CGRC certification?

The CGRC certification opens doors to roles such as Information Security Analyst, Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Specialist, Authorization Professional, and Cybersecurity Consultant, particularly in government and contractor environments.
