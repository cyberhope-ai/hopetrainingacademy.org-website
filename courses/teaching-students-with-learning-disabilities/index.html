What is this field?

Learning disabilities are neurological conditions that affect how individuals receive, process, or express information, impacting areas like reading, writing, or math. These are not indicators of intelligence, but rather reflect different ways the brain works. Common examples include dyslexia, dyscalculia, and dysgraphia, each presenting unique challenges in a learning environment.

For educators, understanding these diverse learning profiles is crucial to creating an equitable and effective classroom. This training program equips you with the knowledge and strategies to identify learning differences, adapt your teaching methods, and foster an inclusive environment where every student can thrive. Think of it like learning to navigate a city with a map that's slightly different from everyone else's; a teacher helps provide a personalized guide.

How the field grew

1975The Education for All Handicapped Children Act (EAHCA) was passed, guaranteeing a free appropriate public education for children with disabilities.
1990EAHCA was reauthorized and renamed as the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), emphasizing person-first language and expanding services.
2004IDEA was reauthorized again, aligning with the No Child Left Behind Act and focusing on highly qualified special education teachers and early intervention.

What the career pays

Special education teachers adapt general education lessons and teach various subjects to students with mild to severe learning disabilities. They develop individualized education programs (IEPs) and collaborate with parents, other teachers, and support staff to foster student growth and independence.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to take this course?

No, this course is designed for professional development and does not require a degree to enroll. However, a bachelor's degree and state licensure are typically required to become a certified special education teacher.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this course is for professional development and focuses on practical skills and knowledge; it does not lead to an external certification exam, so no voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this online course?

This is a self-paced online course, allowing you to learn at your own speed and schedule. You can progress through the material at a pace that fits your lifestyle.

Is there a certification exam associated with this course?

No, this program focuses on enhancing your practical skills and knowledge for supporting students with learning disabilities and does not culminate in a certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after taking this course?

This course enhances the skills of current or aspiring educators, preparing them to better support students with learning disabilities in roles such as general education teachers, special education assistants, or as a foundation for pursuing a special education teaching career.
