What is this field?

Medical laboratory professionals are the unsung heroes of healthcare, performing critical tests on patient samples that provide essential data for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment. They work behind the scenes, ensuring doctors have the precise information needed to make life-saving decisions and monitor patient health.

This introductory program equips you with the fundamental knowledge and skills required to understand the vital operations within a medical laboratory. Think of it like learning the alphabet before you write a novel; this program teaches you the essential language and practices of the medical lab, preparing you for further specialization or entry-level support roles.

How the field grew

1920Early foundations of clinical pathology and laboratory science established.
1960Automation begins to transform laboratory processes, increasing efficiency.
1980Molecular diagnostics emerge, significantly expanding testing capabilities.
2000Digital advancements enhance lab efficiency and data management systems.
2020Heightened global health awareness increases demand for skilled lab professionals.
