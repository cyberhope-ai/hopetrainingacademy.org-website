What is this field?

Medical billing and coding specialists are the unsung heroes of the healthcare system, translating complex medical procedures and diagnoses into standardized codes used for insurance claims and data analysis. They ensure healthcare providers are accurately reimbursed for their services, playing a critical role in the financial health of clinics, hospitals, and other medical facilities.

Think of them as the financial translators between doctors and insurance companies. Just as a librarian organizes books with a precise cataloging system, medical billers and coders organize patient care information into a universal language of codes, ensuring every service rendered is properly documented and processed for payment.

How the field grew

1970sIntroduction of Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes to standardize medical procedures.
1996Passage of HIPAA, establishing national standards for protecting patient health information and impacting billing practices.
2000sWidespread adoption of Electronic Health Records (EHR) systems, digitizing medical records and coding processes.
2015Mandatory transition to ICD-10-CM/PCS coding systems, significantly expanding diagnostic and procedural codes.

What the career pays

Medical billers and coders are responsible for accurately assigning codes to patient diagnoses and procedures, then submitting these claims to insurance companies for reimbursement. They ensure compliance with healthcare regulations, maintain patient data confidentiality, and manage the revenue cycle from start to finish.
