What is this field?

Machine learning is a powerful branch of artificial intelligence that enables computer systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. It involves developing algorithms that can identify patterns, make predictions, and adapt their behavior based on the information they process, transforming raw data into actionable insights.

Think of it like teaching a child to recognize different types of fruit. Instead of giving them a strict set of rules (e.g., 'an apple is red, round, and has a stem'), you show them many examples of apples, oranges, and bananas. Over time, the child learns to distinguish between them on their own. Machine learning algorithms work similarly, learning from vast datasets to perform tasks like image recognition, language translation, or predicting market trends.

How the field grew

1950Alan Turing proposes the Turing Test, a measure of machine intelligence.
1959Arthur Samuel coins the term 'Machine Learning' while developing a checker-playing program.
1980sThe backpropagation algorithm is popularized, enabling neural networks to learn complex patterns.
1990sRise of decision trees, support vector machines, and ensemble methods for data analysis.
2000s-PresentDeep Learning revolutionizes AI with advancements in neural networks and big data processing.
