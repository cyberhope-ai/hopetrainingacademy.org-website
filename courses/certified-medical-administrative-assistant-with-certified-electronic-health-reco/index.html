What is this field?

A Certified Medical Administrative Assistant (CMAA) and Electronic Health Records Specialist (CEHRS) is the organizational backbone of any healthcare facility. These vital professionals manage the administrative duties that keep a medical office running smoothly, from patient scheduling and communication to handling sensitive medical records and billing. They ensure that patient information is accurate, accessible, and secure, playing a critical role in both patient care coordination and operational efficiency.

Think of a CMAA and CEHRS as the central nervous system of a medical practice. Just as the nervous system coordinates all bodily functions, these specialists coordinate the flow of information, appointments, and administrative tasks, ensuring that doctors, nurses, and patients can interact seamlessly. They translate complex medical terminology, manage digital health records, and uphold ethical standards, making them indispensable to modern healthcare.

How the field grew

1989National Healthcareer Association (NHA) established, setting standards for allied health professionals.
1996HIPAA enacted, emphasizing patient privacy and secure health information management.
2009HITECH Act spurred widespread adoption of Electronic Health Records (EHR) systems in healthcare.
PresentOngoing evolution of digital health technologies and administrative best practices in medical offices.
