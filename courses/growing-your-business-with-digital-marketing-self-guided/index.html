What is this field?

Digital marketing encompasses all marketing efforts that use an electronic device or the internet. Businesses leverage digital channels such as search engines, social media, email, and other websites to connect with current and prospective customers. It's about reaching your audience where they spend their time – online – and engaging them effectively to drive business outcomes.

Think of digital marketing like building a modern storefront for your business in a bustling online city. Instead of just a physical sign, you're using targeted ads on digital billboards (SEM), word-of-mouth through online communities (social media), personalized flyers delivered to inboxes (email marketing), and ensuring your store appears prominently on the city map when people search for what you offer (SEO). It's a dynamic way to attract, engage, and convert customers wherever they are online.

How the field grew

1994The first clickable banner ad appears on HotWired.com, marking an early form of digital advertising.
1998Google is founded, rapidly becoming the dominant search engine and laying the groundwork for modern SEO and SEM.
2000Google AdWords (now Google Ads) launches, allowing businesses to bid on keywords and display ads on search results pages.
2004Facebook launches, ushering in the era of social media marketing and direct consumer engagement.
2007The first iPhone is released, accelerating the shift towards mobile-first digital marketing strategies.
