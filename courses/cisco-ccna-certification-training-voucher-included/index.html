What is this field?

The Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification is a globally recognized credential that validates your foundational knowledge and skills in networking. It demonstrates your ability to install, configure, operate, and troubleshoot medium-sized routed and switched networks. This training program prepares you to manage crucial network infrastructure, ensuring smooth daily operations for businesses worldwide.

Think of a network administrator as the architect and caretaker of a city's road system. Just as an architect designs the roads, bridges, and traffic lights, a CCNA professional designs and implements network infrastructure. And like a caretaker who ensures traffic flows smoothly, troubleshoots congestion, and maintains safety, a CCNA-certified individual monitors network performance, resolves connectivity issues, and secures data pathways. This certification proves you have the essential skills to keep digital traffic moving efficiently and securely.

How the field grew

1998Cisco introduces the first CCNA certification, establishing a benchmark for entry-level networking professionals.
2000The CCNA curriculum begins to evolve, incorporating new technologies and expanding its scope to cover broader networking concepts.
2013A significant update to the CCNA program is released, aligning the certification with modern networking paradigms and emerging technologies.
2020Cisco consolidates multiple specialized CCNA tracks into a single, comprehensive certification, emphasizing core enterprise networking skills.
