What is this field?

Brand strategists are the architects behind a company's identity and market presence. They develop comprehensive plans to define, position, and communicate a brand's unique value, ensuring it resonates with target audiences and stands out in a competitive landscape.

Think of a brand strategist as a master storyteller and a master builder rolled into one. They don't just create a logo; they craft the entire narrative, personality, and emotional connection a brand has with its customers, much like an architect designs a building's blueprint, ensuring every detail contributes to its overall purpose and appeal.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyEmergence of advertising and the concept of distinct product identities.
Mid-20th CenturyFormalization of brand management as a strategic business function.
Late 20th CenturyIncreased focus on brand equity, consumer loyalty, and global brand expansion.
Early 21st CenturyDigital transformation drives the need for sophisticated online brand strategies and engagement.
