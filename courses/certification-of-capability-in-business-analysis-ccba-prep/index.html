What is this field?

Business analysis is the critical practice of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. Think of a business analyst as the bridge between an organization's goals and the technical solutions that achieve them. They translate complex business requirements into actionable plans, ensuring projects deliver real value and meet strategic objectives.

The Certification of Capability in Business Analysis (CCBA) is a highly respected credential for mid-level business analysts with 2-3 years of experience. This program is designed to deepen your understanding of the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide) v3.0, refine your skill set, and prepare you thoroughly for the IIBA CCBA exam, positioning you for significant career growth and new opportunities.

How the field grew

2003International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) founded to establish standards for the profession.
2005First version of the BABOK® Guide published, providing a foundational framework for business analysis.
2013IIBA introduces the CCBA certification, recognizing the capabilities of experienced business analysis professionals.
2015BABOK® Guide v3.0 released, expanding on key concepts and techniques in business analysis.
PresentBusiness analysis recognized as a critical role in driving digital transformation and organizational efficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

While a bachelor's degree is often preferred for business analysis roles, this program specifically targets mid-level professionals with 2-3 years of business analysis experience, focusing on preparing you for the CCBA exam rather than entry-level qualifications.

Is the CCBA exam voucher included with this program?

The CCBA exam voucher is not included with this program purchase. You will need to acquire your exam voucher separately through the IIBA.

How long will it take to complete this program?

Which CCBA exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program is fully aligned with the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide) v3.0 and prepares you for the current IIBA Certification of Capability in Business Analysis (CCBA) certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after earning my CCBA?

Earning your CCBA credential can lead to advanced roles such as Business Analyst, Systems Analyst, Process Improvement Specialist, or Requirements Engineer, demonstrating your proven capability in business analysis practices.
