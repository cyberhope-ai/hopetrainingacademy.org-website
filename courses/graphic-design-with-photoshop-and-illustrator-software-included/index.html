What is this field?

Graphic design is the art of visual communication—using images, text, and ideas to convey messages that inspire, inform, or captivate. Think of it as visual storytelling, where designers translate complex concepts into engaging visuals for brands, marketing, and digital experiences.

Our program equips you with the foundational principles of design, including color theory, typography, and composition, while building proficiency in Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. You'll learn to craft everything from logos and marketing materials to web graphics, preparing you for a dynamic career in a field constantly evolving with new technologies like generative AI.

How the field grew

1980sRise of desktop publishing and early graphic software.
1990sAdobe Photoshop and Illustrator become industry standards.
2000sWeb design and digital media expand graphic design's scope.
2010sMobile design and social media drive demand for visual content.
2020sGenerative AI emerges, transforming creative workflows and possibilities.

What the career pays

Graphic designers create visual concepts using computer software or by hand to communicate ideas that inspire, inform, or captivate consumers. They develop the overall layout and production design for advertisements, brochures, magazines, and corporate reports.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a graphic designer?

While some positions may prefer a degree, many successful graphic designers enter the field with strong portfolios and practical skills. This program focuses on building those industry-ready skills and a professional portfolio, not on earning a degree.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, this program does not include an exam voucher. It focuses on skill development and portfolio creation rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced program designed for flexibility, totaling 165 course hours. The actual time to complete will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to build comprehensive graphic design skills using Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator and to help you create a professional portfolio. It does not specifically prepare you for a particular certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Graphic Designer, Visual Designer, Marketing Designer, Production Artist, and other positions requiring strong visual communication and software proficiency.
