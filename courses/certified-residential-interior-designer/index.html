What is this field?

A Certified Residential Interior Designer specializes in creating functional, safe, and aesthetically pleasing interior spaces for homes. This program equips you with the expertise to understand client needs, apply design principles, select materials, and manage projects from concept to completion, culminating in the R.I.D.E. credential and preparation for the Residential Interior Design Qualifying Certification (RIDQC) exam.

Think of a residential interior designer as the architect of a home's inner world, orchestrating color, light, and texture to create a harmonious and personalized environment. Just as a chef blends ingredients for a perfect meal, you'll learn to combine design elements to craft spaces that reflect your clients' unique styles and enhance their daily lives.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll in this program?

No, this program does not require a prior degree. It provides comprehensive training to prepare you for the R.I.D.E. credential and RIDQC exam.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher for the Residential Interior Design Qualifying Certification (RIDQC) exam is not explicitly included with this program purchase. You would need to acquire it separately.

How long does it take to complete this program?

The program is self-paced and designed for completion in approximately 160 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the Residential Interior Design Qualifying Certification (RIDQC) exam through the Designer Society of America (DSA), which is the current industry-recognized certification for residential interior design.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Residential Interior Designer, Interior Design Consultant, Home Stager, or a specialized Kitchen & Bath Designer.
