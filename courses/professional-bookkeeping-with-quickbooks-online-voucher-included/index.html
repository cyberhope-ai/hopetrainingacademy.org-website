What is this field?

Bookkeeping is the systematic recording of financial transactions for a business, providing a clear picture of its financial health. It's the foundation of all accounting, ensuring that every dollar in and out is accurately tracked. Mastering these principles, especially with modern tools like QuickBooks Online, is crucial for any organization, from small businesses to large enterprises.

Think of QuickBooks Online as your business's financial GPS. Just as a GPS helps you navigate complex routes and avoid wrong turns, QuickBooks Online helps you navigate your business's finances, ensuring every transaction is recorded correctly, every bill is paid on time, and you always know exactly where your money is going. It transforms what could be a confusing maze of numbers into a clear, manageable financial roadmap.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyRise of mechanical accounting machines for efficient record-keeping.
1983Intuit introduces Quicken, leading to the development of QuickBooks for small businesses.
2001QuickBooks Online is launched, bringing cloud-based accounting to the forefront.
PresentQuickBooks Online becomes a dominant platform, continually evolving with new features and integrations.
