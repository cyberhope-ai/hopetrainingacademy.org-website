What is this field?

The CompTIA Cybersecurity Analyst (CySA+) certification validates your ability to detect, analyze, and respond to cybersecurity incidents. This program equips you with the intermediate-level skills needed to protect organizations from evolving cyber threats, focusing on practical, hands-on application of security principles.

Think of a Cybersecurity Analyst as a digital detective and first responder. When a system shows signs of a breach or vulnerability, you're the one who investigates the clues, identifies the intruder or weakness, and then helps to contain the damage and prevent future attacks, much like a security guard who also analyzes surveillance footage and reinforces weak points.

How the field grew

Early 2000sRapid increase in cyber threats drives demand for specialized security analysis roles.
2017CompTIA introduces the CySA+ certification to validate intermediate-level cybersecurity analyst skills.
OngoingContinuous evolution of cyber warfare necessitates advanced threat detection and incident response capabilities.

Incident Response Specialist

Investigates and mitigates security breaches, restoring systems to normal operation.

Threat Intelligence Analyst

Researches and analyzes threat data to anticipate and prevent future attacks.
