What is this field?

Mindfulness meditation is a practice that involves focusing your attention on the present moment, observing your thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations without judgment. It's about being fully aware of 'what is' happening right now, whether it's the sensation of your breath, the sounds around you, or the thoughts passing through your mind. This practice helps cultivate a sense of calm, clarity, and perspective.

Think of it like training a muscle for your mind. Just as you might lift weights to strengthen your body, mindfulness meditation trains your attention and awareness. By regularly practicing, you learn to step back from the constant stream of thoughts and emotions, allowing you to respond more thoughtfully to life's challenges rather than reacting impulsively. It's a skill that builds resilience and promotes overall well-being.

How the field grew

1979Jon Kabat-Zinn establishes the Stress Reduction Clinic at the University of Massachusetts Medical Center, developing Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR).
1990sGrowing scientific research begins to validate the health benefits of mindfulness, leading to increased academic interest and clinical applications.
2000sMindfulness practices gain mainstream recognition, integrating into healthcare, education, and corporate wellness programs worldwide.
