What is this field?

Bookkeeping is the foundational practice of recording financial transactions for a business. It involves meticulously tracking income, expenses, assets, and liabilities to provide a clear picture of a company's financial health. This program equips you with the essential skills to manage these critical tasks, focusing on the industry-leading QuickBooks Online platform to streamline financial operations.

Think of a bookkeeper as the meticulous navigator of a ship's log. Just as a navigator records every journey, cargo, and expense to ensure the ship stays on course and its resources are managed, a bookkeeper records every financial transaction for a business. This ensures the business owner knows exactly where their money is coming from and going, helping them make informed decisions and stay financially sound.

How the field grew

AncientEarly forms of record-keeping emerged in ancient civilizations to track trade and resources.
1494Luca Pacioli publishes 'Summa de arithmetica,' detailing the principles of double-entry bookkeeping.
1980sThe advent of personal computers begins to revolutionize accounting, moving from manual ledgers to digital systems.
1990sQuickBooks is introduced, making professional-grade financial management accessible to small businesses.
2000sCloud computing ushers in QuickBooks Online, enabling anytime, anywhere financial management.
