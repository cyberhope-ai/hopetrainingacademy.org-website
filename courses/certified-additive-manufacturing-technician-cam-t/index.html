What is this field?

Additive manufacturing, often known as 3D printing, is a revolutionary process that builds three-dimensional objects layer by layer from a digital design. Unlike traditional subtractive manufacturing, which carves material away, additive manufacturing precisely adds material, leading to less waste, greater design flexibility, and the ability to create complex geometries previously impossible. This technology is transforming industries from aerospace and healthcare to consumer goods.

Our Certified Additive Manufacturing - Technician (CAM-T) training program equips you with the fundamental knowledge and practical skills to excel in this innovative sector. You'll learn about various 3D printing technologies, material science, digital file management, and critical safety protocols, preparing you to operate and manage additive manufacturing processes effectively and contribute to cutting-edge production.

How the field grew

1980Early patents for stereolithography (SLA) and fused deposition modeling (FDM) laid the groundwork for 3D printing.
1990Commercial 3D printing systems became available, primarily for rapid prototyping in industrial settings.
2000The RepRap project and open-source initiatives began democratizing 3D printing technology.
2010Significant advancements in metal additive manufacturing and increased industrial adoption across various sectors.
2020Continued growth in material science, process optimization, and integration into mainstream production workflows.
