What is this field?

A paralegal is a vital legal professional who supports attorneys by performing a wide range of tasks, from conducting in-depth legal research and drafting critical documents to managing cases and interviewing clients. They work in various settings, including traditional law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, and public interest organizations, contributing significantly to the efficiency and effectiveness of legal services.

Think of a paralegal as the highly skilled navigator and co-pilot for a legal team. While the attorney sets the destination and steers the ship, the paralegal meticulously charts the course, gathers crucial supplies (information), and ensures all preparations are in order, making sure the journey through the legal system is smooth and successful.

How the field grew

1960sThe paralegal profession begins to emerge in the United States as legal services expand and demand for cost-effective support grows.
1975The National Association of Legal Assistants (NALA) is founded, establishing a professional organization for paralegals.
1976NALA introduces the Certified Paralegal (CP) examination, setting a benchmark for professional competence and ethics in the field.
2000s-PresentThe paralegal role continues to evolve, with increased emphasis on formal education, specialized skills, and technological proficiency in the legal industry.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Certified Paralegal?

While this program provides comprehensive training, eligibility for the NALA Certified Paralegal (CP) exam typically requires a combination of education and/or experience. This 225-hour program (equivalent to 15 semester hours) can be combined with other college credits or work experience to meet NALA's specific eligibility criteria, such as those outlined in Category 1(e) or Category 2 of their requirements.

Is the exam voucher for the NALA CP exam included with this program?

No, an exam voucher for the NALA Certified Paralegal (CP) exam is not included with this program purchase. You will need to register and pay for the exam separately through NALA once you meet their eligibility requirements.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Paralegal training program?

This program is self-paced and comprises 225 course hours. The actual time to complete it will depend on your individual study schedule and commitment, but it is designed to be flexible to fit your life.

Which Certified Paralegal exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Certified Paralegal (CP) certification exam offered by the National Association of Legal Assistants (NALA). Upon completion, you will also receive NALA membership and access to their CP Exam Review courses.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completing this program, you will be prepared for various paralegal and legal assistant roles across diverse settings. This includes positions in private law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, and public interest organizations, supporting attorneys in areas like litigation, real estate, family law, and more.
