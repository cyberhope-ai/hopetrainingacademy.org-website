What is this field?

The Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) credential is a globally recognized certification for experienced information security professionals. It validates your expertise in managing, designing, overseeing, and assessing an enterprise's information security program. Earning your CISM demonstrates your capability to bridge the gap between business objectives and information security, ensuring that an organization's data and systems are protected effectively.

Think of a CISM-certified professional as the architect and superintendent of a building's security system. They don't just install locks; they design the entire security blueprint, manage the security team, assess potential threats (like structural weaknesses or external dangers), and ensure all security measures align with the building's purpose and regulatory requirements. They are responsible for the overall resilience and safety of the entire structure, not just individual components.

How the field grew

1967ISACA (Information Systems Audit and Control Association) founded.
1978ISACA launches its first certification, the CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor).
2002ISACA introduces the CISM (Certified Information Security Manager) certification to address the growing need for security management expertise.
2006CISM certification achieves ANSI accreditation, enhancing its global recognition and credibility.
2017CISM job practice areas are updated to reflect the evolving landscape of information security management.

No, a college degree is not a prerequisite for this training program. The CISM certification primarily requires significant professional experience in information security management, which this program helps you prepare for.

An exam voucher for the ISACA CISM certification is not included with this specific program offering. You will need to purchase your exam voucher separately.

How long does it take to complete the CISM training program?

The core instruction for this CISM training program consists of 40 intensive course hours, typically delivered over five days. Additional self-study and practice are recommended to fully prepare for the exam.

Which version of the CISM exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current ISACA CISM certification exam, aligning with the latest job practice areas and industry standards.

What kind of job can I get after earning my CISM certification?

With a CISM certification, you can pursue roles such as Information Security Manager, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Security Consultant, or IT Director with a focus on security, leading and managing an organization's security posture.
