What is this field?

Mediation and arbitration are powerful methods for resolving disagreements outside of traditional court systems. Mediators act as neutral facilitators, guiding parties to find common ground and reach mutually acceptable solutions. Arbitrators, on the other hand, hear evidence and testimony from all sides, then render a binding decision, much like a judge.

This Hope Training Academy program equips you with the structured processes and interpersonal skills needed to excel in these roles. Think of it like becoming a highly skilled, impartial referee for complex disagreements, whether in workplaces, communities, or legal contexts, helping individuals and organizations navigate conflict constructively.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Mediation Fundamentals

Justice, Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), INACCORD model overview, phases, and stages.

02
Applied Mediation Skills

Touchstone Skills (questioning, reflecting, reframing), Memorandum of Agreement, ethical considerations.

03
INACCORD Practice

Simulated demos, facilitating interventions, caucus techniques, ethical dilemmas and mitigation.

04
Arbitration Principles

Justice, ethics, INACCORD model for arbitration, preliminary conferences.

05
Arbitration Hearing & Award

Directing hearings, closing procedures, rendering impartial awards, international arbitration.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not a prerequisite for enrollment. This program focuses on practical skills and knowledge, requiring basic computer proficiency and a willingness to learn professional writing.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

The program culminates in four professional certificates awarded by Mediators Without Borders upon successful completion; no separate exam voucher is required or included.

How long does it take to complete the Mediation and Arbitration program?

This is a self-paced online program totaling 200 course hours. The actual completion time will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

What certifications will I earn upon completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will receive four individual certificates from Mediators Without Borders: Mediation, Applied Mediation Practice, Arbitration, and Professional Mediator and Arbitrator.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles as mediators, arbitrators, or conflict resolution specialists in various sectors, including state and local government, finance, insurance, and private practice.
