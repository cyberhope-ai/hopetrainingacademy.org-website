What is this field?

SMART Boards are interactive whiteboards that integrate computer technology with a large display surface, allowing educators to project and manipulate digital content directly on the board. They transform traditional whiteboards into dynamic learning tools, enabling teachers to engage students with multimedia, interactive activities, and collaborative exercises. This technology moves beyond passive viewing, fostering an environment where students can actively participate in lessons.

Think of a SMART Board as a giant tablet for your classroom, but one that's designed specifically for teaching and collaboration. Instead of just writing on a whiteboard or projecting a static image, you can touch, drag, write, and interact with digital content, web pages, and educational software. It's like bringing the digital world directly into your lesson, making abstract concepts tangible and fostering a more engaging, hands-on learning experience for every student.

How the field grew
