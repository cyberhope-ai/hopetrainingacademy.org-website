What is this field?

A Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) is a fitness professional trained to identify and correct muscular imbalances and faulty movement patterns that can lead to pain, injury, or hinder performance. Think of them as a 'movement detective' who can pinpoint why someone might be struggling with a squat or experiencing discomfort during a run, then prescribe specific exercises to fix the underlying issues.

This specialization empowers you to work with clients across all fitness levels, from athletes seeking performance enhancement to individuals recovering from injury or managing chronic pain. You'll learn scientifically-backed methods to assess movement, apply inhibitory techniques to overactive muscles, activate underactive ones, and integrate these improvements into functional movement, helping people move better and live healthier, more active lives.

How the field grew

1987The National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) is founded, pioneering evidence-based fitness education.
Early 1990sNASM establishes its Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) program, setting industry standards for fitness professionals.
Early 2000sRecognizing the growing need for specialized injury prevention and rehabilitation support, NASM develops advanced specializations like the Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES).
PresentNASM continues to evolve its programs, integrating the latest scientific research into practical applications for fitness and health professionals globally.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not strictly required. To pursue the NASM Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) certification, you must hold a current NASM-CPT certification, another NCCA/NBFE/DETC-accredited health and fitness certification, a REPs Level 3 or higher credential (outside the US), a 4-year collegiate degree, or be a current Licensed or Certified Massage Therapist.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher for the NASM Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) certification exam is not included with this program. The instructional materials, including a digital textbook and practice exams, are provided to help you prepare.

How long does it take to complete the Corrective Exercise Specialist program?

This is a self-paced program, offering flexibility to learn at your own speed. You will have six months of access to complete the 60 course hours. Extensions may be available for a fee if needed.

Does this program prepare me for the current certification exam?

Yes, this program is designed to thoroughly prepare you for the current NASM Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) certification exam, equipping you with the knowledge and skills required to succeed.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completing this program, you can enhance your career as a personal trainer, athletic trainer, or sports professional. You'll be equipped to work as a Corrective Exercise Specialist, assisting clients in preventing injuries, improving movement, and supporting post-rehabilitation efforts.
