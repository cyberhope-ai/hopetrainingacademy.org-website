What is this field?

The AWS Cloud Practitioner Developer Associate program equips you with foundational knowledge of Amazon Web Services (AWS) and the practical skills to develop applications within its ecosystem. You'll learn about core AWS services, architectural principles, and best practices for cloud security and deployment, preparing you for two key industry certifications.

Think of it like learning to drive a car (Cloud Practitioner) and then becoming proficient at building and customizing specific components of that car (Developer Associate) within the AWS brand. You'll understand the rules of the road and gain the hands-on ability to construct and maintain cloud-based solutions.

How the field grew

2013AWS introduces its first certification exams, establishing a benchmark for cloud expertise.
2017AWS significantly expands its certification portfolio, including specialized associate-level paths for developers.
2019AWS refines its certification tracks to include foundational and specialty options, catering to diverse IT roles.
PresentAWS continuously updates its certification exams to reflect the latest cloud technologies and best practices, ensuring relevance.
