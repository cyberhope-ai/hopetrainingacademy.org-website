What is this field?

End of life care is a specialized approach focused on providing comfort and support to individuals in the final stages of a terminal illness, as well as their families. It encompasses physical, emotional, social, and spiritual support, aiming to improve the quality of life remaining and ensure dignity and peace during a profoundly challenging time.

This training program equips healthcare professionals and dedicated individuals with the knowledge and skills necessary to deliver compassionate end-of-life care. Think of it like learning to be a skilled guide for someone on a difficult journey, ensuring comfort, dignity, and peace every step of the way, while also supporting their loved ones through the process.

How the field grew

1960sModern hospice movement initiated by Dame Cicely Saunders in the UK.
1970sHospice care begins to emerge in the United States, emphasizing holistic support.
1980sMedicare coverage for hospice services is introduced, expanding access to care.
2000sPalliative care gains recognition as a distinct medical specialty, focusing on symptom relief at any stage of serious illness.

Understanding the philosophy, principles, and goals of hospice and palliative care.

Mastering difficult conversations, active listening, and providing empathetic emotional support.

Navigating advance directives, patient autonomy, and other critical ethical dilemmas.
