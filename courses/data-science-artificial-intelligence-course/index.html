What is this field?

Data Science is the art of extracting insights and knowledge from data, using scientific methods, processes, algorithms, and systems. It's like being a detective for information, uncovering hidden patterns and making predictions. Artificial Intelligence (AI) takes this a step further, enabling machines to learn from data, reason, and make decisions, much like a digital brain.

This comprehensive career training program equips you with the essential technical skills for entry-level data science and AI roles. You'll master Python programming, machine learning, data analysis, and SQL, gaining practical experience in building AI applications, cleaning and visualizing data, and developing models using industry-standard tools like Pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn.

How the field grew

1956The Dartmouth Workshop coins the term 'Artificial Intelligence', marking the birth of the field.
1990sEmergence of 'Data Mining' and early web analytics, laying groundwork for modern data science.
2006The term 'Data Scientist' gains traction, coinciding with the rise of 'Big Data' technologies like Hadoop.
2012Deep Learning breakthroughs, notably AlexNet, ignite a new era of AI capabilities.
2020sThe Generative AI revolution, driven by large language models, transforms human-computer interaction.
