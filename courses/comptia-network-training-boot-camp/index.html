What is this field?

The CompTIA Network+ certification is a globally recognized credential that validates foundational skills in managing, maintaining, troubleshooting, installing, and configuring basic network infrastructure. It's vendor-neutral, meaning the knowledge you gain applies to various network technologies and hardware, making you a versatile asset in any IT environment.

Think of a computer network like a city's transportation system. Just as traffic engineers design roads, bridges, and traffic lights to ensure smooth flow, a network professional designs, builds, and maintains the digital pathways (cables, Wi-Fi), devices (routers, switches), and rules (protocols) that allow data to travel efficiently and securely between computers and devices.
