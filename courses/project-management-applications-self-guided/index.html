What is this field?

Project Management Applications training focuses on the practical, real-world techniques and tools used to successfully plan, execute, and close projects. This isn't just about theory; it's about applying proven methodologies, leveraging software, and mastering the 'tricks of the trade' that experienced project managers use daily to navigate challenges and achieve project goals efficiently.

Think of it like learning to drive a car. You can read the manual (project management theory), but this training puts you behind the wheel, teaching you how to use the gears, navigate traffic, and handle unexpected turns (project applications). You'll gain hands-on experience with the processes and software that bring projects to life, ensuring you can deliver results on time and within budget.

How the field grew

1950sDevelopment of critical path methods (CPM) and Program Evaluation and Review Technique (PERT) for complex projects.
1969Founding of the Project Management Institute (PMI), standardizing project management knowledge.
1980sIntroduction and widespread adoption of personal computer-based project management software.
2001Publication of the Agile Manifesto, shifting focus to iterative development and flexibility.
2010sIntegration of cloud-based tools, data analytics, and AI into project management practices.
