What is this field?

Machine Learning is a powerful branch of artificial intelligence that empowers computer systems to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention. It's the technology behind personalized recommendations, fraud detection, and self-driving cars, constantly evolving to solve complex real-world problems and drive innovation across industries.

Think of it like teaching a child to recognize different animals. Instead of giving them a strict rulebook for each animal, you show them many pictures of cats, dogs, and birds. Over time, they learn to identify new animals on their own by recognizing common features. Machine learning algorithms work similarly, processing vast amounts of data to 'learn' and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.

How the field grew

1950Alan Turing proposes the Turing Test, a foundational concept for AI.
1959Arthur Samuel coins the term 'Machine Learning' while working on a checkers program.
1990sSignificant advancements in algorithms like decision trees and support vector machines.
2012Deep learning achieves breakthrough results in image recognition, fueling rapid progress.
