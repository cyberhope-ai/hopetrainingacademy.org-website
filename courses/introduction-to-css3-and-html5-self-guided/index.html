What is this field?

This program introduces you to HTML5 and CSS3, the foundational languages for building modern websites. You'll learn how to structure web content effectively with HTML5 and then bring it to life with stunning visual styles and layouts using CSS3. This is a crucial first step for anyone aspiring to a career in web development or looking to enhance their digital presence.

Think of HTML5 as the blueprint and structural framework of a house, defining where the walls, rooms, and windows go. CSS3, on the other hand, is the interior designer and decorator, choosing the paint colors, furniture styles, and overall aesthetic to make that house beautiful and functional. Together, they create a complete, user-friendly web experience.

How the field grew

1990Tim Berners-Lee invents HTML, laying the groundwork for the World Wide Web.
1996CSS Level 1 is published, separating document structure from presentation.
2000XHTML 1.0 becomes a W3C Recommendation, aiming for stricter XML-based HTML.
2014HTML5 is finalized, introducing new semantic elements and multimedia support.
2015CSS3 modules continue to evolve, enabling advanced styling and responsive design.
