What is this field?

This program empowers aspiring and experienced writers to master the craft of nonfiction book creation, from initial concept to final publication. You'll learn to develop compelling narratives, conduct thorough research, refine your manuscript through expert editing techniques, and navigate the complex world of publishing to share your unique voice and expertise with the world.

Think of it like building a custom home for your ideas. You'll learn to lay a solid foundation with strong research, frame the structure with a clear outline, furnish the interior with engaging prose, and finally, open the doors to the public through effective publishing and marketing strategies.

How the field grew

1450sGutenberg's printing press democratizes book production, laying groundwork for widespread nonfiction dissemination.
1700sThe Enlightenment era fuels the rise of modern nonfiction genres like essays, biographies, and scientific treatises.
1900sMass market publishing emerges, professionalizing authorship and expanding access to diverse nonfiction topics.
2000sThe digital revolution and self-publishing platforms transform the industry, offering new avenues for authors to reach readers directly.

What the career pays

Nonfiction authors research, write, and edit factual content to inform, educate, or persuade readers on a wide range of subjects. They are responsible for developing compelling narratives, ensuring accuracy, and guiding their work through the publishing process.

Frequently asked questions

No, a college degree is not a prerequisite. This program focuses on developing the practical writing, editing, and publishing skills essential for a successful author career.

This course focuses on skill development and publishing processes, not a certification exam, so an exam voucher is not applicable or included.

Graduates can pursue roles such as nonfiction author (self-published or traditionally published), content writer, editor, or ghostwriter, leveraging their expertise in factual storytelling.
