What is this field?

Six Sigma is a data-driven methodology used to eliminate defects and improve processes within any organization, from manufacturing to service industries. It provides a structured approach to problem-solving, focusing on reducing variation and enhancing quality to achieve near-perfect results.

Think of Six Sigma like a highly precise recipe for baking a perfect cake every single time. Instead of just guessing ingredients or oven temperatures, Six Sigma uses data and scientific methods to measure every step, identify what causes imperfections, and then systematically fix those issues to ensure consistent, high-quality outcomes.

How the field grew

1986Motorola engineers develop Six Sigma as a methodology to improve manufacturing quality and reduce defects.
1990sGeneral Electric widely adopts Six Sigma, popularizing its use across diverse industries and making it a benchmark for operational excellence.
Early 2000sSix Sigma evolves to integrate with Lean principles, creating Lean Six Sigma, and becomes a global standard for operational excellence and continuous improvement.
