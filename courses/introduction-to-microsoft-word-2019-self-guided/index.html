How the field grew

1983First version of Microsoft Word released
1989Word for Windows launched, significantly boosting its popularity
1995Word 95, part of Office 95, introduced tighter integration with other Office applications
2007Major redesign with the Ribbon interface and introduction of the .docx file format
2019Microsoft Word 2019 released, offering enhanced features and cloud integration

What the career pays

Professionals proficient in Microsoft Word create, edit, and manage a wide range of documents, ensuring clear and effective written communication. They are vital in administrative, clerical, and many other roles requiring organized and polished textual output.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Getting Started with Word 2019

Navigating the interface, creating new documents, saving and opening files efficiently.

02
Basic Text Entry and Editing

Typing, selecting, copying, pasting, undo/redo, and using find and replace functions.

03
Formatting Text and Paragraphs

Applying font styles, sizes, colors; setting alignment, line spacing, indents, bullets, and numbering.

04
Structuring Professional Documents

Utilizing styles, creating lists, inserting page breaks, and working with headers and footers.

05
Enhancing Documents with Visuals

Inserting and formatting tables, pictures, and shapes for better presentation.

06
Proofing and Finalizing Documents

Using spell check, grammar check, the thesaurus, and preparing documents for printing or sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this training?

No, this training focuses on practical skills for Microsoft Word 2019 and does not require a prior degree. It's designed for anyone looking to build essential office productivity skills.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This Introduction to Microsoft Word 2019 course focuses on skill development and does not include an exam voucher for a certification.

How long does it take to complete the training?

This is a self-paced online training program. While completion times vary, most students can finish the course content within a few weeks, dedicating a few hours per week.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This course provides foundational skills in Microsoft Word 2019. While these skills are essential for various roles, it is not specifically designed as preparation for a certification exam.

What kind of job can I get with Microsoft Word skills?

Proficiency in Microsoft Word is a core requirement for many administrative and office support roles, such as Administrative Assistant, Office Clerk, Executive Assistant, and Data Entry Specialist, among others.
