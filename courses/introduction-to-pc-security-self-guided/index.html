What is this field?

PC security refers to the practices and technologies used to protect personal computers and the data they store from unauthorized access, damage, or misuse. It encompasses safeguarding hardware, software, and data against various threats, including malware, phishing, and network vulnerabilities. Understanding PC security is the first line of defense in our increasingly connected world, ensuring your digital life remains private and intact.

Think of PC security like securing your home. Just as you lock your doors, install an alarm system, and protect your valuables, PC security involves setting up digital 'locks' (passwords), 'alarms' (antivirus software), and 'safes' (encryption) to keep your computer and information safe from intruders and digital 'thieves.' This foundational course teaches you how to implement these essential protective measures.

How the field grew

1970sEarly network vulnerabilities identified in ARPANET, marking the dawn of digital security concerns.
1980sThe emergence of the first computer viruses and worms, highlighting the need for basic PC protection.
1990sThe rise of the internet leads to widespread adoption of antivirus software and firewalls for personal computers.
2000sSophisticated malware, phishing attacks, and widespread data breaches necessitate more robust PC security strategies.
2010sFocus shifts to comprehensive endpoint protection, cloud security, and user education against evolving cyber threats.
