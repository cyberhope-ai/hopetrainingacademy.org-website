What is this field?

Perinatal care encompasses the comprehensive support provided to individuals and families during pregnancy, childbirth, and the postpartum period. This critical phase involves navigating significant physical, emotional, and social changes, making informed guidance and compassionate assistance invaluable. Professionals in this field address a wide range of needs, from managing health conditions to offering emotional support, ensuring the well-being of both parent and child.

This training program equips you with the foundational knowledge to understand and address common perinatal issues. Think of it like learning the essential 'first aid kit' for supporting new families: you'll gain insights into key medical concerns like gestational diabetes and hypertension, as well as crucial mental health challenges such as postpartum depression. This holistic perspective prepares you to contribute effectively to a supportive network for expecting and new parents.

How the field grew

1970sGrowing emphasis on family-centered childbirth and early parent-infant bonding practices.
1990sIncreased awareness and research into the prevalence and impact of postpartum mental health conditions.
2000sGlobal health initiatives prioritize comprehensive maternal and infant health, emphasizing multidisciplinary support.
