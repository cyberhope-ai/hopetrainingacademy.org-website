What is this field?

Small business marketing on a shoestring is about achieving significant promotional impact for your products or services using minimal financial resources. It involves creative, strategic approaches to reach your target audience, build brand awareness, and drive sales without relying on large advertising budgets. This training focuses on leveraging accessible tools and innovative tactics to maximize your marketing return on investment.

Think of it like cooking a gourmet meal with ingredients you already have in your pantry. Instead of buying expensive, exotic items, you learn to combine common, affordable components in clever ways to create something delicious and impressive. Similarly, shoestring marketing teaches you to use readily available, low-cost resources to craft compelling campaigns that attract customers and grow your business.

How the field grew

1980sRise of direct mail and local advertising as primary small business marketing tools.
1990sEmergence of the internet, allowing early small businesses to establish an online presence.
2000sGrowth of email marketing and search engine optimization (SEO) as affordable digital channels.
2010sExplosion of social media and content marketing, enabling widespread reach with minimal ad spend.
2020sIntegration of AI-driven tools and hyper-personalization, making sophisticated marketing accessible to small budgets.
