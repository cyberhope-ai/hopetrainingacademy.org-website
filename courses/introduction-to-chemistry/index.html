What is this field?

Introduction to Chemistry is your essential first step into understanding the building blocks of the universe. This foundational online program demystifies the core concepts of chemistry, from the structure of atoms to the intricacies of chemical reactions. You'll discover how every process, from cooking to the complex functions within your own body, is fundamentally a chemical dance.

Think of chemistry as the language of matter. Just as learning an alphabet allows you to read and write countless stories, mastering basic chemistry principles equips you to understand and interact with the physical world around you. This program provides that crucial alphabet, enabling you to interpret scientific phenomena and build a strong base for further scientific exploration.

How the field grew

1661Robert Boyle publishes 'The Sceptical Chymist,' distinguishing chemistry from alchemy and laying groundwork for modern chemical thought.
1789Antoine Lavoisier's 'Elementary Treatise on Chemistry' establishes the Law of Conservation of Mass, revolutionizing chemical understanding.
1869Dmitri Mendeleev publishes the first version of the periodic table, organizing elements and predicting new ones.
2020sHope Training Academy expands its online offerings, providing accessible, foundational science education like Introduction to Chemistry to diverse learners.

Foundations of Chemistry
