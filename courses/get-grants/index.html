What is this field?

Grant writing is the specialized skill of crafting compelling proposals to secure financial support from foundations, corporations, and government agencies. It's a critical function for nonprofits, educational institutions, and community organizations seeking to fund their vital programs and initiatives. A skilled grant writer translates an organization's mission and needs into a persuasive narrative that resonates with potential funders.

This online program equips you with the practical expertise to develop successful, fundable grant proposals. You'll learn the strategic process from identifying opportunities to crafting persuasive narratives and managing the submission. Think of it like learning to build a bridge; you're not just connecting two points, you're creating a pathway for essential resources to flow where they're needed most.

How the field grew

1930sExpansion of federal grant-making during the New Deal era, increasing the need for structured proposals.
1960sGrowth of private foundations and government programs (e.g., NEA, NEH) professionalizes grant seeking.
1980sEmergence of grant writing as a distinct, specialized profession, separate from general fundraising.
2000sDigital transformation of grant applications, requiring new technical skills for online submissions and data management.
2020sIncreased focus on impact measurement, data-driven proposals, and strategic partnerships in a competitive funding landscape.
