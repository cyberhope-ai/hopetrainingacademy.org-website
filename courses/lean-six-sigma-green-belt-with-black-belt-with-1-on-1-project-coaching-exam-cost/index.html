What is this field?

The Lean Six Sigma Green Belt with Black Belt program is a comprehensive career training program designed to equip you with advanced skills in process improvement and project management. You'll learn to identify and eliminate waste, reduce defects, and streamline operations using the powerful Lean Six Sigma methodology. This dual certification program prepares you to lead complex projects, drive efficiency, and deliver measurable results that boost customer satisfaction and organizational profitability.

Think of it like being a master mechanic for business processes. Just as a mechanic diagnoses problems in an engine and applies precise tools to fix them, a Lean Six Sigma professional diagnoses inefficiencies in business operations. You'll learn to use a structured approach, known as DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control), to pinpoint root causes of issues and implement sustainable solutions, ensuring every part of the 'engine' runs smoothly and effectively.

How the field grew

1986Motorola develops Six Sigma to reduce manufacturing defects.
1995General Electric widely adopts Six Sigma, popularizing its use across industries.
Early 2000sIntegration of 'Lean' principles (from Toyota Production System) with Six Sigma, forming Lean Six Sigma.
PresentLean Six Sigma becomes a global standard for operational excellence and continuous improvement.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Introduction to Lean Six Sigma

Understanding Lean Six Sigma principles, roles, and the 8 Wastes (DOWNTIME).

02
Define Phase

Project selection, creating project charters, and defining current state problems.

03
Measure Phase

Selecting and planning data collection, measuring current processes, and assessing capability.

04
Analyze Phase

Conducting process and data analysis, brainstorming, and validating root cause hypotheses.

05
Improve Phase

Crafting and filtering solutions, determining solution approaches, and conducting risk management.

06
Control Phase

Creating monitoring plans, developing response plans, and documenting project outcomes.

07
Advanced Black Belt Techniques

Building leadership skills, statistical groundwork, hypothesis testing, and designing experiments.

08
1-on-1 Project Coaching

Personalized guidance from a Master Black Belt to complete your required Black Belt project.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll. This program focuses on practical skills and certifications, making it accessible to individuals from diverse educational backgrounds.

Are the certification exam fees included in the program cost?

Yes, the fees for both the Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Black Belt certification exams are included in your enrollment, and the exams are offered directly within the course.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced program with 125 course hours. While you can progress at your own speed, successful completion of the Black Belt project often requires real-time data from an employer, which can influence the overall timeline.

Does this program prepare me for a specific Lean Six Sigma certification exam?

Yes, this program prepares you for and includes the Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Black Belt certification exams offered directly through the course provider, GoLeanSixSigma.com.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are well-prepared for roles such as Process Improvement Manager, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt Consultant, Operations Excellence Lead, and Quality Assurance Director across various industries, driving efficiency and strategic change.
