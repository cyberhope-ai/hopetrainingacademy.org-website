What is this field?

This professional development program focuses on the critical skill of aligning homework assignments directly with classroom instruction. It's about ensuring every assignment serves a clear educational purpose, reinforcing concepts taught in class, and actively contributing to your students' academic growth. Rather than busywork, homework becomes a powerful tool for learning and mastery.

Think of it like a well-designed bridge connecting two islands: your classroom lessons and your students' independent practice. This program teaches you how to build that bridge, ensuring that the path students take with their homework directly leads them from understanding to application, solidifying their knowledge and preparing them for future learning challenges.
