What is this field?

Project management is the art and science of leading a team to achieve all project goals within the given constraints. This includes planning, executing, and closing projects while managing scope, time, budget, and resources. Effective project managers are critical to an organization's success, ensuring initiatives are completed efficiently and effectively.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. They don't play every instrument, but they ensure all musicians (team members) are in sync, playing their parts at the right time, and contributing to a harmonious final performance (project completion). This suite equips you with the baton and the sheet music to lead your own successful projects.

How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded, establishing a professional body for the field.
1984PMI launched the Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, setting a global standard for expertise.
1987The first edition of the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide) was published, codifying best practices.
1990sThe rise of agile methodologies began to transform project delivery, emphasizing flexibility and collaboration.
