What is this field?

This online program offers an easy and enjoyable path for beginners to master the essentials of conversational Japanese. Whether your goal is to enhance your travel experiences, connect with Japanese culture, or simply learn a new skill for fun, this course provides the foundational vocabulary and phrases needed to communicate effectively in everyday situations.

Learning conversational Japanese is like learning the basic rules of a new game. You start with simple greetings and phrases, then build up to more complex interactions, allowing you to navigate new situations and connect with people on a fundamental level, even if you're not yet a fluent player. It opens doors to new cultural understanding and personal growth.

How the field grew

1600sThe foundations of modern Japanese language solidify during the Edo period, shaping its unique structure.
1868The Meiji Restoration opens Japan to the world, significantly increasing global interest in its language and rich culture.
1980s-PresentThe global rise of Japanese pop culture, including anime, manga, and video games, fuels widespread enthusiasm for learning the language worldwide.
TodayJapanese remains a key language for international business, technology, and cultural exchange, offering numerous personal and professional benefits.
