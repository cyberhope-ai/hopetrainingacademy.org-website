What is this field?

eBay selling empowers individuals to buy and sell products online, leveraging a global marketplace to reach millions of potential customers. This course teaches you how to navigate the platform, identify profitable items, create compelling listings, and manage transactions from start to finish, whether for supplemental income or as a full-time venture.

Think of eBay as your personal digital storefront, available 24/7 to a worldwide audience. Instead of needing a physical shop or complex e-commerce website, you can quickly list items, manage inventory, and process sales with relative ease, transforming everyday items or sourced goods into revenue.

How the field grew

1995Pierre Omidyar founded AuctionWeb, the precursor to eBay, as a personal project.
1997AuctionWeb was officially renamed eBay, reflecting its growing community and diverse offerings.
1998eBay went public, experiencing rapid growth and becoming a household name in online commerce.
2002eBay acquired PayPal, integrating a crucial payment processing system that streamlined transactions for users.
2015eBay spun off PayPal into an independent company, allowing both entities to pursue separate growth strategies.

What the career pays

An eBay seller identifies, sources, and lists products for sale on the eBay marketplace, managing all aspects of the transaction from photography and pricing to shipping and customer service. This entrepreneurial role focuses on maximizing profit margins and building a positive reputation to sustain and grow an online business.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to learn eBay selling?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this course or to become a successful eBay seller. This program is designed to provide practical, hands-on skills for anyone interested in online commerce.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, this course focuses on practical skills for online selling and does not include an exam voucher as there is no associated certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this course?

This is a self-paced online course, typically designed to be completed over six weeks with approximately 24 hours of total course material, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

Is there a certification exam for eBay selling?

There is no formal industry certification exam for general eBay selling. This course provides practical knowledge and skills to help you succeed on the platform.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

This course primarily equips you to become an independent online reseller, an e-commerce entrepreneur, or to start your own small online business. The skills are also valuable for roles like online sales assistant or e-commerce support.
