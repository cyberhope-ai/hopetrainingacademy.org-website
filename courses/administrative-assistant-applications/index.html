What is this field?

Administrative assistants are the organizational backbone of nearly every industry, ensuring that offices run smoothly and efficiently. They manage a wide array of tasks, from scheduling and communication to document preparation and data management, acting as vital support for executives, teams, and clients. Their ability to multitask, organize, and communicate effectively is crucial for business operations.

This training program is like learning to be the conductor of an orchestra, but for an office. You'll gain the skills to coordinate various instruments (tasks, people, technology) into a harmonious and productive performance. By mastering these applications and preparing for the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam, you'll be equipped to step into a role where you can significantly impact an organization's daily success.

How the field grew

1951The Certified Professional Secretary (CPS) exam was first introduced, establishing a benchmark for administrative excellence.
1998The CPS designation evolved and was renamed to the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP), reflecting the expanding scope of administrative roles.
2001The Organizational Management (OM) specialty was added, further broadening the credential's relevance and depth.
TodayThe CAP remains a globally recognized standard for administrative professionals, signifying advanced skills and expertise.
