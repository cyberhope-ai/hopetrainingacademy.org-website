What is this field?

The Writeriffic program is a comprehensive online training experience designed to ignite your creative spark and equip you with practical strategies to overcome common writing challenges. This self-guided program dives deep into the 'published writer's toolbox,' revealing techniques to generate fresh ideas, structure compelling narratives, and maintain consistent creative output.

Think of it like learning to play a musical instrument: you don't just pick it up and play a symphony. You need to learn scales, chords, and techniques. Writeriffic provides you with the 'scales and chords' of creative writing, helping you master the fundamental elements that allow you to compose your own unique and engaging literary pieces.

How the field grew

Ancient GreeceWriters invoked Muses, acknowledging the need for external inspiration and the struggles of creative flow.
1800sRomantic era authors openly grappled with artistic blocks, highlighting the psychological aspect of creative work.
1900sPsychology began studying creativity and its inhibitors, leading to structured approaches for fostering imagination.
PresentThe digital age demands constant content, making effective creativity and overcoming writer's block more crucial than ever.

Writer's block is a common challenge where writers struggle to produce new work or overcome creative stagnation. This program provides proven techniques and strategies from published writers to help you generate ideas, maintain flow, and banish creative hurdles.
