What is this field?

A Veterinary Assistant with Horse Care Management program equips you with the essential skills to support veterinarians and manage equine health. Think of it as becoming the indispensable right hand in both a veterinary clinic and a stable, capable of handling everything from routine check-ups to emergency assistance for a wide range of animals, with a deep specialization in horses.

This comprehensive training goes beyond general animal care, diving deep into equine anatomy, behavior, nutrition, and stable management. Just as a skilled mechanic understands both general vehicle maintenance and the specific intricacies of high-performance engines, you'll gain foundational veterinary knowledge alongside expert-level horse care techniques, making you a versatile and highly sought-after professional in the animal care industry.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyInformal apprenticeships as primary training for animal care roles.
Mid-20th CenturyEmergence of formal veterinary assisting roles to support growing veterinary practices.
Late 20th CenturyIncreased demand for specialized animal care, including equine-specific expertise.
21st CenturyProfessionalization of veterinary support roles and integration of advanced care techniques.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites to enroll in the Veterinary Assistant with Horse Care Management program. A high school diploma or equivalent is generally recommended for entry-level positions in this field, but not required for our training.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

This program focuses on providing comprehensive training and a certificate of completion from Hope Training Academy. An exam voucher for external certification is not included with this specific program.

How long does it take to complete the training?

This self-paced program includes 610 course hours, allowing you to complete the training at a pace that fits your schedule and learning style.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program provides a certificate of completion from Hope Training Academy, affirming your proficiency in veterinary assisting and horse care management. It is designed to equip you with industry-recognized skills rather than preparing for a single, external certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Veterinary Assistant, Equine Caretaker, Large Animal Assistant, and Stable Manager in various settings, including veterinary clinics, equestrian centers, and large animal facilities.
