What is this field?

A backyard nursery business transforms your passion for plants into a thriving enterprise, allowing you to cultivate and sell various flora directly from your home or property. It's an opportunity to become a horticulture entrepreneur, specializing in everything from ornamental plants and herbs to vegetable starts and native species, serving local markets, garden enthusiasts, and even other businesses.

Think of it like a micro-farm for plants: instead of vast fields, you utilize your available space – be it a large garden, greenhouse, or even a patio – to produce high-quality plants for sale. This program equips you with the essential knowledge, from propagation techniques to marketing strategies, to establish and manage a successful, sustainable business that aligns with your lifestyle and love for greenery.

How the field grew

1700sEarly commercial nurseries emerge, often tied to estates or specialized plant collectors, laying groundwork for plant trade.
1800sExpansion of mail-order seed and plant catalogs makes diverse plants accessible to a broader public, fostering home gardening.
1900sRise of garden centers and increased interest in home gardening leads to small-scale growers supplying local markets and specialty niches.
Late 1900s/Early 2000sEmergence of 'micro-nurseries' and direct-to-consumer sales, often leveraging farmers' markets and early internet platforms.
TodayDigital platforms, local food movements, and a demand for unique plants fuel growth in specialized backyard nursery businesses.
