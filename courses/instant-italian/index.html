What is this field?

This dynamic online program is designed to teach you how to express yourself comfortably in Italian, focusing on practical communication for everyday scenarios. You'll engage with dialogues through reading, hearing, and practicing, building a foundational understanding of the language.

Imagine learning to navigate a bustling Italian market, order your favorite dish at a trattoria, or simply introduce yourself to a new friend, all with confidence. This program provides the building blocks to achieve these real-world interactions, making the rich culture of Italy more accessible to you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no formal degree requirements to enroll in the Instant Italian program. It is designed for beginners and those looking to build foundational language skills.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program focuses on practical language acquisition and does not include an external certification exam or voucher.

How long does it take to complete the Instant Italian program?

The program is self-paced and 100% online, allowing you to learn at your convenience. Most learners complete the material within a few weeks.

What job can I get after completing this program?

While this program provides foundational Italian skills, it is not designed for direct job placement. However, proficiency in Italian can be a valuable asset in roles such as tourism, hospitality, customer service, and international business, enhancing your resume and opening new opportunities.

What are the prerequisites for this program?

There are no specific prerequisites beyond basic computer literacy and an eagerness to learn Italian. The program starts with the fundamentals.
