What is this field?

Response to Intervention (RTI) is a multi-tier approach to providing early, high-quality instruction and interventions to students who are struggling academically or behaviorally. Instead of waiting for students to fail, RTI proactively identifies learning challenges and delivers targeted support, continuously monitoring progress to ensure interventions are effective. It's a data-driven framework that helps educators make informed decisions about student learning.

Think of RTI like a tiered support system in a garden: Tier 1 is providing good soil, sunlight, and water for all plants to thrive. Tier 2 is giving extra nutrients or support to plants that aren't growing as expected. Tier 3 is providing intensive, individualized care for plants with significant challenges. This course teaches you how to implement these tiers in your classroom, ensuring every student receives the appropriate level of support to flourish.

How the field grew

Late 1990sEarly research and pilot programs explored multi-tier systems of support, laying the groundwork for RTI.
Early 2000sRTI emerged as a promising alternative to traditional 'discrepancy models' for identifying learning disabilities.
2004The Individuals with Disabilities Education Improvement Act (IDEA) formally recognized and encouraged the use of RTI.
PresentRTI is widely adopted as a framework for providing early intervention and support to students in educational settings.
