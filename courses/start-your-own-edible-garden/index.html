What is this field?

An edible garden is a dedicated space, often in your backyard or on a patio, where you actively grow fruits, vegetables, herbs, and other edible plants for personal consumption. It's about taking control of your food source, enjoying fresh, seasonal produce, and connecting with nature through the process of cultivation.

Think of it as having your own personal, hyper-local grocery store right outside your door. Instead of shopping for produce, you simply step out and harvest what you need, ensuring peak freshness, flavor, and nutritional value, all while knowing exactly how your food was grown.

How the field grew

1940sVictory Gardens during WWII popularized home food production for national self-sufficiency.
1970sThe environmental movement sparked renewed interest in organic, sustainable, and self-sufficient gardening practices.
2000sGrowth of local food movements, urban farming, and community gardens emphasized fresh, locally sourced produce.
PresentIncreasing focus on food security, healthy eating, and environmental sustainability drives widespread adoption of home edible gardens.
