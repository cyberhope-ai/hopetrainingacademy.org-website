What is this field?

This comprehensive career training program from Hope Training Academy is your pathway to becoming a certified IT professional, covering the essential knowledge and skills needed for the CompTIA TECH+, A+, Network+, and Security+ certification exams. You'll build a robust foundation in hardware, operating systems, networking, and cybersecurity, preparing you for a wide range of in-demand roles in the technology sector.

Think of this program like building a robust digital infrastructure from the ground up: TECH+ teaches you the basic tools and concepts, A+ focuses on assembling and maintaining the core computer systems, Network+ connects everything with a reliable network, and Security+ fortifies it all against threats. Each step builds upon the last, ensuring you have a complete and secure understanding of IT operations.

How the field grew

1982CompTIA (The Computing Technology Industry Association) is founded, establishing a vendor-neutral voice for the IT industry.
1993CompTIA launches its A+ certification, quickly becoming the industry standard for entry-level IT support professionals.
1999The CompTIA Network+ certification is introduced, validating essential skills for network administration and support.
2004CompTIA releases Security+, addressing the growing need for cybersecurity professionals and foundational security knowledge.
