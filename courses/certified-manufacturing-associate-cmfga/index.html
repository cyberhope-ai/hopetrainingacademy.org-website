What is this field?

The Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) program provides a crucial entry point into the dynamic world of manufacturing. This industry-recognized credential demonstrates your foundational knowledge in essential manufacturing concepts, preparing you for high-demand, entry-level roles. It's designed for individuals new to the sector, including those transitioning careers or starting their professional journey, who seek to build a solid understanding of modern production environments.

Think of it like learning the fundamental rules and tools of a workshop before you start building complex projects. You'll cover everything from workplace safety and basic shop math to inspection techniques and the principles behind advanced manufacturing technologies like CNC and robotics. This program equips you with the core competencies employers seek, making you an ideal candidate for positions that require a practical understanding of how products are made.

How the field grew

1932SME (Society of Manufacturing Engineers) is founded, dedicated to advancing manufacturing knowledge and education.
1970sSME expands its educational initiatives to address evolving workforce needs in manufacturing.
Early 2000sSME develops foundational certifications to validate essential skills for entry-level manufacturing roles.
PresentThe Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) program is designed to meet the demand for skilled entry-level workers in modern manufacturing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites for this entry-level program. It is designed for individuals new to manufacturing, requiring only basic computer skills.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Information regarding an exam voucher is not available for this program. You will need to arrange for your certification exam separately.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Manufacturing Associate program?

This program consists of 35 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on your schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current SME Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) certification exam, validating your foundational manufacturing knowledge.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for various entry-level manufacturing roles such as assembler, manufacturing associate, production laborer, and production technician.
