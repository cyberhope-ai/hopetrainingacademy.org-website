What is this field?

An Electronic Health Records Specialist (CEHRS) is a vital member of any healthcare team, responsible for managing patient health information within digital systems. This role involves ensuring the accuracy, security, and accessibility of electronic health records (EHRs), which are crucial for patient care, billing, and regulatory compliance. You'll learn the intricacies of EHR software, data management, and the legal frameworks governing patient information.

Think of a CEHRS as the digital librarian for a patient's entire health story. Instead of physical files, you organize, update, and protect sensitive medical data in a secure electronic system, ensuring doctors, nurses, and other healthcare providers have instant access to the information they need to provide the best care.

How the field grew

1990sEarly adoption of practice management systems began digitizing administrative tasks in healthcare.
2004President Bush's executive order called for widespread adoption of interoperable EHRs within a decade.
2009The HITECH Act provided significant financial incentives for healthcare providers to adopt and meaningfully use EHRs.
2015The Medicare Access and CHIP Reauthorization Act (MACRA) introduced MIPS, further encouraging EHR use for quality reporting.
