What is this field?

Effective student assessment is the cornerstone of successful education, providing critical insights into what students have learned and where they need support. It's more than just grading; it's a strategic process of gathering and interpreting evidence of student learning to inform instructional decisions and improve educational programs. This training empowers educators to move beyond traditional testing to create dynamic and meaningful evaluation strategies.

Think of it like a skilled chef tasting their dish throughout the cooking process. They don't just wait until the end to see if it's good; they continuously assess flavors, textures, and temperatures to ensure the final product is perfect. Similarly, educators use various assessment techniques to continuously monitor student understanding, adjust their teaching methods, and ultimately guide students toward mastery.
