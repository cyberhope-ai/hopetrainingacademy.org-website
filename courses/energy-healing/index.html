What is this field?

Energy healing is a holistic approach to well-being that focuses on balancing and restoring the body's natural energy systems. This program introduces you to the fundamental concepts of energy, exploring how it influences physical, mental, and emotional states, and delves into various modalities designed to promote harmony and vitality.

Think of energy healing like tuning a musical instrument. Just as a guitar needs its strings adjusted to produce harmonious sounds, our bodies and minds can benefit from balancing their subtle energy systems to promote overall well-being and vitality.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesPractices akin to energy healing, such as Traditional Chinese Medicine and Ayurveda, have been utilized for millennia across diverse cultures.
1970sA resurgence of interest in holistic health and complementary therapies began to emerge in Western societies.
1990sIncreased scientific inquiry and public acceptance led to a broader integration of complementary therapies into mainstream wellness discussions.
Early 2000sThe expansion of online resources and educational programs made energy healing modalities more accessible to a global audience.
