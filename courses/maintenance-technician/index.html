What is this field?

A Maintenance Technician is the backbone of modern industry, ensuring that complex machinery and systems operate smoothly and efficiently. Imagine them as the highly skilled doctors for industrial equipment, capable of diagnosing issues, performing intricate repairs, and implementing preventative measures to avoid costly breakdowns. This role is critical in manufacturing, where continuous operation directly impacts productivity and profitability.

Our program provides an intensive overview of the skills required for advanced manufacturing maintenance, covering everything from power transfer and rigging safety to programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and robotics. You'll gain a thorough understanding of how machines and systems function, preparing you to keep them running optimally. The program also includes a Meta Quest 3 VR Headset Kit, enabling you to complete virtual reality simulations to enhance your learning experience with practical, hands-on scenarios.

How the field grew

1950sEmergence of dedicated industrial maintenance roles focusing on reactive repair of machinery.
1980sIntegration of programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and early automation, shifting focus to preventative maintenance.
2000sAdoption of Lean, Six Sigma, and Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) methodologies for efficiency and continuous improvement.
2020sAdvanced diagnostics, robotics, and virtual reality tools revolutionize training and predictive maintenance strategies.
