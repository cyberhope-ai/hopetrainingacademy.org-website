What is this field?

Webb's Depth of Knowledge (DOK) is a framework used by educators to classify the cognitive complexity of learning activities and assessments. It helps teachers ensure their lessons provide appropriate levels of challenge, moving students beyond simple recall to deeper understanding, strategic thinking, and extended application of knowledge.

Think of DOK like different levels of a video game. Level 1 (DOK 1) might involve basic button presses to move a character. Level 2 (DOK 2) requires combining moves to solve a simple puzzle. Level 3 (DOK 3) demands strategic planning to overcome complex obstacles, and Level 4 (DOK 4) involves creating your own game level or strategy. This course equips you to design learning experiences that guide students through these increasing levels of cognitive challenge.
