What is this field?

A Certified Manufacturing Technologist (CMfgT) is a skilled professional who bridges the gap between engineering design and practical production, ensuring efficient, high-quality manufacturing processes. This role involves understanding and implementing advanced technologies, from automation and robotics to quality control systems, to optimize how products are made.

Imagine a manufacturing technologist as the architect and builder of modern production lines. They don't just oversee the construction; they design the blueprints for efficiency, select the best materials and tools, and continuously refine the process to ensure every product is built to perfection, leveraging cutting-edge technology to innovate and improve.

How the field grew

1932Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME) founded, establishing a professional body for manufacturing expertise.
1970sGrowing complexity in manufacturing processes highlights the increasing need for specialized technical roles.
1980s-90sAutomation and computer-integrated manufacturing drive demand for technologists skilled in new systems.
Early 2000sSME introduces certifications to standardize knowledge and skills for manufacturing professionals.
PresentThe CMfgT credential evolves to cover advanced manufacturing, Industry 4.0, and smart technologies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not a prerequisite for the Certified Manufacturing Technologist program. While a minimum of four years of combined manufacturing-related education and/or work experience is recommended for certification eligibility, this program is designed to provide the foundational knowledge and skills needed for entry-level roles.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CMfgT certification?

This program prepares you thoroughly for the Certified Manufacturing Technologist (CMfgT) exam offered by SME. However, an exam voucher is not included with this specific program enrollment.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Manufacturing Technologist program?

The program is self-paced and includes 165 course hours. While completion time varies by individual, you can progress through the material at a schedule that fits your life and learning style.

Which CMfgT certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Certified Manufacturing Technologist (CMfgT) certification exam administered by SME, ensuring your knowledge aligns with the latest industry standards.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Manufacturing Technologist, Industrial Engineering Technician, Quality Control Technician, or Automation Technician, applying your skills in advanced manufacturing environments.
