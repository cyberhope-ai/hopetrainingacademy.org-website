What is this field?

The 'Music Made Easy' program introduces you to the core principles of music theory, providing a solid foundation for understanding how music works. You'll learn to decipher musical notation, comprehend basic harmony, and develop the skills to both read and write simple musical pieces.

Think of it like learning the alphabet, grammar, and sentence structure before you write your first story. This program gives you the essential building blocks and rules of music, enabling you to interpret existing compositions and create your own melodies with confidence.

How the field grew

1700sStandardization of musical notation, making it universally readable for composers and performers.
1900sEmergence of diverse theoretical approaches, expanding beyond traditional Western classical music.
2000sIntegration of digital tools and online platforms for music creation, learning, and theory exploration.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Introduction to Music Fundamentals

Explore the basic elements of music: pitch, rhythm, tempo, and dynamics.

02
Module 2: Reading Music Notation

Learn to interpret the staff, clefs, notes, rests, and time signatures.

03
Module 3: Basic Harmony and Chords

Understand intervals, construct triads, and recognize fundamental chord progressions.

04
Module 4: Melody and Form

Discover how melodies are constructed, musical phrases, and common song structures.

05
Module 5: Practical Application

Apply your knowledge to simple composition exercises, playing basic pieces, and ear training.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll in 'Music Made Easy'?

No degree is required to enroll in 'Music Made Easy'; this program is designed for beginners with no prior musical experience.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

An exam voucher is not applicable for this foundational music theory program, as it does not lead to a formal certification.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to complete the material at your own speed and convenience.

Is there a certification exam for 'Music Made Easy'?

There is no formal certification exam associated with the 'Music Made Easy' program; its goal is to build foundational understanding.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program provides foundational music theory skills valuable for aspiring musicians, composers, music educators, or anyone looking to deepen their musical understanding as a stepping stone to various creative careers.
