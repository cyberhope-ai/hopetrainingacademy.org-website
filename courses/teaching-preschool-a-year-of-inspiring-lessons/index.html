What is this field?

Preschool teachers are vital educators who guide the developmental journey of young children, typically aged three to five. They create a nurturing and stimulating environment where children learn foundational skills through play, exploration, and structured activities. This role involves much more than just supervising; it's about intentionally designing experiences that build cognitive, social, emotional, and physical abilities.

Imagine a preschool teacher as an architect of early learning. Just as an architect designs a building with a blueprint, a preschool teacher crafts detailed lesson plans that serve as the blueprint for a child's day. These plans ensure that every activity, from story time to art projects, contributes to specific learning objectives, preparing children for kindergarten and beyond while making learning fun and accessible.

How the field grew

1837Friedrich Froebel opens the first 'kindergarten' in Germany, emphasizing play-based learning.
1907Maria Montessori establishes her first 'Casa dei Bambini' in Rome, pioneering child-centered education.
1965The Head Start program is launched in the U.S., providing comprehensive early childhood education for low-income families.
2000sIncreased focus on early brain development research and the integration of STEM concepts into preschool curricula.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a preschool teacher?

While some positions may prefer a degree, many entry-level roles and assistant positions do not require one. This course provides professional development to enhance your skills and qualifications for the field.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This professional development course focuses on skill acquisition and lesson planning, and therefore does not include an exam voucher as there is no certification exam associated with it.

How long does it take to complete this training?

This is a self-paced online course, allowing you to learn at your own speed. Most participants complete the material within six weeks, dedicating approximately 24 hours to the content.

Is there a certification exam for this course?

No, this course is designed for professional development and skill enhancement in preschool lesson planning. It does not lead to a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after taking this course?

This course prepares you for roles such as Preschool Teacher, Early Childhood Educator, Childcare Provider, or Assistant Teacher in various early learning environments.
