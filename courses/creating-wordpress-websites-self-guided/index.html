What is this field?

WordPress is the most popular website building platform globally, powering over 43% of all websites. It's a versatile content management system (CMS) that allows individuals and businesses to create everything from simple blogs to complex e-commerce sites, all without needing to write extensive code.

Think of WordPress as a powerful, customizable toolkit for building a house. Instead of needing to be a master carpenter (coder) to cut every piece of wood or mix cement, WordPress provides pre-made walls, roofs, and interior designs (themes and plugins) that you can easily assemble and tailor to your vision. This program empowers you to use this toolkit to construct professional, functional, and beautiful websites.

How the field grew

2003WordPress is first released as a blogging platform.

2023WordPress powers over 43% of all websites on the internet.
