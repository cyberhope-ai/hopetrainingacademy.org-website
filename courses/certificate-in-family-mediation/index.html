What is this field?

Family mediation is a specialized process where a neutral third party, the mediator, helps family members resolve disputes constructively. This can range from divorce and child custody disagreements to conflicts involving aging parents, family businesses, or estate planning. Instead of adversarial court battles, mediation fosters open communication and collaborative problem-solving, empowering families to reach mutually acceptable agreements that preserve relationships and reduce stress.

Think of a family mediator as a skilled facilitator for difficult conversations. Just as a conductor guides an orchestra to create harmony from individual instruments, a mediator guides family members through their disagreements, ensuring everyone is heard and understood. They provide a structured, confidential environment, using proven techniques like the INACCORD® conflict resolution model to identify underlying issues, explore options, and craft sustainable solutions tailored to the family's unique needs.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) as a formal field, seeking alternatives to traditional litigation.
1980sGrowth of mediation, particularly in family law, offering a less adversarial path for divorce, custody, and other domestic disputes.
Early 2000sProfessionalization of mediation training and ethical standards, coinciding with the instructor's 15+ years of experience in dispute resolution.
2010sDevelopment and widespread adoption of structured conflict resolution models, such as the INACCORD® model, championed by organizations like Mediators Without Borders.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this comprehensive training program does not require a college degree. It focuses on practical skills and the INACCORD mediation model. However, specific state regulations or advanced roles for court-referred cases may have additional educational preferences or requirements, so always check your local guidelines.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included. This program culminates in four individual certificates from Mediators Without Borders (Mediation, Applied Mediation Practice, Family Mediation, and Professional Family Mediator) upon successful completion, rather than an external certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the Family Mediation program?

This is a self-paced, open enrollment program totaling 240 course hours. You will have six months of access from your enrollment date to complete all materials and earn your certificates.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program does not prepare you for an external certification exam. Instead, upon successful completion, you will receive four distinct certificates from Mediators Without Borders, recognizing your expertise in mediation, applied practice, family mediation, and professional family mediation.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles as independent Family Mediators, Dispute Resolution Specialists, or Conflict Resolution Consultants. The skills learned are also highly valuable for positions within legal offices, human resources departments, and community organizations focused on conflict resolution.
