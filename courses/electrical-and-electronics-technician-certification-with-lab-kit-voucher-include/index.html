What is this field?

An Electrical and Electronics Technician is a skilled professional responsible for installing, testing, maintaining, and repairing a vast array of electronic equipment and electrical systems. They work across diverse industries, from manufacturing and telecommunications to aerospace and smart technologies, ensuring that complex machinery and circuits function correctly and efficiently.

Think of an Electrical and Electronics Technician as a skilled detective for machines and systems. Just as a detective uses clues to solve a mystery, you'll use specialized tools and knowledge to diagnose problems in circuits, identify faulty components, and restore complex electronic equipment to full operation, ensuring everything runs smoothly.

How the field grew

1950sThe dawn of consumer electronics creates initial demand for skilled technicians to install and repair new devices.
1980sRapid advancements in digital technology and microprocessors necessitate specialized training for complex electronic systems.
2000sThe proliferation of interconnected devices and automated systems highlights a critical and growing workforce gap for electronics professionals.
2020sHope Training Academy, a program of Video Game Palooza Inc., launches comprehensive online training to equip a new generation of Electrical and Electronics Technicians.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Electrical Systems

Basic electrical safety, fundamental circuit theory, and hands-on flashlight assembly.

02
Mathematics and Ohm's Law

Essential math for electrical fields, understanding voltage, resistance, and applying Ohm's Law.

03
Circuit Components and Diagnostics

Using multimeters, interpreting electrical schematics, and understanding conductors and resistors.

04
DC Circuits and Troubleshooting

Energy, power, DC power supplies, series and parallel circuits, and advanced troubleshooting techniques.

05
Electromagnetism and AC Circuits

Principles of magnetism, AC power, generators, motors, and using oscilloscopes for analysis.

06
Capacitors and Inductors

Types, characteristics, circuit applications, testing, and troubleshooting of capacitors and inductors.

07
Reactive Circuits and Transformers

Introduction to RLC circuits, filtering, transformer operation, and electronic power supplies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree or prior experience for this program?

No, this program requires no prior experience or degree. It's designed for entry-level learners, focusing on practical skills and foundational knowledge to launch your career.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

Yes, this program includes a voucher for the current Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) exam, covering the cost to sit for the certification upon eligibility.

How long does it take to complete this training?

This is a self-paced program totaling 160 course hours. The time it takes to complete depends on your individual study schedule, but it's designed for flexible learning.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) certification developed by the Electronics Technicians Association, International, Inc. (ETA-I).

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for entry-level roles such as Electronics Technician, Field Service Technician, Industrial Electronics Repairer, or an Assistant Avionics Technician, among others in various industries.
