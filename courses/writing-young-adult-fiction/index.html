What is this field?

Young Adult (YA) fiction is a vibrant literary genre targeting readers typically aged 12 to 18. It encompasses a wide range of genres, from fantasy and science fiction to contemporary realism and historical fiction, often exploring themes of identity, first love, friendship, and navigating the complexities of growing up. This course is designed to guide aspiring authors through the unique craft of writing for this dynamic audience.

This program provides the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to conceptualize, write, and refine a young adult novel. You'll delve into character development, plot structuring, world-building, and mastering the voice that resonates with YA readers. Beyond the creative process, the course also covers essential insights into the publishing industry, preparing you to navigate the path from manuscript to published book.

How the field grew

1967S.E. Hinton's 'The Outsiders' is published, often cited as a foundational work in modern YA literature, addressing realistic teen issues.
1997The release of 'Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone' sparks a global phenomenon, significantly expanding the YA market and its appeal.
2005Stephenie Meyer's 'Twilight' series further popularizes paranormal romance in YA, leading to a boom in genre fiction for teens.
2008Suzanne Collins' 'The Hunger Games' series ignites the dystopian YA trend, showcasing the genre's capacity for complex social commentary.
2010s-PresentYA fiction diversifies significantly, embracing a wider range of voices, cultures, and contemporary issues, reflecting a growing demand for inclusive storytelling.
