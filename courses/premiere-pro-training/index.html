What is this field?

Adobe Premiere Pro is the industry-standard software for professional video editing, used across film, television, and digital media. This powerful tool allows creators to transform raw footage into polished, compelling stories through a wide array of features for cutting, color correction, audio mixing, and visual effects. It's the backbone of modern video production, enabling everything from short social media clips to feature-length documentaries.

Think of Premiere Pro as your digital film studio. Just as a chef uses a well-equipped kitchen to turn raw ingredients into a gourmet meal, a video editor uses Premiere Pro to take disparate video clips, audio tracks, and graphics, and craft them into a cohesive, engaging narrative. You'll learn to navigate this studio, mastering the techniques to bring your creative vision to life on screen.

How the field grew

1991Adobe Premiere, the precursor to Premiere Pro, is first released for Macintosh.
1993Premiere 1.0 for Windows brings professional video editing to PC users.
2003Adobe Premiere Pro is launched, a complete rewrite designed for enhanced performance and features.
2013Premiere Pro CC (Creative Cloud) introduces a subscription model and continuous updates, solidifying its industry leadership.
