What is this field?

Civil 3D is a powerful design software used by civil engineers, drafters, and surveyors to create 3D models and construction documentation for infrastructure projects. Imagine building a complex highway interchange or a new housing development; Civil 3D allows professionals to design every aspect, from grading and drainage to roads and utilities, all within a precise digital environment.

This hands-on program builds upon your existing AutoCAD knowledge, guiding you through real-world projects to develop intermediate-level Civil 3D skills. You'll learn to manage surveying data, design transportation elements like alignments and corridors, and plan land development features such as grading and pipe networks, ultimately preparing you to produce comprehensive construction plans.

How the field grew

1982AutoCAD's initial release revolutionized digital drafting capabilities.
Early 1990sSpecialized CAD applications for civil engineering began to emerge, enhancing design workflows.
2000sAutodesk Civil 3D integrated various civil design functions into a comprehensive, model-based platform.
OngoingContinuous updates and integration with Building Information Modeling (BIM) principles advance Civil 3D's capabilities.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific degree prerequisites for enrolling in this Intermediate Civil 3D Training program. It is designed to build upon existing AutoCAD experience.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher for the Autodesk Certified Professional in Civil 3D for Infrastructure Design (ACP) Exam is not included with this program.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This self-paced program includes 90 course hours, and you will have three months of access to complete it from your enrollment date.

What certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Autodesk Certified Professional in Civil 3D for Infrastructure Design (ACP) Exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This training can prepare you for roles such as Civil Drafter, Civil 3D Designer, or CAD Technician, focusing on creating construction documents and models for infrastructure projects.
