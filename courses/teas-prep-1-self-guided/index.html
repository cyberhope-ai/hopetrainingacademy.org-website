What is this field?

The Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized exam required for admission to many nursing and allied health programs. It assesses your foundational academic knowledge in reading, mathematics, science, and English and language usage. Passing the TEAS is a critical step towards pursuing a rewarding career in healthcare, demonstrating your readiness for rigorous academic study.

This specialized online training program focuses specifically on the Reading and English Language portions of the TEAS exam. Think of it like a dedicated personal trainer for these specific academic muscles. You'll not only refresh your knowledge but also develop powerful test-taking strategies, ensuring you approach the exam with confidence and a clear understanding of what to expect.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this TEAS Prep program?

No, this program is designed to prepare you for the TEAS exam, which is typically an admissions requirement for college-level allied health and nursing programs. There are no specific degree prerequisites to enroll in this prep course.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

An exam voucher for the TEAS test is not included with this preparatory course. You will need to register and pay for the official TEAS exam separately through ATI Testing.

How long does it take to complete this TEAS Prep course?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to study at your own convenience. Access to the course content is provided within one business day of purchase, and you can progress through the material at a speed that suits your learning style and schedule.

Which version of the TEAS exam does this course prepare me for?

This course is designed to prepare you for the current TEAS exam, focusing on the fundamental Reading and English Language skills assessed across all versions. The core academic content remains consistent, ensuring relevant preparation.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

This course is a preparatory step for gaining admission into allied health and nursing programs, such as those for Registered Nurses, Licensed Practical Nurses, Dental Hygienists, or Radiologic Technologists. It does not directly lead to a job but is crucial for starting your journey in these healthcare professions.
