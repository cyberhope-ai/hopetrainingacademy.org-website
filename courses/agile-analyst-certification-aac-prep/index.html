What is this field?

The Agile Analyst Certification (IIBA®-AAC) validates a business analyst's proficiency in applying agile best practices to deliver superior business outcomes. This program focuses on developing in-demand Agile analysis skills, demonstrating how effective analysis within an agile context directly translates to increased business and customer value.

Think of an Agile Analyst as a skilled navigator on a fast-moving ship. They don't just follow a rigid map; they constantly scan the horizon, adapt to changing winds, and guide the crew to the best possible destination, ensuring the ship delivers maximum value quickly and efficiently.

How the field grew

2001Agile Manifesto published, laying the groundwork for agile methodologies.
2003International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) founded to advance the business analysis profession.
2017IIBA releases the Agile Extension to the BABOK® Guide v2.0, integrating agile principles into business analysis.
2018IIBA®-AAC certification launched, specifically recognizing agile analysis expertise from a business analyst's perspective.
