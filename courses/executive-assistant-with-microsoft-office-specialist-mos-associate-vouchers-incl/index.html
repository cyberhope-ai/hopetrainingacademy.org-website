What is this field?

An Executive Assistant is the strategic backbone of an organization's leadership, providing high-level administrative support that goes far beyond typical secretarial duties. This comprehensive training program equips you with the advanced organizational, communication, and technical skills needed to manage complex schedules, facilitate critical communications, and streamline operations for top executives, ensuring their productivity and success.

Think of an Executive Assistant as the air traffic controller for a busy airport. Just as an air traffic controller expertly manages multiple flights, schedules, and potential conflicts to ensure smooth operations and safety, an Executive Assistant orchestrates the demanding schedules, communications, and projects of executives, keeping everything on track and preventing organizational turbulence.

How the field grew

1942The International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) is founded, later developing the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) certification.
1997Microsoft introduces the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program to validate proficiency in its widely used software suite.
Early 2000sThe MOS program expands globally, becoming a benchmark for essential digital literacy in the workplace.
Present DayBoth CAP and MOS Associate remain leading credentials, continuously updated to reflect modern office technologies and administrative best practices.
