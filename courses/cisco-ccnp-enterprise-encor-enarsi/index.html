What is this field?

The Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification validates your expertise in implementing core enterprise network technologies and advanced routing solutions. This comprehensive program covers everything from dual-stack architecture and virtualization to network assurance, security, and automation, equipping you with the skills demanded by today's complex digital infrastructures.

Think of it like becoming a master architect and city planner for a vast digital metropolis. You'll learn not just how to build the roads (network paths) and buildings (servers, devices), but also how to ensure they're secure, efficient, and can grow with demand, using sophisticated tools and strategies to keep everything running smoothly and securely.

How the field grew

1984Cisco Systems founded, pioneering multi-protocol routers essential for modern enterprise networks.
1990sCisco establishes its dominant position in enterprise networking hardware and solutions.
Early 2000sIntroduction of the Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP) certification, setting a high standard for network expertise.
2010sEvolution of enterprise networking towards software-defined networking (SDN), cloud integration, and increased automation.
2020Major overhaul of Cisco's certification program, introducing the current CCNP Enterprise track with a focus on core technologies and advanced specializations.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Core Enterprise Network Technologies (ENCOR)

Campus architecture, device fundamentals, VLANs, STP, EtherChannels, inter-VLAN routing, and first-hop redundancy.

02
Advanced Routing and Network Services

OSPFv2, EBGP, Network Address Translation (NAT), Multicast, GRE and IPSec VPNs, and Virtual Routing and Forwarding (VRF).

03
Network Management and Security

NTP, Syslog, SNMP, NetFlow, SPAN, IPSLA, QoS, device access security, AAA, ACLs, CoPP, and Next-Gen Firewalls.

04
Next-Generation Campus and Automation

SD-Access, LISP, VXLAN, SD-WAN, DNA Center, data formats, NetConf/RestConf, Python, Chef, Puppet, Ansible, and SaltStack.

05
Advanced Routing and Services (ENARSI)

EIGRPv4/v6, OSPFv2/v3, Multi-Protocol BGPv4, redistribution, policy-based routing, and IPSLA.

06
VPNs and Infrastructure Services

VRF-Lite, MPLS, L3 VPNs, DMVPN, DMVPN IPSec, device access, IOS management tools, Syslog, SNMP, NetFlow, DHCPv4/v6, uRPF, CoPP, and IPv4/v6 ACLs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll or get certified?

No, a college degree is not a prerequisite for enrolling in this program or for achieving the Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification. This program focuses on practical skills and industry-recognized credentials.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This career training program does not include an exam voucher. You will need to purchase your exam vouchers separately when you are ready to sit for the current Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification exams.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and includes 130 hours of course material. The actual time to complete it will depend on your individual study schedule and prior experience.

Which Cisco CCNP Enterprise exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Cisco CCNP Enterprise core exam (ENCOR) and the current Cisco CCNP Enterprise concentration exam (ENARSI).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Network Architect, Senior Network Engineer, Enterprise Network Specialist, or Solutions Architect (Networking), focusing on complex enterprise network infrastructures.
