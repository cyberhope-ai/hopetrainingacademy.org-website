What is this field?

Building Information Modeling (BIM) with Autodesk Revit is a powerful process that creates intelligent 3D models for architectural design and construction. Unlike traditional Computer-Aided Design (CAD), Revit models contain rich data about every component, allowing architects, engineers, and construction professionals to design, visualize, and collaborate on projects with unprecedented efficiency and accuracy.

Think of BIM not just as drawing a house, but as building a digital twin of it. Every wall, window, and pipe in your Revit model isn't just a line on a screen; it's an intelligent object with properties like material, cost, and energy performance. Change a window in one view, and it updates everywhere – from floor plans to cost estimates – ensuring all project documentation remains consistent and coordinated.

How the field grew

1982Charles River Software, the original developer of Revit, is founded.
1997The company rebrands as Revit Technology Corporation, focusing on parametric building modeling.
2000Revit 1.0 is officially released, introducing a revolutionary approach to BIM.
2002Autodesk acquires Revit, integrating it into its suite of design software and expanding its reach.
2006Autodesk expands the Revit platform with specialized versions for Structure and MEP disciplines, solidifying its role in comprehensive BIM workflows.
