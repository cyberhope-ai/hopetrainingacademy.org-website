What is this field?

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt training equips professionals with powerful methodologies to identify and eliminate inefficiencies, reduce defects, and streamline operations. It's about combining the speed and waste reduction of 'Lean' principles with the data-driven problem-solving of 'Six Sigma' to achieve significant, sustainable improvements in any business process.

Think of it like a master mechanic for business processes. Just as a mechanic diagnoses engine problems and fine-tunes performance, a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt professional analyzes workflows, identifies bottlenecks, and implements data-backed solutions to make operations run smoother, faster, and more reliably, ultimately boosting an organization's bottom line and customer satisfaction.

How the field grew

1950sToyota Production System pioneers 'Lean' manufacturing principles focused on waste reduction.
1980sMotorola develops 'Six Sigma' as a data-driven approach to minimize defects and variability.
Late 1990sThe methodologies begin to converge, recognizing the complementary strengths of Lean and Six Sigma.
Early 2000sLean Six Sigma emerges as a unified, comprehensive strategy for continuous process improvement.
