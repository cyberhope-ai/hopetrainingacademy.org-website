How the field grew

1934The Securities Exchange Act establishes the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and a framework for regulating securities markets.
1938The Maloney Act amends the Securities Exchange Act, leading to the creation of self-regulatory organizations (SROs) like FINRA's predecessors.
2018FINRA introduces the Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) exam to streamline entry into the securities industry, separating foundational knowledge from product-specific exams.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to take the SIE exam or this training program?

No, there are no prerequisites for this training program, and the SIE exam itself is open to anyone aged 18 or older, regardless of educational background.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the SIE certification?

An exam voucher is not included with this training program, and you will need to register separately for the current FINRA SIE exam.

How long does it take to complete this training program?

This self-paced program includes 60 course hours, and you will have access to the materials for three months from your registration date.

Does this program prepare me for a specific version of the SIE exam?

Yes, this program is designed to thoroughly prepare you for the current Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after passing the SIE exam?

Passing the SIE exam opens doors to various entry-level roles in the financial services industry, such as financial analyst, investment associate, brokerage operations specialist, or wealth management support.
