What is this field?

Digital marketing encompasses all marketing efforts that use an electronic device or the internet. Businesses leverage digital channels such as search engines, social media, email, and other websites to connect with current and prospective customers. It's about reaching your audience where they spend their time, which is increasingly online.

Think of digital marketing as having a highly targeted, always-on storefront and advertising campaign that can reach anyone, anywhere. Instead of a single billboard, you have a network of digital signs, each tailored to show the right message to the right person at the right time, all while tracking exactly how many people see it and respond.

How the field grew

1990sEmergence of the World Wide Web and early online advertising like banner ads and email marketing.
2000sRise of search engines (Google), social media platforms (MySpace, Facebook), and blogging as key marketing channels.
2010sDominance of mobile devices, sophisticated content marketing, and the widespread adoption of data analytics for campaign optimization.
2020sIntegration of artificial intelligence, personalized marketing experiences, and the explosion of video content across all digital platforms.
