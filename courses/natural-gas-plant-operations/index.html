What is this field?

Natural gas plant operators are the skilled professionals who oversee the complex systems and equipment that transform raw natural gas into a usable product. This involves monitoring processes, ensuring safety protocols are followed, and managing the separation of purified gas into valuable components like ethane, propane, butane, and methane. They are crucial for delivering clean energy to homes and industries.

Think of a natural gas plant operator as a chef in a highly specialized kitchen. Instead of raw ingredients, they work with raw natural gas. Their job is to meticulously follow recipes (operational procedures) to purify, separate, and prepare the gas, ensuring it meets quality standards before it's sent out for consumption. They manage the 'cooking' process from start to finish, making sure everything runs smoothly and safely.

How the field grew

1821First natural gas well drilled in the United States, marking the beginning of the industry.
Early 20th C.Development of large-scale industrial processing techniques for natural gas.
Mid-20th C.Significant advancements in gas separation and purification technologies, improving efficiency.
21st C.Increased focus on safety, environmental regulations, and automation in plant operations.

What the career pays

Natural gas plant operators monitor and control the systems that process raw natural gas into usable products, ensuring purity and managing distribution. They are responsible for the safe and efficient operation of plant equipment and the flow of gas to consumers.

Foundational Safety & Math

Basic hazard communication, lockout-tagout, and essential mathematics for industrial applications.

02
Applied Physics & Mechanics

Principles of physics, heat exchangers, lubrication, bearings, and industrial valves.

03
Electrical Systems & Instrumentation

Electrical safety, electricity for operators, basic process instrumentation, and controls.

04
Pump Operations & Refining Basics

Fundamentals of pumps, centrifugal pump operation, and an introduction to petroleum refining and distillation.

05
Natural Gas Exploration & Processing

Introduction to natural gas, drilling processes, and in-depth gas plant operations.

06
Gas Purification & Compression

Understanding natural gas compression, refrigeration systems, and various purification methods.

07
Technical Documentation

How to effectively read and interpret process drawings and technical schematics.
