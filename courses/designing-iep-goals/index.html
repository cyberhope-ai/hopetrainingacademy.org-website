What is this field?

An Individualized Education Program (IEP) is a legally binding document that outlines the educational goals and services for a student with disabilities. It's a cornerstone of special education, ensuring that every student receives tailored support to meet their unique learning needs and thrive academically.

Think of an IEP as a personalized roadmap for a student's educational journey. Just as a GPS guides you to a specific destination with turn-by-turn directions, an IEP provides clear, measurable goals and strategies to help a student reach their full potential, adapting to their individual pace and learning style.

How the field grew

1975The Education for All Handicapped Children Act (EAHCA) is enacted, mandating free and appropriate public education for children with disabilities, including the requirement for IEPs.
1990EAHCA is reauthorized and renamed the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), further strengthening protections and services for students with disabilities.
2004IDEA is reauthorized again, emphasizing accountability, highly qualified special education teachers, and the importance of evidence-based practices in IEP development.

Jobs it opens

Special Education Teacher

Develops and implements IEPs for students with disabilities across various educational settings.

General Education Teacher

Collaborates on IEPs, implements accommodations, and integrates students with special needs into the general classroom.

IEP Coordinator

Manages the IEP process, ensures compliance, and facilitates communication among all stakeholders.

Educational Diagnostician

Assesses student needs and provides data to inform the development of effective IEPs.
