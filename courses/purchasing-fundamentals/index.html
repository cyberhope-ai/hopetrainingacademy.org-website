What is this field?

Purchasing is the strategic process of acquiring goods, services, or works from an external source. It's a critical business function that ensures an organization has the necessary resources to operate, produce, and deliver value, while also managing costs and supplier relationships. Effective purchasing directly impacts a company's profitability and operational efficiency.

Think of a purchasing professional as the smart shopper for a large company. Just as you might compare prices, check quality, and negotiate deals for your household, a purchasing agent does this on a much larger, more strategic scale for an entire business, ensuring they get the best value for every dollar spent on supplies, equipment, or services.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyEmergence of 'Purchasing Departments' as distinct functions within manufacturing, focused on securing raw materials.
Mid-20th CenturyShift from purely transactional buying to more strategic 'procurement,' emphasizing supplier relationships and cost reduction.
Late 20th CenturyIntegration of purchasing into broader 'supply chain management,' leveraging technology for efficiency and global sourcing.
21st CenturyFocus on ethical sourcing, sustainability, risk management, and data analytics to optimize purchasing decisions.
