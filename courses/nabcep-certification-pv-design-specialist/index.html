What is this field?

A PV Design Specialist is a critical professional in the rapidly expanding solar energy sector, responsible for engineering and optimizing solar photovoltaic (PV) systems. This role involves a comprehensive understanding of customer needs, site assessment, mechanical and electrical design principles, equipment selection, and navigating complex permitting and regulatory landscapes. Specialists ensure that solar installations are efficient, safe, compliant, and tailored to maximize energy generation and client satisfaction.

Think of a PV Design Specialist as the architect and engineer of a solar power system. Just as an architect designs a building to be safe, functional, and aesthetically pleasing, a PV Design Specialist crafts a solar solution that maximizes energy generation, meets specific client needs, and complies with all regulations, ensuring a seamless and efficient transition to solar energy.

How the field grew

2002The North American Board of Certified Energy Practitioners (NABCEP) is established to certify renewable energy professionals.
2003NABCEP introduces its first certification for PV Installers, setting industry standards for quality and safety.
2010sNABCEP expands its credentialing to include specialized design roles, recognizing the growing complexity of solar system engineering.
2028The solar market is projected to triple in size, driving unprecedented demand for skilled PV Design Specialists.

01
Customer & Project Foundations

Understanding client expectations, defining project criteria, and conducting thorough site assessments.

02
Mechanical System Design

Configuring system layouts, considering available space, orientation, tilt angle, and mounting structures.

Selecting appropriate solar panels, inverters, wiring, and optimizing energy production and system reliability.

Preparing detailed project specifications and navigating local building codes, utility regulations, and interconnection requirements.
