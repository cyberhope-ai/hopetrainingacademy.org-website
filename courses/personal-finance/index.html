What is this field?

Personal finance is the art and science of managing your money, savings, and investments to achieve your individual financial goals. It encompasses everything from budgeting and debt management to saving for retirement and making informed investment decisions. This critical life skill empowers you to navigate economic challenges, build wealth, and ensure long-term financial security for yourself and your family.

Think of personal finance as building a sturdy house for your future. Each brick is a financial decision – how you save, spend, invest, and protect your assets. Without a solid foundation and a well-thought-out plan, your financial 'house' might crumble under pressure. This program provides you with the blueprint and tools to construct that strong foundation, helping you protect your assets and discover how best to achieve all your financial goals.

How the field grew

1970sThe rise of consumer credit and complex financial products highlighted the growing need for personal financial literacy among the general public.
1980sA significant shift from employer-managed pensions to individual-managed 401(k)s placed greater responsibility on individuals for their own retirement planning and investment decisions.
2008The global financial crisis underscored the critical importance of sound personal financial management, risk assessment, and understanding economic principles.
2020sThe proliferation of digital banking, fintech innovations, and accessible online learning platforms has made personal finance education more crucial and widely available for navigating modern economic landscapes.
