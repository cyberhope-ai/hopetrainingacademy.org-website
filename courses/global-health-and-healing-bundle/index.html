What is this field?

The Global Health and Healing program offers healthcare professionals a comprehensive overview of how alternative health practices complement conventional medical systems. You'll explore diverse healing modalities and gain essential knowledge in preventing infectious diseases, equipping you to address health challenges from a broader, more integrated perspective. This program is designed to deepen your understanding of wellness beyond traditional approaches.

Think of it like learning to cook with a wider range of ingredients and techniques. While you might be skilled in traditional cooking (conventional medicine), this program introduces you to new flavors and methods (alternative practices) that can create more diverse and nourishing meals (holistic patient care). It's about expanding your toolkit to offer more comprehensive and culturally sensitive health solutions.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of the holistic health movement, emphasizing mind-body-spirit connection.
1990sIncreased public and scientific interest in Complementary and Alternative Medicine (CAM).
2000sGrowth of global health initiatives and the integration of diverse healing traditions into mainstream discussions.
2010sExpansion of integrative medicine programs in academic and clinical settings, focusing on evidence-based holistic care.
