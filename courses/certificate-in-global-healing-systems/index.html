What is this field?

Global healing systems encompass the vast and varied approaches to health, well-being, and illness prevention developed across different cultures and civilizations throughout history. These systems often integrate physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual aspects of health, offering perspectives that can complement conventional medical practices. From ancient herbal remedies to energy-based therapies, they represent humanity's collective wisdom in fostering vitality and balance.

Imagine health and wellness as a vast, intricate garden. While conventional medicine might focus on tending to specific plants with specialized tools, global healing systems explore the entire ecosystem—the soil, the climate, the interconnectedness of all life within it. This program introduces you to the rich tapestry of traditional and alternative approaches to health from around the world, offering a broader perspective on well-being and how different cultures achieve it.

How the field grew

Ancient OriginsDiverse healing systems emerge across continents, rooted in local philosophies and environments.
Mid-20th CenturyWestern medicine begins to acknowledge and research traditional practices from other cultures.
1970sThe rise of holistic health and alternative medicine movements gains traction globally.
1990sThe World Health Organization (WHO) promotes the integration and recognition of traditional medicine worldwide.
Present DayGrowing interest in integrative, personalized wellness and evidence-based complementary therapies.
