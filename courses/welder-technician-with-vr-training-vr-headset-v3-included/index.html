What is this field?

The Welder Technician career training program provides an intensive overview of essential welding skills, from fundamental metal cutting and rigging principles to advanced safety guidelines and manufacturing management. This comprehensive program includes a Meta Quest 3 VR headset with controllers, a carrying case, and an Elite Strap, allowing you to engage in five video-based simulation labs for immersive, virtual hands-on experience.

Think of it like learning to fly a plane in a simulator before stepping into a real cockpit. Our VR training lets you practice complex welding tasks in a safe, virtual environment, building muscle memory and confidence before you ever strike an arc on real metal. This approach ensures you gain practical experience and proficiency without needing prior VR experience.

What the career pays

A Welder Technician is a skilled professional responsible for joining metals using various welding processes, cutting, and fabricating components. They ensure structural integrity, adhere to strict safety protocols, and often work with blueprints and advanced machinery.
