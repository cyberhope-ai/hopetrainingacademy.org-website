What is this field?

Keyboarding is the fundamental skill of entering text and commands into a computer using a keyboard, primarily through touch-typing. It focuses on developing speed and accuracy without looking at the keys, allowing for seamless interaction with digital systems. Mastering keyboarding is crucial for productivity in virtually every modern profession, from administrative tasks to complex data analysis.

Think of mastering keyboarding like learning to play a musical instrument by touch. Just as a pianist learns to find notes without looking at their hands, a proficient typist learns to locate keys by muscle memory. This frees up cognitive resources, allowing you to focus on the content you're creating or the task you're performing, rather than the mechanics of typing.

How the field grew

1874The Sholes and Glidden typewriter, featuring the QWERTY layout, is introduced, laying the groundwork for modern keyboarding.
1930sAlternative keyboard layouts like the Dvorak Simplified Keyboard emerge, aiming for greater efficiency and comfort.
1980sThe rise of personal computers makes keyboarding a widespread and essential skill for individuals across all sectors.
2000sThe internet and digital communication solidify fast, accurate keyboarding as a universal requirement for professional and personal life.
