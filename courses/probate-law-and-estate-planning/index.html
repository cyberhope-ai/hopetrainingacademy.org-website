What is this field?

Probate law deals with the legal process of validating a will and administering the estate of a deceased person. Estate planning, on the other hand, is the proactive process of arranging for the management and disposal of one's assets during life and after death, often involving wills, trusts, and powers of attorney. Together, these fields ensure an individual's final wishes are honored and their beneficiaries are protected.

Think of it like mapping out a journey and then navigating it. Estate planning is like creating a detailed roadmap for your assets, ensuring they go where you want them to, while probate is the legal GPS system that guides your estate through the courts after you're gone, confirming the map is followed correctly. Understanding both is crucial for legal professionals supporting clients through these complex life events.
