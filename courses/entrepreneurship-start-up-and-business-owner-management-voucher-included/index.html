What is this field?

Entrepreneurship is the dynamic process of identifying opportunities, creating innovative solutions, and building a business from the ground up. This program equips you with the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to navigate the complexities of launching and managing your own venture, from initial idea generation to sustainable growth.

Think of it like building a house. You start with a vision (your business idea), then you learn about zoning laws and permits (legal structures), secure financing (funding), draw up blueprints (business plan), and gather your tools and materials (operations and marketing) to construct a sturdy, profitable enterprise.

How the field grew

1987Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship (NFTE) founded, promoting youth entrepreneurship.
1997Certiport established, becoming a leading provider of performance-based certification exams.
2017Certiport, in partnership with Intuit and NFTE, introduced the Entrepreneurship and Small Business (ESB) certification.
