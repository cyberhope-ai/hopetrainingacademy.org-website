What is this field?

The Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, offered by the Project Management Institute (PMI), is a globally recognized credential that validates your expertise in leading and directing projects. It signifies that you possess the advanced skills required to manage projects efficiently, mitigate risks, and deliver successful outcomes, making you a highly sought-after professional in virtually any industry.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. Just as a conductor ensures each musician plays their part in harmony to create a beautiful symphony, a project manager coordinates diverse teams, resources, and tasks to bring a project to a successful conclusion. The PMP certification is your maestro's baton, proving you have the knowledge and leadership to guide complex initiatives.

How the field grew

1969Project Management Institute (PMI) founded to advance the project management profession.
1984PMI initiates the development of the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide).
1987The first edition of the PMBOK Guide is published, establishing foundational project management standards.
1993The Project Management Professional (PMP) certification is launched, quickly becoming a global benchmark.
2021PMP exam content updated to reflect modern project management approaches, including agile and hybrid methodologies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to pursue PMP certification?

The Project Management Institute (PMI) requires either a four-year degree with 36 months of project leadership experience, or a high school diploma/associate's degree with 60 months of project leadership experience, along with 35 hours of project management education (which this program provides).

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course prepares you comprehensively for the PMP exam, but the exam voucher is a separate purchase directly from the Project Management Institute (PMI).

How long does it take to complete this PMP prep program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to study at your own convenience. The time to completion varies based on individual study habits, prior knowledge, and the time you dedicate to the material.

Which PMP certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is meticulously designed to prepare you for the current Project Management Professional (PMP) certification exam offered by the Project Management Institute (PMI), covering the latest exam content outline.

What kind of jobs can I get after earning my PMP certification?

PMP certification significantly enhances your career prospects for various project management roles, including Project Manager, Program Manager, Project Coordinator, and Project Lead across diverse industries such as IT, construction, healthcare, manufacturing, and finance.
