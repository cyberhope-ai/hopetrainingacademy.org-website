What is this field?

Digital Literacy Foundations is your essential starting point for mastering the technology skills required in nearly every modern job. This program takes you from the very basics, like turning on a device and sending an email, through essential software like Microsoft Office, to crucial online safety and career search techniques. It's designed for anyone new to computers or those looking to solidify their existing skills with a recognized credential.

Think of digital literacy as the universal language of the modern workplace. Just as you need to read and write to succeed in most professions, you need to understand and interact with digital tools. This program provides that foundational vocabulary and grammar, ensuring you can effectively communicate, organize, and create using computers and the internet, opening doors to a wide array of entry-level career opportunities.

How the field grew

1980sPersonal computers become accessible, creating the initial need for basic computer operation skills.
1990sThe rise of the internet and email establishes online communication as a core digital skill.
2000sMicrosoft Office suite becomes ubiquitous, making word processing and spreadsheets essential for office work.
2010sCloud computing, social media, and mobile devices expand the scope of necessary digital competencies.
2020sIncreased focus on cybersecurity, remote work tools, and the emergence of AI highlight evolving digital literacy demands.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for complete beginners and requires no prior experience or specific educational background. It's an ideal starting point for anyone looking to build foundational digital skills.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, an exam voucher is not included with this program. Your certificate and digital badge are earned by scoring 85% or higher on the proctored assessment provided within the course.

How long does it take to complete the Digital Literacy Foundations program?

This program is 100% online and self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own speed. You can take as much time as you need to master the material and revisit topics as often as you like.

Is there a specific certification exam version I need to prepare for?

This program culminates in a recognized certificate and digital badge for each of 18 skill areas upon successful completion of an internal proctored assessment. There isn't an external certification exam with version numbers associated with this foundational course.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

This program provides foundational digital skills essential for a wide range of entry-level roles across various industries. It prepares you for positions like Office Assistant, Customer Service Representative, Data Entry Clerk, and many other administrative or support roles that require basic computer proficiency.
