What is this field?

A Certified Professional Life Coach empowers individuals to achieve their personal and professional goals. Through structured guidance, coaches help clients clarify their vision, identify obstacles, and develop actionable strategies for success. It's about unlocking potential and fostering self-discovery, helping people navigate life's challenges and opportunities with confidence and purpose.

Think of a life coach as a personal trainer for your life goals. Just as a fitness trainer helps you define your physical aspirations and provides the exercises and motivation to reach them, a life coach helps you define your life's aspirations—whether in career, relationships, or personal growth—and provides the tools, accountability, and support to achieve them. This program equips you with the methodologies and ethical framework to guide clients effectively.

How the field grew

1995Founding of the International Coach Federation (ICF), establishing early industry standards.
Early 2000sRapid growth of professional coaching, increasing demand for structured training and ethical guidelines.
2010sEmergence of specialized coaching bodies, like the International Association of Professional Recovery Coaches (IAPRC), focusing on specific niches and certifications.
PresentContinued evolution of coaching, emphasizing ICF Core Competencies, ethical practice, and business acumen for successful coaching careers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites or specific degree requirements to enroll in the Certified Professional Life Coach training program. It is designed for individuals eager to develop their coaching skills.

Is a certification exam voucher included with this program?

The final certification exam, leading to the Certified Professional Coach (CPC) certification from the IAPRC, is integrated into the program upon successful completion.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Professional Life Coach program?

This program consists of 60 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on a schedule that fits your life.

Which certification exam will I be prepared for?

Upon successful completion of the program and its final exam, you will earn the Certified Professional Coach (CPC) certification issued by the International Association of Professional Recovery Coaches (IAPRC).

What kind of jobs can I get with this certification?

With this certification, you can pursue roles such as an independent Certified Professional Life Coach, Corporate Coach, Wellness Coach, or a Career Development Specialist, helping individuals achieve their personal and professional goals across various sectors.
