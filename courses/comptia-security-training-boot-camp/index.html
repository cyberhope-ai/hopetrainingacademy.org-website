What is this field?

CompTIA Security+ is a foundational, vendor-neutral certification that validates the core skills needed to perform essential security functions and pursue an IT security career. This program equips you with the practical knowledge to identify and mitigate risks, ensuring the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of digital systems across various environments.

Think of it like learning to be a digital detective and a security guard rolled into one. You'll learn how to spot potential threats, understand how they operate, and implement the right tools and strategies to protect valuable information and systems from cyberattacks, much like a security professional safeguards a physical building.

How the field grew

2002CompTIA introduces the Security+ certification, establishing a global standard for foundational IT security skills.
2005The certification undergoes its first major revision, adapting to evolving cyber threats and technologies.
2011Subsequent updates continue to broaden the scope, incorporating new domains like risk management and incident response.
2017The exam framework is refined to emphasize practical application and hands-on problem-solving in cybersecurity.
2023The latest version of the exam is released, addressing modern challenges such as cloud security, IoT, and advanced persistent threats.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. While prior experience with information systems and networking (roughly 12-24 months) is recommended, your dedication and willingness to learn are most important.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

No, an exam voucher for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam is not included with this specific training program. You will need to purchase the exam voucher separately.

How long does it take to complete the CompTIA Security+ training?

Which CompTIA Security+ exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current/latest CompTIA Security+ certification exam, covering the most up-to-date objectives and cybersecurity practices.

What job roles can I pursue after earning the CompTIA Security+ certification?

With the CompTIA Security+ certification, you can pursue various in-demand roles such as Security Administrator, Network Administrator, Cybersecurity Specialist, and Junior Penetration Tester.
