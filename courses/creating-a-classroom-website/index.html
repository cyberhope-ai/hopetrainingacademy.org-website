What is this field?

A classroom website serves as a central online hub for teachers, students, and parents, providing a dedicated space for sharing assignments, resources, announcements, and fostering communication. It's a digital extension of your classroom, accessible 24/7, designed to enhance learning, organization, and engagement beyond traditional classroom hours.

Think of it like a personalized digital bulletin board and resource library combined. Instead of handing out paper notices or emailing individual attachments, you can post everything from homework assignments and project guidelines to helpful links and class calendars in one organized location. This streamlines communication, ensures everyone has access to the latest information, and empowers students to take more ownership of their learning.

How the field grew

1990sEarly internet adoption, static web pages used for basic information sharing by pioneering educators.
2000sRise of basic content management systems and personal teacher pages, increasing interactivity and resource sharing.
2010sWeb 2.0 era introduces dynamic websites, blogs, and integrated tools for assignments, grades, and parent communication.
2020sFocus on accessibility, mobile responsiveness, and seamless integration with learning management systems for blended learning environments.
