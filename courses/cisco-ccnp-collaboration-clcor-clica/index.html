What is this field?

The Cisco CCNP Collaboration certification validates your expertise in implementing and deploying core collaboration and networking technologies. This includes critical areas like infrastructure design, signaling protocols, gateway and media resources, call control, Quality of Service (QoS), and a suite of Cisco collaboration applications. It signifies a professional's ability to handle complex unified communications environments.

Think of a company's communication system as a vast, intricate highway network. A CCNP Collaboration professional is the master architect and traffic controller for this network, ensuring that voice calls, video conferences, instant messages, and presence information flow smoothly, securely, and efficiently across all devices and locations. They design the roads, manage the traffic lights, and integrate all the different vehicles (phones, video endpoints, messaging apps) into one seamless, high-performance system.

How the field grew

Early 2000sEmergence of IP Telephony, integrating voice over data networks.
Mid-2000sCisco's leadership in Unified Communications Manager (CUCM) for centralized call control.
2010sExpansion into video conferencing, instant messaging, and presence, unifying communication channels.
Late 2010sFocus on secure remote access, cloud integration, and advanced collaboration applications like Jabber and Unity Connection.
