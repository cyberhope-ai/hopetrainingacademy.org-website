What is this field?

An Electrical and Electronics Technician is a skilled professional responsible for installing, maintaining, testing, and repairing electrical and electronic equipment. This can range from industrial control systems and communication devices to consumer electronics. They interpret schematics, diagnose malfunctions, and apply troubleshooting techniques to ensure systems operate safely and efficiently, often working with complex circuits and components.

Think of an Electrical and Electronics Technician as a detective for anything that runs on electricity. When a device or system isn't working, they use their knowledge of circuits and components, along with specialized tools, to find the 'culprit' (the faulty part or connection) and fix it, much like a car mechanic diagnoses and repairs engine problems.

How the field grew

1800sFoundational discoveries in electricity and magnetism by Volta, Ampere, Faraday, and Maxwell laid the groundwork for electrical technology.
Early 1900sThe advent of radio and early electronic devices created a need for specialized technicians to build and maintain these complex systems.
1947The invention of the transistor at Bell Labs revolutionized electronics, enabling smaller, more reliable devices and expanding the technician's role.
1970s-1980sThe rise of microprocessors and personal computers led to a boom in electronics manufacturing and the demand for technicians skilled in digital circuits.
Present DayOngoing advancements in IoT, AI, and smart technologies continue to evolve the field, requiring technicians to adapt and master new systems.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Electrical Theory

Basic electrical safety, fundamental concepts of electricity, electromagnetism, and building basic circuits.

02
Mathematics and Circuit Analysis

Essential math for electrical technicians, Ohm's Law, and applying formulas to circuit problems.

03
Testing, Troubleshooting, and Schematics

Using multimeters, interpreting electrical drawings, understanding resistors and conductors, and basic troubleshooting techniques.

04
DC and AC Circuits

In-depth study of series, parallel, and series-parallel DC circuits, introduction to AC power, waveforms, and generators.

05
Passive Components and Their Applications

Understanding capacitors and inductors, their types, characteristics, and operation in various circuits.

06
Reactive Circuits and Transformers

Analysis of RLC circuits, resonance, filtering, and the principles and uses of transformers.

07
Semiconductors and Modern Electronics

Introduction to diodes, LEDs, transistors, and their application in switches and amplifiers.

08
Practical Application and Lab Work

Hands-on labs, circuit simulations, and real-world workplace scenarios to reinforce learning and build practical skills.
