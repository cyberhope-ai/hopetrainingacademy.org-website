What is this field?

The Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) Passenger Endorsement course provides the crucial theoretical knowledge required by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) to add a 'P' endorsement to your existing Commercial Driver's License (CDL). This endorsement is mandatory for anyone operating a commercial motor vehicle designed to transport 16 or more passengers, including the driver. Our program ensures you understand the specific regulations, safety protocols, and operational procedures vital for safe passenger transport.

Think of the Passenger Endorsement as a specialized permit for your CDL, much like a pilot needs an instrument rating to fly in certain conditions. While your CDL allows you to operate a commercial vehicle, the 'P' endorsement specifically qualifies you to carry passengers, ensuring you're trained in the unique responsibilities and safety considerations that come with transporting people. This course covers the 'book knowledge' you'll need before taking the practical driving test.

How the field grew

2012The Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21) mandates Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements for commercial drivers.
2016FMCSA publishes the final rule establishing comprehensive ELDT requirements for individuals seeking a CDL or specific endorsements, including the Passenger Endorsement.
2022Federal ELDT regulations become effective, requiring all new CDL applicants and those upgrading or adding endorsements to complete specific theory and behind-the-wheel training from an approved provider.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. To enroll in this Passenger Endorsement program, you only need to possess a valid Commercial Driver's License (CDL) or a CDL Permit.

Is an exam voucher included with this training program?

Information regarding an exam voucher is not available. This course focuses on providing the required theoretical knowledge for the Passenger Endorsement.

How long does it take to complete the ELDT Passenger Endorsement training?

This self-paced online program is designed to be completed in approximately 10 course hours, offering flexibility to fit your schedule.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

Yes, this course is specifically designed to prepare you for the theory portion of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Passenger Bus Endorsement exam, which is required to add a 'P' endorsement to your CDL.

What kind of jobs can I get with a CDL Passenger Endorsement?

With a Passenger Endorsement, you can pursue various roles such as a transit bus driver, school bus driver, motorcoach operator, or shuttle bus driver for hotels, airports, or corporate services.
