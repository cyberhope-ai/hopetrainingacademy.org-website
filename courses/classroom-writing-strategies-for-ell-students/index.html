What is this field?

This program is designed for educators seeking to enhance their ability to support English Language Learners (ELLs) throughout the entire writing process. You will gain practical, hands-on strategies for guiding students from initial ideas to polished compositions, fostering their confidence and proficiency in written English.

Think of it as becoming an architect for language development. You'll learn to design the scaffolding and frameworks that empower ELL students to construct strong, coherent written pieces, much like an architect provides the blueprints for a sturdy building, ensuring every component supports the overall structure.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Understanding the ELL Writer

Explore the unique linguistic and cultural backgrounds of ELL students and their impact on writing development.

02
Effective Pre-Writing Strategies

Learn techniques for brainstorming, outlining, and organizing ideas to build a strong foundation for ELL writers.

03
Guiding the Drafting Process

Develop methods for supporting ELLs in constructing sentences, paragraphs, and coherent texts with appropriate vocabulary.

04
Post-Writing and Revision Techniques

Implement strategies for peer feedback, self-editing, and teacher conferencing to refine ELL students' writing.

05
Integrating Writing Across the Curriculum

Discover how to apply writing strategies in various subject areas and effectively assess ELL writing progress.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this training program?

This professional development program is designed for current or aspiring educators. While a degree is typically required for teaching positions, this course focuses on enhancing specific classroom skills.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this program does not include a separate exam voucher as it is a professional development course, not a certification exam preparation course.

How long does it take to complete this training?

This is a 100% online, self-paced course. The time to complete it will vary based on your individual schedule and learning pace.

What specific teaching certification does this course prepare me for?

This course is designed to enhance your teaching strategies for English Language Learners, not to prepare you for a specific teaching certification exam. It focuses on practical classroom application.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program enhances the skills of educators, making you more effective in roles such as Elementary, Middle, or High School Teacher, especially when working with English Language Learners. It strengthens your ability to support diverse student populations.
