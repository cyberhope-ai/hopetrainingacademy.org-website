What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint 365 is the industry-standard software for crafting dynamic and engaging visual presentations. It allows users to combine text, images, charts, videos, and animations into a series of slides, effectively communicating ideas, data, and stories to an audience.

Think of PowerPoint as your personal visual storyteller. Instead of just speaking, you're building a digital flipbook that guides your audience through your narrative, making complex information digestible and memorable, whether for a business pitch, a school project, or a community update.

How the field grew

1987First release by Forethought Inc. (Presenter)
1987Acquired by Microsoft and renamed PowerPoint
1990First Windows version (PowerPoint 2.0) released
2013Integrated into the new Office 365 subscription service
