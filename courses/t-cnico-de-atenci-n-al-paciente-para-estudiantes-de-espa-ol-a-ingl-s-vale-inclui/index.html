What is this field?

A Patient Care Technician (PCT) is a vital member of the healthcare team, providing direct patient care under the supervision of nurses and other medical professionals. This role involves assisting patients with daily living activities, monitoring vital signs, performing basic medical procedures like phlebotomy and EKG, and ensuring patient comfort and safety. PCTs work in various settings, including hospitals, long-term care facilities, and clinics, making a significant difference in patients' lives.

This specialized training program is designed for Spanish-speaking individuals aspiring to become PCTs in English-speaking healthcare environments. It uniquely combines comprehensive patient care instruction with targeted English language development, focusing on medical terminology and effective communication. Think of it as building a dual foundation: mastering the hands-on skills of a PCT while simultaneously gaining the linguistic confidence to excel in a diverse healthcare setting, much like a bilingual flight attendant ensures clear communication for all passengers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, this career training program does not require a college degree. It is designed to equip you with the specific skills and certifications needed for a Patient Care Technician role.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

Information regarding an exam voucher is not available. Please inquire directly for the latest details on certification exam costs.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The program is self-paced, totaling 485 course hours, and you will have 12 months of access to complete your training.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for industry-recognized certifications such as the Certified Patient Care Technician (CPCT) exam through the NHA or the Patient Care Technician Certification (PCTC) through AMCA.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Patient Care Technician, Certified Patient Care Technician, or Nursing Assistant in various healthcare settings like hospitals, clinics, and long-term care facilities.
