What is this field?

Veterinary medical terminology is the specialized language used by animal care professionals to describe animal anatomy, physiology, diseases, and treatments. It's a precise system of prefixes, suffixes, and root words that allows for clear and accurate communication among veterinarians, technicians, and assistants, ensuring the best care for animals.

Just as a mechanic needs to know the parts of an engine by their specific names, animal care professionals need to understand the precise language of veterinary medicine. This course provides that foundational vocabulary, enabling you to comprehend medical records, communicate effectively with colleagues, and contribute confidently in any animal health setting.
