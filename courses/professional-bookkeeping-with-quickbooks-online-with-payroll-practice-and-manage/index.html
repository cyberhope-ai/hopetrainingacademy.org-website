What is this field?

Bookkeeping and payroll management are the financial backbone of any successful organization, especially small businesses. This program equips you with the critical skills to meticulously track financial transactions, manage accounts, and ensure accurate, compliant payroll processing using industry-standard tools like QuickBooks Online.

Think of a bookkeeper and payroll specialist as the financial navigator and crew for a business. Just as a ship needs a navigator to chart its course and a crew to manage its operations, a business relies on these professionals to keep its financial journey on track, ensuring every dollar is accounted for and every employee is paid correctly and on time.

How the field grew

1980sThe advent of personal computers begins to transform traditional, manual ledger keeping.
1990sDesktop accounting software emerges, simplifying financial tracking for small and medium-sized businesses.
2000sThe internet revolutionizes business operations, laying the groundwork for cloud-based financial management solutions.
2010sCloud accounting platforms like QuickBooks Online gain widespread adoption, enabling remote and real-time financial oversight.
PresentIntegrated bookkeeping and payroll skills are essential for efficient, compliant, and modern business operations.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our Professional Bookkeeping with QuickBooks Online with Payroll Practice and Management program. This program is designed for individuals seeking practical skills and industry certifications.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

A voucher for the QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) exam may be included with your enrollment, depending on the specific package you choose. A voucher for the Fundamental Payroll Certification (FPC) exam is not included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This comprehensive program is designed for self-paced learning and includes approximately 200 course hours. You can progress at a pace that fits your schedule.

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) exam and the current Fundamental Payroll Certification (FPC) exam offered by PayrollOrg.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Bookkeeper, Payroll Specialist, Accounting Assistant, or Small Business Financial Coordinator, particularly in organizations utilizing QuickBooks Online.
