How the field grew

1983Athletics and Fitness Association of America (AFAA) founded, pioneering group fitness education.
2005AFAA's certification programs achieve accreditation by the National Commission for Certifying Agencies (NCCA), signifying high standards.
2015AFAA joins the National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) family, expanding its reach and resources.

What the career pays

Group Fitness Instructors lead and motivate groups through various exercise formats, ensuring safe and effective workouts. They apply exercise science principles to design engaging classes and foster a positive fitness environment.

$24,960entry
$45,910median
$81,000experienced

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. To sit for the AFAA-GFI certification exam, you must be 18 years or older, have a high school diploma or equivalent, and possess current CPR/AED certification with a hands-on training component.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, your enrollment in this program includes a voucher for the AFAA Group Fitness Instructor certification exam, covering the cost of your certification attempt.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced program, offering flexibility to learn at your own speed. You will have six months of access to complete the 60 course hours.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program specifically prepares you for the current AFAA Group Fitness Instructor (GFI) certification exam, a nationally recognized credential in the fitness industry.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and certification, you will be prepared for roles such as a Group Fitness Instructor in gyms, fitness studios, community centers, or even to lead private group classes.
