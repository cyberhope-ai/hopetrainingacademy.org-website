What is this field?

Dyslexia is a common learning difference that primarily affects reading and spelling, making it challenging for individuals to process language. This course provides educators with the knowledge and practical strategies needed to identify, understand, and effectively support students with dyslexia, transforming classroom environments into places where every student can thrive.

Think of it like this: for a student with dyslexia, reading can feel like trying to navigate a map where the street names constantly shift or blur. This program equips you with the tools to help them clarify those names, provide alternative routes, and build their confidence to explore the entire map successfully.

How the field grew

1970sIncreased awareness and research into various learning disabilities, including dyslexia, began to shape educational approaches.
1990sLandmark legislation, such as the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), mandated support services for students with learning disabilities, emphasizing individualized education.
2000sGrowing emphasis on early intervention and evidence-based strategies for dyslexia emerged, driven by scientific advancements in cognitive psychology.
2010s-PresentContinued advancements in neuroscience and educational technology further refine understanding and support, promoting inclusive practices and personalized learning for dyslexic students.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Understanding Dyslexia

Explore the definition, common challenges, and typical symptoms observed in students with dyslexia.

02
Identifying Learning Differences

Learn to observe and recognize the signs of dyslexia in the classroom setting to provide timely support.

03
Classroom Strategies for Reading

Discover effective techniques for phonological awareness, decoding, fluency, and comprehension in dyslexic learners.

04
Classroom Strategies for Writing

Implement strategies to assist with spelling, grammar, written expression, and organizational skills.

05
Creating an Inclusive Environment

Learn about differentiation, accommodations, and the use of assistive technology to support all students.

06
Fostering Student Success

Develop methods to build confidence, motivation, and self-advocacy skills in students with dyslexia.
