What is this field?

Microsoft Excel is the world's leading spreadsheet software, an indispensable tool for organizing, analyzing, and visualizing data across virtually every industry. From finance and marketing to operations and human resources, professionals rely on Excel to manage budgets, track performance, create detailed reports, and make informed business decisions. Mastering Excel empowers you to efficiently handle large datasets, perform complex calculations, and present information clearly.

Think of Excel as a super-powered digital ledger combined with a sophisticated calculator and a dynamic charting tool. It allows you to not only store vast amounts of information but also to manipulate it, identify trends, forecast outcomes, and communicate findings through compelling visuals. This foundational skill is a cornerstone for data literacy and career advancement in today's data-driven world.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel's first release for the Apple Macintosh, challenging existing spreadsheet software.
1987Introduction of Excel for Windows, quickly gaining market dominance due to its graphical interface.
1993Excel 5.0 integrates Visual Basic for Applications (VBA), allowing users to automate tasks and create custom functions.
2007Major interface overhaul with the 'Ribbon' and the introduction of the XML-based .xlsx file format.
2019Release of Excel 2019, enhancing features for data analysis, charting, and collaboration for perpetual license users.
