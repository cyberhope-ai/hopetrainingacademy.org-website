What is this field?

Integrating Technology in the Classroom is a professional development program designed for educators seeking to leverage digital tools for enhanced learning. This course moves beyond simply using computers to strategically embedding technology into daily lessons, curriculum design, and student engagement strategies. You'll explore how modern tools can transform traditional teaching methods into dynamic, interactive experiences.

Think of it like a chef learning to use new kitchen appliances. It's not just about having a blender; it's about understanding how to use it to create smoother sauces, faster preparations, and entirely new dishes. Similarly, this program teaches educators how to skillfully employ apps, assistive technologies, and digital platforms like blogs to foster deeper understanding, cater to diverse learning needs, and prepare students for a technology-driven world.

How the field grew

1980sPersonal computers begin entering classrooms, primarily for basic computing skills.
1990sThe internet and multimedia tools emerge, offering new resources for research and presentation.
2000sInteractive whiteboards and learning management systems (LMS) gain traction, centralizing digital content.
2010sMobile devices, cloud computing, and personalized learning platforms revolutionize access and instruction.
2020sArtificial intelligence, virtual/augmented reality, and blended learning models further expand educational possibilities.
