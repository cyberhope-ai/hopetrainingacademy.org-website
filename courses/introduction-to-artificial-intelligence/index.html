What is this field?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a groundbreaking field dedicated to creating computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. This includes learning from data, understanding natural language, recognizing patterns, making decisions, and solving complex problems. It's the science of making machines smart, enabling them to perceive, reason, and act in ways that mimic human cognitive abilities.

Think of AI like teaching a child to recognize different animals. Instead of programming specific rules for each animal, you show the child many pictures of cats, dogs, and birds, and they learn to identify them on their own. Similarly, AI systems learn from vast amounts of data to identify patterns and make predictions, allowing them to automate tasks, personalize experiences, and drive innovation across industries.

How the field grew

1950Alan Turing proposes the Turing Test, a benchmark for machine intelligence.
1956The Dartmouth Workshop coins the term 'Artificial Intelligence,' marking the field's birth.
1997IBM's Deep Blue computer defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
2012AlexNet wins the ImageNet competition, igniting the deep learning revolution.
2022ChatGPT's public launch brings generative AI to mainstream attention.
