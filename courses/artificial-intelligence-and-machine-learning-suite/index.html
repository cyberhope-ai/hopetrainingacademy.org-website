What is this field?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are rapidly transforming industries by enabling computer systems to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. AI encompasses the broader concept of machines mimicking cognitive functions like problem-solving and learning, while ML is a subset of AI that focuses on systems learning from data to identify patterns and make predictions without explicit programming.

Think of AI as the brain and ML as the learning process within that brain. Just as you learn from experience to recognize faces or understand language, ML algorithms learn from vast datasets to power everything from personalized recommendations on streaming services to self-driving cars. Mastering these foundational concepts is crucial for anyone looking to contribute to the next generation of technological innovation.

How the field grew

1950Alan Turing publishes 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence,' introducing the Turing Test.
1956The Dartmouth Workshop officially coins the term 'Artificial Intelligence,' marking its birth as a field.
1997IBM's Deep Blue computer defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov, showcasing early AI prowess.
2012AlexNet's victory in the ImageNet challenge ignites the deep learning revolution, transforming computer vision.
