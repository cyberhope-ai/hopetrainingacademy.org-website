What is this field?

This program is the final step in preparing you for the CompTIA A+ certification, focusing on critical networking, security, and troubleshooting skills. It's designed to equip you with the knowledge needed to manage and protect modern IT infrastructures, from configuring network devices to defending against cyber threats and resolving complex technical issues.

Think of it like learning to be the 'IT detective' and 'network guardian' all rolled into one. You'll gain the ability to diagnose why a computer isn't connecting to the internet, secure systems from malicious attacks, and ensure all devices communicate smoothly and safely within a network environment. This foundational expertise is vital for any aspiring IT professional.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA (The Computing Technology Industry Association) is founded.
1990sThe CompTIA A+ certification is introduced, becoming a benchmark for PC hardware and software proficiency.
Early 2000sA+ evolves to include broader IT concepts, incorporating networking fundamentals and operating system management.
2010sThe certification adapts to new technologies like mobile devices, cloud computing, and enhanced security practices.
PresentCompTIA A+ undergoes continuous updates to reflect the latest IT infrastructure, emerging technologies, and best practices in the industry.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this program or to pursue the CompTIA A+ certification. This program is designed to provide you with the practical skills and knowledge needed for entry-level IT roles.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This specific program does not include an exam voucher for the CompTIA A+ certification exam. Vouchers must be purchased separately.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn at your own speed. The time it takes to complete will depend on your individual study habits and schedule.

Which CompTIA A+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current CompTIA A+ certification exam, covering the latest objectives for networking, security, and troubleshooting.

What kind of job can I get with CompTIA A+ certification?

With CompTIA A+ certification, you can pursue roles such as Help Desk Technician, IT Support Specialist, Field Service Technician, Desktop Support Analyst, and other entry-level IT positions.
