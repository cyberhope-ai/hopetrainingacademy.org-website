What is this field?

The CompTIA Data+ certification validates your foundational skills in data management, analysis, and reporting. This program equips you to collect, process, and interpret data effectively, transforming raw information into actionable insights for business decisions.

Think of data as the raw ingredients in a kitchen. Without proper handling, cleaning, and preparation, these ingredients are just a mess. A CompTIA Data+ professional is like a skilled chef who knows how to select the best ingredients, prepare them correctly, and combine them to create a delicious, insightful meal that helps everyone understand what's happening and what to do next.

How the field grew

Early 2000sThe rise of 'Big Data' highlights the increasing need for specialized skills in data handling and analysis.
2010sData analytics emerges as a critical business function, driving significant demand for skilled professionals.
2022CompTIA introduces the Data+ certification, establishing a vendor-neutral credential for foundational data skills.
PresentHope Training Academy offers comprehensive training for the current/latest CompTIA Data+ certification, preparing individuals for in-demand data roles.
