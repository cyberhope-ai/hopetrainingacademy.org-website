What is this field?

The Robotics in Manufacturing Fundamentals (RMF) program provides the foundational knowledge crucial for anyone entering or advancing in the dynamic field of robotics. This industry-recognized credential, developed by leading organizations like SME and FIRST®, covers essential topics from manufacturing basics and applied mathematics to robotic systems, applications, and safety protocols. It's designed to equip you with the critical understanding needed to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot robotic systems in modern production environments.

Think of it like learning the alphabet before you can write a novel. The RMF program teaches you the fundamental 'letters' and 'words' of robotics – how robots are built, how they move, how they're programmed, and how they fit into a manufacturing line. This essential groundwork prepares you to understand more complex robotic 'sentences' and 'paragraphs' as you progress in your career, making you a valuable asset in any advanced manufacturing setting.

How the field grew

1932SME (Society of Manufacturing Engineers) founded, advancing manufacturing knowledge and practices.
1961First industrial robot, Unimate, deployed, marking the dawn of manufacturing automation.
1989FIRST® (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) founded, inspiring youth in science and robotics.
2012Robotics Education & Competition (REC) Foundation established, expanding competitive robotics programs globally.
TodayHope Training Academy offers the RMF credential, building on decades of robotics innovation to prepare the next generation of manufacturing professionals.
