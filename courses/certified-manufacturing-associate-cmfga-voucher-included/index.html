What is this field?

The Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) program provides foundational knowledge and skills essential for entry-level roles in the modern manufacturing industry. It's designed for individuals new to the sector, offering a clear pathway to demonstrate basic competency and readiness for high-demand positions. This credential validates your understanding of core manufacturing concepts, from safety protocols to quality control.

Think of the CMfgA as your foundational blueprint for a manufacturing career. Just as a builder needs to understand basic construction principles before tackling a complex project, this program equips you with the fundamental knowledge of how products are made, inspected, and improved, preparing you for hands-on roles in a dynamic industry.

How the field grew

1932Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME) founded, establishing a legacy of advancing manufacturing knowledge.
Mid-20th CenturySME expands its focus to include education and professional development for the evolving manufacturing workforce.
Early 2000sIndustry demand for standardized skills validation leads to the development of entry-level certifications like CMfgA.
Recent YearsThe CMfgA credential becomes a recognized standard for individuals seeking to enter the technology-rich manufacturing sector.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals new to manufacturing and does not require a college degree. It focuses on providing foundational skills for entry-level roles.

Is the exam voucher included with the program?

Yes, an exam voucher for the current Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) certification exam is included with your enrollment.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Manufacturing Associate program?

The program involves approximately 35 course hours and is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on your schedule.

Which CMfgA certification exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Certified Manufacturing Associate (CMfgA) certification exam, ensuring your knowledge is up-to-date with industry standards.

What kind of jobs can I get after earning the CMfgA credential?

The CMfgA credential prepares you for various entry-level manufacturing roles such as Manufacturing Associate, Production Assembler, Production Laborer, or Warehouse Worker in a manufacturing setting.
