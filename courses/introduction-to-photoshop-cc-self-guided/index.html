What is this field?

Photoshop CC is the industry-standard software for image manipulation, graphic design, and digital art. It empowers users to transform ordinary photos into extraordinary visuals, create captivating compositions, and develop professional-grade digital assets for various media. This course focuses on the Creative Cloud version, ensuring you learn the most current tools and techniques.

Think of Photoshop as your ultimate digital darkroom and art studio combined. Just as a chef uses various tools to prepare a meal, Photoshop provides a comprehensive toolkit—from brushes and filters to layers and masks—allowing you to precisely craft, enhance, and combine images, turning your creative vision into reality.

How the field grew

1987Thomas and John Knoll begin developing ImagePro, the precursor to Photoshop.
1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 is officially released, revolutionizing digital image editing.
2003Adobe introduces the Creative Suite (CS) branding, bundling Photoshop with other design tools.
2013Photoshop CC (Creative Cloud) is launched, transitioning to a subscription-based model with continuous updates.
