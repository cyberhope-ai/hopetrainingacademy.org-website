What is this field?

This program is your essential first step into the dynamic and compassionate world of nursing. Designed for aspiring healthcare professionals, it provides a comprehensive overview of what it truly means to be a Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) or a Registered Nurse (RN), detailing the diverse roles, responsibilities, and the profound impact these professionals have on patient well-being.

Think of this program as your personal career GPS, guiding you through the various educational requirements, daily duties, and exciting opportunities available in nursing. It helps you navigate the landscape of healthcare, allowing you to make an informed decision about pursuing a career that is both challenging and deeply rewarding.

What the career pays

Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs) and Registered Nurses (RNs) are vital healthcare professionals who provide direct patient care, administer medications, and educate patients and families. They work in diverse settings, from hospitals and clinics to schools and home healthcare, making a profound impact on community well-being.

Jobs it opens

Registered Nurse (RN)

Provides direct patient care, administers medications, and educates patients and families.

Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)

Delivers fundamental patient care, monitors health, and assists with daily living activities.

Charge Nurse

Oversees a nursing unit, coordinating patient care and supervising other nursing staff.

School Nurse

Provides health services to students and staff within an educational setting.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to start this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals with no prior nursing experience, serving as an introductory exploration of the field.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this introductory program does not include an exam voucher as it is designed for career exploration, not certification.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This online program is designed to be completed in approximately 24 course hours, typically over six weeks.

Does this program prepare me for a specific nursing exam?

This program provides an overview of the nursing profession and educational paths for LPNs and RNs, but it is not a test preparation course for licensure exams.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program helps you understand the roles of Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs) and Registered Nurses (RNs), guiding you toward further education and training needed for these rewarding careers.
