What is this field?

This hands-on training program is your entry point into the dynamic world of solar energy, the fastest-growing power generation method globally. You'll acquire essential, in-demand skills to understand, install, and maintain photovoltaic (PV) systems, preparing you for a career in an industry experiencing exponential growth driven by federal legislation and environmental priorities.

Think of solar panels like tiny, silent power plants on your roof or in a field, converting sunlight directly into clean electricity. This program teaches you how these 'power plants' work, how to design them efficiently, and how to keep them running safely, empowering you to contribute to a sustainable future.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program has no prerequisites and is designed for individuals eager to enter the solar industry, regardless of prior academic experience.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This specific program focuses on foundational knowledge. While it does not include a certification exam voucher, it provides excellent preparation for further specialized solar certifications.

How long does it take to complete the Solar Energy Training program?

This self-paced program is designed for completion in approximately 40 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

What certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program provides comprehensive foundational knowledge in solar energy. It serves as an excellent stepping stone for various industry certifications, such as the current/latest NABCEP PV Associate exam, which would require additional, specialized training.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this training?

Graduates can pursue entry-level roles such as Solar Panel Installer, Solar PV Technician, or Renewable Energy Specialist, contributing to the design, installation, and maintenance of solar energy systems.
