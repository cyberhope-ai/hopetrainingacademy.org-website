What is this field?

A video design professional is a creative storyteller who brings visual narratives to life using powerful software tools like Adobe Photoshop and Premiere Pro. They transform raw footage, graphics, and audio into polished, engaging video content for everything from social media campaigns to feature films. This role demands both artistic vision and technical mastery to craft compelling visual experiences that captivate audiences.

Think of it like being a master architect for digital stories. You're not just building a structure; you're designing an experience, carefully selecting each visual element and transition to guide the viewer through a narrative, ensuring every detail contributes to the final impact. No prior design or editing experience is required to start this journey; just a passion for creativity and basic computer literacy.

How the field grew

1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 released, revolutionizing image manipulation.
1991Adobe Premiere (later Premiere Pro) launched, setting a new standard for video editing.
2003Adobe Creative Suite introduced, integrating powerful design and video tools.
2012Adobe Creative Cloud launched, providing subscription-based access to industry-standard software.
