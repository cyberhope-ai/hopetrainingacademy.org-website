What is this field?

Retrieval practice is a powerful learning strategy where students actively recall information from memory, rather than passively re-reading or reviewing. In the math classroom, this technique moves beyond rote memorization, encouraging deeper conceptual understanding and long-term retention of mathematical principles and problem-solving methods.

Think of it like exercising a mental muscle. Just as physical exercise strengthens your body, retrieval practice strengthens your brain's ability to access and apply mathematical knowledge. This course equips educators with the tools to integrate these evidence-based strategies, transforming how students engage with and master math concepts.
