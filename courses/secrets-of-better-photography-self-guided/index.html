What is this field?

Photography is the art, application, and practice of creating durable images by recording light or other electromagnetic radiation, either electronically by means of an image sensor, or chemically by means of a light-sensitive material such as photographic film. It's a powerful medium for storytelling, preserving memories, and expressing creativity, requiring both technical understanding and an artistic eye.

This program focuses on demystifying your camera and its settings, transforming you from a casual snapper into a confident photographer. Think of it like learning to drive a stick shift car: initially daunting with many controls, but once you understand the clutch, gears, and accelerator, you gain complete control and can navigate any road with precision and artistry.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this photography program?

No, a degree is not required to enroll in our Secrets of Better Photography program. This course is designed for beginners and enthusiasts eager to learn and improve their photographic skills.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this course does not include an exam voucher as it is a skills-based training program focused on practical photography techniques rather than a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this online photography course?

This is a self-paced online course, allowing you to learn at your own speed. The time to complete it will vary based on your individual schedule and learning style.

Is there a certification exam associated with this course?

This program is focused on developing practical photography skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam. Your achievement is measured by your improved ability to capture stunning images.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

While this course provides foundational skills, it can open doors to various photography-related pursuits, including freelance photography, assisting professional photographers, or specializing in areas like event, portrait, or landscape photography.
