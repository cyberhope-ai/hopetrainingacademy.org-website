What is this field?

Microsoft Access is a powerful database management system included in the Microsoft Office suite, designed to help individuals and small to medium-sized businesses organize, manage, and report on large volumes of information. It allows users to create custom applications for data entry, storage, and analysis, providing a structured way to handle everything from customer lists to inventory records.

Earning your Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Access certification validates your expertise in this essential tool, demonstrating to employers that you can efficiently design, customize, and optimize databases. This credential can significantly enhance your resume, opening doors to roles where strong data management and analytical skills are highly valued, and potentially leading to higher earning potential.

How the field grew

1992Microsoft Access 1.0 is released, offering a powerful desktop database solution.
Early 2000sThe Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program is established, including Access as a core credential.
OngoingMicrosoft regularly updates Access and its corresponding MOS certification exams to reflect the latest software features and industry best practices.
