What is this field?

Adobe Photoshop CC for the Digital Photographer is a comprehensive training program designed to equip you with the essential skills to expertly manipulate and enhance your digital images. This course moves beyond basic adjustments, diving deep into Photoshop's powerful tools for retouching, color correction, compositing, and creative effects, all tailored specifically for photographers.

Think of Photoshop as your digital darkroom, but with infinitely more possibilities. Just as a chef uses a range of tools and techniques to transform raw ingredients into a culinary masterpiece, you'll learn to use Photoshop's brushes, layers, and filters to turn your raw photographic captures into stunning visual stories, correcting imperfections and unleashing your creative vision.

No, a college degree is not required to enroll. This program is designed for individuals seeking practical, job-ready skills in digital photography editing.

This online program is self-paced, offering approximately 24 hours of course content. Most learners complete it within six weeks, dedicating a few hours per week.
