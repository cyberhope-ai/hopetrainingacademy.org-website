What is this field?

An HVAC/R Technician is a skilled professional responsible for installing, maintaining, and repairing heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems. This vital role ensures comfortable indoor environments and proper preservation of goods by managing temperature, humidity, and air quality in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.

Think of HVAC/R systems as the circulatory and respiratory systems of a building. Just as your body needs to regulate temperature and breathe clean air to function, buildings rely on HVAC/R technicians to keep their internal climate perfectly balanced, whether it's a cozy home, a bustling office, or a critical data center.

How the field grew

1930sRefrigeration becomes widespread in homes and commercial settings, creating demand for skilled technicians.
1970sEnergy crises drive innovation in HVAC/R, focusing on efficiency and new system designs.
1987The Montreal Protocol is signed, leading to the phase-out of ozone-depleting refrigerants and the establishment of EPA 608 certification requirements.
2000sEmergence of smart HVAC systems, geothermal technology, and increased focus on sustainable and green building practices.
