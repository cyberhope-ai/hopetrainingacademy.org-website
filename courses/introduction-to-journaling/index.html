What is this field?

Journaling is a powerful practice of regularly recording your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. It serves as a personal laboratory for self-discovery, allowing you to gain clarity on your dreams, values, and beliefs, while also processing emotions and fostering personal growth.

Think of journaling as your personal mental gym. Just as physical exercise strengthens your body, journaling strengthens your mind, helping you build resilience, enhance creativity, and develop a deeper understanding of yourself to navigate life's challenges and opportunities more effectively.

How the field grew

Ancient TimesEarly forms of personal record-keeping and philosophical reflection emerge in various cultures.
10th CenturySei Shōnagon's 'The Pillow Book' in Japan showcases a significant early example of personal observation and reflection.
17th CenturySamuel Pepys' Diary provides a detailed personal and historical account, highlighting the power of daily written reflection.
Mid-20th CenturyTherapeutic journaling gains recognition as a valuable tool in psychology for self-exploration and emotional processing.
21st CenturyThe rise of digital journaling platforms and apps makes reflective practices more accessible for personal growth and career development.
