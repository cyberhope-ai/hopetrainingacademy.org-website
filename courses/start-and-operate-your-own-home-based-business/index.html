How the field grew

1970sEmergence of early personal computing and telecommuting, laying groundwork for remote work.
1990sThe internet boom democratizes access to markets, enabling widespread e-commerce and online services from home.
2000sGrowth of the gig economy and specialized online platforms lowers barriers to entry for independent contractors and home-based entrepreneurs.
2020sGlobal events accelerate the adoption of remote work and highlight the resilience and potential of home-based business models.

What the career pays

Home-based business owners are visionaries who identify market needs and build ventures from their own space, managing all aspects of their operations. They are responsible for strategic planning, financial oversight, marketing, and delivering products or services directly to customers.

Registered Apprenticeship — via the United States Department of Labor

Hope Training Academy offers United States Department of Labor registered apprenticeship pathways, providing a structured, online route to a real job and not just a certificate. This unique approach de-risks your career transition by combining hands-on learning with mentorship, leading to a recognized credential and practical experience.

Hope Training Academy Online · a United States Department of Labor (USDOL) Registered Apprenticeship pathway

If you're in Indiana: hands-on labs and in-person apprenticeship components run from our downtown Indianapolis center. (Online coursework and the registered apprenticeship pathway are available more broadly.)

Why Hope Training Academy

As a workforce-development program of Video Game Palooza, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity, Hope Training Academy reinvests every enrollment dollar into expanding access to quality career training. Your participation directly funds free tech training and living-wage career opportunities for underserved communities, creating a ripple effect of positive change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific educational degree requirements to enroll in the Start and Operate Your Own Home-Based Business program. It is designed for individuals ready to learn entrepreneurial skills.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on practical business skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore an exam voucher is not applicable or included.

How long does it take to complete this training?

The course content is designed to be completed at your own pace within a structured online format. While specific hours are not listed, typical ed2go courses of this nature often span several weeks.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to equip you with the practical knowledge and skills to launch and manage your own home-based business, rather than preparing you for a specific industry certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completing this program, you will have the foundational knowledge to become your own boss, launching and operating various types of home-based businesses, such as an e-commerce store owner, freelance consultant, or a virtual service provider.
