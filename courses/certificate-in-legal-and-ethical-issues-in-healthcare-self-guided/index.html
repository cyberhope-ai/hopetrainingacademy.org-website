What is this field?

This program introduces the essential legal and ethical principles that govern the healthcare industry. You'll explore patient rights, confidentiality laws, professional responsibilities, and the framework for ethical decision-making in medical settings. Understanding these foundations is crucial for ensuring patient safety, maintaining trust, and adhering to regulatory standards.

Think of it like learning the rules of the road before you drive. Just as traffic laws ensure safety and order on highways, healthcare laws and ethics provide the guidelines for safe, respectful, and compliant patient care. This foundational knowledge helps prevent errors, protect patient privacy, and guide healthcare professionals in making sound, ethical choices every day.

How the field grew

1996Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) established, profoundly impacting patient privacy and data security.
2000sIncreased focus on patient-centered care and informed consent, emphasizing ethical treatment and communication.
2010Affordable Care Act (ACA) introduced, bringing new regulatory complexities and ethical considerations for access to care.
PresentOngoing evolution of telemedicine, AI in healthcare, and data analytics, continually reshaping legal and ethical boundaries.
