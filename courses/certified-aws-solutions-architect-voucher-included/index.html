What is this field?

An AWS Solutions Architect is a cloud computing professional who designs and implements distributed systems on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) platform. They are responsible for understanding client requirements and translating them into secure, scalable, and cost-effective cloud architectures using a wide array of AWS services. This role is crucial for organizations migrating to or optimizing their presence in the cloud.

Think of an AWS Solutions Architect as a master builder for digital infrastructure. Just as an architect designs a blueprint for a physical building, ensuring it's strong, efficient, and meets the owner's needs, an AWS Solutions Architect designs the digital 'blueprint' for applications and services in the cloud, making sure they are reliable, performant, and secure.

How the field grew

2006Amazon Web Services (AWS) officially launches, offering foundational cloud infrastructure services.
2013AWS introduces its first certification program, including the Solutions Architect - Associate credential, to validate cloud expertise.
2018AWS updates its Solutions Architect - Associate certification to reflect the evolving landscape of cloud services and best practices.
OngoingAWS regularly updates its certification exams to ensure they cover the latest services, features, and architectural patterns.
