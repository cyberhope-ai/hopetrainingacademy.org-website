What is this field?

The Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) credential, offered by the International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP), signifies a high level of expertise in office administration. It validates your proficiency across a broad spectrum of critical functions, from organizational communication and technology management to human resources and financial tasks. Earning your CAP demonstrates to employers that you possess the comprehensive knowledge and practical skills needed to manage complex administrative responsibilities and contribute significantly to an organization's success.

Think of a CAP as the central nervous system of an office. Just as the nervous system coordinates all bodily functions to keep an organism running smoothly, a CAP professional orchestrates the flow of information, manages resources, and ensures operational efficiency, allowing an organization to function effectively and achieve its goals. This program equips you with the tools to become that indispensable professional.

How the field grew

1942Founded as the National Secretaries Association (NSA) to elevate the secretarial profession.
1974Launched the Certified Professional Secretary (CPS) exam, the precursor to the CAP credential.
1981Renamed to Professional Secretaries International (PSI) to reflect a broader scope.
1998Rebranded as the International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) and introduced the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) credential, expanding its global reach and modernizing its focus.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites to enroll in this training program. However, to be eligible to sit for the IAAP CAP certification exam, you will need to meet specific experience requirements, which vary based on your education level (e.g., 4 years of relevant experience without a degree, 3 years with an Associate's, etc.).

Does this program include the CAP exam voucher?

Yes, this Certified Administrative Professional program includes the exam voucher, covering the fee for the current IAAP CAP certification exam. It also includes IAAP membership and a study guide.

How long will it take to complete this training program?

This program is self-paced and includes 100 course hours. The actual time to completion will depend on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Which CAP exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) certification exam offered by the International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP). The content is regularly updated to align with the latest exam objectives.

What kind of jobs can I get with a CAP certification?

A CAP certification can open doors to various roles such as Administrative Assistant, Executive Assistant, Office Manager, Administrative Coordinator, and other high-level support positions across diverse industries like education, technology, government, and healthcare.
