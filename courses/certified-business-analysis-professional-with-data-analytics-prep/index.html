What is this field?

A Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) is a senior expert who bridges the gap between business needs and technological solutions. They are adept at identifying business problems, gathering and analyzing requirements, and working with stakeholders to design and implement effective solutions. This role requires a deep understanding of business processes, strategic objectives, and the ability to communicate complex information clearly to diverse audiences.

This program elevates your expertise by integrating Certified Business Data Analyst (CBDA) skills, enabling you to leverage data to inform strategic decisions. Just as a master architect designs a building based on client needs and structural integrity, a CBAP with data analytics skills designs business solutions by understanding organizational goals, stakeholder input, and the critical insights hidden within data, ensuring robust and effective outcomes.

How the field grew

2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) is founded to promote the business analysis profession.
2006The Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide) Version 1.0 is released, establishing a common framework.
2008The Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP®) certification is launched for experienced practitioners.
2017BABOK® Guide Version 3.0 is released, providing the current comprehensive standard for business analysis.
2018The Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) is introduced, focusing on data-driven business insights.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

While a degree can be beneficial, the CBAP certification primarily requires significant professional experience in business analysis, typically 5-7 years, rather than a specific degree. Our program focuses on preparing you for the certification exams and real-world application.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program provides comprehensive training for the certification exams. Exam vouchers are not included and must be purchased separately through the IIBA.

How long will it take to complete this program?

Which certification exams does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for both the current IIBA Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP®) and the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exams.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be well-prepared for senior-level roles such as Senior Business Analyst, Data Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, or Process Improvement Consultant, equipped to lead complex projects and drive data-driven strategies.
