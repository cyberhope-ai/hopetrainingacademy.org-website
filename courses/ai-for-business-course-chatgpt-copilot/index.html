What is this field?

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a revolutionary technology that creates new content—from text and images to code—by learning from vast amounts of existing data. Unlike traditional AI that primarily analyzes information, Generative AI produces original outputs, acting as a powerful co-pilot for human creativity and problem-solving. Tools like ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot exemplify this, enabling users to generate ideas, draft documents, analyze data, and streamline workflows with unprecedented efficiency.

Imagine having a highly intelligent assistant who can instantly draft emails, summarize complex reports, brainstorm marketing ideas, or even create presentation slides based on simple instructions. This course teaches you how to effectively communicate with these AI tools, known as "prompt engineering," to harness their full potential across various business functions and personal tasks, making you significantly more productive and competitive in today's evolving job market.

How the field grew

1956The Dartmouth Workshop officially coins the term 'Artificial Intelligence,' marking the birth of the AI field.
2017Google introduces the Transformer neural network architecture, a foundational breakthrough for large language models (LLMs).
2022OpenAI launches ChatGPT, bringing advanced conversational AI to the mainstream and sparking widespread interest in Generative AI.
2023Microsoft integrates Copilot, an AI-powered assistant, across its Microsoft 365 suite, enhancing productivity in everyday applications.
