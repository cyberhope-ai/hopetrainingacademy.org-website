What is this field?

Social entrepreneurship is a dynamic field where individuals apply innovative business strategies to solve pressing societal problems. Unlike traditional businesses focused solely on profit, social enterprises prioritize creating positive social or environmental impact while maintaining financial sustainability. This approach allows for scalable, long-term solutions to community challenges.

Think of it like a community-focused chef: instead of just opening a restaurant for profit, a social entrepreneur chef might create a sustainable farm-to-table business that also trains at-risk youth, provides healthy meals to underserved populations, and reinvests profits into local food initiatives. It's about blending purpose with enterprise to achieve meaningful, lasting change.
