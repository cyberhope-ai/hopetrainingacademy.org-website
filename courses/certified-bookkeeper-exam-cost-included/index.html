What is this field?

A Certified Bookkeeper (CB) is a highly skilled professional responsible for maintaining accurate financial records for businesses and organizations. This role goes beyond basic data entry, encompassing a deep understanding of accounting principles, financial transactions, and compliance. CBs ensure that a company's financial health is transparent and well-documented, providing crucial information for decision-making and tax reporting.

Think of a Certified Bookkeeper as the meticulous financial historian and scorekeeper for a business. Just as a sports scorekeeper tracks every point, foul, and time-out to maintain the integrity of a game's record, a CB meticulously records every financial transaction, ensuring accuracy and compliance. They are essential for understanding a company's past performance and guiding its future financial strategies.

How the field grew

1987The American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers (AIPB) was founded to elevate the profession.
1999The AIPB introduced the Certified Bookkeeper (CB) designation, establishing a national standard for excellence.
OngoingThe CB designation continues to be recognized as a benchmark for professional competence in bookkeeping across various industries.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to become a Certified Bookkeeper?

No, a college degree is not required for the AIPB Certified Bookkeeper designation. However, this program is designed for experienced bookkeepers and requires at least two years (3,000 hours) of practical bookkeeping experience to qualify for the certification.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program prepares you thoroughly for the AIPB Certified Bookkeeper exam. Any specific details regarding exam costs or vouchers would be communicated directly by the AIPB or during your enrollment process.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Bookkeeper training program?

This self-paced program includes 140 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your study schedule and prior experience, but you will have ample time to prepare for the certification exam.

Which version of the AIPB Certified Bookkeeper exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current AIPB Certified Bookkeeper certification exam. The content is regularly updated to align with the latest exam objectives and industry standards.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon earning your Certified Bookkeeper designation, you'll be qualified for roles such as Certified Bookkeeper, Accounting Clerk, Payroll Specialist, or Junior Accountant, working across various industries.
