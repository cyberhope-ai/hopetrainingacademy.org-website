What is this field?

Speed reading is a set of techniques designed to increase your reading rate while maintaining or improving comprehension. The Merrill Ream method, taught in this program, focuses on practical strategies to eliminate inefficient reading habits, expand your visual span, and enhance your focus, allowing you to process information more quickly and effectively.

Think of it like upgrading from a bicycle to a high-performance car for navigating information. Instead of slowly pedaling through text, you'll learn to drive through it efficiently, absorbing key details and concepts at a significantly accelerated pace. This skill is invaluable for professionals, students, and anyone overwhelmed by the sheer volume of information in today's world.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Understanding Your Current Reading Habits

Assessing your baseline reading speed and comprehension, identifying common inefficiencies.

02
Module 2: Eliminating Inefficient Reading Behaviors

Techniques to reduce subvocalization, regression, and fixations for smoother reading.

03
Module 3: Expanding Your Visual Span

Strategies to perceive more words per glance and improve eye movement efficiency.

04
Module 4: Enhancing Comprehension and Retention

Active reading methods, critical thinking while reading, and memory techniques.

05
Module 5: Applying Speed Reading to Diverse Materials

Adapting techniques for different types of text, from reports to digital content.

06
Module 6: Sustaining and Measuring Your Progress

Practice routines, setting goals, and tools to track improvements in speed and understanding.

Frequently asked questions

Speed reading is a universal skill that enhances performance in nearly any role requiring significant reading, such as analysts, researchers, managers, and students, boosting overall productivity and information retention.
