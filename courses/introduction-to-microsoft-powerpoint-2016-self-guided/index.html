What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 is a powerful presentation software, now integrated with Office 365, designed to help you create dynamic and visually compelling slide shows. It's the industry standard for conveying information, ideas, and data in an engaging format, whether for business meetings, academic lectures, or personal projects. This training equips you with the skills to design, organize, and deliver presentations that capture attention and effectively communicate your message.

Think of PowerPoint as your digital canvas for storytelling. Instead of just speaking, you can illustrate your points with text, images, charts, and videos, guiding your audience through complex information with clarity and impact. This program focuses on practical application, teaching you how to leverage features like slide and layout masters to maintain a consistent, professional look across all your presentations with minimal effort, making global design changes a snap.

How the field grew

1987Forethought Inc. develops 'Presenter,' the precursor to PowerPoint.
1987Microsoft acquires 'Presenter' and renames it PowerPoint, marking its entry into the presentation software market.
1990PowerPoint 2.0 for Windows is released, significantly expanding its user base on the dominant PC platform.
2007PowerPoint 2007 introduces the 'Ribbon' interface, a major user experience overhaul that became standard across Microsoft Office.
2016Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 is released, enhancing collaboration features, presentation tools, and integration with cloud services.
