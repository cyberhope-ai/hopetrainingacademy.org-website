What is this field?

Medical Billing Specialists are the financial navigators of the healthcare system, ensuring that services provided are accurately coded, billed, and reimbursed. They translate complex medical procedures into standardized codes, submit claims to insurance companies, and manage the entire revenue cycle to keep healthcare facilities financially healthy. Without their expertise, providers would struggle to receive payment for the vital care they deliver.

Electronic Health Records (EHR) Specialists, on the other hand, are the meticulous librarians of patient health data. They manage and maintain digital patient records, ensuring timely access to accurate information crucial for patient safety, treatment, and operational efficiency. Together, these roles form the backbone of modern healthcare administration, combining financial precision with digital data management to support seamless patient care.

How the field grew

1960sIntroduction of Medicare and Medicaid, significantly increasing the complexity of medical billing.
1996HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) enacted, establishing national standards for patient data privacy and security.
2009HITECH Act promotes widespread adoption and meaningful use of Electronic Health Records (EHRs) across healthcare.
2015Implementation of ICD-10-CM/PCS coding system, requiring greater specificity in diagnostic and procedural coding.
