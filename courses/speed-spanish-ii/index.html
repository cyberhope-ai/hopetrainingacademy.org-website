What is this field?

Speed Spanish II is an interactive online program designed to build upon foundational Spanish skills, focusing on practical communication for real-world scenarios. Through engaging lessons, you'll see and hear words pronounced correctly, gaining ample opportunities to practice and develop conversational fluency. This program is ideal for those looking to expand their Spanish vocabulary and grammar for everyday use.

Think of it like learning to navigate a new city. Speed Spanish II provides you with the essential phrases and cultural insights to confidently explore conversations, helping you understand directions, order food, and connect with locals more effectively, making your journey smoother and more enjoyable.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in Speed Spanish II?

No, this program has no degree prerequisites. It's designed for anyone looking to build practical Spanish skills.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

This course focuses on practical language acquisition and does not include an external certification exam or voucher.

How long does it take to complete Speed Spanish II?

The course is self-paced and typically takes about 24 hours of engagement over six weeks, though you can work at your own speed.

What job can I get after completing Speed Spanish II?

While not a direct job-training course, Speed Spanish II significantly enhances your marketability for roles requiring bilingual communication, such as customer service, healthcare support, sales, and community outreach.

What is the current exam version covered?

Speed Spanish II is a language acquisition course focused on practical communication skills, not preparation for a specific certification exam.
