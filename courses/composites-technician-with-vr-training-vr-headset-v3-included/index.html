What is this field?

Composites technicians are skilled professionals who work with advanced materials like carbon fiber and fiberglass, which are stronger and lighter than traditional metals. They are essential in industries from aerospace and automotive to marine and wind energy, fabricating, assembling, and repairing components that demand high performance. This program equips you with the expertise to handle these cutting-edge materials.

Think of it like building with advanced LEGOs, but for airplanes or high-performance cars. You're not just snapping pieces together; you're understanding the science behind each material, precisely cutting, molding, and bonding them, and ensuring every component meets rigorous quality standards. Our VR training lets you practice these complex tasks in a safe, simulated environment before you step onto the production floor.

How the field grew

1940sFiberglass composites gain prominence, revolutionizing boat building and other industries.
1960sCarbon fiber and other advanced composites emerge, critical for aerospace and high-performance applications.
2010sVirtual Reality (VR) technology begins its integration into industrial training for hands-on skill development.
2020sVR becomes a cornerstone of technical education, offering immersive, risk-free practice for complex manufacturing processes.
