What is this field?

An Executive Assistant is far more than a traditional secretary; they are strategic partners who provide high-level administrative support to top executives, ensuring the smooth operation of an organization's leadership. This critical role demands exceptional organizational skills, proactive problem-solving, and the ability to manage complex schedules, communications, and projects, often acting as a gatekeeper and information conduit, and this program prepares you for the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) exam.

Think of an Executive Assistant as the highly skilled air traffic controller for a busy airport. Just as the controller manages complex flight paths, communication, and unexpected events to keep operations flowing safely and efficiently, an Executive Assistant expertly navigates an executive's demanding schedule, priorities, and communications, ensuring everything runs on time and without collision.

How the field grew

1942International Association of Administrative Professionals (IAAP) founded
1974IAAP establishes the Certified Professional Secretary (CPS) certification
2001CPS certification evolves into the Certified Administrative Professional (CAP) to reflect changing roles
PresentCAP remains a leading credential for administrative professionals globally
