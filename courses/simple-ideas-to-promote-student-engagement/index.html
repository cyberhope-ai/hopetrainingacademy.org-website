What is this field?

Student engagement is the active involvement of students in their learning process, encompassing their behavior, cognitive effort, and emotional connection to educational activities. It's crucial for effective learning, improved academic outcomes, and fostering a positive classroom environment where students feel valued and motivated.

Think of student engagement like the fuel in a car: without it, even the best engine won't go anywhere. Engaged students are fueled by curiosity and purpose, actively participating, thinking critically, and connecting emotionally with the material, rather than passively observing. This course provides practical tools to ignite that fuel in your classroom.
