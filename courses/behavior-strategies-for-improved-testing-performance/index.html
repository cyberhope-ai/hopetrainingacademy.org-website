What is this field?

This professional development course equips educators with practical methods to analyze, support, and teach testing behaviors, enabling students to perform their best on assessments. You'll gain a deep understanding of the psychological and behavioral factors that influence student performance during tests, moving beyond traditional study techniques to address the underlying challenges.

Think of it like a sports coach who not only teaches game strategy but also helps athletes manage pre-game anxiety, focus under pressure, and develop mental resilience. This program provides you with the tools to be that coach for your students, helping them develop the behavioral skills necessary to approach tests with confidence and demonstrate their true knowledge.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for professional development and does not require a specific degree for enrollment. It is beneficial for current educators and those in instructional roles.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, an exam voucher is not included with this course. This program focuses on professional development strategies rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this online course?

This course is 100% online and self-paced, allowing you to complete the modules at your convenience according to your schedule.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This course focuses on professional development strategies for educators and does not lead to an external certification exam. Its goal is to enhance your skills in supporting student testing performance.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

This training enhances the skills of current educators, instructional coordinators, and educational support professionals, enabling them to more effectively help students succeed in academic assessments.
