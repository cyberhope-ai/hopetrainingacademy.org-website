What is this field?

The Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification validates your expertise in implementing, managing, and troubleshooting enterprise-level networks. This comprehensive training program covers both core enterprise network technologies (ENCOR) and advanced routing services (ENARSI), equipping you with the in-depth knowledge needed to design, secure, automate, and maintain complex network infrastructures.

Think of it like becoming a master architect and builder for a city's entire transportation system. You learn not just how to lay roads (basic routing) but also how to design complex interchanges, manage traffic flow efficiently, implement security checkpoints, and even automate signal systems across the whole metropolis. This program elevates your skills from managing individual network devices to architecting and optimizing entire enterprise networks.

How the field grew

Early 1990sCisco introduces its first professional certification programs to validate networking expertise.
1998The Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert (CCIE) emerges as a top-tier industry credential.
Early 2000sCisco expands its certification hierarchy, establishing Associate (CCNA) and Professional (CCNP) levels across various technology tracks.
2020Cisco revamps its entire certification portfolio, consolidating and modernizing tracks to align with current industry demands, including the new CCNP Enterprise.

Inside the course, module by module

01
ENCOR: Enterprise Network Core Technologies

Campus architecture, device fundamentals, VLANs, STP, EtherChannels, OSPFv2, EBGP, NAT, VPNs, VRF, management protocols, security, and next-gen campus networks.

02
ENCOR: Network Automation and Programmability

SD-Access, LISP, VXLAN, SD-WAN, DNA Center, data formats, data modeling, NetConf, RestConf, Python, Chef, Puppet, Ansible, SaltStack.

03
ENARSI: Advanced Routing Protocols

EIGRPv4/v6, Named EIGRP, OSPFv2/v3, OSPF Address-Families, Multi-Protocol BGPv4, BGP core operations, and troubleshooting.

04
ENARSI: Redistribution and Advanced Routing Control

Redistribution configuration, filtering, challenges, IPv6 redistribution, Policy-Based Routing, and IPSLA.

05
ENARSI: VPN and MPLS Technologies

VRF-Lite, MPLS overview, MPLS L3 VPNs, GRE review, DMVPN configuration, and DMVPN IPSec integration.

06
ENARSI: Infrastructure Services and Security

Device access, authentication, IOS management tools, Syslog, SNMP, NetFlow, DHCPv4/v6, uRPF, CoPP, IPv4/IPv6 ACLs, and IPv6 First Hop Security.
