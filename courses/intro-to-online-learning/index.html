What is this field?

Online learning instruction involves adapting traditional teaching methods and materials for effective delivery in a digital environment. It encompasses everything from designing virtual lesson plans and managing online classrooms to fostering student engagement and ensuring equitable access for all learners.

Think of it like a seasoned chef learning to master a new, state-of-the-art kitchen. While the core culinary principles remain, they must adapt their techniques, utilize new equipment, and understand the nuances of the digital space to consistently deliver high-quality, engaging educational experiences.

How the field grew

Early 2000sRise of basic Learning Management Systems (LMS) for course delivery.
Mid-2010sExpansion of Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) and blended learning models.
2020Global pandemic rapidly accelerates the necessity and adoption of online instruction.
PresentContinuous innovation in educational technology and remote teaching methodologies.
