What is this field?

The Microsoft Excel Expert certification validates your advanced proficiency in the world's most widely used spreadsheet software. This credential demonstrates your ability to manipulate complex data, automate tasks, and create sophisticated reports, setting you apart as a master of data management and analysis.

Think of it like being a master architect for data. Instead of just building basic structures, you're designing intricate systems, optimizing flows, and creating stunning visualizations that reveal hidden patterns and drive critical business decisions.

How the field grew

1985Microsoft Excel 1.0 released for Macintosh, revolutionizing spreadsheet software.
1987Excel for Windows launched, quickly becoming the industry standard for business.
1990sExcel becomes integral to business operations, with increasing complexity and features.
2000sIntroduction of advanced features like PivotTables, VBA macros, and robust data analysis tools.
TodayContinues to evolve with cloud integration (Microsoft 365) and powerful new functions for modern data challenges.
