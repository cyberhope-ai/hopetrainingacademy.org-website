What is this field?

Web design is the art and science of creating the visual layout and user experience of websites. It involves a blend of graphic design, user interface (UI) design, and user experience (UX) principles to ensure a site is not only aesthetically pleasing but also intuitive, functional, and accessible to all users.

Think of a website as a digital storefront or a public library. Just as a well-designed physical space guides visitors, a well-designed website makes information easy to find and interactions smooth, encouraging engagement and trust. This program teaches you to craft these digital spaces effectively.

How the field grew

1990sEmergence of the World Wide Web with basic HTML and text-based sites.
2000sRise of CSS for styling, Flash for interactivity, and early content management systems.
22010sFocus on responsive design for mobile devices, user experience (UX), and web accessibility.
2020sIntegration of AI, advanced animation, and emphasis on performance and security.
