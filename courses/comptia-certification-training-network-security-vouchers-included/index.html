What is this field?

Network and security professionals are the architects and guardians of our digital world. They are responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining the complex infrastructure that allows computers and devices to communicate, while simultaneously defending these systems from an ever-evolving landscape of cyber threats. Their work ensures that data flows securely and reliably, underpinning nearly every modern organization and service.

Think of it like building and protecting a city's vital utilities. Network professionals lay down the roads, power lines, and communication cables, ensuring everything connects and operates efficiently. Security professionals act as the city's defense force, constantly monitoring for intruders, fortifying critical infrastructure, and responding swiftly to any breaches to keep the entire system and its inhabitants safe and operational.

How the field grew

1982CompTIA founded to advance the global tech industry through education and certification.
1990sCompTIA introduces early certifications, establishing benchmarks for IT professional skills.
1999CompTIA Network+ certification launched, becoming a foundational credential for networking careers.
2002CompTIA Security+ certification introduced, addressing the growing need for cybersecurity expertise.
TodayCompTIA certifications remain industry benchmarks, validating essential skills for IT and cybersecurity roles worldwide.
