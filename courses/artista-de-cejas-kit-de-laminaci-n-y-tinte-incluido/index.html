What is this field?

An eyebrow artist is a skilled beauty professional specializing in enhancing and shaping eyebrows through various techniques. This program equips you with the expertise in eyebrow design, microblading, lamination, and henna application, alongside essential digital literacy, marketing, and business development skills. You'll learn to create customized brow looks that complement individual facial features, helping clients achieve their desired aesthetic.

Think of an eyebrow artist as an architect for the face. Just as an architect designs structures that are both functional and beautiful, an eyebrow artist meticulously crafts and refines brows to frame the eyes, balance facial symmetry, and enhance natural beauty, building a foundation for confidence and style.

How the field grew

Ancient EgyptKohl used to define and darken brows, symbolizing status.
1920s-1930sThin, elongated, and often downturned brows gain popularity.
1980sEmphasis shifts to fuller, natural, and untamed brows.
2010sMicroblading emerges as a semi-permanent brow enhancement technique.
2020sEyebrow lamination and henna tinting become widespread trends.
