What is this field?

A Veterinary Office Manager is the backbone of an animal care facility, ensuring smooth daily operations beyond direct patient care. This vital role combines administrative expertise with a deep understanding of veterinary practice, overseeing everything from financial management and client relations to staff coordination and inventory control. They are essential for maintaining a thriving and efficient clinic environment.

Our career training program equips you with the comprehensive skills needed to excel in this dynamic position. You'll learn critical business management techniques, master essential software like Microsoft Office and QuickBooks Online, and gain foundational knowledge in veterinary assistant duties. Think of it like being the conductor of an orchestra, where every instrument (staff, finances, supplies, clients) plays in harmony under your expert direction to deliver the best possible care.

How the field grew

1950sRise of specialized veterinary clinics, increasing administrative complexity.
1980sIntroduction of early computer systems for enhanced record-keeping in practices.
2000sGrowth of large animal hospitals and multi-doctor practices, demanding dedicated management.
2010sDigital transformation and cloud-based software streamline practice management.
PresentComplex regulatory environments and advanced medical care require highly skilled office managers.

What the career pays

Veterinary Office Managers orchestrate the non-clinical aspects of animal care facilities, ensuring efficient operations and exceptional client service. They manage staff, finances, and administrative tasks, allowing veterinary professionals to focus on patient health.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our Veterinary Office Manager career training program. This program is designed to provide you with the practical skills and knowledge needed for the role.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, upon successful completion of the program, you will receive a voucher to sit for the QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) exam, covering the cost of the certification test.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This self-paced program includes 382 course hours. The actual completion time depends on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program specifically prepares you for the current QuickBooks Certified User (QBCU) certification exam, validating your proficiency in QuickBooks Online.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for roles such as Veterinary Practice Manager, Animal Hospital Administrator, Clinic Coordinator, or Veterinary Office Supervisor, managing the business operations of animal care facilities.
