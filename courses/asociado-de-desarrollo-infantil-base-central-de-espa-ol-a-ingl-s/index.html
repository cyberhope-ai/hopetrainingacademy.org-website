What is this field?

The Child Development Associate (CDA) training program is your pathway to a rewarding career in early childhood education. This comprehensive program is specifically designed for Spanish-speaking individuals seeking to enhance their English language skills while gaining the foundational knowledge and practical competencies required to become a certified CDA. You'll learn about child development from birth through age five, covering physical, cognitive, social, and emotional growth, all aligned with the eight CDA competencies and NAEYC professional standards.

Think of it like building a strong bridge between your existing knowledge and a new professional opportunity. You'll start by strengthening your English communication skills, specifically tailored for the early childhood education field. As your language proficiency grows, you'll delve into expert-led modules that prepare you for the CDA credential, including portfolio development, verification visit preparation, and understanding curriculum planning. This dual-focus approach ensures you're not only credentialed but also confident in communicating effectively in your new career.

How the field grew

1975The Child Development Associate (CDA) credential is first introduced as a national professional credential for early childhood educators.
1985The Council for Professional Recognition is established to administer the CDA credential, ensuring its quality and standards.
2013The CDA credential undergoes a significant renewal process, updating its competencies and assessment methods to reflect current best practices in early childhood education.
PresentThe CDA remains the most widely recognized credential in early childhood education, serving as a vital stepping stone for career advancement.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll. This program is designed to prepare you for the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, which is a nationally recognized professional certification for early childhood educators.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CDA assessment?

No, an exam voucher for the CDA assessment is not included with this training program. You will be responsible for obtaining your CDA assessment directly through the Council for Professional Recognition.

How long does it take to complete the CDA training program?

The program requires approximately 280 course hours. Since it is self-paced, the total completion time will vary based on your individual study schedule and prior English language proficiency.

Which CDA exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Child Development Associate (CDA) credential assessment administered by the Council for Professional Recognition, ensuring you are ready for the latest standards.

What kind of jobs can I get after earning my CDA credential?

With a CDA credential, you can pursue roles such as a preschool teacher, lead teacher in a childcare center, family child care provider, or an early childhood educator in various learning environments.
