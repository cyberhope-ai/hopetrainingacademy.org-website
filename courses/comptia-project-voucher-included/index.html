What is this field?

CompTIA Project+ is a globally recognized certification that validates the fundamental skills needed to manage small-to-medium-size projects effectively. It covers the entire project lifecycle, from initiation and planning to execution, monitoring, and closure, ensuring you can navigate common challenges and deliver successful outcomes. Unlike some certifications focused solely on IT, Project+ is designed for professionals across various industries, making your skills highly transferable.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. Just as a conductor ensures all musicians play in harmony, on cue, and contribute to the final masterpiece, a project manager coordinates diverse teams, resources, and timelines to achieve a specific goal. This program equips you with the baton and the sheet music to lead your team through complex projects, ensuring every 'note' is played correctly and the 'performance' is a success.

How the field grew

2001CompTIA Project+ certification first launched, establishing a vendor-neutral standard for project management skills.
Early 2010sThe certification evolved to reflect the growing demand for project management expertise across diverse business sectors, beyond traditional IT.
Mid 2010s-PresentContinual updates ensure the certification covers modern project methodologies, including Agile and hybrid frameworks, and addresses contemporary business challenges.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, a degree is not required. While CompTIA recommends at least 12 months of cumulative project management experience or equivalent education, this program is designed to provide the knowledge needed to prepare for the certification exam.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, your enrollment in the CompTIA Project+ training program includes a voucher for the certification exam, providing prepaid access to sit for the certifying exam upon eligibility.

How long will it take to complete the program?

This self-paced program consists of 75 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study schedule and pace.

Which CompTIA Project+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current CompTIA Project+ certification exam, ensuring your knowledge is up-to-date with the latest industry standards and best practices.

What kind of job can I get with CompTIA Project+ certification?

With CompTIA Project+ certification, you can pursue roles such as Project Coordinator, Junior Project Manager, Business Analyst, or Operations Specialist, in various industries both inside and outside of IT.
