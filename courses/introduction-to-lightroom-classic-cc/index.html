What is this field?

Adobe Lightroom Classic CC is a powerful, industry-standard software designed for professional photographers and enthusiasts to manage, edit, and organize vast libraries of digital images. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools for everything from basic exposure adjustments to advanced color grading and retouching, all within a non-destructive editing environment.

Think of Lightroom Classic CC as your ultimate digital darkroom and photo library combined. Instead of physical negatives and prints, you have a centralized catalog for all your digital photos, allowing you to quickly find, enhance, and share your work with unparalleled efficiency and creative control.

How the field grew

2007Adobe Photoshop Lightroom 1.0 is officially released, offering a new paradigm for digital photo management and editing.
2017Adobe rebrands the desktop version as Lightroom Classic CC, emphasizing its traditional, file-based workflow for professional photographers.
2019Significant performance improvements are introduced, enhancing speed and responsiveness for large catalogs and complex edits.
2020sContinued integration of AI-powered features and ongoing enhancements to editing tools, ensuring its position as a leading professional photo editor.
