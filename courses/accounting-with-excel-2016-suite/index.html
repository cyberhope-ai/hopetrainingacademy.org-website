What is this field?

This training program is designed to equip you with a dual mastery: foundational accounting principles and advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel. You'll learn how to understand, manage, and report financial data effectively, leveraging Excel's powerful capabilities to streamline processes and enhance accuracy in various business settings.

Think of it like learning to navigate a complex city (accounting) while simultaneously mastering a high-performance vehicle with advanced GPS (Excel). You'll not only understand the rules of the road but also how to use the best tools to get to your financial destinations efficiently, making you an invaluable asset in any organization.

How the field grew

1979VisiCalc, the first electronic spreadsheet program, revolutionizes financial planning.
1985Microsoft releases Excel 1.0, beginning its journey to become the industry standard for spreadsheets.
Early 2000sIncreased regulatory scrutiny (e.g., Sarbanes-Oxley Act) heightens demand for meticulous financial record-keeping and skilled Excel users in accounting.
2016Microsoft Excel 2016 is released, introducing new features for data analysis, charting, and collaboration, which are central to modern accounting practices.

Jobs it opens

Bookkeeper

Manages daily financial transactions, reconciles accounts, and maintains ledgers.

Accounting Assistant

Supports accounting departments with data entry, report generation, and administrative tasks.

Accounts Payable/Receivable Clerk

Processes invoices, manages vendor payments, or handles incoming customer payments.

Financial Data Entry Specialist

Focuses on accurately inputting and organizing financial information into various systems, often using Excel.
