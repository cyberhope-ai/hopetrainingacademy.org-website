What is this field?

The Grammar Refresher II program is designed for individuals seeking to elevate their command of the English language beyond foundational principles. This self-guided online experience focuses on advanced language mechanics, complex sentence structures, and nuanced rules of punctuation, empowering you to produce sophisticated and error-free written communication.

Think of it like a master chef honing their knife skills. While basic chopping gets the job done, precision cuts transform a dish. This program provides the linguistic tools to make your writing not just correct, but truly impactful, ensuring your message is always clear, credible, and compelling.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Mastering Advanced Punctuation

Explore complex uses of commas, semicolons, colons, dashes, and parentheses.

02
Module 2: Crafting Complex Sentence Structures

Learn to construct varied sentence types, including compound-complex and parallel structures.

03
Module 3: Understanding Verbals and Moods

Differentiate between gerunds, infinitives, participles, and master subjunctive mood.

04
Module 4: Precision in Pronoun Usage

Address advanced pronoun agreement, case, and common ambiguous references.

05
Module 5: Eliminating Common Grammatical Pitfalls

Identify and correct run-on sentences, fragments, and tricky subject-verb agreement issues.

06
Module 6: Refining Word Choice and Style

Improve vocabulary, differentiate confusing words, and write with conciseness and impact.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this program?

No, this program focuses on practical skills development, making it accessible regardless of prior academic degrees. Strong foundational writing skills are beneficial.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this course is designed to enhance your grammar and writing skills and does not conclude with a certification exam or voucher.

How long does it take to complete this course?

This self-guided online course allows you to learn at your own pace, with access to materials for a flexible period to accommodate your schedule.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is a skill-building course focused on advanced grammar and language mechanics, not preparation for a specific certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get with improved grammar skills?

Strong grammar skills are essential for roles like technical writer, editor, content creator, marketing specialist, and any professional position requiring clear, credible written communication.
