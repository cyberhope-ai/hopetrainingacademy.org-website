What is this field?

This program empowers educators to harness the full potential of SMART Board and SMART Notebook technology. You'll learn to design and deliver dynamic, interactive lessons that go beyond traditional teaching methods, fostering a more engaging and effective learning experience for students. It's about turning your classroom into a vibrant hub of discovery.

Think of a SMART Board as a giant, interactive tablet that replaces your traditional whiteboard or projector. Instead of just showing content, you can touch, write, move objects, and collaborate directly on the screen. This training teaches you how to orchestrate this powerful tool, much like a conductor leads an orchestra, to create harmonious and impactful learning symphonies for your students.

How the field grew

1991Introduction of the first SMART Board, pioneering interactive whiteboard technology for classrooms.
Early 2000sWidespread adoption of interactive whiteboards, transforming traditional teaching methods globally.
Mid-2000sDevelopment of SMART Notebook software as the industry standard for creating interactive lesson content.
PresentContinuous evolution of interactive display technology and software, integrating with modern blended learning environments.
