What is this field?

Differentiated assessment is a teaching approach that tailors evaluation methods to meet the diverse learning needs, styles, and abilities of individual students within a classroom. Instead of a one-size-fits-all test, it involves using a variety of assessment tools and strategies to accurately measure what each student knows and can do, while accounting for their unique strengths and challenges.

Think of it like a chef preparing a meal for guests with different dietary restrictions. Instead of serving the same dish to everyone, a skilled chef offers variations – perhaps a gluten-free option, a vegetarian dish, or a less spicy version – ensuring that each guest can enjoy and benefit from the meal. Similarly, differentiated assessment ensures every student has a fair opportunity to demonstrate their understanding, leading to more accurate insights into their learning and progress.
