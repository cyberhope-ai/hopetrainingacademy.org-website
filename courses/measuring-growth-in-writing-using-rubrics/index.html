What is this field?

This specialized online training empowers educators to master the art of assessing and fostering writing development in young learners from kindergarten through third grade. You'll delve into typical writing expectations for this crucial age group, explore common writing tasks, and gain the expertise to create robust assessment tools. The core of the program focuses on designing both holistic and analytic rubrics, providing a clear framework to evaluate student work and guide their progress.

Think of a rubric as a detailed map for student writing. Instead of just knowing if a student reached the destination, a rubric shows you exactly where they are on the journey, what they've accomplished, and what steps they need to take next. This course teaches you how to draw these maps effectively, ensuring consistent, fair, and actionable feedback that drives genuine improvement in young writers.

How the field grew

1980sEmergence of performance-based assessment in education, shifting focus from rote memorization to demonstrating skills.
1990sWidespread adoption of rubrics in K-12 education as a transparent tool for evaluating complex tasks like writing.
Early 2000sIncreased emphasis on clear learning objectives, student self-assessment, and using rubrics for actionable feedback.
PresentIntegration of rubrics into data-driven instruction and personalized learning approaches to track individual student growth.
