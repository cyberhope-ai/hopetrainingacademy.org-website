What is this field?

Paralegals are indispensable members of the legal team, performing critical tasks that support attorneys in various settings, from law firms to corporate legal departments and government agencies. They conduct essential legal research, draft key documents, and manage case information, ensuring the smooth operation of legal processes and contributing significantly to successful outcomes.

Think of a paralegal as the highly skilled navigator and co-pilot for a legal journey. While the attorney is the pilot steering the case, the paralegal meticulously charts the course, gathers all necessary information, and prepares the flight plan, making sure every detail is accurate and ready for takeoff.

How the field grew

1970sThe paralegal profession begins to gain formal recognition and structure within the legal system.
1975The National Association of Legal Assistants (NALA) is established to promote professional development and standards.
1976NALA introduces the Certified Paralegal (CP) exam, setting a benchmark for professional competency.
2000sTechnology integration, including advanced legal research platforms, transforms paralegal responsibilities.
PresentParalegals are essential, highly skilled professionals in diverse legal environments, with growing demand.
