What is this field?

A Certified Credit and Financial Health Counselor is a trusted guide who helps individuals navigate the complexities of personal finance, from managing debt and improving credit scores to budgeting and planning for long-term financial goals. You'll learn to assess a client's current financial situation, identify challenges, and develop personalized strategies to improve their economic well-being.

Think of it like a personal trainer for finances. Just as a fitness coach helps clients set and achieve health goals, a financial counselor provides the knowledge, tools, and motivation for clients to build strong financial habits, overcome obstacles, and ultimately reach their financial aspirations. This program equips you with both the practical financial knowledge and the effective counseling techniques needed to make a real difference.

How the field grew

1960sEmergence of consumer credit counseling services as personal debt grew significantly.
1970sEstablishment of non-profit credit counseling agencies across the United States to assist consumers.
1990sIncreased emphasis on financial literacy and education, expanding beyond just debt management.
Early 2000sExpansion into holistic financial health counseling, driven by economic shifts and consumer needs.
PresentIntegration of digital tools and a focus on comprehensive financial well-being, including credit, savings, and future planning.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Introduction to Financial Counseling

Defining the role, components of financial well-being, and establishing client relationships.

02
Client Engagement & Communication

Rapport building, active listening, asking effective questions, and understanding client variables.

03
Demystifying Consumer Credit

Banking basics, credit fundamentals, wise credit use, and understanding credit reports/scoring.

04
Understanding and Dealing with Debt

Causes of debt, debt collection, consequences of unpaid debt, and strategies for getting out of debt.

05
Specialized Financial Areas

Housing, student financial aid, retirement planning, and the Debt Management Plan.

06
Ethics & Consumer Protection

Ethical guidelines for counselors, consumer resources, and relevant consumer protection legislation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific educational prerequisites required to enroll in the Certified Credit and Financial Health Counselor program. It is designed to be accessible to individuals eager to start a new career path.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program does not include an exam voucher. You will receive two certifications directly from the National Association of Certified Credit Counselors upon successful completion and passing the final exams within the course.

How long does it take to complete the program?

You will have six months to complete the 105 course hours. The program is self-paced, allowing you to study at your convenience within this timeframe.

What certifications will I earn?

Upon successful completion and passing the final examinations, you will earn two distinct designations: a Financial Health Counseling Certification and a Credit Counseling Certification from the National Association of Certified Credit Counselors.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Graduates are prepared for roles such as Certified Credit Counselor, Financial Health Counselor, Housing Counselor, and Student Loan Counselor, working to guide clients toward financial stability and well-being.
