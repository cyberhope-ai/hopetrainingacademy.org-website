What is this field?

A business plan is a comprehensive document that outlines a company's objectives, strategies, financial projections, and operational details. It serves as a strategic roadmap, guiding entrepreneurs and managers through the process of launching, managing, and growing a successful enterprise. This foundational document is critical for clarifying vision, attracting investors, and making informed decisions.

Think of a business plan like a detailed blueprint for building a house. Just as a blueprint guides contractors through every step from foundation to roof, a business plan provides a structured guide for your venture, ensuring every component — from market analysis to financial forecasts — is carefully considered and aligned for long-term success.

How the field grew

1950sPost-WWII economic boom spurs formal business management and strategic planning.
1970sRise of venture capital necessitates detailed business plans for securing investment.
1990sDot-com era introduces concepts of lean startups and agile business planning.
2010sDigital transformation and global markets demand dynamic, data-driven business strategies.
