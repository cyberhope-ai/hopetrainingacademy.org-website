What is this field?

Introduction to Networking provides the essential knowledge and practical skills needed to understand how computer networks operate. This program covers fundamental concepts from network types and components to IP addressing, protocols, and basic security measures. You'll learn the language of networking and gain a solid foundation for further specialization in IT.

Think of a network like a city's road system. Just as roads connect different buildings and allow traffic to flow, a computer network connects devices like computers, phones, and servers, enabling them to share information and resources. This program teaches you how to build, manage, and troubleshoot these digital roadways, ensuring smooth and secure communication.

How the field grew

1969ARPANET launched, laying the groundwork for modern packet-switched networks.
1983TCP/IP protocol suite became the standard for ARPANET, forming the backbone of the internet.
1990The World Wide Web was introduced, making networking accessible to a wider audience.
2000sWidespread adoption of Wi-Fi and broadband internet revolutionized home and business connectivity.
2010sCloud computing and the Internet of Things (IoT) significantly expanded the scale and complexity of network infrastructures.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals seeking to enter the IT field without a prior degree. Our focus is on practical skills and career readiness.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

No, this Introduction to Networking program does not include an exam voucher as it is a foundational course, not tied to a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online training program, allowing you to learn at your own speed. Completion time varies based on individual study habits and schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program provides foundational networking knowledge crucial for many entry-level IT roles, but it is not directly aligned with a specific certification exam. It builds the groundwork for future certifications like CompTIA Network+.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for entry-level roles such as Network Technician, Help Desk Specialist, or IT Support Specialist, with a strong understanding of networking fundamentals.
