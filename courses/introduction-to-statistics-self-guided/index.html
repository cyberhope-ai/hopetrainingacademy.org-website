What is this field?

Statistics is the science of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, presenting, and organizing data. In today's data-driven world, understanding statistics is crucial for making informed decisions across virtually every industry, from business and healthcare to science and government. This foundational course equips you with the essential tools to make sense of numerical information.

Think of statistics as a powerful lens that helps you see patterns and draw conclusions from a sea of numbers. Just as a detective uses clues to solve a mystery, a statistician uses data to uncover insights, predict trends, and evaluate outcomes, transforming raw information into actionable knowledge.

How the field grew

17th CenturyFoundations of probability theory laid, enabling early data analysis.
19th CenturyDevelopment of modern statistical inference and experimental design.
Mid-20th CenturyEmergence of computing power, revolutionizing complex statistical calculations.
21st CenturyStatistics becomes a core discipline for data science and big data analytics.
