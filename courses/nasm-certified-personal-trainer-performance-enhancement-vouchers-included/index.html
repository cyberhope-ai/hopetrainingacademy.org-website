What is this field?

The NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) and Performance Enhancement Specialist (PES) program equips you with the scientific knowledge and practical skills to guide individuals toward their health and fitness goals. You'll learn to assess client needs, design personalized exercise programs, and motivate them to achieve lasting results, whether they're looking to lose weight, build muscle, or improve overall well-being.

Beyond general fitness, the Performance Enhancement Specialist credential focuses on optimizing athletic potential. Think of it like being a specialized coach for athletes, from weekend warriors to professionals. You'll master techniques to improve speed, agility, power, and injury prevention, helping clients reach peak physical condition and excel in their chosen sport or activity.

How the field grew

1987National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) founded, pioneering evidence-based fitness education.
Early 2000sNASM introduces the Optimum Performance Training (OPT) Model, a systematic approach to program design.
2007NASM CPT program receives NCCA accreditation, solidifying its industry recognition and credibility.
OngoingContinuous curriculum updates and development of advanced specializations like Performance Enhancement Specialist (PES).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll. This program is designed to provide you with the specific knowledge and certifications needed to become a successful personal trainer and performance enhancement specialist.

Does this program include exam vouchers?

Yes, this program includes vouchers for both the NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) exam and the NASM Performance Enhancement Specialist (PES) exam.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced program with 140 course hours. While completion time varies by student, you can typically finish within 3-6 months, depending on your study schedule.

Which NASM exam version does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current, NCCA-accredited NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) examination and the NASM Performance Enhancement Specialist (PES) certification.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be prepared for roles such as Certified Personal Trainer, Health Fitness Specialist, Private Personal Trainer, or Community Health Specialist, working in gyms, wellness centers, or independently.
