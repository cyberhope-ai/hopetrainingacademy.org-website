What is this field?

Academic discourse refers to the structured, purposeful conversations that drive deeper learning in educational settings. It moves beyond simple question-and-answer sessions, encouraging students to analyze, synthesize, and articulate their understanding through thoughtful dialogue and debate.

This program equips educators with the essential tools to design, facilitate, and implement these powerful discussions. Think of it like upgrading a traditional lecture into a dynamic, interactive workshop where every student actively contributes to building knowledge and critical thinking skills.
