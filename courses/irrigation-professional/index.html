What is this field?

An Irrigation Professional is a skilled technician responsible for the installation, maintenance, diagnosis, and repair of irrigation systems in residential, commercial, and municipal settings. This role is crucial for ensuring efficient water usage, maintaining healthy landscapes, and implementing sustainable water management practices. With the increasing demand for water conservation and smart landscaping solutions, these professionals are vital in optimizing system performance and extending equipment lifespan.

Think of an Irrigation Professional as a landscape's 'plumber and electrician' combined. They not only ensure water flows where it's needed but also troubleshoot complex electrical issues, program smart controllers, and optimize systems for maximum efficiency, much like a smart home technician optimizes your home's energy usage. Their expertise keeps green spaces thriving while minimizing water waste.

How the field grew

1960sWidespread adoption of automated sprinkler systems for residential and commercial landscapes.
1980sEmergence of drip irrigation technology, significantly improving water efficiency for plants.
2000sIntroduction of 'smart' irrigation controllers, leveraging weather data to optimize watering schedules.
2010s-PresentIntegration of advanced diagnostics, IoT, and sustainable practices for complex water management.

Inside the course, module by module

Beginner 101: Irrigation System Basics

Learn irrigation part names, replace spray heads and rotors, repair drip manifolds, and glue PVC pipe.

02
Intermediate 201: System Evaluation and Repair

Evaluate systems for leaks, reroute pipes, repair PVC breaks, install drip retro kits, and replace control valves.

03
Advanced Diagnostics and Repair 401

Replace multi-valve manifolds, install master valves, troubleshoot faulty wires, and use locating equipment to find breaks.

Develop standardization for profitable customer connections, schedule strategically, and communicate professionally.
