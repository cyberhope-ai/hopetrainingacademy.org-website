What is this field?

Sales training equips individuals with the fundamental techniques and strategies needed to effectively connect with customers, understand their needs, and successfully offer solutions. It covers the entire sales cycle, from initial contact and building rapport to presenting value, overcoming objections, and ultimately closing deals.

Think of it like learning to play a musical instrument: you start with basic notes and scales (communication, product knowledge), then move to chords and melodies (identifying needs, presenting solutions), and eventually master complex pieces (negotiation, closing). This program provides the structured practice and expert guidance to help you become a skilled sales professional, capable of orchestrating successful customer interactions.

How the field grew

18th CenturyRise of traveling salesmen during the Industrial Revolution, expanding markets.
Early 20th CenturyEmergence of 'scientific selling,' focusing on standardized processes and psychology.
Mid-20th CenturyShift towards 'consultative selling,' emphasizing customer needs and problem-solving.
Late 20th CenturyIntegration of technology with Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems.
21st CenturyEvolution to digital sales, inbound marketing, and data-driven customer engagement.

Jobs it opens

Sales Representative

Engages with customers to sell products or services, managing accounts and meeting quotas.

Account Manager

Maintains and grows relationships with existing clients, ensuring satisfaction and identifying new opportunities.

Business Development Representative

Focuses on identifying and qualifying new sales leads and opportunities for the sales team.

Inside Sales Specialist

Conducts sales activities primarily over the phone or internet, often managing a high volume of leads.
