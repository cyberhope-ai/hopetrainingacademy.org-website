What is this field?

This online professional development program equips educators with the essential skills to create diverse and effective writing prompts. You'll learn a structured methodology to design prompts that not only align with grade-level standards but also foster critical thinking, creativity, and deep engagement in students across narrative, creative, expository, persuasive, and reading response genres.

Think of it like being a master chef who knows how to select the perfect ingredients and techniques to create a meal that satisfies every palate. In this program, you're the chef, and writing prompts are your ingredients. You'll learn to combine purpose, structure, and student needs to 'cook up' prompts that inspire every student to produce their best written work, moving beyond generic assignments to truly impactful learning experiences.

How the field grew

1960sBloom's Taxonomy influences educational objectives and prompt design, emphasizing cognitive levels.
1980sThe process writing movement gains traction, advocating for open-ended prompts that encourage student voice and iterative drafting.
2000sStandards-based education drives the need for prompts meticulously aligned with specific learning outcomes and assessment criteria.
2010sThe rise of digital tools and online learning platforms expands the possibilities for interactive and multimedia-rich prompt delivery.
PresentContemporary pedagogy emphasizes prompts that foster critical thinking, creativity, and the ethical use of AI in writing.
