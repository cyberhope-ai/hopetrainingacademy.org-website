What is this field?

A Veterinary Assistant is a crucial support professional in animal healthcare, working alongside veterinarians and veterinary technicians to ensure the well-being of animal patients. This role involves a wide range of duties, from assisting with examinations and procedures to maintaining a clean and safe environment, managing inventory, and educating pet owners.

Think of a Veterinary Assistant as the backbone of an animal clinic, much like a medical assistant in a human hospital. They handle everything from preparing examination rooms and sterilizing instruments to comforting anxious pets and their owners, ensuring the entire operation runs smoothly and compassionately. It's a hands-on role that requires both technical skill and a deep love for animals.

How the field grew

Early 1900sInformal apprenticeships provided basic animal care support in emerging veterinary practices.
Mid-20th CenturyAs veterinary medicine advanced, the need for dedicated support roles, like assistants, grew significantly.
Late 20th CenturyIncreased pet ownership and specialized veterinary services led to more defined responsibilities for assistants.
21st CenturyFormal training programs and professional development became standard, emphasizing comprehensive skills and client education.

No, a college degree is not required. While a high school diploma or equivalent is recommended, this program provides all the necessary training to prepare you for a successful career as a Veterinary Assistant.

Does this program include an exam voucher for certification?

This career training program focuses on equipping you with the practical skills and knowledge needed for the role of a Veterinary Assistant. It does not include a specific exam voucher for an external certification.

How long does it take to complete the Veterinary Assistant program?

This is a self-paced online program designed for flexibility, comprising 210 course hours. You can progress through the material at a speed that fits your schedule and learning style.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to provide comprehensive training for the role of a Veterinary Assistant, covering the core competencies and practical skills needed in a clinical setting. It prepares you for the job itself, rather than a single certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion of this program, you will be prepared for entry-level positions such as a Veterinary Assistant, Veterinary Aide, Animal Care Attendant, or Kennel Assistant in various animal healthcare settings.
