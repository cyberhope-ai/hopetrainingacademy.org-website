How the field grew

1990sDawn of e-commerce and early online ventures.
2000sEmergence of social media and specialized digital marketing.
2010sGrowth of the gig economy and accessible online business tools.
2020sIncreased demand for comprehensive digital business and financial literacy, leading to programs like this.

An individual trained in Digital Business Essentials for Entrepreneurs manages all facets of an online business, from financial planning and marketing to sales and customer retention. They are adept at leveraging digital platforms to generate revenue and build sustainable ventures.

Registered Apprenticeship — via the United States Department of Labor

Hope Training Academy is proud to offer United States Department of Labor (USDOL) registered apprenticeship training, accessible entirely online. This pathway provides structured, on-the-job learning combined with our expert-led curriculum, significantly de-risking your career change by offering paid work experience and a recognized credential.

Hope Training Academy Online · a United States Department of Labor (USDOL) Registered Apprenticeship pathway

If you're in Indiana: hands-on labs and in-person apprenticeship components run from our downtown Indianapolis center. (Online coursework and the registered apprenticeship pathway are available more broadly.)

Why Hope Training Academy

As a program of Video Game Palooza Inc, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Hope Training Academy reinvests all proceeds back into our mission. This allows us to provide free training programs to underserved communities, fostering a cycle of growth and opportunity for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program has accessible entry requirements and does not require a prior degree. It's designed for aspiring and current entrepreneurs from various backgrounds.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program focuses on practical skills and knowledge for entrepreneurs and does not include a certification exam voucher.

How long will it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced program comprising 260 course hours. The actual completion time will depend on your individual study schedule and dedication.

Is there a certification exam for this program?

This program is designed to provide comprehensive skills in digital business essentials for entrepreneurs, rather than preparing you for a specific industry certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you'll be well-equipped to launch and manage your own digital business, become an online business owner, work as a freelance digital marketer, or serve as a small business consultant.
