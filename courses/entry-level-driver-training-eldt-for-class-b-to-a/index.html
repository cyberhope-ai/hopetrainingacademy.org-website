What is this field?

Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) for a Class A Commercial Driver's License (CDL) is the foundational knowledge program required by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) for individuals seeking to operate heavy, combination vehicles like tractor-trailers. This online theory program covers critical regulations, safe operating procedures, vehicle systems, and non-driving activities essential for professional truck drivers.

Think of it like learning to fly a large commercial aircraft. Before you ever touch the controls, you must thoroughly understand aerodynamics, navigation, and safety protocols in a classroom setting. This ELDT program provides that crucial theoretical grounding, preparing you for the practical, behind-the-wheel training and state-administered CDL skills test necessary to earn your Class A CDL.

How the field grew

2012Congress passes the Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21), mandating ELDT for commercial drivers.
2016The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) publishes the final rule establishing comprehensive ELDT requirements.
2022ELDT regulations officially take effect on February 7, 2022, requiring all new drivers to complete approved training.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Basic Operation

Vehicle controls, pre- and post-trip inspections, basic maneuvering, shifting, backing, coupling, and uncoupling.

02
Safe Operating Procedures

Visual search, communication, distracted driving, speed and space management, night operation, and extreme weather conditions.

03
Advanced Operating Practices

Hazard perception, skid control, recovery techniques, jackknifing prevention, and railroad-highway grade crossings.

04
Vehicle Systems & Malfunction Reporting

Identification and diagnosis of vehicle malfunctions, roadside inspections, and essential maintenance practices.

05
Non-Driving Activities

Hours of service, fatigue awareness, cargo handling, environmental issues, post-crash procedures, and trip planning.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required. This program is designed for individuals seeking to meet the federal Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) theory requirements to upgrade their Class B CDL to a Class A CDL.

Does this program include an exam voucher for the CDL test?

No, this program focuses solely on the theoretical ELDT requirements. It does not include a voucher for the state-administered CDL skills test or any other state exam charges.

How long does it take to complete this ELDT program?

This self-paced online program can typically be completed in three months or less, with a total of 25 course hours dedicated to theory instruction.

Does this course cover the current ELDT regulations?

Yes, this program is specifically designed to meet all current Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) theory requirements established by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) for upgrading to a Class A CDL.

What kind of job can I get after completing this training?

Upon successful completion of this theory training and subsequent behind-the-wheel training and passing your state's Class A CDL test, you will be qualified for roles operating heavy combination vehicles like tractor-trailers, such as long-haul, regional, or local freight drivers.
