What is this field?

This comprehensive online training program equips you with the core culinary competencies essential for any professional kitchen. You'll delve into critical knife skills, understand vital safety and sanitation protocols, and master fundamental moist-heat and dry-heat cooking methods, all through a flexible, multimedia-rich learning experience.

Think of it as learning the alphabet and basic grammar before writing a novel. This program provides the essential building blocks – the 'alphabet' of cooking – ensuring you understand how ingredients behave, how heat transforms them, and how to work safely and efficiently, preparing you to confidently tackle any recipe or kitchen challenge.

How the field grew

17th-18th CenturyEmergence of formal culinary training in European royal courts and early professional kitchens.
19th CenturyAuguste Escoffier's standardization of French cuisine and kitchen brigade systems, influencing global culinary education.
Early 20th CenturyEstablishment of dedicated culinary schools and programs, formalizing professional chef training.
Late 20th CenturyExpansion of culinary education to include diverse cuisines, food science, and business management.
21st CenturyGrowth of online culinary education and emphasis on sustainable practices and global food trends.

Jobs it opens

Prepares specific dishes on an assigned station in a restaurant kitchen.

Prep Cook

Handles initial food preparation, including chopping, slicing, and measuring ingredients.

Culinary Assistant

Supports chefs and kitchen staff with various tasks, learning on the job.

Cook (Institutional/Catering)

Prepares meals in settings like hotels, schools, hospitals, or catering operations.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our Foundational Culinary Arts training program. This program is designed to provide essential skills for entry-level culinary roles.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on foundational culinary skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, so no exam voucher is included.

How long will it take to complete this training program?

This program consists of 115 course hours. It is self-paced, and you will have 12 months of access to complete the material at your convenience.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to build a strong foundation in culinary arts and does not directly prepare you for a specific industry certification exam. It focuses on practical skills.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be prepared for entry-level positions such as a Line Cook, Prep Cook, Culinary Assistant, or Cook in various professional kitchen environments.
