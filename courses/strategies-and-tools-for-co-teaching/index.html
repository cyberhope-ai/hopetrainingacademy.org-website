What is this field?

Co-teaching is a powerful instructional approach where two or more educators work together in a single classroom, sharing responsibility for planning, instructing, and assessing a diverse group of students. This collaborative model is designed to leverage the strengths of multiple teachers to provide more individualized support, richer instruction, and a more inclusive learning environment for all students, including those with special needs.

Think of it like two expert chefs collaborating on a complex dish, each bringing their unique skills to create a richer, more nuanced outcome than either could alone. In the classroom, this means two educators working seamlessly to support all students, adapting lessons, and providing varied perspectives to ensure every learner thrives.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to enroll in this program?

While a teaching degree is typically required for a teaching career, this particular program is designed for professional development to enhance existing educators' skills. There are no specific degree prerequisites to enroll in this co-teaching strategies program.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included with the Strategies and Tools for Co-Teaching program, as it is a professional development course and does not lead to a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete the program?

This program is 100% online and self-paced. The time to complete it can vary based on your schedule and learning style, but it is designed to be flexible for busy professionals.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

No, the Strategies and Tools for Co-Teaching program is focused on enhancing your practical skills and knowledge in collaborative teaching methods, rather than preparing for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program is designed to enhance the skills of current educators, making you more effective in roles such as Elementary, Middle, or High School Teacher, particularly in inclusive or collaborative classroom settings, including Special Education Co-Teacher positions.
