What is this field?

Digital arts training equips you with the technical and creative skills to produce visual content using software and digital tools. It encompasses everything from editing photographs and creating intricate illustrations to designing logos and marketing materials. This field merges traditional artistic principles with modern technology, allowing for dynamic and versatile creative expression.

Think of it like learning to play a musical instrument, but instead of a guitar, your instrument is a computer and software like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. You learn the fundamentals of composition, color, and form, then apply them using digital brushes, layers, and vector paths to create stunning visuals for various purposes, from advertising to fine art.

How the field grew

1987Adobe Illustrator 1.0 is released, revolutionizing vector graphics and digital illustration.
1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 makes its debut, setting the standard for digital image manipulation.
Mid-1990sThe explosion of the internet and desktop publishing drives a surge in demand for digital artists across various industries.
Early 2000sDigital photography becomes widely accessible, further integrating digital imaging into the creative workflow of artists and designers.
2013Adobe transitions its creative software to a cloud-based subscription model (Creative Cloud), fostering continuous updates and collaborative workflows for digital artists worldwide.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for adult learners interested in building skills and a portfolio for a design career, with no specific prerequisites beyond basic computer literacy.

Does this program include an exam voucher for any certification?

No, this program is a certificate course focused on skill development and portfolio building. It does not include an exam voucher for external certifications.

How long will it take to complete the Digital Arts Certificate program?

This self-paced program consists of 360 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own speed and complete it on a schedule that fits your life.

Is there a specific certification exam associated with this program?

This program culminates in a Digital Arts Certificate from Hope Training Academy, demonstrating your mastery of the covered skills. It does not prepare you for a specific vendor certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue entry-level roles such as Graphic Designer, Illustrator, Digital Artist, or Production Artist, creating visual content for various industries.
