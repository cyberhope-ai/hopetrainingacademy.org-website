What is this field?

A Cloud and AI Security Engineer is a specialized professional responsible for safeguarding an organization's digital assets across cloud platforms, generative AI, and other intelligent systems. This role combines core cloud security principles with advanced techniques to protect AI services, data flows, and applications, ensuring robust defense against evolving cyber threats.

Think of this role as a digital bodyguard for your company's most valuable online infrastructure and its intelligent AI 'brains.' You're not just locking doors; you're designing the entire security system, monitoring for intruders, and building defenses specifically tailored to the unique vulnerabilities of artificial intelligence, all within the dynamic environment of the cloud.

How the field grew

2006Amazon Web Services (AWS) launches S3, marking a significant shift towards cloud storage and services.
2008Microsoft Azure is announced, further solidifying the enterprise cloud computing landscape.
Early 2010sCloud security emerges as a distinct and rapidly growing field, addressing unique challenges of distributed infrastructure.
Late 2010sFocus expands to securing complex hybrid and multi-cloud environments, emphasizing governance and automation.
Early 2020sThe rapid adoption of generative AI introduces new security paradigms, leading to dedicated AI security roles and practices.
