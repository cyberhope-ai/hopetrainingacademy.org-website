What is this field?

This career training program is designed for individuals who want to become HVAC/R technicians but need to enhance their English language proficiency first. It focuses on building essential vocabulary and communication skills specific to the heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration industry, alongside general workplace safety concepts. Participants begin with a Proficiency Assessment (PA) to determine their current English level, then progress through tailored modules until they reach the High Intermediate level, which allows them to enroll in the full HVAC/R technical training.

Think of it like learning the specific language of a complex machine before you start operating it. Just as a pilot learns aviation terminology before flying, this program equips you with the specialized English needed to understand technical manuals, communicate with colleagues and clients, and safely perform tasks in the HVAC/R field, setting a strong foundation for your hands-on technical training.

What the career pays

HVAC/R technicians install, maintain, and repair heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems in residential and commercial settings. They diagnose mechanical and electrical malfunctions, ensuring optimal system performance and indoor comfort.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in this career training program. Your dedication to learning English for the HVAC/R field is the primary prerequisite.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included as this program focuses on English language proficiency for the HVAC/R field, not a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and designed for 160 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your initial English proficiency and study schedule.

What specific certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to build your English language skills and technical vocabulary for the HVAC/R industry, preparing you for subsequent technical training and entry into the field, rather than a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and reaching the High Intermediate English level, you'll be prepared to enroll in our technical HVAC/R training programs, leading to roles such as an HVAC/R Technician, Installer, or Service Apprentice.
