What is this field?

Supply Chain Management (SCM) is the art and science of overseeing the entire journey of a product, from raw materials to the final customer. It involves coordinating and integrating all the processes that transform raw materials into finished goods and deliver them to consumers, ensuring efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and customer satisfaction.

Think of SCM as the conductor of a grand orchestra, where each instrument—from sourcing and manufacturing to warehousing and delivery—must play in perfect harmony. Effective supply chain management ensures that the right products are in the right place, at the right time, and at the right cost, keeping businesses competitive and customers happy.

How the field grew

1980sThe concept of "Supply Chain Management" is formally introduced, emphasizing integration beyond traditional logistics.
1990sGlobalization and the rise of information technology drive the need for more sophisticated, interconnected supply chains.
2000sFocus expands to include sustainability, risk management, and ethical sourcing within global supply networks.
2010sDigital transformation, big data analytics, and automation begin to revolutionize supply chain operations and decision-making.
