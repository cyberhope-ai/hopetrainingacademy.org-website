What is this field?

HVAC/R technicians are the skilled professionals who install, maintain, and repair the heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems that keep our homes, businesses, and even critical infrastructure comfortable and operational. From ensuring your home stays cool in summer to maintaining complex commercial refrigeration units, these experts are essential for modern living and commerce.

Think of an HVAC/R technician as a building's climate control doctor. Just as a doctor diagnoses and treats ailments in the human body, an HVAC/R technician diagnoses and repairs issues in a building's environmental systems, ensuring they run efficiently and effectively to provide optimal comfort and air quality.

How the field grew

1902Willis Carrier invents modern air conditioning, laying the foundation for the industry.
1930sWidespread adoption of refrigeration in homes and commercial settings begins.
1970sIncreased focus on energy efficiency and environmental impact leads to new regulations and technologies.
1987The Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer drives innovation in refrigerant management.
PresentIntegration of smart technologies, automation, and sustainable practices define modern HVAC/R systems.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Fundamentals of HVAC/R

Heat, temperature, pressure, matter, energy, refrigeration cycles, and refrigerants.

02
Safety, Tools, and System Basics

General safety, essential tools, piping, leak detection, evacuation, and system cleanup.

03
Electrical and Automatic Controls

Basic electricity, magnetism, automatic control components, DDCs, pneumatics, and motor controls.

04
Commercial Refrigeration Systems

Evaporators, condensers, compressors, expansion devices, ice machines, and specialized applications.

05
Air Conditioning and Heating Systems

Residential and commercial air conditioning, electric, gas, oil, hydronic heat, and indoor air quality.

06
Advanced Systems and Diagnostics

Heat pumps (air source, geothermal), energy auditing, troubleshooting, and all-weather systems.

07
Refrigerant Management and Regulations

Recovery, recycling, reclaiming, retrofitting refrigerants, and EPA laws for safe handling.

08
Professional Skills for Technicians

Communication, decision-making, time management, problem-solving, and customer service.
