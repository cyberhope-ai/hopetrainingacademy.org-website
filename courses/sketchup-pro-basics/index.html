What is this field?

SketchUp Pro is an industry-leading 3D modeling software widely used across architecture, interior design, construction, and entertainment. Known for its intuitive interface and flexibility, it empowers professionals to create detailed 3D models, components, topography, and landscaping with ease, integrating seamlessly with various design tools and plugins.

Think of SketchUp Pro as your digital workshop where you can build anything from a tiny house to a sprawling park, all in 3D, before a single brick is laid or a tree is planted. It's like having a virtual set of LEGOs, but with professional-grade tools to bring your most complex visions to life with precision and clarity.

How the field grew

2000SketchUp founded by @Last Software.
2006Google acquires SketchUp, making it widely accessible.
2012Trimble Inc. acquires SketchUp from Google, investing in its professional development.
2013SketchUp Pro 2013 is released, enhancing professional features.
2020sContinues to be a leading 3D modeling software, integrating with various industry tools and workflows.
