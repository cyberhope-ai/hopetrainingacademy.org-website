What is this field?

Nature photography is the art of capturing elements of the natural world, from vast landscapes and majestic wildlife to intricate close-ups of flora and fauna. This program equips you with the technical skills and artistic vision to use your digital camera effectively, allowing you to create stunning images that convey the wonder and beauty of our planet.

Think of your digital camera as a sophisticated paintbrush and the natural world as your boundless canvas. This program teaches you how to master that paintbrush, understanding its settings and capabilities, so you can translate the scenes you observe into captivating photographs, much like an artist brings a vision to life.

How the field grew

1981Sony introduces the Mavica, the first electronic still camera concept, paving the way for digital imaging.
1990Kodak releases the DCS 100, the first commercially available digital single-lens reflex (DSLR) camera.
2000sThe widespread adoption of consumer digital cameras makes photography accessible to millions, fueling interest in specialized genres like nature photography.
2010sAdvancements in mirrorless camera technology and smartphone cameras further democratize high-quality nature photography.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a nature photographer?

No, a formal degree is not typically required. Strong technical skills, a compelling portfolio, and a keen eye for composition are far more important in this field.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on skill development and does not lead to a certification exam, so no voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-guided online program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. The time to complete will vary based on your schedule and dedication.

Is there a specific exam I need to pass?

No, this program is designed to build practical photography skills and does not culminate in a formal certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

While this program provides foundational skills, it can open doors to roles such as a freelance nature photographer, a contributor to stock photography sites, or a specialized photographer focusing on landscapes, wildlife, or botanical subjects.
