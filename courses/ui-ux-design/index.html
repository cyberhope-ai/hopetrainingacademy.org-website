What is this field?

UI/UX Design focuses on creating digital products that are both beautiful to look at (User Interface) and effortless to use (User Experience). This field is crucial for ensuring websites, apps, and software are not just functional, but also engaging, accessible, and intuitive for every user. It blends creativity with psychology and technology to solve real-world problems through design.

Imagine a well-designed car: the UI is the dashboard layout, the seat fabric, and the exterior paint – all the visual and tactile elements. The UX is how smoothly it drives, how comfortable the seats are on a long trip, and how easily you can find and use the controls. UI/UX designers ensure the entire digital journey is as pleasant and efficient as possible.

How the field grew

1980sFoundational research in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) begins to shape user-focused design principles.
1990sDon Norman, while at Apple, popularizes the term 'User Experience' (UX), emphasizing the holistic interaction with products.
2007The launch of the iPhone revolutionizes mobile computing, making intuitive touch-based UI/UX design paramount.
2010sUser-centered design becomes a mainstream practice, with a strong emphasis on research, testing, and accessibility.
2020sThe field expands to include ethical design, AI integration, and inclusive design principles for diverse user needs.
