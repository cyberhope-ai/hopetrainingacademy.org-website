What is this field?

This introductory training provides an in-depth exploration of criminal law, covering its core principles, procedures, and the real-world application within the justice system. You'll examine the critical roles played by prosecutors, defense attorneys, and the paralegals who are indispensable to their work, offering a comprehensive overview for anyone considering a legal support career.

Think of it like learning the basic rules and plays of a complex game. Before you can be a player or coach, you need to understand how the game works, what each position does, and the fundamental strategies involved. This training gives you that essential playbook for the criminal justice system, preparing you to understand legal processes and contribute effectively to a legal team.

How the field grew

1215Magna Carta establishes foundational principles of due process and rule of law, influencing criminal justice.
1829Establishment of the Metropolitan Police Service in London marks the beginning of modern policing, impacting criminal law enforcement.
1960sExpansion of legal aid and public defender systems in the U.S., emphasizing rights of the accused and increasing demand for legal support roles.
1970sFormalization of the paralegal profession, recognizing the vital role these professionals play in legal practice, including criminal law.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Criminal Law

Defining criminal law, sources of law, and the purpose of the criminal justice system.

02
Elements of a Crime

Understanding actus reus, mens rea, causation, and different classifications of crimes.

03
Criminal Procedure

Overview of arrest, arraignment, bail, grand jury, and the trial process.

04
Roles in the Justice System

Exploring the functions of prosecutors, defense attorneys, and paralegals in criminal cases.

05
Defenses and Sentencing

Common criminal defenses, types of sentencing, and post-conviction processes.

06
Ethics in Criminal Practice

Professional responsibility and ethical considerations for legal professionals in criminal law.
