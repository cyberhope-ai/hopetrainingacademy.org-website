What is this field?

This program, CompTIA A+ Certification Prep 1: Hardware, is your foundational step into the world of IT support, specifically focusing on the physical components of computers. You'll gain practical skills in configuring, maintaining, and troubleshooting computer hardware, preparing you for the first part of the globally recognized CompTIA A+ certification.

Think of it like becoming a skilled auto mechanic, but for computers. Instead of engines and transmissions, you'll be learning about motherboards, processors, and storage devices. You'll understand how each part works, how to put them together, and how to diagnose and fix issues when they arise, ensuring systems run smoothly and efficiently.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA A+ certification first introduced, setting an industry standard for IT support.
2001CompTIA A+ receives ANSI accreditation, affirming its quality and credibility.
2010sThe certification evolves to include critical areas like mobile devices, networking, and cloud technologies.
PresentCompTIA A+ remains a global benchmark for entry-level IT professionals, continuously updated to reflect current technologies.
