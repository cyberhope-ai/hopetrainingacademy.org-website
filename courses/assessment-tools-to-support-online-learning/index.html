What is this field?

This program introduces educators to essential assessment tools and strategies specifically designed for online learning environments. You will explore various methods to effectively evaluate student understanding, engagement, and progress in virtual classrooms, ensuring robust and fair assessment practices.

Just as a chef tastes their dish to ensure it's perfectly seasoned, educators need reliable tools to 'taste' student understanding in the online classroom. This training provides the techniques and instruments to accurately gauge learning, allowing you to adapt your instruction for optimal student success.
