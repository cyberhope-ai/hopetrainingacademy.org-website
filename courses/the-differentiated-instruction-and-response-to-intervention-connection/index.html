What is this field?

Differentiated Instruction (DI) is an educational approach that tailors teaching methods and content to meet the individual needs of students, recognizing that not all learners grasp concepts in the same way or at the same pace. Response to Intervention (RTI) is a multi-tier approach to the early identification and support of students with learning and behavior needs. Together, these strategies create a flexible and responsive classroom environment where every student has the opportunity to thrive.

Think of it like a master chef preparing a meal for a diverse group of diners. Instead of serving the same dish to everyone, the chef adjusts ingredients, cooking methods, and presentation to accommodate different dietary restrictions, preferences, and skill levels, ensuring everyone enjoys a satisfying and nourishing experience. Similarly, DI and RTI allow educators to customize their teaching to fit each student's unique learning 'diet'.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of individualized education programs (IEPs) and special education legislation, laying groundwork for tailored instruction.
1990sCarol Ann Tomlinson popularizes and refines the concept of Differentiated Instruction, emphasizing student-centered approaches.
Early 2000sResponse to Intervention (RTI) gains prominence as a framework for early academic and behavioral support.
2004Individuals with Disabilities Education Improvement Act (IDEA) formally integrates RTI as a method for identifying learning disabilities.
PresentDI and RTI are recognized as cornerstone strategies for inclusive and effective teaching practices across diverse classrooms.
