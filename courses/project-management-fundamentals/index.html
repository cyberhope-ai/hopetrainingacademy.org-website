What is this field?

Project management is the art and science of leading a team to achieve all project goals within given constraints. It involves careful planning, execution, monitoring, control, and closure of work to meet specific success criteria. From developing new software to constructing a building, project managers are the orchestrators who ensure everything runs smoothly and on schedule.

Think of a project manager as the conductor of an orchestra. Just as a conductor ensures each musician plays their part at the right time and in harmony to create a beautiful symphony, a project manager coordinates various team members, resources, and tasks to deliver a successful project outcome. They keep everyone aligned, anticipate challenges, and guide the project from its initial concept to its triumphant completion.

How the field grew

1950sDevelopment of PERT and Critical Path Method (CPM) for complex engineering projects.
1960sEmergence of project management as a distinct discipline with formal methodologies.
1980sFounding of the Project Management Institute (PMI) and growth of professional standards.
1990sIncreased adoption of project management software and global expansion of practices.
2000sRise of Agile and Scrum methodologies, emphasizing flexibility and iterative development.
