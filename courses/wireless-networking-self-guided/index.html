What is this field?

Wireless networking allows devices to connect and communicate without cables, using radio waves. It's the technology behind Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular data, enabling seamless connectivity in homes, offices, and public spaces. Understanding wireless networks is crucial for anyone managing modern IT infrastructure.

Imagine a bustling city where all information travels instantly, not through physical roads or wires, but through invisible pathways in the air. Wireless networking is like building and managing those invisible highways, ensuring data flows smoothly, securely, and efficiently between all connected devices, from your smartphone to complex business servers.

How the field grew

1997First IEEE 802.11 wireless standard released, laying the groundwork for Wi-Fi.
1999The Wi-Fi Alliance is established to promote interoperability and certify wireless products.
2009Introduction of 802.11n, significantly boosting wireless speeds and range.
2014Release of 802.11ac, bringing multi-gigabit speeds to wireless local area networks.

01
Wireless Networking Fundamentals

Introduction to wireless technology, IEEE 802.11 standards, and radio frequency basics.

02

Conducting site surveys, capacity planning, and selecting appropriate hardware and topologies.

03
Wireless Network Deployment

Access Point installation, configuration, and integration into existing network infrastructures.

04
Wireless Security Protocols

Understanding WEP, WPA/WPA2/WPA3, authentication methods, and detecting rogue access points.

Client configuration, network access control, and optimizing wireless network performance.

Diagnosing common connectivity issues, addressing signal interference, and resolving performance problems.
