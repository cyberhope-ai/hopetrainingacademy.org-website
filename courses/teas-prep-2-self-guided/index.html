What is this field?

The Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized exam often required for admission into nursing and allied health programs. This self-guided online program is meticulously designed to equip you with the fundamental math and science knowledge, alongside crucial test-taking strategies, necessary to confidently approach and succeed on the TEAS exam.

Think of this program like a specialized fitness trainer for your academic muscles. Just as a trainer helps you target specific areas to build strength for a physical challenge, our TEAS prep focuses on strengthening your math and science competencies and refining your test-taking technique for this critical academic hurdle.

How the field grew

Early 2000sIntroduction of the Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) to standardize admissions for health science programs.
OngoingContinuous refinement of exam content and structure by Assessment Technologies Institute (ATI) to reflect current healthcare education needs.
RecentIncreased availability of online proctoring and digital resources to enhance accessibility for aspiring healthcare professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required to enroll in this TEAS prep program?

No, this program is designed to help you prepare for the TEAS exam, which is often a prerequisite for healthcare programs, not a degree in itself.

Does this course include an exam voucher for the TEAS exam?

An exam voucher is not included with this self-guided TEAS prep program. You will need to register and pay for the TEAS exam separately.

How long does it take to complete this TEAS preparation program?

This is a self-guided online program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. The time required will vary based on your individual study habits and prior knowledge.

Which version of the TEAS exam does this program prepare me for?

This program provides comprehensive preparation for the current Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) exam, focusing on foundational math and science concepts.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this TEAS prep?

Successfully passing the TEAS exam allows you to apply to various healthcare programs, such as nursing (RN, LPN), medical assisting, dental hygiene, and radiologic technology, which then lead to specific healthcare careers.
