What is this field?

Microsoft SharePoint 365 is a web-based collaboration and document management platform that helps organizations store, organize, share, and access information from any device. It serves as a central hub where teams can create websites, manage projects, and collaborate on documents, fostering seamless communication and efficiency across departments.

Think of SharePoint as your organization's digital workspace, a powerful intranet where all your team's resources, files, and projects live. It streamlines workflows, automates tasks, and ensures that everyone has access to the information they need, when they need it, transforming how businesses manage content and collaborate effectively.

How the field grew

2001Initial release of SharePoint Portal Server, establishing its role in enterprise content management.
2007Major overhaul with Microsoft Office SharePoint Server (MOSS) 2007, expanding collaboration and business intelligence features.
2010Introduction of SharePoint 2010, bringing significant user interface improvements and enhanced social computing capabilities.
2013Further integration with cloud services and the introduction of a new app model, paving the way for SharePoint Online.
OngoingContinuous evolution of SharePoint Online as a core component of Microsoft 365, emphasizing cloud-first collaboration and modern experiences.
