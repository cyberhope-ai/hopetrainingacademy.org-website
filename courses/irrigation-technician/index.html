What is this field?

An Irrigation Technician is a skilled professional responsible for the installation, maintenance, and repair of sprinkler and drip irrigation systems. They ensure efficient water delivery for landscapes, agricultural fields, and recreational areas, playing a crucial role in water conservation and environmental sustainability.

Think of an Irrigation Technician as a water detective for landscapes. Just as a detective identifies clues to solve a mystery, you'll diagnose issues in complex water systems, pinpoint leaks, and implement precise repairs to keep green spaces thriving without wasting a single drop.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Irrigation System Fundamentals

Basic components, part identification, hydraulics, and wiring principles.

02
Spray Head & Rotor Repair

Diagnosing, replacing, and adjusting nozzles, spray heads, and rotors.

03
Drip System & Pipe Repair

Repairing drip manifolds, installing drip retro kits, and fixing PVC pipe breaks.

04
Valve & Controller Management

Diagnosing and replacing control valves, master valves, and system controllers.

05
Advanced Troubleshooting & Installation

Evaluating leaks, rerouting pipes, installing water meters, and resolving wiring faults.
