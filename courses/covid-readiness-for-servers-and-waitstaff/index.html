What is this field?

In today's hospitality landscape, demonstrating a commitment to health and safety is paramount. This specialized training program provides servers and waitstaff with the critical knowledge and practical skills needed to prevent the spread of COVID-19, ensuring both staff and guest well-being. You'll gain a deep understanding of current health regulations, proper cleaning techniques, and personal protective equipment (PPE) usage, transforming you into a trusted professional.

Think of this program as your essential toolkit for navigating the new normal in food service. Just as a chef needs the right knives, a server now needs the right knowledge to maintain a safe dining experience. You'll learn how to identify symptoms, implement effective sanitation practices, and communicate confidence to patrons, making you an invaluable asset to any restaurant or food service establishment.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals seeking practical, hands-on skills in hospitality safety and does not require a college degree.

Does this program include an exam voucher for a certification?

This program focuses on essential skills and knowledge for COVID readiness in hospitality and does not culminate in a separate certification exam or include a voucher.

How long will it take to complete this training?

This program is designed for self-paced learning, with approximately 40 course hours. You will have three months of access to complete the material.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is a standalone training focused on practical COVID-19 readiness for servers and waitstaff and does not prepare you for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program enhances your qualifications for roles such as server, waitstaff, and other food service positions, demonstrating your commitment to health and safety in the hospitality industry.
