What is this field?

The Cisco CCNP Enterprise certification validates your expertise in implementing and operating core enterprise network technologies, as well as designing advanced enterprise networking solutions. This program focuses on two critical exams: ENCOR (Implementing and Operating Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies) and ENSLD (Designing Cisco Enterprise Networks), covering everything from advanced routing and security to network automation and Software-Defined Access.

Think of it like becoming an architect and lead builder for a city's entire infrastructure. While a CCNA might qualify you to build individual roads and buildings, the CCNP Enterprise empowers you to design the complete layout, integrate complex systems like power grids and water treatment, and ensure everything operates efficiently and securely across a vast, interconnected metropolitan area.

How the field grew

1993Cisco introduces its first certification programs, establishing a benchmark for networking professionals.
1998The Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP) certification is launched, signifying a higher level of expertise.
2020Cisco revamps its certification portfolio, introducing the current CCNP Enterprise track to align with modern network architectures and emerging technologies.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Core Enterprise Network Technologies (ENCOR)

Campus architecture, device fundamentals, VLANs, STP, EtherChannels, inter-VLAN routing, and first-hop redundancy protocols.

02
Advanced Routing and VPNs

OSPFv2, EBGP, Network Address Translation (NAT), Multicast protocols, GRE tunnels, IPSec VPNs, and Virtual Routing and Forwarding (VRF).

03
Network Management and Security

NTP, Syslog, SNMP, NetFlow, SPAN, QoS, Embedded Event Manager, AAA, Access Control Lists (ACLs), and Next-Gen Firewalls.

04
Next-Generation Campus Networks & Automation

SD-Access, LISP, VXLAN, SD-WAN, DNA Center, data formats, Python, NetConf, RestConf, Chef, Puppet, Ansible, and SaltStack.

05
Advanced Addressing & Routing Solutions (ENSLD)

IPv4/IPv6 addressing, routing protocol design (IS-IS, EIGRP, OSPF, BGP), and secure scaling of enterprise networks.

06
Enterprise Campus & WAN Design

High availability (HA) design, L2/L3 convergence, redistribution, summarization, SD-Access architecture, and various WAN options and VPN designs.

07
Quality of Service (QoS) & Network Management

End-to-end QoS strategies (IntServ/Diffserv), classification, marking, shaping, policing, queuing, and in-band/out-of-band network management techniques.

08
Network Automation & Programmability Design

YANG data models, Netconf/Restconf differences, and model-driven telemetry for modern network operations.
