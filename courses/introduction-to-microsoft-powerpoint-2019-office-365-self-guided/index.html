What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint is the industry-standard software for creating dynamic and engaging presentations. It allows users to combine text, images, charts, and multimedia into a structured slideshow format, making complex information digestible and visually appealing for audiences. From business proposals to educational lectures, PowerPoint is an indispensable tool for effective communication across virtually all professional fields.

This hands-on training program guides you through the core functionalities of PowerPoint 2019 and Office 365. Think of it like learning to build a house: you'll start with the foundation (setting up slides), learn to add the walls and rooms (inserting content and visuals), and finally, decorate and present it beautifully (applying themes, animations, and delivery techniques). By the end, you'll be equipped to craft professional presentations that capture attention and convey your message clearly.

How the field grew

1987Forethought Inc. develops 'Presenter', later renamed PowerPoint.
1987Microsoft acquires PowerPoint, integrating it into its suite of office products.
1990PowerPoint 2.0 is released, marking its first version for Microsoft Windows.
2007A significant redesign introduces the 'Ribbon' interface, streamlining access to features.
