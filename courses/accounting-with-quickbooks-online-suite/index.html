What is this field?

Accounting is the systematic recording, analyzing, and reporting of financial transactions. It's the language of business, providing crucial insights into a company's health and performance. Understanding accounting principles allows individuals and organizations to make informed decisions, manage budgets effectively, and ensure financial compliance.

QuickBooks Online is a cloud-based accounting software designed to simplify financial management for small businesses. Think of it as your digital ledger and financial assistant, helping you track income and expenses, manage invoices, reconcile bank accounts, and generate essential financial reports without needing complex spreadsheets or manual calculations.

How the field grew

1983Intuit, the creator of QuickBooks, was founded.
1992The first version of QuickBooks Desktop software was released.
2001QuickBooks Online (originally QuickBooks for the Web) was launched, bringing accounting to the cloud.
2013QuickBooks Online underwent a significant redesign, enhancing user experience and features.
2020sContinued evolution with advanced features like AI-powered insights and deeper integrations.
