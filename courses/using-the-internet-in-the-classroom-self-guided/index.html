What is this field?

This training program empowers educators to seamlessly integrate the vast resources of the internet into their daily teaching practices. You'll discover how to leverage online tools and platforms to create dynamic, interactive, and relevant learning experiences that captivate students and streamline your instructional efforts.

Think of it like upgrading your classroom from a traditional chalkboard to a fully interactive digital whiteboard. Instead of just presenting information, you'll learn to curate engaging content, facilitate collaborative projects, and utilize digital assessment tools, making every lesson more impactful and accessible for your students.

How the field grew

1990sEarly internet adoption begins to connect schools to global information.
Early 2000sEducational software and dedicated online learning resources emerge for classroom use.
Mid-2010sCloud-based tools and collaborative platforms become integral for student projects and communication.
Early 2020sRemote and hybrid learning models accelerate the need for advanced digital literacy in education.
