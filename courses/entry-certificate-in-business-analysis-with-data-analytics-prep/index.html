What is this field?

Business analysis is the practice of identifying business needs and determining solutions to business problems. These solutions often include a systems development component, but may also consist of process improvement, organizational change, or strategic planning and policy development. A skilled business analyst acts as a crucial liaison, translating complex business requirements into actionable plans for technical and operational teams.

Data analytics, on the other hand, involves examining raw data to uncover trends, extract insights, and make informed decisions. When combined, business analysis and data analytics empower professionals to not only understand 'what' is happening in a business but also 'why' and 'what could happen next.' Think of it like a detective (business analyst) who uses forensic evidence (data analytics) to solve a case and advise on future prevention.

How the field grew

1980sEmergence of systems analysis roles focused on IT project requirements.
2000sFormalization of business analysis as a distinct discipline with dedicated methodologies.
2010sExplosion of big data led to the integration of data analytics into business strategy.
PresentBusiness and data analysis converge, driving demand for hybrid skill sets.
