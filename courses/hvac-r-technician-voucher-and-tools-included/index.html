What is this field?

An HVAC/R Technician is a skilled professional responsible for installing, maintaining, and repairing heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems. These critical systems regulate indoor temperatures, air quality, and preserve perishable goods, impacting comfort, health, and commerce in residential, commercial, and industrial settings. This program equips you with the practical knowledge and hands-on experience to excel in this essential trade.

Think of an HVAC/R system as the climate control center for any building, much like the thermostat and refrigerator in your own home, but on a larger, more complex scale. Just as you rely on your fridge to keep food fresh and your AC to keep you cool, businesses and homes depend on these systems to function perfectly. HVAC/R technicians are the experts who ensure these vital systems run efficiently, safely, and reliably, making environments comfortable and operations smooth.

How the field grew

Early 1900sWillis Carrier invents modern air conditioning, initially for industrial process control.
1970sEnvironmental concerns lead to regulations for refrigerants, shaping modern HVAC/R practices.
1997North American Technician Excellence (NATE) is founded, establishing industry-recognized certification standards.
2000s-PresentIncreased focus on energy efficiency, smart home integration, and sustainable refrigeration technologies.

  • Applying safety protocols and environmental regulations
