What is this field?

Video game design and development is the exciting process of conceptualizing, creating, and bringing interactive digital worlds to life. It combines artistic vision with technical skill, requiring professionals to craft compelling narratives, design engaging gameplay mechanics, and build immersive environments. Imagine being the architect of virtual realities, where every decision shapes the player's experience.

This comprehensive career training program equips you with the foundational programming knowledge, mathematical concepts, 3D modeling techniques, and game engine proficiency needed to thrive in this dynamic industry. You'll gain hands-on experience by developing three complete games, including a 3D arcade game, a first-person adventure, and a custom project of your own design, using the industry-standard Unity engine.

How the field grew

1970sEarly arcade games lay the foundation for interactive entertainment.
2000sModern game engines like Unity emerge, democratizing game development.
2010sIndependent game development flourishes, showcasing diverse creative visions.
2020sAdvanced graphics, AI, and immersive technologies redefine gaming experiences.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Video Game Designer?

No, this career training program requires no prior training or experience. It focuses on practical skills and portfolio building, which are highly valued in the industry, offering an alternative path to traditional degrees.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program focuses on developing comprehensive skills and a portfolio of completed games rather than preparing for a single certification exam, so an exam voucher is not applicable.

How long does this career training program take to complete?

This self-paced program includes 500 course hours, allowing you to learn at a pace that fits your schedule while ensuring thorough mastery of the material.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to provide you with a robust skill set in video game design and development using Unity and C#, enabling you to enter the industry directly rather than preparing for a single, specific vendor certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Video Game Designer, Game Developer, Level Designer, Technical Artist, or even launch their own independent game projects.
