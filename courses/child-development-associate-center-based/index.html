What is this field?

The Child Development Associate (CDA) credential is a nationally recognized certification for early childhood educators, demonstrating your expertise in nurturing young children's growth and development. This comprehensive career training program equips you with the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to excel in diverse early learning environments, from infant-toddler care to preschool settings. It also integrates essential English language development to ensure you master the academic vocabulary and concepts crucial for success in this field.

Think of the CDA as your professional compass in early childhood education. Just as a compass guides a ship through uncharted waters, the CDA credential guides you in providing high-quality care and education, aligning with the highest standards set by organizations like the National Association for the Education of Young Children (NAEYC). This program prepares you to confidently navigate the complexities of child development, fostering safe, stimulating, and inclusive learning experiences for children from birth through age five.

How the field grew

1926National Association for the Education of Young Children (NAEYC) founded, setting early standards for child care.
1975The Child Development Associate (CDA) credential is established, offering a competency-based pathway for early childhood professionals.
1985The Council for Professional Recognition is founded to administer and advance the CDA credential, ensuring its national recognition and quality.
Early 2000sIncreased national focus on professional development and standardized credentials for early childhood educators to improve program quality.
PresentContinued emphasis on professional development and competency-based training, with the CDA remaining a cornerstone credential for early childhood professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, a college degree is not required to enroll in our Child Development Associate career training program. This program is designed to provide you with the specific competencies and credential needed for a career in early childhood education.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher for the CDA credential is not included with this career training program. You will be responsible for applying for and scheduling your CDA Verification Visit and exam directly with the Council for Professional Recognition.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and includes 120 course hours. The actual time to completion will vary based on your individual study habits and schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific CDA exam version?

This program prepares you for the Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, which involves a portfolio submission, a verification visit, and a comprehensive exam. It aligns with the current CDA competency standards set by the Council for Professional Recognition, rather than a versioned exam like IT certifications.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion, you will be prepared for roles such as a Child Care Worker, Preschool Teacher Assistant, Early Childhood Educator, or Daycare Provider in various center-based settings.
