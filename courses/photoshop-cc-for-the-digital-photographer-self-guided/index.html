What is this field?

Adobe Photoshop Creative Cloud (Photoshop CC) is the industry-standard software for image manipulation, graphic design, and digital art. For photographers, it's an indispensable tool that allows for precise control over every aspect of an image, from basic exposure and color correction to advanced retouching, compositing, and creative effects. This program empowers you to bring your photographic vision to life, ensuring your images stand out with professional polish and artistic flair.

Think of Photoshop CC as your digital darkroom and art studio combined. Just as a chef uses various tools and ingredients to create a culinary masterpiece, a digital photographer uses Photoshop's extensive toolkit—layers, masks, filters, and adjustment brushes—to refine, enhance, and transform raw images into their final, captivating form. It's where technical correction meets creative expression, enabling you to fix imperfections, craft moods, and tell compelling visual stories.

How the field grew

1987Thomas Knoll begins developing ImagePro, the precursor to Photoshop.
1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 is officially released, revolutionizing digital imaging.
2003Adobe introduces the Creative Suite (CS) brand, integrating Photoshop into a powerful collection of creative tools.
2013Adobe transitions to the Creative Cloud (CC) subscription model, launching Photoshop CC with continuous updates and cloud integration.
