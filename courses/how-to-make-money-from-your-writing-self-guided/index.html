What is this field?

This hands-on program guides you through the essential steps to turn your writing skills into a profitable venture. Whether you're looking to launch a new career, supplement your current income, or build a robust writing business, you'll gain the practical knowledge to succeed. It's designed for aspiring and current writers who want to understand the business side of their craft, from identifying market opportunities to securing clients and managing projects.

Think of it like learning to bake a cake, but for your writing career. You'll get the recipe (strategies), learn about the ingredients (your skills and market needs), and understand the process from mixing to presentation (finding clients and delivering quality work). Instead of just hoping your cake tastes good, you'll learn how to consistently bake delicious, profitable writing projects.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to make money from my writing?

No, a formal degree is not required to succeed as a professional writer. This program focuses on practical skills, portfolio building, and business strategies that are highly valued by clients and employers.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

No, this program is a skill-building course focused on practical application, not a certification exam. Therefore, an exam voucher is not applicable or included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-guided online program, allowing you to learn at your own pace. While access is provided for a generous period, most learners typically complete the core content within 3 months.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to equip you with the practical skills and business acumen needed to earn money from your writing, rather than preparing you for a specific certification exam.

What kind of jobs can I get after taking this course?

This program prepares you for various roles such as a freelance writer, content creator, copywriter, blogger, or even to start your own writing-focused business. It equips you with the skills to find and secure paying writing work.
