What is this field?

An inclusive classroom is a dynamic learning environment where every student, regardless of their background, abilities, or learning style, feels valued, supported, and has equal access to educational opportunities. It's about designing instruction and fostering a culture that celebrates diversity and removes barriers to participation, ensuring all learners can achieve their full potential.

Think of it like a master chef learning to adapt recipes for every dietary need, ensuring everyone at the table enjoys a delicious and nourishing meal. This training equips educators with the tools to 'adapt their recipes'—their teaching methods—so every student can savor and benefit from the learning experience.
