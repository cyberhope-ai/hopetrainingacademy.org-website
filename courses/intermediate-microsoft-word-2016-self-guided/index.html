What is this field?

Intermediate Microsoft Word training equips you with the skills to move beyond basic document creation, enabling you to leverage powerful features for professional communication and data management. This program focuses on enhancing your ability to produce sophisticated documents, integrate various content types, and automate routine tasks, making you a more valuable asset in any office environment.

Think of basic Word skills as knowing how to drive a car on a straight road. Intermediate skills are like learning to navigate complex city streets, use advanced GPS features, and even perform basic maintenance. You're not just getting from point A to B; you're doing it efficiently, with style, and ready for more challenging journeys.

How the field grew

1983Microsoft Word 1.0 released, pioneering word processing on personal computers.
1990Word for Windows 1.0 launched, quickly becoming the dominant word processing software.
2007Introduction of the Ribbon interface, a significant redesign that streamlined access to features.
2013Deeper integration with Office 365, emphasizing cloud collaboration and accessibility.
2016Microsoft Word 2016 released, further enhancing collaboration tools and user experience.
