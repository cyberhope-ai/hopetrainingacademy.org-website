What is this field?

Speed reading is a collection of techniques designed to significantly increase your reading rate while maintaining or even improving your comprehension. It involves training your eyes and brain to process information more efficiently, moving beyond traditional word-by-word reading to absorb larger chunks of text at once. This skill is not about skimming or skipping content, but rather about optimizing your focus and processing speed to understand more in less time.

Think of speed reading as upgrading your brain's operating system for information intake. Instead of passively consuming text, you'll learn active strategies to quickly identify key ideas, filter out unnecessary details, and retain critical information. This makes you a more effective learner, a more productive professional, and better equipped to handle the vast amount of information encountered daily.

How the field grew

1920sMerrill Ream, a pioneer in reading research, began developing methods to improve reading efficiency and comprehension.
1930sReam's foundational work, often referred to as the 'Ream method,' laid the groundwork for modern speed reading techniques, focusing on eye movement and perception.
PresentThe principles of the Merrill Ream method continue to be a cornerstone of effective reading instruction, adapted for contemporary learning environments and digital texts.

What the career pays

While speed reading is not a job role itself, it is a foundational skill that significantly enhances performance across virtually all professional fields. Professionals who master speed reading can process reports, emails, research materials, and industry updates more quickly, leading to improved decision-making and overall productivity.
