What is this field?

Great decision-making is the cornerstone of success in any career. It's the ability to analyze situations, weigh options, and choose the most effective path forward, even under pressure. This course equips you with structured approaches to navigate complex choices, minimize risks, and achieve desired outcomes, transforming uncertainty into opportunity.

Think of decision-making skills as your internal GPS. Just as a GPS guides you through traffic and detours to your destination, these skills provide a systematic way to process information, anticipate consequences, and select the best route to your goals. It's not just about making a choice, but about making the *right* choice consistently.

How the field grew

1950sEarly theories of decision-making emerge, including Herbert Simon's concept of 'bounded rationality'.
1970sDaniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky's groundbreaking work on cognitive biases and heuristics revolutionizes understanding of human judgment.
2000sThe rise of data analytics and evidence-based practices emphasizes quantitative approaches to decision support.
2020sIntegration of artificial intelligence and machine learning tools begins to augment and transform human decision-making processes.

Frequently asked questions

This course focuses on developing practical soft skills and does not conclude with a certification exam, so no exam voucher is necessary or included.
