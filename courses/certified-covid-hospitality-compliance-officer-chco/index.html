What is this field?

A Certified COVID Hospitality Compliance Officer (CHCO) is a dedicated professional responsible for implementing and enforcing health and safety protocols related to COVID-19 within the hospitality sector. This role is crucial for enabling restaurants, event venues, and other public-facing businesses to operate safely, protecting both customers and staff from the spread of infectious diseases.

Think of a CHCO as the 'safety captain' for a hospitality business. Just as a ship's captain ensures all maritime rules are followed for a safe voyage, a CHCO ensures all health guidelines—from personal hygiene to disinfection and regulatory compliance—are meticulously observed, allowing guests to enjoy their experience with confidence and staff to work securely.
