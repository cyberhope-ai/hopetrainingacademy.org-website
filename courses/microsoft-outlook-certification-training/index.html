What is this field?

Microsoft Outlook Certification training equips you with the essential skills to master one of the most widely used email and personal information management applications in the business world. This program goes beyond basic usage, delving into advanced features for managing communications, scheduling, contacts, and tasks efficiently.

Think of it like becoming a master conductor of your daily professional symphony. Instead of juggling countless instruments (emails, meetings, contacts) haphazardly, you learn to orchestrate them seamlessly, ensuring every note is played on time and in harmony. This certification validates your ability to leverage Outlook's full power, making you an indispensable asset in any office environment.

How the field grew

1997Microsoft Office User Specialist (MOUS) program launched, establishing a benchmark for Office proficiency.
2003The certification program was rebranded as Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS), expanding its global recognition.
2010sMOS certifications evolved to incorporate cloud-integrated features with the rise of Office 365.
PresentMOS remains the industry-standard credential for demonstrating expertise in Microsoft Office applications, including Outlook.
