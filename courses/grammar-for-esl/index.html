What is this field?

This online program is specifically designed for English as a Second Language (ESL) learners who are preparing for college-level studies. It provides a comprehensive foundation in English grammar and structural principles essential for academic success, helping you confidently navigate complex texts, write clear essays, and participate effectively in classroom discussions.

Think of grammar as the operating system for your language. Just as a computer needs a stable OS to run applications smoothly, mastering English grammar provides the essential framework for clear communication, allowing your ideas to be expressed precisely and understood without misinterpretation in an academic setting.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Module 1: Parts of Speech

Master nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.

02
Module 2: Sentence Structure

Understand subject-verb agreement, sentence types, clauses, and phrases for clear expression.

03
Module 3: Verb Tenses

Learn simple, continuous, and perfect tenses, including active and passive voice.

04
Module 4: Punctuation and Capitalization

Apply correct usage of commas, periods, semicolons, apostrophes, and capitalization rules.

05
Module 5: Modifiers and Parallelism

Effectively use adjectives and adverbs, avoiding dangling modifiers and ensuring parallel structure.

06
Module 6: Common Grammar Errors

Identify and correct run-on sentences, fragments, pronoun agreement issues, and confusing words.

07
Module 7: Academic Writing Style

Develop formal language, clarity, conciseness, and avoid colloquialisms in academic contexts.

Frequently asked questions

Is a degree required to enroll?

No, there are no formal degree requirements to enroll in this program. It is designed for English as a Second Language learners preparing for college.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on foundational grammar skills and does not lead to a certification exam, therefore no voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This is a self-paced online program. While typical completion times vary, it is designed to be flexible to fit your schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

No, this program is focused on building essential English grammar and structure for academic success, not a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program provides foundational English language skills crucial for success in higher education and any professional role requiring strong written and verbal communication. It is a stepping stone to various academic and career paths.
