What is this field?

Business entity formation involves the legal process of establishing a new company or organization. This critical step determines the legal structure of a business, such as a sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, or corporation, which impacts everything from liability and taxation to ownership and operational flexibility. Understanding these structures is fundamental for anyone working in legal support, business administration, or entrepreneurship.

Think of it like choosing the right foundation for a house. Just as a strong, suitable foundation ensures the house stands firm and meets its purpose, selecting the correct business entity provides the legal framework for a company's stability, growth, and compliance with regulations.

How the field grew

1800sEarly forms of partnerships and sole proprietorships emerge as primary business structures.
Early 1900sThe modern corporation gains prominence, offering limited liability to owners.
1977Wyoming enacts the first Limited Liability Company (LLC) statute in the United States.
1990sLLCs are widely adopted across all U.S. states, offering hybrid benefits of corporations and partnerships.
Present DayContinued evolution of entity types and regulatory frameworks to meet diverse business needs.
