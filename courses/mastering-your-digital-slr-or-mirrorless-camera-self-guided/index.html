What is this field?

This hands-on training program teaches you to move beyond automatic settings and take full control of your digital SLR or mirrorless camera. You'll delve into the essential elements of photography, including how to manipulate aperture for depth of field, shutter speed for capturing motion, and ISO for light sensitivity. Understanding these core concepts allows you to consistently achieve the creative vision you have for your images.

Think of it like learning to drive a stick shift instead of an automatic car. While automatic is convenient, mastering manual controls gives you unparalleled precision and creative freedom. This program provides the foundational knowledge and practical techniques to confidently adjust your camera's settings, ensuring you capture professional-quality photos in any situation, from portraits to landscapes and action shots.

How the field grew

1975First digital camera prototype developed, laying groundwork for future innovation.
1990sEarly commercial digital cameras emerge, offering a glimpse into the filmless future.
2000sDigital SLR cameras become widely accessible, revolutionizing professional and enthusiast photography.
2010sMirrorless cameras gain significant market share, offering compact size with professional-grade performance.
2020sAdvanced computational photography and AI integration enhance camera capabilities and image quality.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to pursue photography?

No, formal degrees are not typically required for a career in photography. A strong portfolio showcasing your technical skills and artistic vision is paramount.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses on practical skill development and does not lead to a certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this online course?

As a self-guided online course, completion time varies based on your pace. Most students dedicate approximately 20-30 hours to master the material.

Is this course for a specific camera model?

No, the fundamental principles and techniques taught in this program apply universally to all digital SLR and mirrorless cameras, regardless of brand or specific model.

What kind of jobs can I get after mastering my camera?

Mastering your camera is a foundational skill for various photography careers, including portrait photographer, event photographer, photojournalist, commercial photographer, and fine art photographer.
