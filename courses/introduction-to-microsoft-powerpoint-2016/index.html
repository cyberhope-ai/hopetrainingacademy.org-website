What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 is a powerful presentation software that allows you to create dynamic and engaging visual aids for various purposes, from business proposals to educational lectures. It's like having a digital canvas where you can combine text, images, charts, and multimedia to tell a compelling story or convey complex information clearly and effectively. This course focuses on mastering the tools within PowerPoint 2016 to build professional-grade presentations.

Learning PowerPoint 2016 equips you with a fundamental skill for nearly any professional environment. Whether you're presenting to clients, training colleagues, or summarizing project progress, the ability to design clear, impactful slides is crucial. This program will guide you through creating visually appealing presentations that captivate your audience and ensure your message resonates.

How the field grew

1987Forethought Inc. develops 'Presenter', later acquired by Microsoft and renamed PowerPoint.
1990Microsoft releases PowerPoint 2.0 for Windows, expanding its user base.
1997PowerPoint 97 (part of Office 97) introduces advanced animation, sound, and video capabilities.
2007The Ribbon interface is introduced with Office 2007, significantly changing the user experience.
2016PowerPoint 2016 is released, focusing on enhanced collaboration and cloud integration.
