What is this field?

Digital literacy is the foundational ability to find, evaluate, create, and communicate information using digital technologies. In today's fast-paced world, nearly every job and daily task requires comfort with devices, online platforms, and cloud-based tools. This program equips you with the practical skills to navigate the digital landscape confidently, from managing emails and virtual meetings to using productivity suites and understanding online security.

Think of digital literacy as learning to drive in the information superhighway. Just as you need to know how to operate a car, follow traffic laws, and use GPS to get around safely and efficiently, digital literacy teaches you how to operate computers and smartphones, understand online etiquette, and protect yourself from cyber threats to thrive in our connected society. It's the essential skillset for participating fully in modern life and the workforce.

How the field grew

Early 2000sInternet access becomes widespread, making basic computer skills crucial for daily life and work.
Late 2000sSmartphones and social media emerge, expanding the need for digital communication and online presence.
2010sCloud computing and collaborative online tools revolutionize workplaces, demanding new productivity skills.
2020sRemote work, advanced cybersecurity threats, and AI tools elevate digital literacy from a convenience to a critical necessity.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no prerequisites for this program. It is designed for beginners and professionals alike, focusing on practical skills rather than academic degrees.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

An exam voucher is not included with this program, as digital literacy is a foundational skill set rather than a single certification.

How long will it take to complete this training?

This self-paced program is designed to be completed in approximately 61 course hours, allowing you to learn at your own schedule.

Is there a certification exam for digital literacy?

While there isn't a single universal certification exam for 'digital literacy,' this program prepares you with the foundational skills that are often assessed in various entry-level IT or administrative certifications, enhancing your overall employability.

What kind of jobs can I get with digital literacy skills?

Digital literacy is a universal skill essential for nearly all modern jobs. It opens doors to roles like administrative assistant, office clerk, customer service representative, and any position requiring effective online communication, cloud productivity, and secure digital practices.
