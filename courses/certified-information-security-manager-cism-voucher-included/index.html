What is this field?

The Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) credential is a globally recognized certification for experienced information security professionals who manage, design, oversee, and assess an enterprise's information security. It validates your expertise in information security governance, program development, incident management, and risk management, positioning you as a strategic leader in the cybersecurity domain.

Think of an Information Security Manager as the chief architect and builder of a fortress, but for an organization's digital assets. They don't just put up walls; they design the entire security strategy, manage the guards (security teams), plan for potential sieges (cyberattacks), and ensure the fortress evolves to protect against new threats. This program equips you with the knowledge to lead these critical efforts effectively.

How the field grew

Early 2000sGrowing recognition of the critical need for dedicated information security management roles in organizations.
2002ISACA launches the CISM certification, establishing a global standard for information security leadership and management.
2010sEscalating cyber threats, data breaches, and complex regulatory demands solidify CISM's importance for senior security professionals.
PresentCISM remains a premier credential, validating the skills required to manage and develop enterprise-wide information security programs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in the CISM program?

While there are no formal prerequisites to take this course, ISACA, the certifying body, recommends at least five years of work experience in information security to qualify for the CISM certification itself. This program is designed for professionals with existing technical expertise looking to move into management.

Is the CISM exam voucher included with this program?

Yes, your enrollment in this CISM career training program includes a prepaid voucher for the current CISM certification exam, covering the cost of sitting for the exam upon eligibility.

How long does it take to complete the CISM training program?

This is a self-paced online program totaling 75 course hours. You can progress through the material at your own speed, allowing flexibility to fit your schedule.

Does this program prepare me for the current CISM certification exam?

Absolutely. This program is meticulously designed to prepare you for the current Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) certification exam, covering all the necessary domains and objectives.

What kind of job can I get with CISM certification?

A CISM certification can qualify you for leadership roles such as Information Security Manager, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Security Consultant, IT Audit Manager, and other senior positions focused on information security governance and risk management.
