What is this field?

SOLIDWORKS is a powerful computer-aided design (CAD) software used by engineers and designers worldwide to create 3D models of products and parts. It allows you to transform conceptual ideas into detailed, manufacturable designs, from individual components to complex assemblies and technical drawings. This program provides the foundational skills to master its core tools and workflows.

Think of SOLIDWORKS as a digital sculptor's studio, but instead of clay, you're shaping virtual materials with precision tools. You can design anything from a simple bolt to an entire espresso machine, visualize it from every angle, and even simulate how its parts fit and move together, all before a single physical prototype is ever made.

How the field grew

1995SOLIDWORKS 95, the first commercial version, is released, making 3D CAD accessible on Windows platforms.
1997Dassault Systèmes, creators of CATIA, acquires SOLIDWORKS Corporation, integrating it into their leading design software portfolio.
Early 2000sSOLIDWORKS expands its capabilities with the introduction of Product Data Management (PDM) and integrated simulation tools.
2010sContinued innovation focuses on cloud-based design solutions (3DEXPERIENCE platform) and advanced manufacturing tools, broadening its industry reach.

Inside the course, module by module

01
SOLIDWORKS Interface & Core Tools

Navigate the user interface, apply sketching tools, constraints, and dimensions to drive parametric models.

02
3D Part Modeling Fundamentals

Build 3D parts using essential features like extrude, revolve, cut, fillet, shell, and pattern, designing with intent.

03
Assemblies & Sub-Assemblies

Create and animate assemblies with advanced mates, configurations, and exploded views to communicate design.

04
Professional 2D Drawings

Produce professional 2D drawings with views, dimensions, annotations, custom templates, and tolerances.

05
Advanced Modeling Techniques

Master specialized tools including sheet metal, weldments, boundary/loft features, and surfacing.

06
Project-Based Application & Visualization

Complete cohesive projects like a playground and espresso machine, incorporating rendering and final presentations.
