What is this field?

American Sign Language (ASL) is a complete, natural language with its own grammar and syntax, used primarily by people who are Deaf or hard of hearing in the United States and Canada. It's a vibrant visual language that relies on handshapes, movements, facial expressions, and body postures to convey meaning. Learning ASL opens doors to new communities, cultures, and communication opportunities.

This program is designed to immerse you in ASL through silent instructional videos, emphasizing proper sign techniques and crucial facial expressions for effective communication. Think of it like learning to play a musical instrument by watching and mimicking a master; you'll build muscle memory and expressive fluency to engage in conversations confidently, mastering fingerspelling and a wide array of signs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program does not require a prior degree. It is designed to teach American Sign Language from the ground up, focusing on practical communication skills.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program focuses on language acquisition and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long will it take to complete the program?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to learn at your own speed. The time to completion will vary based on your individual learning style and schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific ASL certification exam?

This program provides foundational skills in American Sign Language for communication. While it builds a strong base, it is not specifically designed to prepare for professional ASL interpreter certification exams, which typically require advanced training and proficiency.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Completing this program equips you with fundamental ASL communication skills, which can be beneficial for roles requiring interaction with the Deaf community, such as support staff, community advocates, or as a stepping stone towards further training to become a professional Sign Language Interpreter.
