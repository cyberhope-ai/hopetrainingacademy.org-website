What is this field?

This program is designed for educators ready to harness the power of the internet to create more engaging, efficient, and relevant learning experiences for their students. You'll explore practical applications of online resources, communication tools, and digital safety protocols, making technology a seamless part of your instructional toolkit.

Think of the internet as an expansive, ever-evolving digital library and collaboration space. This training teaches you how to effectively navigate and curate this vast resource, turning it into a personalized, interactive learning environment that sparks curiosity and fosters critical thinking in your students.

How the field grew

1990sEarly internet adoption in classrooms for basic research and email communication.
Early 2000sEmergence of educational websites, interactive whiteboards, and digital learning software.
Late 2000sRise of Web 2.0 tools, social media, and collaborative online platforms for student projects.
2010sWidespread integration of cloud-based learning management systems and mobile devices in education.
2020sFocus on personalized learning, AI integration, and advanced virtual environments to enhance instruction.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for professional development and does not require a specific degree. It's ideal for current educators or those aspiring to integrate technology into teaching.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, this program focuses on skill development and practical application, and does not include an exam voucher.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This online program is typically completed over 6 weeks, with new sessions starting monthly, allowing you to learn at a flexible pace.

Is there a certification exam associated with this program?

This program is a professional development course focused on practical skills rather than a specific certification exam. You will gain valuable knowledge and a certificate of completion.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

This program enhances the skills of current teachers, instructional coordinators, and curriculum developers, preparing you to effectively integrate internet tools into various educational roles and settings.
