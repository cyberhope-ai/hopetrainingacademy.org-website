What is this field?

A business plan is a comprehensive document that outlines a company's objectives, strategies, financial projections, and operational details. It serves as a roadmap for your venture, guiding decision-making and providing a clear vision for growth. Whether you're seeking investment, launching a new product, or simply aiming for structured growth, a well-crafted business plan is indispensable.

Think of a business plan as the architectural blueprint for your dream building. Just as an architect meticulously designs every aspect of a structure before construction begins, a business plan details every component of your enterprise. It ensures stability, identifies potential challenges, and articulates how your vision will come to life, attracting the necessary resources and support.

How the field grew

1950sFormalization of strategic planning in corporate settings, laying groundwork for structured business development.
1970sRise of venture capital, standardizing the requirement for detailed business plans to secure funding.
1990sThe internet era emphasizes rapid growth and adaptable business models, making agile planning crucial.
2010sLean Startup methodology promotes iterative, customer-centric planning, evolving the traditional business plan approach.
