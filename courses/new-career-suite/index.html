What is this field?

The New Career Suite is a comprehensive program designed for individuals seeking to transition into a new professional field or enhance their foundational skills for entry-level roles. It focuses on building essential competencies that are highly valued across various industries, empowering you with the confidence and practical knowledge needed to embark on a fresh career journey.

Think of it like building a versatile toolkit for your professional journey. Instead of specializing in one specific trade, you're gathering essential tools – communication, organization, basic software proficiency – that can be applied across many different entry-level roles, making you adaptable and ready for various opportunities.

Frequently asked questions

Is a college degree required for this program?

No, our New Career Suite is designed to equip individuals with practical skills and knowledge, making a college degree optional for entry into many of the roles it prepares you for.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on foundational career readiness and does not typically lead to a single certification exam, therefore an exam voucher is not included.

How long does it take to complete the New Career Suite?

The New Career Suite is designed for flexibility, allowing you to progress at your own pace. Completion time varies based on individual dedication and the specific modules you choose to focus on.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

The New Career Suite is a broad program focused on foundational skills for career readiness and exploration, rather than preparing for one specific industry certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this suite?

This suite prepares you for a variety of entry-level administrative and general office support roles, such as Office Assistant, Administrative Clerk, or Data Entry Specialist, where versatile foundational skills are highly valued.
