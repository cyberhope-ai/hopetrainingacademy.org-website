What is this field?

Legal and ethical issues in healthcare encompass the complex rules, regulations, and moral principles that govern medical practice, patient care, and health information. This foundational knowledge is essential for ensuring patient safety, protecting privacy, and maintaining the integrity of healthcare operations, guiding professionals in making sound decisions that comply with the law and uphold ethical standards.

Think of it like the operating system for a computer: just as an OS ensures all software runs smoothly and securely, understanding healthcare law and ethics provides the essential framework that allows healthcare providers to deliver effective, safe, and responsible care within established boundaries.

How the field grew

1996HIPAA enacted, establishing national standards for patient data privacy and security.
2009HITECH Act expanded HIPAA's scope, strengthening enforcement and promoting electronic health records.
2010Affordable Care Act (ACA) brought major reforms, influencing healthcare access, quality, and ethical considerations.
PresentOngoing evolution of telehealth regulations, AI ethics, and patient rights continue to shape the field.
