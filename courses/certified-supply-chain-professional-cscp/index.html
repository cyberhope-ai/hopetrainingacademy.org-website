What is this field?

A Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) is an expert in optimizing the flow of goods, information, and finances from raw materials to the final customer. This includes everything from sourcing and procurement to manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, and customer service. CSCP certification validates your comprehensive understanding of global supply chain best practices, strategic planning, and operational execution.

Think of a supply chain as a complex river system, where products, information, and funds flow from their source (raw materials) to the ocean (the end customer). A CSCP is like the chief engineer of this river system, ensuring every tributary, dam, and channel operates efficiently, sustainably, and cost-effectively, preventing blockages and optimizing the entire journey.

How the field grew

1980s-1990sThe concept of 'Supply Chain Management' emerges as a critical business discipline.
2006APICS launches the Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) program to address global supply chain complexities.
2018APICS rebrands to the Association for Supply Chain Management (ASCM), expanding its global reach and resources.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no specific degree prerequisites to enroll in this career-training program. However, the ASCM CSCP certification itself has eligibility requirements, typically involving a degree or significant professional experience.

Is the CSCP exam voucher included with this program?

An exam voucher for the Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) exam is not included with this training program. You will need to purchase the exam voucher separately from ASCM.

How long does it take to complete the CSCP training program?

This self-paced program consists of 120 course hours. The total time to complete it will vary based on your individual study schedule and commitment.

Which version of the CSCP exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) certification exam offered by the Association for Supply Chain Management (ASCM), covering the latest industry standards and best practices.

What kind of jobs can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and certification, you'll be prepared for roles such as Supply Chain Manager, Logistician, Purchasing Manager, Operations Analyst, Inventory Control Specialist, and other positions focused on optimizing global supply chain operations.
