What is this field?

Stress management involves developing effective strategies and techniques to cope with and reduce the negative impacts of stress on your physical and mental health. It's about understanding the root causes of stress, recognizing its symptoms, and implementing proactive measures to maintain balance and enhance overall well-being.

Think of stress like a car's dashboard warning light. It's not inherently bad; it's a signal that something needs attention. Stress management is learning how to interpret those signals, understand what they mean for your 'engine' (your body and mind), and then apply the right 'maintenance' to keep running smoothly, rather than letting the warning light lead to a breakdown.

How the field grew

1936Hans Selye introduces the concept of 'stress' and the General Adaptation Syndrome.
1970sEmergence of structured stress reduction techniques, including relaxation and biofeedback.
1990sIncreased focus on workplace stress and the development of corporate wellness initiatives.
2010sWidespread adoption of mindfulness-based practices and resilience training for personal and professional development.
