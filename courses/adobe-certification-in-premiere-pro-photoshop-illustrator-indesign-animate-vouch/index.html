What is this field?

The Adobe Certified Professional program at Hope Training Academy equips you with mastery over five essential Adobe Creative Cloud applications: Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, InDesign, and Animate. This comprehensive training goes beyond basic software use, immersing you in hands-on projects that build real-world proficiency and prepare you for the current Adobe Certified Professional certification exams.

Think of it as becoming a digital Swiss Army knife for the creative industry. Just as a single tool can tackle multiple tasks, you'll gain the diverse skills to handle everything from stunning visual design and branding to dynamic video production and animation, making you an invaluable asset across various creative fields.

How the field grew

1990Adobe Photoshop 1.0 launched, revolutionizing digital image editing.
1993Adobe Premiere (later Premiere Pro) introduced, setting a standard for video editing.
1999Adobe InDesign debuted, transforming desktop publishing and layout design.
2005Adobe acquired Macromedia, integrating Flash (now Animate) into its creative suite.
2012Adobe Creative Cloud launched, offering subscription access to the full suite of creative tools.
