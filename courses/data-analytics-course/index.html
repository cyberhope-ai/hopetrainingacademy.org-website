What is this field?

Data analytics is the process of inspecting, cleansing, transforming, and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, informing conclusions, and supporting decision-making. In today's data-driven world, organizations across every industry rely on skilled analysts to interpret complex datasets and extract valuable insights that propel growth and innovation.

Think of a data analyst as a detective for businesses. Just as a detective pieces together clues to solve a mystery, a data analyst gathers, organizes, and examines information to uncover patterns and trends that help companies understand their customers, optimize operations, and predict future outcomes. This program equips you with the essential tools and techniques to become that indispensable detective.

How the field grew

1980s-1990sThe rise of personal computing and spreadsheets like Microsoft Excel revolutionized data handling, making basic analysis accessible to a wider audience and laying the groundwork for business intelligence.
Early 2000sAs data volumes grew, Structured Query Language (SQL) became the standard for managing and querying large databases, enabling more sophisticated data retrieval and manipulation.
2010sThe explosion of big data led to the widespread adoption of powerful programming languages like Python for advanced statistical analysis, machine learning, and automation, alongside visual analytics tools like Tableau for intuitive data storytelling.
Late 2010s-PresentIntegration of machine learning and artificial intelligence has become crucial, allowing analysts to build predictive models and automate complex data processes, moving beyond descriptive analysis to prescriptive insights.
