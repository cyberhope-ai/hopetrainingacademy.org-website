What is this field?

Business law, also known as commercial law, is the body of law that governs business and commercial transactions. This foundational course introduces you to the legal principles that underpin nearly every aspect of commerce, from forming a business to managing contracts and ensuring compliance with regulations. Understanding business law is critical for anyone involved in business operations, legal support, or compliance.

Think of it like learning the rules of the road before you drive a car; business law provides the essential legal framework for business operations. It ensures fairness, resolves disputes, and protects the rights and responsibilities of all parties involved in commercial activities, making it an indispensable skill set for navigating the professional world.

How the field grew

1800sDevelopment of modern commercial law principles, influenced by common law and mercantile customs.
Early 1900sRise of corporate law and increased government regulation of business practices.
1960sFormalization and growth of paralegal and legal assistant roles to support legal professionals.
2000sIncreased complexity in digital transactions, global commerce, and regulatory compliance requirements.
