What is this field?

The PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP) program is designed for professionals who work on Agile teams or whose organizations are adopting Agile approaches. This training covers a broad spectrum of Agile methodologies, including Scrum, Kanban, Lean, extreme programming (XP), and test-driven development (TDD). It equips you with the practical skills to manage projects effectively, adapt to change, and foster collaboration within your team and with stakeholders.

Think of Agile project management like navigating a complex journey with a flexible map. Instead of planning every single step upfront, you break the journey into smaller segments, constantly checking your progress, adapting to new information, and ensuring everyone on your team is moving efficiently towards the destination. The PMI-ACP credential validates your expertise in guiding these dynamic, iterative processes to deliver successful outcomes.

How the field grew

2001The Agile Manifesto is published, defining core principles for iterative software development.
2011The Project Management Institute (PMI) introduces the PMI-ACP certification to validate Agile practitioners' skills.
2017PMI publishes the Agile Practice Guide, integrating Agile principles into the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this training program?

No, there are no prerequisites to enroll in our PMI-ACP training program. However, to sit for the PMI-ACP certification exam, PMI requires a secondary degree, 21 contact hours of Agile training (which this program provides), 12 months of general project experience within the last 5 years, and 8 months of Agile project experience within the last 3 years.

Is the PMI-ACP certification exam voucher included with this purchase?

No, an exam voucher for the PMI-ACP certification exam is not included with the purchase of this training program.

How long will I have to complete the PMI-ACP training program?

This self-paced program provides 60 course hours of content. You will have access to the course for 3 months from your enrollment date, allowing ample time to complete the material.

Does this program prepare me for the current PMI-ACP certification exam?

Yes, this program is designed to thoroughly prepare you for the current PMI-ACP certification exam, covering all the necessary domains and knowledge areas required by the Project Management Institute.

What kind of jobs can I get with a PMI-ACP certification?

A PMI-ACP certification opens doors to various roles such as Agile Project Manager, Scrum Master, Product Owner, Agile Coach, or a team member in an Agile environment, demonstrating your expertise in leading and contributing to Agile projects across industries.
