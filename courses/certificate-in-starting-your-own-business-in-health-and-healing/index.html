What is this field?

This program provides a comprehensive roadmap for aspiring entrepreneurs in the health and healing sector. You'll learn how to transform your passion for wellness into a viable business, covering everything from initial concept development to sustainable growth strategies. It's designed for individuals ready to take control of their career and build a venture that aligns with their values.

Think of it like building a house: you have a vision, but you need a blueprint, materials, and a construction plan. This program gives you that blueprint and guides you through gathering your 'materials' (financing, market knowledge) and executing your 'construction plan' (marketing, operations) to build a sturdy and successful health and healing business from the ground up.

How the field grew

1953The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) is established, formalizing support for entrepreneurs.
1980sPersonal computing revolutionizes business operations, making small business management more accessible.
2000sThe rise of e-commerce and digital marketing opens new global markets for health and wellness businesses.
2010sIncreased public awareness and demand for holistic health and healing services fuels industry growth.
2020sThe pandemic accelerates digital transformation, making online health and healing businesses more prevalent.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

No, this program is designed for individuals eager to start their own health and healing business, regardless of prior academic degrees. It focuses on practical, actionable skills.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

This program focuses on business development skills and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete this program?

This program consists of 11 contact hours, which can be completed at your own pace online.

Is there a certification exam associated with this program?

No, this program is a skill-building course focused on the practical aspects of starting a business, rather than preparing you for a specific industry certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon completion, you will be equipped to start and manage your own health and healing business, becoming a self-employed entrepreneur, business owner, or founder in the wellness sector.
