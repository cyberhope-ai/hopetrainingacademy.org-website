What is this field?

Holistic and integrative health focuses on the whole person, considering physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual well-being. This foundational program introduces you to key principles that promote overall wellness, including effective stress management, balanced nutrition, beneficial physical activity, and creating supportive healing environments.

Think of it like building a strong, resilient house: you need a solid foundation that supports all its parts. Similarly, this program provides the essential building blocks for understanding and promoting health in a comprehensive way, ensuring all aspects of well-being are considered for a more robust and balanced life.

How the field grew

1970sEmergence of alternative and complementary medicine practices gaining public interest.
1990sGrowing recognition and research into the benefits of integrating traditional healing with modern healthcare.
2000sIncreased focus on preventive care, wellness, and personalized health approaches within mainstream medicine.
TodayHolistic and integrative health principles are widely adopted for promoting well-being and managing chronic conditions.
