What is this field?

The General Educational Development (GED) test is a nationally recognized exam that certifies academic skills equivalent to a high school diploma. Passing the GED demonstrates your proficiency in core subjects like language arts, math, science, and social studies, serving as a vital credential for adults seeking to advance their education or career.

Think of the GED as a universal key. Just as a single key can unlock many different doors, the GED credential unlocks numerous opportunities that require a high school diploma, from college admissions and vocational training programs to a wide range of entry-level and skilled employment positions.

How the field grew

1942First GED tests administered for returning WWII veterans
1971All 50 U.S. states adopt the GED as their official high school equivalency exam
2002Major revision to the GED test content and scoring
2014Launch of the current computer-based GED test series, emphasizing career and college readiness
