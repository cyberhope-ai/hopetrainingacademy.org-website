What is this field?

A Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) is a highly skilled professional responsible for optimizing manufacturing processes, designing production systems, and improving efficiency within an organization. They apply advanced engineering principles to transform raw materials into finished products, ensuring quality, cost-effectiveness, and innovation.

Think of a Certified Manufacturing Engineer as the architect and conductor of a factory floor. They don't just build the machines; they design the entire flow, from how materials enter to how products leave, constantly seeking ways to make the process smoother, faster, and more reliable, much like a master chef meticulously plans every step of a complex recipe for perfect execution.

How the field grew

Early 20th CenturyRise of scientific management and mass production, laying groundwork for manufacturing engineering disciplines.
Mid 20th CenturyPost-WWII industrial expansion drives focus on efficiency, quality control, and specialized manufacturing roles.
Late 20th CenturyIntegration of computer-aided design (CAD), robotics, and lean manufacturing principles revolutionizes production.
Early 21st CenturyEmergence of Industry 4.0, smart manufacturing, and additive manufacturing reshapes the industry landscape.
OngoingThe Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) credential evolves to validate expertise in cutting-edge manufacturing practices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this program?

While a degree can be beneficial, this program is designed for individuals with advanced manufacturing engineering experience, those in leadership positions, or professionals supporting manufacturing practices. The certification itself has specific experience and education requirements, which can be met through a combination of work experience and/or prior education.

Does this program include an exam voucher?

Yes, an exam voucher for the current Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) certification exam is included with your enrollment in this program.

How long will it take to complete this program?

This program is self-paced and includes 160 course hours. The actual time to complete it will depend on your individual study schedule and prior knowledge, but you can work through the material at your own convenience.

Which Certified Manufacturing Engineer exam does this program prepare me for?

This program prepares you for the current Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE) certification exam offered by SME (Society of Manufacturing Engineers).

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon earning your CMfgE certification, you'll be qualified for roles such as Manufacturing Engineer, Process Engineer, Production Engineer, or Advanced Manufacturing Specialist, working to optimize and innovate production systems across various industries.
