What is this field?

WordPress is the world's most popular content management system, powering over 43% of all websites on the internet. From personal blogs to complex corporate sites and e-commerce stores, its versatility and user-friendliness make it an indispensable tool for individuals and businesses alike. This comprehensive program guides you from foundational concepts to advanced techniques, enabling you to confidently build, customize, and manage professional-grade websites.

Think of WordPress as a powerful digital toolkit: instead of needing to craft every single piece of a house from raw materials, WordPress provides the framework, walls, and roof, allowing you to focus on the interior design, landscaping, and adding unique features. Our program teaches you how to wield this toolkit effectively, turning your creative visions into fully functional online presences without needing to write complex code from scratch.

How the field grew

2003WordPress 1.0 'Davis' released, offering a simple blogging platform.
2005Introduction of themes and plugins, vastly expanding customization and functionality.
2010sWordPress grows to dominate the CMS market, becoming the platform of choice for millions of websites globally.
2018Gutenberg editor introduced, revolutionizing content creation with block-based editing.
2022Full Site Editing (FSE) capabilities mature, allowing comprehensive site design directly within WordPress.

What the career pays

A WordPress developer designs, builds, and maintains websites using the WordPress content management system. They are responsible for implementing themes, plugins, and custom code to meet client specifications and ensure optimal site performance.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a WordPress developer?

No, formal degrees are not typically required. Employers prioritize practical skills and a strong portfolio, which this program helps you build.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

This program focuses on skill development and does not lead to a specific certification exam, therefore no exam voucher is included.

How long does it take to complete the Creating WordPress Websites Series?

The program consists of approximately 48 course hours, designed for flexible, self-paced learning to fit your schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific WordPress certification exam?

While there isn't a single official 'WordPress certification exam,' this program equips you with the practical skills and knowledge highly valued by employers for WordPress development roles.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Graduates can pursue roles such as WordPress Developer, Web Content Manager, Website Administrator, or Front-End Developer specializing in WordPress.
