What is this field?

Information security is the practice of protecting digital assets—data, systems, and networks—from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction. It involves a comprehensive strategy to identify, assess, and mitigate risks, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of an organization's critical information.

Think of an information security professional as a digital fortress architect and guardian. They design the defenses (like firewalls and encryption), set the rules for who gets in (access controls), regularly inspect for weaknesses (audits), and respond swiftly if an attack occurs, much like a castle's master builder and its vigilant guards.

How the field grew

1967ISACA founded, laying groundwork for IT audit standards.
1978CISA certification introduced, establishing a benchmark for IT audit professionals.
1989(ISC)2 formed, dedicated to certifying information security professionals.
1994CISSP certification launched, becoming a global standard for security expertise.
2001CISM certification released, focusing on information security management.
