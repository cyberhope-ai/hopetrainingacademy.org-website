What is this field?

Microsoft PowerPoint 365 is the industry-standard software for creating dynamic and compelling presentations. It's a powerful tool that allows you to combine text, images, charts, and multimedia into visually appealing slides, essential for conveying information effectively in academic, professional, and community settings.

Think of PowerPoint as your personal stage director for ideas. Just as a director orchestrates actors, sets, and lighting to tell a story, PowerPoint helps you arrange your data, visuals, and narrative into a cohesive and impactful presentation, ensuring your message resonates with your audience.

How the field grew

1987Forethought Inc. releases 'Presenter', later renamed PowerPoint.
1987Microsoft acquires PowerPoint, recognizing its potential for business communication.
1990PowerPoint 2.0 for Windows is released, integrating it deeply into the Microsoft Office suite.
2003PowerPoint 2003 introduces significant user interface and feature enhancements, solidifying its market dominance.
2013PowerPoint 2013 brings cloud integration and touch-friendly features, adapting to modern computing.
2019As part of Microsoft 365, PowerPoint evolves with continuous updates, AI-powered features, and enhanced collaboration tools.
