What is this field?

Homeschooling is a dynamic approach to education where parents or guardians take primary responsibility for their children's learning, typically outside of a traditional school setting. It offers flexibility and personalized instruction, allowing education to be tailored to a child's unique needs, interests, and learning style. This method empowers families to integrate learning into daily life, fostering a deep connection between education and personal growth.

This program demystifies the homeschooling journey, guiding you through establishing a robust academic foundation while ensuring your children thrive socially. Think of it like a personalized GPS for your educational adventure: it provides clear directions, highlights key landmarks, and helps you navigate any unexpected detours, ensuring you reach your destination of a well-rounded, successful home education.

How the field grew

1970sModern homeschooling movement begins to gain traction in the United States, driven by educational reform and parental choice.
1980sHomeschooling faces legal challenges but gradually gains recognition and legal status in many states.
1990sMost U.S. states legalize homeschooling, leading to increased resources and support networks for families.
2000sThe internet revolutionizes homeschooling with access to online curricula, communities, and educational tools.
PresentHomeschooling continues to grow, diversifying in methods and philosophies, supported by extensive resources and a strong community.

An exam voucher is not included with this self-guided program, as its focus is on practical skills and knowledge for homeschooling rather than a certification exam.

As a self-guided program, the completion time for 'Homeschool With Success' is flexible and varies based on your pace and schedule. You can progress through the material at your convenience.
