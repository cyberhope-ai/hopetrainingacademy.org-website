What is this field?

QuickBooks Online is the leading cloud-based accounting software designed to help businesses of all sizes manage their finances with ease. From tracking income and expenses to invoicing customers and reconciling bank accounts, it provides a comprehensive suite of tools to keep financial records accurate and organized. This program focuses on intermediate features, building upon foundational knowledge to tackle more complex financial tasks.

Think of QuickBooks Online as your business's digital financial command center. Just as a pilot uses an advanced dashboard to monitor every aspect of a flight, QuickBooks Online empowers you to see your business's financial health at a glance, make informed decisions, and ensure compliance, all accessible from anywhere with an internet connection.

How the field grew

1983Intuit, the creator of QuickBooks, is founded.
1992QuickBooks desktop software is first released, revolutionizing small business accounting.
2004QuickBooks Online (QBO) officially launches, bringing cloud-based financial management to businesses.
OngoingContinuous innovation and updates enhance QBO's features, solidifying its position as an industry leader.
