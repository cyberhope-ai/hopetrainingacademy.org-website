How the field grew

2013CompTIA launches its first Cloud+ certification, recognizing the growing importance of cloud infrastructure skills.
2016The certification undergoes its first major revision, updating content to reflect evolving cloud technologies and best practices.
2019Further updates are introduced, expanding coverage to include hybrid cloud environments and advanced security concepts.
2022The program is refreshed to address the latest trends in cloud deployment, operations, and automation.
PresentCompTIA Cloud+ continues to evolve, ensuring IT professionals are equipped with the most current skills for managing diverse cloud environments.

What the career pays

A CompTIA Cloud+ certified professional designs, implements, and maintains cloud infrastructure, ensuring optimal performance, security, and resource management. They are critical to organizations leveraging cloud technologies for scalability and efficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll?

No, a college degree is not required. While prior IT certifications like CompTIA A+ and Network+ or equivalent industry experience are strongly recommended, this program is designed to equip individuals with the skills needed for a successful cloud computing career regardless of formal degrees.

Is the certification exam voucher included?

Yes, your enrollment in this CompTIA Cloud+ training program includes an exam voucher, covering the cost to sit for the current CompTIA Cloud+ certification exam upon eligibility.

How long do I have to complete the program?

You will have six months of access to complete the CompTIA Cloud+ training program from your registration date, allowing you to learn at your own pace.

Which version of the CompTIA Cloud+ exam does this program prepare me for?

This program is designed to prepare you for the current CompTIA Cloud+ certification exam, ensuring you are ready for the latest industry standards and practices.

What kind of jobs can I get with CompTIA Cloud+ certification?

With a CompTIA Cloud+ certification, you can pursue roles such as Cloud Specialist, Cloud Engineer, Systems Engineer, or Computer Network Architect, all of which are in high demand across various industries.
