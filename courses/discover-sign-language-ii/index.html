What is this field?

Discover Sign Language II is an online program designed to deepen your understanding and fluency in American Sign Language (ASL). Building upon foundational knowledge, this course introduces more complex vocabulary, advanced grammatical structures, and essential cultural insights into the Deaf community. It's about moving beyond basic phrases to engage in more nuanced and meaningful conversations.

Think of it like moving from basic tourist phrases in a foreign language to being able to hold a real conversation with a native speaker. You'll learn to express a wider range of ideas, understand the subtleties of non-manual markers (facial expressions and body language), and gain a greater appreciation for the rich history and traditions of Deaf culture, ultimately fostering more effective and respectful communication.

How the field grew

1755Abbé de l'Épée establishes the first public school for the deaf in Paris, pioneering systematic sign language instruction.
1817The American School for the Deaf is founded in Hartford, CT, marking the first permanent school for the deaf in the United States.
1960sLinguistic research by William Stokoe at Gallaudet University formally recognizes American Sign Language (ASL) as a complete and distinct language.
1988The 'Deaf President Now' movement at Gallaudet University highlights the importance of Deaf leadership and cultural identity.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in Discover Sign Language II?

No, there are no degree requirements to enroll in this program. It is designed for individuals looking to build upon their foundational sign language skills.

Is an exam voucher included with this program?

No, an exam voucher is not included as this program focuses on skill development rather than a specific certification exam.

How long does it take to complete Discover Sign Language II?

This is a flexible online learning program, allowing you to progress at your own pace. The total duration will depend on your individual study habits and schedule.

Does this program prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This program is designed to enhance your general American Sign Language communication skills and cultural understanding, rather than preparing you for a specific certification exam.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

While this program is not direct job training, the enhanced communication skills gained are valuable in roles like customer service, healthcare support, education, and community engagement, where interacting with the Deaf community is beneficial.
