What is this field?

Business Data Analytics is the process of examining large datasets to discover patterns, correlations, and other insights that can inform strategic business decisions. It's about translating complex data into clear, understandable information that helps organizations improve operations, identify market trends, and gain a competitive edge. Professionals in this field bridge the gap between data science and business strategy, ensuring that data-driven insights lead to tangible business value.

Think of a Business Data Analyst as a detective for your company's performance. Instead of solving crimes, they solve business puzzles using data. They gather clues (data), analyze them to find patterns (insights), and then present their findings in a way that helps leaders make smarter choices, much like a detective presenting evidence to guide a jury's decision. The Certified Business Data Analytics (CBDA) credential validates your expertise in this critical domain.

How the field grew

Early 2000sEmergence of business intelligence and data warehousing as strategic tools.
2003The International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) is founded, establishing standards for business analysis.
2010sExplosion of 'Big Data' and advanced analytics, creating a demand for specialized data analysis roles.
2018IIBA introduces the Certified Business Data Analytics (CBDA) certification to validate data analytics competencies.
PresentBusiness data analytics becomes a cornerstone of modern business strategy and digital transformation.
