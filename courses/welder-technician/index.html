What is this field?

A Welder Technician is a skilled professional who specializes in joining metals using various welding techniques, cutting, and fabrication processes. They are crucial to industries ranging from construction and automotive to aerospace and manufacturing, ensuring structural integrity and precision in countless products and infrastructure projects. This role demands a blend of technical expertise, precision, and a strong commitment to safety.

Think of a Welder Technician as a structural artist or a metal sculptor. Just as an artist uses tools to shape and join materials into a cohesive piece, a Welder Technician uses specialized equipment to fuse metal components, creating strong, durable bonds that are fundamental to everything from bridges and buildings to intricate machinery and consumer goods.

How the field grew

Early 1900sDevelopment of arc welding processes, revolutionizing industrial manufacturing and construction.
1940sIntroduction of Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW) and Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), enhancing precision and speed.
1980sIntegration of robotics into welding, improving automation, consistency, and safety in production lines.
PresentContinuous advancements in materials science and digital controls, expanding welding applications and efficiency.

Jobs it opens

Welder

Performs manual or automated welding tasks across various industries.

Fabricator

Assembles metal structures from raw materials, often involving cutting, bending, and welding.

Solderer

Joins metals using solder, a metal alloy with a lower melting point than the base metals.

Brazier

Joins metals using brazing, a high-temperature joining process that uses a filler metal.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Welding Fundamentals & Safety

Introduction to welding processes, safety essentials, PPE, fumes, electrical safety, material tests, weld types and defects.

02
Metal Fabrication & Cutting

Metal cutting safety, band saw operation, press brake operations, oxyfuel and plasma cutting applications.

03
Inspection & Quality Assurance

Basic measurement, blueprint reading, GD&T, in-line inspection, ISO 9001, customer-focused quality.

04
Materials Science & Shop Math

Physical and mechanical properties of metals, steel classification, heat treatment, math fundamentals, geometry, trigonometry.

05
Rigging, Robotics & Mechanical Systems

Machine rigging, equipment, inspection, lifting, moving, rigging mechanics, robot troubleshooting and installations.

06
Lean Manufacturing & Management

Lean overview, continuous improvement, 5S, Six Sigma, troubleshooting, Kaizen, SPC, metrics, setup reduction.

07
Advanced Welding Techniques

GMAW, SMAW, GTAW applications, electrical power for arc welding, soldering overview.

08
Professional & Workforce Essentials

Communication, leadership, performance management, conflict resolution, team leadership, manufacturing management, personal effectiveness.
