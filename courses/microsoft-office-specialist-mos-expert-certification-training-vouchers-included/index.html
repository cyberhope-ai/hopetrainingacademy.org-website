What is this field?

The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) Expert certification is a globally recognized credential validating your advanced proficiency across the core Microsoft Office applications: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Access. This program goes beyond basic functionality, diving deep into sophisticated features and workflows that empower you to create, manage, and analyze information with unparalleled efficiency and professionalism.

Think of it like becoming a master craftsman of digital tools. Just as a skilled carpenter can build anything from a simple shelf to an intricate cabinet, an MOS Expert can transform raw data into insightful reports, draft compelling presentations, and streamline complex administrative tasks, making you an indispensable asset in any modern workplace.

How the field grew

1983Microsoft Word, the foundational word processing application, is first released.

2000sThe Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certification program gains widespread recognition as a benchmark for digital literacy.
PresentMOS Expert remains a highly valued credential, demonstrating advanced proficiency in critical business software.
