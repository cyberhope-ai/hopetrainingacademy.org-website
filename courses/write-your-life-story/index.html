How the field grew

Ancient TimesEarly forms of autobiography emerge in philosophical texts and personal letters, often focusing on spiritual or political journeys.
15th CenturyThe invention of the printing press democratizes publishing, making it possible for personal narratives to reach a wider audience.
18th-19th CenturiesThe rise of the novel and Romanticism popularizes the individual's story, leading to more formal autobiographical works like Rousseau's 'Confessions'.
20th CenturyAutobiography diversifies, encompassing memoirs, celebrity tell-alls, and personal histories, often exploring social and cultural shifts.
21st CenturyDigital platforms and self-publishing tools empower anyone to share their life story, fostering a boom in personal narrative creation.

What the career pays

A writer of life stories meticulously gathers memories, reflects on personal experiences, and crafts them into an engaging narrative. They structure events, develop themes, and use their unique voice to create an inspirational and professional autobiography for their intended audience.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to enroll in this program?

No, there are no formal educational prerequisites to enroll in our 'Write Your Life Story' program. We believe in accessible learning for everyone.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

This course focuses on personal development and writing skills, and therefore does not include an exam or a certification exam voucher.

How long does it take to complete the 'Write Your Life Story' course?

This online course is designed to be completed over 6 weeks, with approximately 24 course hours of material, allowing you to learn at a flexible pace.

Is there a certification exam for writing my life story?

This program is designed to help you create your personal autobiography, not to prepare you for a specific industry certification exam. Your completed life story is your credential!

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

While this course is primarily for personal enrichment, the writing and storytelling skills you develop are highly transferable. They can support roles such as a freelance writer, content creator, or even help you pursue a career as an author or memoirist, with a median salary for writers and authors around $74,650.
