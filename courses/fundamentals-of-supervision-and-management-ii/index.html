What is this field?

Supervision and management are the art and science of guiding individuals and teams to achieve organizational goals. It involves everything from setting clear expectations and delegating tasks to fostering a positive work environment and resolving conflicts. This program builds upon foundational principles, equipping you with advanced strategies to lead with confidence and inspire productivity.

Think of a manager as the conductor of an orchestra. While each musician is skilled, the conductor ensures everyone plays in harmony, sets the tempo, and brings out the best in each section to create a beautiful symphony. Similarly, a skilled supervisor orchestrates their team's efforts, ensuring individual talents contribute to a cohesive and successful outcome.

How the field grew

1911Frederick Taylor publishes 'The Principles of Scientific Management,' introducing efficiency and standardization to the workplace.
1920s-1930sThe Hawthorne Studies highlight the importance of human factors, social dynamics, and employee motivation in productivity.
1954Peter Drucker introduces 'Management by Objectives' (MBO), emphasizing goal setting and employee involvement.
1980sTotal Quality Management (TQM) gains prominence, focusing on continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.
2000s-PresentThe rise of agile methodologies and servant leadership, prioritizing adaptability, empowerment, and employee well-being.
