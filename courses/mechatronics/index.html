What is this field?

Mechatronics is an interdisciplinary field that combines mechanical engineering, electronics, computer engineering, robotics, and control systems. It's the science behind modern automated systems, integrating hardware and software to create intelligent machines and processes that drive industries from manufacturing to aerospace.

Think of mechatronics like the "brain and brawn" behind smart machines. It's not just about building a robot; it's about designing its mechanical structure, wiring its electrical components, programming its intelligence, and ensuring all these parts work seamlessly together to perform complex tasks efficiently and safely.

How the field grew

1969The term 'Mechatronics' was coined by Tetsuro Mori of Yaskawa Electric Corporation in Japan, defining the synergy of mechanics and electronics.
1980sThe rise of industrial robotics and advanced manufacturing processes significantly expanded the application and demand for mechatronic principles.
2000sIntegration of embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and the Internet of Things (IoT) further propelled mechatronics into diverse, intelligent automation solutions.
2020sVirtual reality (VR) and advanced simulation tools became integral to mechatronics training and development, offering immersive, hands-on learning experiences.
