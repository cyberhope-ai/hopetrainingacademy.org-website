What is this field?

This online training is designed for educators who want to elevate their field trips from mere excursions to deeply impactful, instructionally-aligned learning opportunities. You'll learn how to meticulously plan, integrate content seamlessly, and create engaging experiences that directly support your curriculum objectives.

Think of it like upgrading from a casual sightseeing tour to a meticulously curated educational expedition. Instead of just visiting a museum, you'll learn to design an experience where every exhibit reinforces specific learning goals, sparking curiosity and deeper understanding in your students long after the trip ends.

How the field grew

Early 1900sProgressive education movements emphasize 'learning by doing,' laying foundational principles for experiential learning.
1960s-1970sRise of constructivist theories and hands-on learning approaches, increasing focus on active student participation in educational outings.
1990s-2000sStandards-based education initiatives necessitate explicit curriculum alignment for all learning activities, including field trips, to demonstrate educational value.
PresentEducators increasingly integrate technology and data-driven insights to optimize educational excursions for maximum instructional impact and measurable outcomes.

Inside the course, module by module

01
Foundations of Field Trip Planning

Understanding the educational value of field trips, setting clear learning objectives, and initial logistical considerations.

02
Curriculum Alignment and Content Integration

Strategies for connecting field trip activities directly to academic standards and classroom content for seamless learning.

03
Maximizing Student Engagement

Techniques for fostering active participation, critical thinking, and memorable experiences before, during, and after the trip.

04
Practical Logistics and Safety

Managing transportation, permissions, budgeting, risk assessment, and ensuring a safe and organized outing.

05
Assessment and Reflection

Methods for evaluating learning outcomes, gathering feedback, and reflecting on the field trip's overall impact and areas for improvement.
