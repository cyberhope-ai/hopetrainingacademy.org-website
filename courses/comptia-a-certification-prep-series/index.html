What is this field?

CompTIA A+ is the foundational certification for entry-level IT professionals, validating essential skills across a wide range of hardware and software technologies. It covers everything from installing operating systems and configuring networks to troubleshooting common technical issues, making it a critical stepping stone for anyone aspiring to a career in IT support.

Think of CompTIA A+ as the 'mechanic's license' for computers. Just as a mechanic understands how car engines, brakes, and electrical systems work together, an A+ certified professional understands the inner workings of PCs, mobile devices, and networks, enabling them to diagnose and fix problems efficiently.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA introduces the A+ certification, establishing a benchmark for PC technician skills.
Late 1990sA+ gains widespread industry recognition as the essential entry-level IT credential.
Early 2000sThe certification evolves to include broader topics like networking and security fundamentals.
2010sA+ adapts to cover mobile devices, cloud computing basics, and modern operating systems.
PresentContinues to be the leading vendor-neutral certification for IT support, regularly updated to reflect current technologies.
