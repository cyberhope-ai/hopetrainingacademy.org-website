What is this field?

A Certified Physical Therapy Aide plays a crucial role in supporting physical therapists and physical therapy assistants. These dedicated professionals handle the non-medical tasks essential for a smooth-running clinic, ensuring patients receive the best possible care. From preparing treatment areas to assisting with patient transfers and maintaining equipment, they are the backbone of a rehabilitative environment.

Think of a Physical Therapy Aide as the essential stage manager for a physical therapy clinic. While the physical therapist directs the performance (patient treatment), the aide ensures all props are ready, the set is prepared, and everything runs efficiently behind the scenes, allowing the main actors to focus entirely on their craft.

How the field grew

1960sEmergence of physical therapy as a distinct healthcare profession, increasing demand for support roles.
1980sGrowing recognition of the need for trained support staff to enhance efficiency in physical therapy clinics.
Early 2000sFormalization of Physical Therapy Aide roles and the development of specialized certifications like the AMCA PTTC.
PresentContinued expansion of the physical therapy field, driving a significant and ongoing demand for skilled aides.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to become a Certified Physical Therapy Aide?

No, you do not need a college degree. To sit for the AMCA PTTC certification exam, you must be at least 18 years old and possess a high school diploma or equivalent.

Is the certification exam voucher included with this program?

Yes, the program includes a voucher for the AMCA Physical Therapy Technician/Aide Certification (PTTC) exam, covering the cost of your certification attempt.

How long does it take to complete the Certified Physical Therapy Aide training program?

This self-paced program consists of 202 course hours. You can complete it at your own speed, fitting it into your schedule.

Which certification exam does this program prepare me for?

This program specifically prepares you for the AMCA Physical Therapy Technician/Aide Certification (PTTC) exam, a nationally recognized credential.

What kind of job can I get after completing this program?

Upon successful completion and certification, you will be qualified for roles such as a Certified Physical Therapy Aide, Rehabilitation Aide, or Clinic Support Specialist in various healthcare settings like physical therapist offices, hospitals, and nursing homes.
