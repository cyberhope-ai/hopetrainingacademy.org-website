What is this field?

This comprehensive career training program provides a robust foundation in essential information technology concepts, preparing you for a dynamic career in the rapidly evolving tech industry. You'll gain expertise in critical areas like PC hardware, operating systems, applications, networking, programming, databases, and security, culminating in preparation for both the CompTIA TECH+ and A+ certification exams.

Think of it like learning the fundamentals of driving and basic car mechanics before becoming a professional driver or auto technician. The CompTIA TECH+ credential ensures you understand the 'rules of the road' for IT, while the CompTIA A+ certification equips you with the practical 'under-the-hood' skills to diagnose, troubleshoot, and maintain computer systems, making you an indispensable asset in any organization.

How the field grew

1993CompTIA A+ certification launched, establishing a global benchmark for IT support professionals.
2018CompTIA introduced the ITF+ certification, a foundational credential designed for aspiring IT professionals.
RecentCompTIA rebranded its foundational ITF+ certification to TECH+, reflecting its expanded scope in essential IT knowledge.
