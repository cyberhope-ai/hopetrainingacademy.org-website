What is this field?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from digital attacks, damage, or unauthorized access. It's a critical field that safeguards everything from personal privacy to national security, ensuring the integrity and availability of information in an increasingly connected world. Professionals in this domain are the digital guardians, constantly evolving their strategies to counter sophisticated threats.

This introductory program is designed to help you explore the foundational concepts of cybersecurity and understand the diverse range of job roles available within the industry. Think of it as a guided tour through a complex digital landscape, showing you the different paths you can take and helping you determine which direction best suits your interests and skills, without requiring prior technical experience.

How the field grew

1970sEarly ARPANET security concerns and the first computer viruses emerge.
1980sThe rise of personal computers leads to more widespread malware and the development of antivirus software.
1990sThe internet boom and e-commerce necessitate greater focus on network and data security.
2000sSophisticated cybercrime, data breaches, and the emergence of nation-state sponsored attacks become prevalent.
2010s-PresentCloud computing, IoT, and AI introduce new attack surfaces, driving demand for advanced cybersecurity professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a college degree to start a career in cybersecurity?

While a degree can be beneficial, many entry-level cybersecurity roles prioritize practical skills and certifications. This course helps you explore various pathways that don't always require a traditional degree.

Does this course include an exam voucher?

No, this introductory course is designed for career exploration and does not include an exam voucher for a certification.

How long will it take to complete this course?

This is a self-paced, introductory course. The time it takes to complete will depend on your individual learning speed and schedule, allowing you to fit it into your life.

Does this course prepare me for a specific certification exam?

This course focuses on exploring the cybersecurity field and potential career paths, rather than preparing you for a specific certification exam. It's your first step to understanding the landscape.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

This course helps you understand the diverse landscape of cybersecurity jobs and identify which roles might be a good fit for you, serving as a foundational step in your career journey. It's designed to inform your next steps, not guarantee a specific job immediately.
