What is this field?

Guided reading is a powerful instructional approach where a teacher works with a small group of students who read at similar levels, providing targeted support as they engage with a text. This allows for personalized instruction, helping students develop reading strategies, comprehension skills, and fluency in a supportive environment.

Differentiated instruction, on the other hand, is a teaching philosophy that recognizes and responds to students' diverse learning needs. It involves tailoring content, process, products, and learning environment to ensure all students can access the curriculum and achieve success. Think of it like a master chef adjusting a recipe for different dietary needs while still delivering a delicious meal for everyone.
