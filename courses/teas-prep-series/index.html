What is this field?

The Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized, multiple-choice exam designed to assess a prospective student's academic readiness for health science programs, particularly nursing. It evaluates foundational knowledge in reading, mathematics, science, and English and language usage, ensuring you possess the core competencies necessary to succeed in a rigorous healthcare curriculum.

Think of the TEAS exam as a crucial academic 'fitness test' before you embark on your nursing career journey. Just as an athlete needs to demonstrate basic strength and endurance before advanced training, the TEAS ensures you have the fundamental academic muscle to tackle the demanding coursework of nursing school. Our program helps you build that strength, making your path to a healthcare career smoother and more confident.

How the field grew

Early 2000sIntroduction of the Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) by ATI (Assessment Technologies Institute) to standardize nursing program admissions nationwide.
Ongoing EvolutionRegular updates ensure the TEAS exam remains aligned with current nursing education standards and the evolving demands of the healthcare industry.
Present DayThe TEAS exam serves as a critical benchmark, assessing foundational academic skills vital for success in demanding healthcare training programs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a degree to enroll in this TEAS Prep Series?

No, this program is designed to prepare you for the TEAS exam, which is typically a prerequisite for admission into nursing and allied health programs, not a requirement for enrollment in our prep course.

Is an exam voucher included with this course?

An exam voucher for the TEAS test is not included with the purchase of this prep series. You will need to register and pay for the TEAS exam separately through the official testing provider.

How long does it take to complete the TEAS Prep Series?

This is a self-paced online program, allowing you to study at your own convenience and speed. The time it takes to complete will depend on your individual study habits and prior knowledge.

Does this course prepare me for the current TEAS exam?

Yes, this TEAS Prep Series is designed to cover the content areas assessed on the current Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) exam, ensuring you are well-prepared for the latest version.

What kind of job can I get after completing this course?

This course prepares you to pass the TEAS exam, which is a crucial step for gaining admission into nursing programs (e.g., for Registered Nurses or Licensed Practical Nurses) and other allied health fields. Your job prospects will depend on the healthcare program you pursue after passing the TEAS.
